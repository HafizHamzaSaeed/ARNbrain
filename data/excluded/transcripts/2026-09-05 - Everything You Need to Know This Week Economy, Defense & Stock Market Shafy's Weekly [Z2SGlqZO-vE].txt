Agli khabar hamare paas aa rahi hai ke government jo hai woh diesel ke margins ko kam karne ka soch rahi hai jo ke refineries ko milte hain. To hukoomat chahti hai ke diesel ki price ko kam kiya jaye is liye ek dafa phir se yeh baat ki ja rahi hai ke jo margin hai us ko kam kiya jayega hamari local refineries ke liye. Government ka kehna hai ke yeh 30 dollar per barrel ka jo margin hai yeh bhi bohat reasonable hai lekin jo refineries hain un ka kehna hai ke yeh is waqt hamein jo zyada margin mil raha hai is se hamein help out hogi aage jo humne refineries ki upgrades karni hain us mein. To yeh news aane ke baad jo hai woh refineries ke jo stock price thi, us mein bhi hamein kaafi kami dekhne ko mili lekin market close hone ke baad jo government hai us ne yeh jo saari speculation chal rahi thi, government ne kaha hai ke yeh speculations hain aur totally deny kar diya ke aisi koi baat nahi hai, hum jo hai woh yeh margins ko cap karne ka bilkul nahi soch rahe. To most likely hamein next week mein yeh clear pata chal jayega ke jo refineries ke margins hain diesel ke upar, woh cap hone ja rahe hain ya nahi.
