In charon problems ke bahut hi aasan hal hain, aaiye aapko ek ek karke batate hain.

First two problems ka ek hi hal hai. Aapne kisi bhi rishtedar, janne wale ya kisi bhi third-party ko apni investment nahi deni. Government of Pakistan ne ek special category banayi hui hai jo ke Stock Exchange aur SECP registered broker kehlata hai. Aap sirf inke sath hoi account khol sakte hain. List of registered SECP and PSX brokers aasani se aapko internet pe mil jayegi. Even agar aapne ek registered broker ke sath account khulwaya hua hai aur kabhi bhi wo aapko kahe ke "Sir, is baar thodi problem hai, kisi aur account mein paise jama kara dein ya cash de dein to bahut acha ho jaega." Aapne foran alert ho jana hai. Hukumat-e-Pakistan ki guidelines ke mutabiq aap even registered stock brokers ko sirf unki company ke account mein bank ke through cross cheque se payment karenge. Second and very important, aapne sabse pehle ek CDC investor account kholna hai. CDC investor account ek personal bank account ke barabar hai, jismein aapko full custody milti hai apne saare shares ki. To next aap jab bhi koi share khareedein aur do din baad uski jab settlement ho to aapne foran apne shares CDC investor account mein transfer karwane hain. CDC yaani ke Central Depository Company ki ek bahut hi user-friendly website aur app bhi hai. Yahan tak ke agar aap bahar ke mulk travel bhi kar rahe hain aur emergency mein aapko apne koi stocks bechne pad jayein to aap aasani se apne mobile ke through apne shares apne broker ke account mein transfer kar sakte hain. CDC ek hukumati idara hai aur inki ek helpline bhi hai, agar aapko koi bhi problem ho aap inko call karke ek se do minute mein solution bhi le sakte hain. Aap soch rahe honge ke jab mein property khareedta hoon to mein broker ko sirf uski fees deta hoon, mein usko puri property ki value nahi thama deta ke mere behalf pe ja ke seller ko de dena, to jab aap shares khareed rahe ho to is baat ka dhyan rakhein aur apne saare shares CDC investor account mein rakhein. CDC investor account mein aapke shares bilkul secured aur aapki full custody mein hote hain. CDC investor account ki fees bahut hi nominal, taqreeban 0.0004% jab bhi aap apne shares transfer karte hain aur ye aapko kisi bhi brokerage house related fraud se protect karti hai. Pichle kuch saalon mein NCCPL ne bahut zyada security features pe kaam kiya hai, jaise ke aap koi bhi shares buy, sell karein to aapke mobile pe message aa jata hai, lekin phir bhi experts batate hain ke abhi bhi kuch tareeqe hain jiske through brokerage house aapke account mein maujood shares ko sell karke aapki investment le kar bhag sakte hain. So is cheez ka best solution yehi hai ke aap apne shares ki custody apne pass khud rakhein aur ek CDC investor account open karwayein. CDC ki branch Pakistan ke har bade shehar mein maujood hai aur jin documents ke sath aapne apna brokerage account khulwaya hai, unhi documents pe aapka CDC investor account bhi khul jayega aasani se.

Ab baat kar lete hain third type of fraud ki and I feel a lot of retail investors would have experienced this. Hum sabne kisi na kisi time pe ek aise share mein zaroor invest kiya hoga jiski share price bahut kam thi, ye soch ke ke thode se paison mein main is company ke lakhon share ka malik ban sakta hoon. Ek cheez aapne aaj ghaur se samajh leni hai. Ek company ki share price zyada ho ya kam ho, is se koi farq nahi padta, asal cheez hoti hai market cap, yaani ke puri company ki value. Misal ke tor pe, ek company hai jismein 10 arab shares hain aur agar uska share sirf 5 rupay ka hai to uski total market value 50 arab rupay ban jati hai, lekin ek dusri company jiske 10 million shares hain aur uska share 1000 rupay ka hai tab bhi uska market cap sirf 10 billion rupay banta hai. So please ye note kar lein ke share price is not important, market value is the real thing. Secondly, hum aapko teen tips denge jismein aap sirf 5 minute mein ye pata laga sakte hain ke whether this company is risky or not. First is leverage, aap zaroor check karein ke is company ki total liabilities ya phir isne bank se kitna qarza liya hua hai. Agar to company ki liabilities bahut high hain to ho sakta hai aaj se kuch saalon baad ye company default kar jaye. Secondly, aap zaroor check karein ke company ka auditor kaun hai. Agar to inka auditor koi gumnam company hai to it's best to avoid this company. Aap ye bhi check karein ke is company ne last 5 years mein kitne dividends pay kiye hain. Agar kisi company ne last 5 years mein koi bhi dividend pay nahi kiya to ho sakta hai is company mein cash flow ki problems hon. In teen criteria ka hargiz ye matlab nahi hai ke ye companies future mein acha return nahi de sakti but kyunki aap ek financial expert nahi hain and as a part-time investor, aap in red flags ko importance zaroor dein.
