Pakistan ka generation segment single buyer model pe operate karta hai, yaani ke is market mein sirf ek khareedar hai aur wo hai Hukumat-e-Pakistan. Isi wajah se Hukumat-e-Pakistan ko single buyer model ke andar jo bhi power plant lagta hai us ko ek capacity payment pay karni hoti hai, taake wo apni investment recover kar sake. Distribution segment mein, K-Electric ke ilawa, almost saare DISCOs hukumat ki malkiyat ke andar hain. Is segment mein asal issue hai bijli ki chori aur bills ki recovery. Distribution segment mein losses bahut zyada hain. Ab humne bijli ka system achhi tareeqe se samajh liya. Ab dekhte hain ke har stage pe main masail kya hain. Generation side pe bijli mehangi hone ki char main wajuhaat hain. Mehanga ya phir imported fuel. Aap jaise ke abhi tak samajh gaye honge ke ek investor jo ke power plant mein invest kar raha hai, us ko sirf apna plant available karna hai. Usay is se koi gharz nahi hai ke wo bijli mehangi produce kare ya sasti. Jab tak us ka plant available hai, us ko capacity payment milti rahegi. Isi wajah se IPPs mein investors imported fuel ko tarjeeh dete hain, kyunke koi bhi problem ho jaaye, un ki fuel ki supply secure rehti hai.

Doosri problem hai over-invoicing in cost of plants. Aap ne zaroor suna hoga ke ek plant jo ke duniya ke kisi doosre hisse mein 1 billion dollar ka lagta hai, wo Pakistan mein dedh billion dollar ka lagaya gaya. To hum is baat ko nahi bhula sakte ke ho sakta hai is mein investors ne over-invoicing ki ho aur hukumat ne koi correction bhi ki ho. Agar ek dafa investor ne hukumat se ek additional cost approve kara li, to wo to agle 25 se 30 saalon ke liye us ne ek higher capacity payment lock kar li.

Third reason is loan duration. Pakistan mein jo nuclear power plants install hue hain un ki life 50 se 60 saal hai, lekin un ke liye jo bankon se qarza liya gaya wo 12 saal mein ada karna hai. To isi wajah se shuru ke 12 saalon mein capacity payments ki amount bahut zyada ho jaati hai.

Chotha reason hai the balance between load shedding and adding too many plants. Hum ye behas roz karte hain ke kya zaroorat thi itne zyada plants lagane ki?
