Let's start with the famous P/E ratio.

P/E ratio yaani ke price-to-earnings ratio. Aasan alfaaz mein, agar ek business ek saal mein Rs.1 earn kar raha hai, to aaj us ki price kitne times hai. All things equal, agar ek company ka P/E ratio is lower than the other company, it is considered to be a better investment. But as you know, all things are never equal. P/E ratio mein price is determined by the market, so ek cheez jis mein saari confusion lie karti hai, that is the earnings.

First of all, there are two types of earnings: trailing earnings and forward earnings. In our experience, because stock market is forward looking, it is better to use forward earnings, yaani ke ek company next year mein kitna earn karne wali hai. Kisi bhi company ki reported earnings aap ko teen tareeqon se deceive kar sakti hain.

First of all is the cash flow problem. Kisi bhi company ne agar Rs.10 ki earnings show ki hain, to zaroori nahi hai us ko Rs.10 cash mein bhi receive hua ho. Is ki example hai Pakistan ka power sector jahan pe circular debt ke bohat se problems hain. Is ke ilawa bhi, even textile companies mein, bohat dafa jab wo munafa kamati hain, to wo munafa actually reinvest ho jata hai in buying higher priced cotton, jis ko hum inventory management kehte hain. Similarly, OMCs jo ke petrol pumps operate karne ke business mein hain, kabhi bhi agar un ko oil ki price barhne ki wajah se profit ho, to un ko next time mehanga oil bhi khareedna parta hai. Isi wajah se reported earnings can have an element of cash flow problems.

Second aspect jo P/E ratio cover nahi karta, that is debt repayments. Profit and loss accounts mein finance cost to cover ho jati hai, lekin loan repayments cover nahi hotin. Isiliye ek aisa business jis pe bohat zyada karze hain, us ka P/E ratio ek aise business se compare karna jo ke debt free hai, ek fair comparison nahi hoga.

And third aspect is cyclical earnings. To counter this problem, we always encourage you to use an average of last five years earnings. Agar aap in teen aspects ko combine kar lein aur actually dekh lein ke earnings mein kya kya problems ho sakti hain, so then P/E ratio becomes a very good indicator.

Our second ratio is operating margin. Ye ratio humein batata hai ke ek business ki profitability kaisi hai. To after deducting all its costs, business ko kitne paise bachte hain. This ratio is much better than gross profit margin kyunki kuch industries mein operational costs are much higher than cost of goods sold. Jab bhi aap do companies ki profitability compare kar rahe hon, to best ratio to use is operating margin. Ye ratio business ki efficiencies ko portray karta hai, lekin is ratio mein bhi ek problem hai. Aaiye aap ko explain karte hain.

Operating margin does not take into account the asset turnover ratio. For example, ek factory hai jis ko lagane mein 1 billion ka capex hota hai aur jab wo factory operate karti hai to wo 1 billion ka revenue generate karti hai, aur ye company kaafi efficient hai aur is ka 25% operating margin hai. So 1 billion ki investment kar ke aap ne 250 million ka operating profit kama liya.

Second example ek shop ki hai jis mein aap ki investment sirf Rs.1 crore ki hogi, lekin aap us 1 crore invest kar ke saal ka 10 crore ka revenue generate kar sakte hain, aur agar aap ka operating margin sirf 5% bhi hai, to bhi aap 50 lakh operating profit kama rahe hain har saal jo ke almost 50% of your investment hai. So jab bhi aap operating margin ko dekh rahe hon, always look at the asset turnover ratio as well. Jab aap in dono ko combine kar lenge, so you will actually know how much profit the business is making relative to the investment made in the business.

So this example shows us ke agar koi company us ka operating margin lower bhi hai but us ka asset turnover ratio bohat zyada hai, that is actually more profitable and is earning more profits on its invested capital. Pro tip for Pakistan: Pakistan mein bohat saari companies aisi hain jinhon ne plant mein investment 1980s ya even 1970s mein ki thi, to jab bhi aap kisi ratio mein fixed assets ka portion dekhein, to make sure ke aap fixed assets ki value aaj ke hisab se rakhein. In ratios mein jab aap plant ki value replacement cost ke hisab se include karenge, to phir aap ko ek behtar picture portray hogi ke future mein agar ye business expand hota hai, to us ki profitability kya hogi.

Second and a very important financial ratio is cash conversion cycle. This ratio covers ke jis din aap ne apne raw materials order kiye to the day jis din aap ke customer ne aap ko paise diye, wo time period kitna hai. Financial language mein is ko average debtor days plus average stock days minus average creditor days se calculate kiya jata hai. Ek high cash conversion cycle ka matlab hai ke aap business mein jo profits earn kar rahe hain, wo aap tak pohanchne mein bohat zyada time lagega. Aur us se bhi zyada important cheez, agar aap ne is business ko two times, three times expand karna hai, to aap ko us ke liye mazeed working capital required hoga. In my experience, I have seen very few amazing businesses with a cash conversion cycle of more than 45 days. Kuch asset-light businesses maine zaroor dekhein hain, jin ka cash conversion cycle 30 to 40 days hai, but maine aisa koi bhi business nahi dekha jis mein plant mein investment bhi zyada ho aur phir cash conversion cycle bhi lamba ho, aur us ke baad bhi wo apne invested capital pe acche returns kama sakein.

Ab aap soch rahe honge ke agar long cash conversion cycle itna bura hai, to agar kisi company ka negative cash cycle ho to wo to zabardast company hai. Aap bilkul theek soch rahe hain. Duniya ki do top companies Apple aur Amazon, dono ka negative cash cycle hai. Ye apne customer se advance mein paise lete hain aur apne supplier ko do se teen mahine baad paise dete hain. Pakistan ke case mein koi bhi aisa product jis mein aap ko advance mein payment karni ho, wo ek negative cash cycle wala business ho sakta hai. Kuch examples jo mind mein aati hain, wo hain cars, bikes, tractors. In teeno examples mein most of the time customer advance mein paise de raha hota hai, aur usi cash se business jo hai apne raw materials order kar raha hota hai. Negative cash cycle ke faide bohat zyada hain, but ek nuksan bhi hai. Agar aap ke paas pricing power nahi hai aur agar aap ke raw materials mehnge ho gaye, to phir aap ka to loss ho jayega.

So jab bhi aap dekhein ke kisi company ke paas negative cash cycle hai, to zaroor check karein kya un ke paas pricing power bhi hai ke nahi, otherwise aap ki company mein raw material mehnga hone ki wajah se loss bhi ho sakta hai. Pricing power is tarah se check karte hain ke agar kisi company ki cost barh jaye, to can it pass it on to the customer? Humne dekha hai, jab bhi dollar mehnga hota hai, foran do din nahi guzarte aur gaariyon ki companies apni price barha deti hain. That means they have the pricing power.

Our number one investing ratio is... return on invested capital, and especially future return on invested capital.

Is ratio ko agar aasan alfaaz mein samajhna ho, so it means ke ab aap is business mein agar Rs.100 ki investment karenge, to is pe aap ko kya return milega. Agar ek business ka future ROIC 25% hai, so that means ke us business ki value next 10 years mein 10 times ho jayegi. Agar aap thodi der ke liye stock market ki volatility ko bhool jayein, aur next 10 to 20 years ka sochein, to I can guarantee you ke ek business ka jo future ROIC hoga, wahi us ki share price growth hogi. Kyunki 10 se 20 saalon mein jo stock price increase hoti hai, that is actually reflected by the increase in the value of the business. Agar aap ne koi aisa business dhoond liya hai, jis ka future ROIC 35% hai, this means you have found a 100x multi-bagger in the next 15 years.

Ab aap search kar rahe honge ke ye indicator milta kahan se hai? Foran main ye indicator nikalun aur apni saari companies check karun ke mujhe future mein kitna return dengi. Ye ratio aap ko kisi bhi paid website pe bhi nahi milega. Yahan tak ke Bloomberg Terminal jis ki fees hazaron dollars mein hoti hai, wo bhi aap ko ye ratio calculate kar ke nahi de sakta. Is ratio ko calculate karne ke liye aap ko poora business samajhna parta hai. Past ROIC will not reflect future ROIC, so please don't fool yourself ke mere business ka past ROIC bohat accha hai to future mein bhi wo accha hoga. This is a dynamic world, things keep changing, aur future business growth will depend on future economics of the business. This is why we always say invest in what you fully understand. Pareshan bilkul mat hon, abhi hum aap ko batayenge ke ye ratio aap easily kaise calculate kar sakte hain aur different industries mein aap is ko kaise apply kar sakte hain.

Future return on invested capital ke do portions hain: numerator mein aata hai future profitability, aur denominator mein aata hai the amount of invested capital required to earn that profit. Capital requirement ke teen aspects hain: first is plant and machinery, second is working capital, and third is ke koi bhi one-off expense jo ke aap ko maybe ek technology acquire karne mein required ho ya phir aap ko apne plant pe koi ek aisi machinery lagani hai jo ke one-time expense hai. In teeno ko jab aap combine kar lenge, to aap ko invested capital ki requirement ki figure mil jayegi, aur aap aasani se future profitability ko is figure se divide kar ke is business ka future ROIC nikal sakte hain. Main aap ko thoda sa explain kar deta hun.

Let's say aap ek cement ki company analyze kar rahe hain. Is case mein invested capital ki amount zyada tar plant and machinery mein investment se aayegi. Cement companies ka working capital cycle itna lamba nahi hota, aur na hi koi aisi technology acquire karni hoti hai with the expansion in each plant. So is particular industry mein future ROIC bohat aasani se estimate kar sakte hain by dividing the future profits by the cost of the plant.

Oil and gas exploration companies mein ye thoda complicated hai. Is mein aap ko samajhna parega ke oil drill karne ki cost kya hai, wo success ratio kya hai ke jab ek company oil well drill karti hai, to udhar se oil nikalne ke chances kya hain. In sab cheezon ko jab aap samajh lenge, to phir aap oil and gas companies ka future ROIC calculate kar sakte hain. High ROIC ko yaani ke 20% plus ROIC ko Warren Buffett ki language mein moat kehte hain ke is business ki apni industry mein itni acchi positioning hai ke ye future mein bohat high profits earn kar sakta hai. Main aap ko ek investing advice dunga. Jab bhi aap ko koi aisa business mile jis ka future ROIC 30% plus ho, to phir aap us ko buy sell karne ki koshish na kijiyega. Ye business apni value hi itni tezi se barha raha hota hai ke it's best to remain invested in this business. Agar aap is business mein invested rahenge, to next 20 years mein aap ki investment 200 times ho jayegi.
