ARN: Ab hum aapko batate hain hamare three rules which will help you decide ke aapke portfolio mein number of stocks kitnay honay chahiye. First rule is, how much of your net worth ya phir savings are you investing in the stock market? If you are a businessman, the correct metric is percentage of your non-business net worth. If the answer is above 50% then you should have minimum seven to eight stocks in your portfolio. Agar aapne less than 25% of your net worth stock market mein invest ki hui hai, then you should have no more than five stocks in your portfolio. Let me explain why. Stocks are nothing but businesses. You are an equal partner but aapke paas management control nahi hai. Let's assume aapne 30 companies research ki hain aur aap unko arrange kar lete hain in descending order in terms of their upside. Aapki jo top five companies hongi, wo probably aapko next 20 years mein 100 se 200 times returns de rahi hongi. Jo next top companies hongi, wo probably aapko 50 se 100 times returns de rahi hongi in 20 years. Aap jaise jaise apne portfolio mein more and more companies add karte jayenge, aapke portfolio ka average return kam hota jayega. Peter Lynch ek famous investor, is process ko "diversification" ke bajaye "diworseification" kehte hain. Yani ke, aap apne portfolio mein worse and worse businesses add karte ja rahe hain. Agar aap less than 25% of your net worth stock market mein invest kar rahe hain, to iska matlab hai aapne already kaafi diversification outside of stock market kar li hai. Probably aapke paas thodi bohot real estate, thodi bohot fixed income already part of your portfolio hai, to phir jab aap stock market ka portfolio construct kare, so you should focus on your best ideas. Is case mein agar aapke portfolio mein teen se paanch stocks bhi honge, tab bhi each stock is less than 10% of your net worth. If you are like me and you are investing more than 50% of your savings in the stock market, phir mein aapko recommend karunga ke aap minimum seven to eight stocks rakhein apne portfolio mein. Ab aap soch rahe honge ke why seven to eight companies? Iske peeche do reasons hain. First is, jab aap seven to eight companies apne portfolio mein add karte hain, from 300 listed companies, to aap top companies add kar rahe hote hain. Aap diversification nahi kar rahe hote. Wo company jo ke aapki list mein seven to eight number pe aati hai, wo bhi bohot accha return de rahi hoti hai. And the second reason is, value realization. Aapko samajhna hoga ke as minority shareholders, we do not control value realization. Value realization means, ke aapne jis company mein invest kiya hua hai, stock market uski fair value ko kab recognize karegi. To agar aapne apni net worth ka 50% se zyada stock market mein invest kiya hua hai, to probably aap har hafte ya har mahine to zaroor apna portfolio check karte honge. Aur agar aapke portfolio mein teen se char companies hongi, to aisa ho sakta hai ke stock market badhti jaye but aapki companies kuch time ke liye na barhein. This can lead to anxiety and can lead to wrong decisions. Is cheez se bachna bohot zaroori hai, isiliye I recommend investing in seven to eight companies kyunke aisa bhi ho sakta hai ke wo company jo ke aapki list mein eighth number pe aati hai, wo kisi wajah se sabse zyada return de jaye in the short term. So, having seven to eight companies in your portfolio gives you the flexibility without compromising on returns. Our second rule is, are you investing a lump sum amount, ya phir aap over the next two to three years stock market mein investment karne ka plan rakhte hain? Our third rule is, kuch aise situations which can give you the liberty to break the first two rules, aaiye dekhte hain. Our first exception is, you are no longer a minority shareholder. Agar aapka stake kisi bhi business mein ek aise level pe pahunch gaya hai, that you are part of the decision making process, phir aapko itni zyada diversification karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Probably aapke case mein three to four companies are more than enough, jaise ke Charlie Munger ka portfolio hota tha.
