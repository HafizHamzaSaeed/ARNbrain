Number 5 on our list is Irrational Exuberance by Robert Shiller. Robert Shiller in his book explains ke duniya mein jahan jahan stock market mein ek massive bull market aayi hai, it has been coupled by a narrative ke ye mulk ya koi aisi technology aane wali hai duniya mein jo ke duniya ko badal degi. Late 1990s mein US tech bubble mein ek aisa rujhan ban gaya tha ke log sochne lag gaye thay ke internet to duniya ko hi badal dega, aur internet ki koi bhi company ho, us ko jis price pe bhi khareedo, wo justified hai. Pakistan ke case mein, ye learnings hum 2017 pe apply kar sakte hain. 2017 mein ek aisa rujhan ban gaya tha ke Pakistan 2030 mein G20 ka part hoga, aur saath hi saath Pakistan emerging markets ke MSCI index mein bhi include hone wala tha. Is future ki story ne Pakistani stock market ko ek boht hi over-valued range mein la khara kiya, aur jab ye cheezein materialize nahi huin to ek boht hi lambi bear market experience karni parhi investors ko.

Second important takeaway from this book is that, is book mein Robert Shiller examples dete hain ke kuch emerging markets mein stock market mein aise aise decline bhi aaye ke un ko apne high tak recover hone mein dollar terms mein 20 saal lag gaye. Ye to hum sab jaante hain ke long term mein stock market sab se zyada return deta hai, but ye sochein kisi ek retail investor ke liye ke kya wo bardasht kar sakta hai aise 20 saal, jis mein stock market continuously dollar terms mein negative perform kar rahi ho? Dusri emerging markets ka trend dekhte hue humein ye seekhna chahiye ke Pakistan mein boom-bust cycles ko predict karna kaafi important hai, aur har retail investor ko in ko study kar ke koi framework banana chahiye taake wo apni investments ko protect kar sakein.

Number 4 on our list is Benjamin Graham's Security Analysis. Benjamin Graham, Warren Buffet ke teacher thay, aur inhone ek aur boht mashhoor book likhi hai, Intelligent Investor, but Security Analysis is a more detailed version of Intelligent Investor. Agar aap seriously investing learn karna chahte hain to I would recommend ke aap ye book cover to cover parhein. This was my first investing book in 2019. Maine apne aap ko ye challenge diya tha ke agar maine ye 800 se 900 pages ki kitab parh li to iska matlab hai investing is my passion. Mujhe ye book itni helpful lagi ke maine is ke 500 page ke appendix bhi parhay, jis mein jo saari important learnings thin, un ko examples ke saath explain kiya gaya tha. Ye book boht hi comprehensive hai. Ye aap ko ye bhi batayegi ke companies kaise accounting terminologies ko bend kar ke apne profits increase kar leti hain, aur saath hi saath ye 1920s ki bull market ko bhi cover karegi aur 1930s ki full bear market ke baare mein bhi aap ko batayegi. I guarantee you agar aap ne ye book parh li to aap ek apna independent investing framework bana sakein ge, aur jo herd mentality stock market mein chalti hai, aap us se stand out kar sakte hain. Is book mein sirf ek short-coming hai, aur us short-coming ko poora karne ke liye I recommend ke aap Intelligent Investor ka Chapter 9 zaroor parhein.

Chapter 9 mein bataya gaya hai ke kaise retail investors ghalat investment fund choose kar lete hain based on short-term results. America ki stock market Pakistan se boht zyada bari hai aur unki history bhi almost 200 years ki hai. To is book mein aap ko bataya jata hai ke zyada tar US investors kya karte hain ke har monthly magazine mein jab investment funds ke returns aate hain to jis fund ne last month mein sab se acha perform kiya hota hai, wo us mein invest kar dete hain, ye soch ke ke is fund ne last month mein acha perform kiya tha to ye next few months mein bhi acha perform karega. But Intelligent Investor ka Chapter 9 aap ko batata hai ke future returns of a fund have no correlation with past returns. Yahan tak ke wo aap ko ye bhi kehte hain ke ek fund manager ke agar do se teen saal ke returns ache hon to zaroori nahi hai ke wo agle do se teen saal mein bhi acha perform karega. Using these learnings, aap kisi bhi fund ko jab choose karein ya kisi bhi financial advisor ko hire karein, to always understand their investing strategy in detail. Past returns are important but they may not necessarily be repeated in the future. To aap unki investing strategy detail mein samjhein and if you are comfortable with the investing strategy, then invest with that fund.

Number 3 on our list is Financial Shenanigans. Jaise ke is book ka title aap ko batata hai, ye book aap ko company ke accounts samajhne mein boht help karegi. Ye company ke accounts is tareeqe se dissect karegi jaise ek surgeon karta hai. Accounting is the language of investing. Ye book aap ko sikhayegi ke kaise aap ne different companies ki financial statements ko ache tareeqe se parhna hai. Ek previous video mein humne aapko bataya tha ke reported earnings can actually be very different from real earnings. Jab aap ye kitab parh lein ge, to you can reach from reported earnings to real earnings in no time. Boht zyada log sochte hain ke ye Pakistani companies hi hain jo ke accounts ko manipulate karti hain, but aap jab ye book parhein ge to aap ko samajh mein aayegi ke ye phenomenon US mein Pakistan se bhi zyada hai. Shayad wahan par companies ka free float boht zyada hota hai aur management pe boht zyada pressure hota hai ke wo apni share price ko elevated level pe rakhein. Isi wajah se US mein jo companies ki managements hain, wo boht hi advanced techniques use karti hain to increase their reported earnings, aur ye book aap ko un sab advanced techniques se bhi familiarize kar degi. Hum sab apni investing mein P/E ratio boht zyada use karte hain. Jab aap is book ko parh lein ge and you can actually reach the real earnings of a company, phir aap ache tareeqe se P/E ratio ko bhi apply kar sakte hain kisi bhi company pe.

Number 2 on our list is The Great Crash 1929. Zyada tar logon ne ye book kabhi nahi parhi, but this is my favorite book. I have read it twice in the last one year to detach myself from the emotions of the stock market. This book is a detailed account, sometimes on a weekly basis, and sometimes on a daily basis of 1928 to 1932. 1928 se 1929 was the peak of the bull market, and 1930 se le ke 1932 was the great depression, jis mein stock market bure tareeqe se crash kar gayi thi. Ye book aap ko detail mein samjhayegi on a daily basis ke bull market mein log kis tareeqe se behave karte hain. Kaise ek bull market ke time pe taxi drivers bhi stocks recommend karna shuru kar dete hain. Ye book aap ko ye bhi batayegi ke us time pe banks aur companies ki managements ne kaise apni companies ke shares ko manipulate kiya taake wo ek short-term mein acha profit bana sakein. Day to day commentary isliye zaroori hai taake aap connect kar sakein ke jab jab stock market mein bull market hoti hai ya bear market hoti hai to log kaise behave karte hain, aur aap in lessons ko Pakistan stock market pe bhi apply kar sakte hain in your daily life. Jab bhi Pakistan mein next bull market aayegi aur log is tarah se behave kar rahe hon ge ke that there is no tomorrow, to you will already be prepared and you will be able to protect your savings. Second half of this book is based on the crash of 1930 till 1932. Is time period mein kaafi companies ki managements pe investigation bhi huin, aur 500 dollar wale share ek ek dollar se bhi kam ke ho gaye thay. If you read in detail about this time period, aap next Pakistan ki bear market ke liye fully prepared ho jayein ge. I am 100% sure agar aap ye book detail mein parhein ge, so this will become your number 1 investing book.
