Number one cheez jo ek investor ko ek budget mein analyze karni chahiye before any specific taxes is macroeconomic stability. Macroeconomic stability ka number one indicator primary surplus hota hai. Aur is budget mein in numbers ke hisab se primary surplus 2% of GDP ban raha hai. Aur expectation yeh hai ke IMF ke saath FY25 ka primary surplus target 1.5% hoga. Yeh sab kyun important hai? Isliye kyunke stock market mein re-rating ka process yani ke P/E ratio ka barhna is highly linked with macroeconomic stability. Aur next video mein hum aap ko detail mein explain karenge ke how primary surplus is the number one indicator of macroeconomic stability. Primary surplus yani ke government jitne bhi total taxes collect karti hai minus all the expenditures excluding interest on debt. Yeh number aap ko primary surplus bata deta hai. A positive primary surplus number indicates ke government fiscal discipline ke sath chal rahi hai. Kaafi log yeh keh rahe hain and rightly so ke yeh jo 13,000 arab ka tax target hai yeh to bilkul achieve nahi hone wala. Aur FBR ne jo naye taxes lagaye hain in the budget, woh taqreeban 1500 arab ke hain. To in calculations ke hisab se bhi 13,000 arab ka target achieve hota hua nazar nahi aa raha. Isliye mini budget to pakka aana hai. Aaiye aap ko batate hain ke shayad aisa na ho aur uski kya wajuhaat hain. Most important number in the budget for IMF programme is primary surplus. Aur jaise ke humne aap ko pehle bataya ke is budget mein IMF ke target ke hisab se taqreeban 0.5% se lekar 0.6% ka buffer already maujood hai yani ke 600 arab ka buffer. To agar hukoomat chahe aur us ko tax collection karne mein dushwari ho rahi hogi, to woh IMF ke saath primary surplus ka target achieve karne ke liye yahan bhi thoda bohat cut laga sakti hai. So, is budget mein kaafi room hai ke agar aap apni tax collection achieve nahi bhi kar paye phir bhi aap primary surplus ka target achieve kar sakte hain. So, let's start from the top. Auto sector mein teen major changes hain. First is ke local hybrid gaariyon pe sales tax has been increased from 8.5% to 25%. Lately, Indus Motors ne Corolla Cross launch ki thi in the hybrid category. Aur Haval brand jo ke Sazgar company ke andar aati hai, woh bhi apni hybrid gaariyan kaafi ache tareeqe se sell kar rahi hai. So, is sales tax ki percentage ki wajah se in dono companies pe impact aa sakta hai. Aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain ke pehle is sales tax policy ki wajah se hybrid gaariyon aur normal gaariyon mein taqreeban das barah lakh rupay ka hi farq tha. Ab yeh farq barh kar 25 se 30 lakh tak bhi ja sakta hai to ho sakta hai ab consumers hybrid gaariyan buy na karein. But aisa zaroori nahi hai. Ho sakta hai hybrid gaariyon ke consumers bohat loyal hon. Aur kyunke is budget mein hybrid imported gaariyon pe bhi duties increase kar di gayi hain to ho sakta hai abhi bhi Corolla Cross aur Sazgar ki hybrid gaariyan in customers ki best choice hon. Ek pareshani ki baat yeh hai ke imported hybrid gaariyon pe jo duties increase hui hain uski announcement to ho gayi hai lekin woh Finance Act mein maujood nahi hai. Aur is cheez ko Ferguson ki budget commentary ne bhi highlight kiya hai. So, most probably yeh final budget mein include ho jayegi but aap is cheez ko follow karte rahein. Agar aap ne check karna ho ke ek company kitni percentage of cars hybrid category mein sell karti hai to aap us company ke latest results mein effective sales tax percentage dekh sakte hain. Agar woh bohat zyada hybrid gaariyan sell kar rahi hogi to uski sales tax percentage kaafi kam hogi. Third change in the auto sector is ke tractors pe ab se 10% sales tax apply hoga. Pehle tractors exempt category mein aate thay jis mein woh taqreeban 6 se 7 percent sales tax pay kar rahe thay aur ab yeh increase 10% tak ho gaya hai. Iska koi bohat zyada impact nahi hoga aur tractor sales will be more determined by agri-economics. Agar to future ki faslein rice and other crops agar woh achi hongi aur farmer ke profit ache honge to tractor sales bhi increase karengi. No major change in cables and white goods manufacturers. Next year mein kyunke interest rates aahista aahista niche aayenge aur economy bhi thodi chalna shuru hogi to inki demand mein increase expected hai. Cement sector pe 1600 arab ka PSDP is a big positive. But isko aap follow karte rahiye ga ke yeh actual mein materialize bhi hota hai ke nahi. Iske ilawa cement bag pe 50 rupay FED barha di gayi hai. Aur sales tax agar hum add kar lein to yeh expected hai ke cement ki bag ke price 60 rupay se barh jayegi. Again, yeh koi bohat bada change nahi hai but it can slow down the recovery in cement sales. But number one factor again will remain agri-economics and construction sector. Agar farmers ka profit accha hoga to woh mehanga cement khareed lenge. Aur agar construction sector mein ek boom aane lag gaya to phir cement ki demand automatically increase ho jayegi. Budget ke sath sath Prime Minister ne ek electricity package bhi announce kiya hai jis mein local industries ko sasti bijli dene ka plan hai. Is step se un companies ka kaafi faida ho sakta hai jo ke grid ki bijli abhi bhi use karti hain. I think Bestway Cement, Kohat Cement, Gharibwal Cement aur Power Cement are still using electricity from the grid. But abhi yeh dekhna hoga ke yeh exact package kis kis industry pe apply hota hai. No major impact on chemical sector. Yeh sector jo hai, zyada tar import substitution pe chalta hai. Aur har har company jo jo product bana rahi hoti hai, uski jo international price hoti hai, that is the number one determinant of their profitability. Chemical sector mein zyada tar companies captive power hi use karti hain. To Prime Minister ke electricity package ka bhi impact shayad bohat zyada na ho. No major impact on the banking sector. IFRS 9 ki provisioning losses ki amount tax purposes ke liye disallow kar di gayi hai, but again nothing big. Two important things for the steel sector in this budget. First is ke jo Prime Minister package electricity ka announce hua hai, is se steel sector ko kaafi faida hoga. Almost saari companies is sector mein grid pe hi operate karti hain. Secondly and very important. FATA/PATA ki sales tax exemption khatam kar di gayi hai. Even though abhi bhi log isko try kar rahe hain ke ek saal ki extension mil jaye, but abhi ke Finance Act ke hisab se yeh exemption khatam ho gayi hai. But keep following the story. Agar yeh exemption khatam ho jati hai to phir steel sector apni pricing bhi behtar tareeqe se kar payega aur shayad uski volume bhi increase ho jaye. So agar yeh dono cheezein implement ho jati hain to next year mein steel sector liye teen positives hain. First is electricity price, second is FATA/PATA exemption and third is declining interest rates. No change for fertilizer sector in the budget. For food companies, 18% sales tax milk pe apply kar diya gaya hai. High chances hain ke yeh measure final budget mein reverse ho jayega. Glass sector ke liye kuch imported glass products pe duty barha di gayi hai but nothing major. Electricity package will definitely help these companies. Insurance sector maine abhi tak detail mein study nahi kiya, so I shouldn't be commenting on that sector. POS pe sales tax percentage has been increased to 18%. So yeh Bata aur Service ke business ko affect kar sakti hai. Exporters pe jo tax laga hai, us se Service Group ki jo footwear exports hain, unki profitability kam ho sakti hai. But Service Group ka jo new venture hai, Service Longmarch Tyres, us pe yeh export tax apply nahi hoga kyunke woh SEZ mein located hain aur unke paas 10 saal ki income tax exemption hai. Har saal budget mein jo dividend ki expectations hoti hain related to OGDC, PPL, woh fulfill nahi hotin. So this is not a very reliable metric. Aur agar yeh theme continue raha to yeh companies bhi accha perform kar sakti hain. Is budget mein PDL ki limit has been increased to 80 rupees per liter. Budget mein imported paper products pe duty increase kar di gayi hai, and this can benefit Packages Limited and Century Paper. Iske ilawa jo small paper segment mein companies hain, unko electricity package se kaafi faida ho sakta hai. Pharma sector mein non-drugs pe sales tax has been increased to 18%. So is sector ke liye aap individual company ko track karte rahein aur dekhte rahein ke uski payments kaise time pe ho rahi hain ya nahi. Refineries mein diesel pe custom duty has been reduced from 11% to 0%. This is a major negative. Aur is se refineries ke profits almost aadhe bhi ho sakte hain. And this can derail the entire refinery policy. But abhi chances yeh hain ke yeh cheez bhi reverse ho jayegi in the final budget. Nothing major for the sugar sector. Mobile phones pe sales tax has been increased from fixed tax regime to 18%. Is measure se Air Link ka business kaafi zyada affect ho sakta hai. Abhi Pakistan mein jo low-end mobile phones thay, un pe kaafi kam sales tax apply hota tha. Aur isi wajah se jab yeh mobile phones locally assemble kiye jaate thay to inki price kaafi kam ho jati thi compared to imported mobile phones. But ab yeh advantage khatam ho gaya hai. So let's see ke Air Link ka business is measure se kaise affect hota hai in the future. Major negative for textile exporters in this budget, especially agar aap ek bohat high margin pe operate karte hain. Pehle exporters jitna bhi export karte thay, us ka fixed 1% income tax dete thay. But ab yeh facility khatam kar di gayi hai, aur ab aap ka jo bhi profit hoga, aap us ke hisab se 39% income tax denge including super tax. So let's say, ek company 15% profit before tax margin pe operate karti hai. Last year us ko 1% fixed tax dena tha, aur thoda bohat super tax dena tha, jis se uski profit after tax percentage taqreeban 13% ban jati thi. But ab, jab 15% profit before tax margin hoga, to profit after tax margin almost 9% हो jayega, aur is se uski profit after tax ki value almost 25 se 30 percent kam ho sakti hai. Again, there are chances ke is step ko reverse kar diya jaye ya phir thodi bohat changes ho jayein. Electricity package will only help companies jin ke paas captive power nahi hai kyunke jo 35 rupay ka rate bataya ja raha hai, woh abhi bhi RLNG aur gas ke blend ke rate se zyada hai. Aur yeh companies jab gas se bijli banati hain, to inko steam free mein mil jati hai. To most probably, agar Rs. 35 ka package diya jayega, to woh companies jin ke paas already captive power maujood hai, un pe koi khaas impact nahi aayega. PIA privatization ke liye ek special measure include kiya gaya hai in the budget. To attract more investors and get a better price for PIA, yeh measure adopt kiya gaya hai ke PIA ke tax losses 10 saal tak adjust ho sakte hain. To jab bhi isko koi private investor khareedega aur next few years mein profit kamayega, to uska tax burden kam ho sakega. Is se yeh nazar aata hai ke hukoomat is serious about PIA privatization.
