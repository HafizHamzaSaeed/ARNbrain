Hamara pehla aur sab se important macroeconomic indicator hai primary surplus. Aaj kal Pakistan mein jo stock market ki rally chal rahi hai aur last one year mein jo almost 100% returns aaye hain uski sab se bari wajah primary surplus hai. Pakistan ne almost 20 saal baad primary surplus achieve kiya hai. Primary surplus ko agar asan alfaz mein samajhna ho to it is the total revenues of the federal government minus all its expenditures excluding interest costs.

Zyadatar economies mein interest rate aur inflation almost similar levels pe hote hain. To koi bhi economy agar primary surplus achieve kar le aur usmein sirf do se teen percent bhi GDP growth ho jaye to over the years uska debt to GDP kam ho jata hai. Is macroeconomic stability ki wajah se stock market mein jo companies hoti hain, investors unpe ek higher P/E ratio dena shuru kar dete hain. They are more confident about future of Pakistan.

Primary surplus ya mamuli sa primary deficit agar koi country achieve kar le to isko fiscal discipline bhi kehte hain. Aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain ke jab jab Pakistan ne primary surplus ya mamuli sa primary deficit achieve kiya hai to stock market ne bohat acha perform kiya hai. Saath hi saath aap ye bhi dekh sakte hain ke Pakistan primary surplus ya mamuli sa primary deficit tabhi achieve karta hai jab wo IMF program mein ho.

Zyadatar log sochte hain ke IMF program ka sab se bara faida ye hota hai ke Pakistan ko dollar mein qarze mil jate hain. But ye ek secondary thing hai. Asal cheez hoti hai ke Pakistani economy mein fiscal discipline aa jata hai aur isi ki wajah se phir doosre lenders jo hote hain wo Pakistan ko loans de dete hain.

Our second most important macroeconomic indicator is interest rates. Warren Buffet kehte hain ke interest rates are like gravity for stock prices. The higher the interest rates, the more they pull down the stock prices. Interest rate kisi bhi company ki stock price ko teen tareeqon se influence karta hai. First is ke interest rate ke barhne se ya girne se us company ka business affect ho jata hai. Us company mein cyclical demand hai jo ke interest rate girne se barh jati hai ya phir interest rate increase hone se kam ho jati hai. Aur kyunke us company ki capacity utilization change ho jati hai interest rate ki wajah se, so us se uske gross margins bhi affect ho jate hain.

Second and very obvious is finance cost. Company ne jo bhi loans liye hote hain agar to interest rate barh jayenge to uski financing cost bhi barh jayegi. Third and the most powerful for stock market is a lower interest rate increases your net present value of future earnings. Ab aap mujhse poochenge ke ek company ki fair value kaise determine karte hain? So, a fair value of a company is the present value of all its future earnings yaani ke ye company future mein jitne bhi profits kamayegi uski aaj ki value is its stock price.

To jab jab interest rate barh jate hain the present value of these future earnings decreases. Main personally ye sochta tha ke stock market investing mein current interest rate ke muqable mein three year interest rate aur five year interest rate zyada important hota hai, but data shows ke Pakistan ke case mein current interest rate ko zyada ahemeyat di jati hai.

World renowned economist aur Nobel Prize winner Robert Shiller apni kitab 'Irrational Exuberance' mein batate hain ke the fair P/E multiple for any market is 20 minus long-term interest rates. But Pakistan ke case mein ye formula 20 minus current interest rates pe zyada ache tareeqe se apply hota hai agar hum last 20 years ka data dekhein. So let's say ke agle ek se do saal mein macroeconomic stability maintain rehti hai aur interest rates 12 se 13 percent ki range mein aa jate hain to hum ye expect kar sakte hain ke Pakistan Stock Exchange ka P/E multiple 7 ki range mein aa sakta hai.

Our third most important macroeconomic indicator is current account deficit. Aur current account deficit is highly linked to oil prices. Is indicator mein hum aapko ye bhi samjhayenge ke kaise ek indicator kuch time pehle tak agar uska relationship stock market se positive tha to ab wo negative mein bhi badal sakta hai aur uski kya kya wajah ho sakti hain.

2000 se lekar 2010 tak Pakistan ka oil import bill uske overall import bill ka taqreeban 15% hota tha aur jab jab oil prices barhti thin to Pakistan ki macroeconomic stability pe sawaliya nishan khara nahi hota tha. Is wajah se jab oil prices 2000 se lekar 2010 tak barh rahi thin aur Pakistan ka current account deficit bhi barh raha tha, phir bhi stock market acha perform kar rahi thi. But is case mein ek second reason bhi hai jo ke is se zyada powerful hai.

Late 2000s mein OGDC aur PPL jo ke hukumat ki do bohat bari companies Stock Exchange pe listed thin, inka weight in KSE-100 index was almost 30% aur us time pe in companies mein circular debt ka problem bhi nahi hota tha aur ye companies ek ache P/E ratio pe trade karti thin. To jab bhi oil prices barhti thin, Pakistan ka current account deficit to barh jata thaa lekin saath hi saath in companies ki earnings bhi barh jati thin aur kyunke inka index mein bohat zyada weight tha, wo stock market ke index ko upar le jata tha.

But ab aisa bilkul nahi hai. Agar hum oil aur LNG ke imports ko combine kar lein kyunke LNG ki prices bhi oil prices se linked hain to ye dono commodities ke imports Pakistan ke overall import bill ka almost 25 to 30% ban jate hain. To jab bhi oil prices barhti hain aur uski wajah se current account deficit barhta hai to Pakistan ki macroeconomic stability pe sawaliya nishan khara ho jata hai aur default ke fears aa jate hain.

Saath hi saath yehi dono companies jinka weight index mein pehle 30% hota tha, ab 7 se 8 percent tak hai aur in companies mein circular debt ki problems bhi aa gayi hain. Jaisa ke aap jaante hain Pakistan ke imports aur exports mein bohat zyada imbalance hai to jab jab oil prices ki wajah se ya kisi aur wajah se current account deficit barhta hai to phir Pakistan ke dollar reserves tezi se kam ho jate hain.

Aur jab ye dollar reserves single digit mein aa jate hain to phir logon ko lagta hai ke Pakistan ka future acha nahi hai aur Pakistan ki macroeconomic stability pe sawaliya nishan uth jata hai. Isi wajah se koi companies jo ke pehle ek rupiya earn kar rahi hoti thin, log unke liye che se saat rupaye price dene ko tayar hote the but ab in fears ki wajah se wo kehte hain ab to hum in companies ke liye teen se char rupaye hi denge kyunke Pakistan ka kya pata do se teen saal baad ye default hi na kar jaye.
