Speaker 1: Achha Abdul Rehman bhai, ek baat to batain mujhe, kya ek retail investor ko IPO mein invest karna chahiye?

ARN: Meri to IPO mein invest karte waqt kaanpein taangti hain, aur is ke teen reasons hain. Pehla reason ye hai ke IPO jabhi aata hai jab ek sponsor ko apni company ki ek achi price mil rahi hoti hai. Koi bhi sponsor se ye expect karna ke woh apni company bohat kam price mein bech dega, ye unrealistic hai. Secondly, jab bhi koi IPO aata hai to uski last 2 years ki books usi waqt pehli dafa reveal hoti hain public ko, to high chances hote hain ke company ne apni taraf se best koshish ki ho apni company ko best possible way mein portray karne ki. And thirdly, jo aap ko IPO sell kar raha hota hai, un ke commissions for an IPO company is 1% of the value. Aur agar woh koi normal share sell karte hain, that is 0.15% of the value. To in teen reasons ki wajah se, jo hai IPO mein invest karna ek retail investor ke liye problematic ho sakta hai. Is ka hargiz ye matlab nahi hai ke har IPO mein aane wali company ek buri investment hai, lekin in teen reasons ki wajah se retail investors ko IPO mein invest karte waqt cautious rehna.
