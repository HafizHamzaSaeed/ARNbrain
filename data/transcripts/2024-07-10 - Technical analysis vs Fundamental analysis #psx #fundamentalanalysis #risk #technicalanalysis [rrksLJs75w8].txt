Speaker 1: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, mujhe ek baat toh batayein, ye technical analysis behtar hai ya fundamental analysis behtar hai?

ARN: Oye technical analysis hoti hai go with the flow. Majorly ye is pe depend karti hai ke dusre participants kisi asset ko buy kar rahe hain ya sell kar rahe hain, toh aap human behavior pe bohot zyada rely kar rahe hote hain. Jo fundamental analysis hoti hai usme investor ye soch raha hota hai ke agar ek asset me earning power hai, toh main isko le lunga mujhe kisi dusre aadmi ki zaroorat nahi hai. Woh usko buy kare ya na kare, thode se time me iski earning sab ko show ho jayegi. Bohot zyada log ye sochte hain ke fundamental analysis long-term hai aur technical short-term, halan ke aisa bilkul nahi hai, aap technical analysis bhi long-term pe kar sakte hain aur fundamental analysis log short-term pe bhi kar sakte hain. Success stories dono ki hain, technical analysis ki bhi hain aur fundamental analysis ki bhi hain, but aisi bohot kam success stories hain jinhone dono ko combine kiya ho.
