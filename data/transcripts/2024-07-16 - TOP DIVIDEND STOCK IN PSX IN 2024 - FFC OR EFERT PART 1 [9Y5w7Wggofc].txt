Subsidy ki definition aise hai ke koi bhi cheez agar aap cost se kam price pe bechenge, to it is technically a subsidy. Agar aap $11 ki LNG $10 pe bhi Punjab mein bechenge, so it is a subsidy. Aur agar aap koi aisi gas jo ke dedh dollar ki cost karti hai, usko aap do dollar pe bhi bech denge, to it is not a subsidy. Yeh cheez aapne khud bhi dekhi hogi, jab bhi IMF program hota hai to exporters ko jo $9 ki gas mil rahi hoti hai, usko most probably discontinue kar diya jata hai because it is a subsidy. So, subsidy is based on the cost of that product. Subsidy has no relation with what your competitor is paying. Ab thodi si baat kar lete hain ke yeh disparity kab tak continue ho sakti hai.
