Speaker 1: Acha Abdur Rehman bhai, interest rate niche aane lag gaye hain. Iska kya effect hoga stock market pe?

ARN: Interest rate niche aane se teen tarikon ke impact hote hain. First, ek company ki sale jo hai affect ho jati hai. Zyaadatar companies ki interest rate niche aane se sale bahut zyada badh jati hai. Ye impact cyclical industries mein bahut obvious hota hai, jaise ki gaariyon ki industry. So, ek dum se aapki sale bhi badh jati hai, aur capacity utilization improve hone se aap ke margins bhi badh jaate hain. Second, most obvious, ke aapki finance cost ek dum se niche aa jati hai aur aapke profits badh jaate hain. Bahut zyada log yehi soch ke saare un companies mein invest kar dete hain jinhone zyada karza liya hota hai. Halanke ye bahut galat strategy hai. Third and the most powerful is ke har company ki present value of future profits, jo ke us company ki fair value hoti hai, wo increase ho jati hai. Even jo banks hain, jinke interest rate niche aane se profits kam ho jate hain lekin unki fair value badh jaati hai, because present value of future profits increase ho jati hai.
