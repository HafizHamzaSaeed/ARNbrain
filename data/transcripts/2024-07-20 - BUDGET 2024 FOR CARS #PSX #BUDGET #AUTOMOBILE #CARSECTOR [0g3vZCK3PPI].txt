**Speaker 1:** Abdur Rehman bhai, maine suna tha ke gaadiyon ki qeemtein barh jaani thin budget wale dinon mein. Magar kuch hua nahi to iska kya impact hoga companies par?

**ARN:** Is budget mein jo imported gaariyan thin, specially 1300 cc se upar, unpe bohat ziada duties barha di gayi hain. Saath-saath jo local gaariyan hain unpe jo registration tax hota hai usko bohat ziada barha diya gaya hai. To gaariyon ki price company level pe to increase nahi hogi, but jo end buyer pay karta hai usmein zaroor increase aayega. Second aur very important jis pe har bande ki nazar thi, aapne pichle kuch mahino mein dekha hoga Pakistan mein hybrid gaariyan bohat ziada locally ban'ne lag gayi hain. To initial budget mein us pe sales tax ki percentage ko increase kar diya gaya tha jo ke final budget mein reverse ho gaya hai. To jo hum expect kar rahe the ke Corolla Cross aur Haval ki jo hybrid gaariyan hain unki price increase honi thi, but ab wo reversal ki wajah se ab nahi hogi. Ye saari cheezein budget se related hain. Is sector mein jo number one theme hoga next year mein wo ye hai ke car sales abhi itni kam hain ke next year jab interest rate neeche aayenge aur devaluation agar nahi hui Pakistani rupee ki, phir car sales improve hongi aur saari companies ko faida hoga.
