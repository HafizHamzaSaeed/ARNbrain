Jab aap FFC ka dividend estimate kar rahe hon to always look at unconsolidated earnings. FFC ne apni doosri companies mein bhi investment ki hui hai aur us company se jo earnings aati hain zaroori nahi hai ke wahan se dividend bhi aaye. To isliye jab bhi aapne FFC ka dividend estimate karna ho to aap uske sirf fertilizer business pe focus karein jo ke unconsolidated results mein show hota hai. Historically, FFC ki payout percentage high hai, but lately yeh jo 70% tak decline aa gaya hai, iski do wajahat ho sakti hain. First is, ke yeh fertilizer company ek apni gas supply ko improve karne ke liye investment kar rahi hain. Engro Fertilizer yeh investment debt le kar kar rahi hai zyada tar, but FFC jo hai, wo koshish kar rahi hai ke ismein apne cash ko bhi use kare, so is wajah se dividend payout thoda kam kar diya hai. Secondly, FFC ki book pe GIDC ki amount Engro Fertilizer se kaafi zyada hai, almost 4 to 5 times. So is amount pe jo fixed income se profit aata hai, maybe FFC is reluctant to pay it out. Aur uski wajah yeh bhi ho sakti hai ke yeh case abhi tak court mein hai aur ho sakta hai company ki soch ho ke kal ko ek aisa faisla bhi aa sakta hai ke company ko court kahe ke aap yeh amount with profit wapas karein.
