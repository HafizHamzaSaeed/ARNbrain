Hamare hisab se ye gas price pe jo difference hai ye itni jaldi hal nahi hone wala aur agar ye continue raha tou FFC ke profits bohat zyada barh jayein ge compared to Engro Fertilizer aur is cheez ko investors ko detail mein samajhna bhi chahiye aur track bhi karna chahiye in the coming months.
