Cement industry ka number one concept: Cement cannot travel. Cement ek bahut hi low value commodity hai jiski price taqreeban 40 se 50 dollar per ton hoti hai. To aapko yeh samajhne ki zaroorat hai ke aap cement ko zyada door road se transport nahi kar sakte, warna aapka jo freight hai woh aapke profit se zyada ho jayega. Zyadatar industries mein aapne dekha hoga ke agar us industry ka raw material taqreeban 10,000 rupay per ton hai to uska finished product taqreeban 20,000 rupay per ton hoga, but cement industry mein yeh hisaab ulta hai. Yahan pe raw material yaani ke coal taqreeban 100 dollar per ton hai, jabke cement jo ke finished product hai taqreeban 40 se 50 dollar per ton hai. Isi wajah se cement companies koshish karti hain ke unka plant demand center ke kareeb ho taake unko apna finished product zyada door transport na karna pade. Transport ki economics to aapko already pata hongi. Aap 1,000 dollar per ton ki cheez transport karayein ya 20 dollar per ton, transport ke charges utne hi hote hain.

Cement cannot travel ke concept ko agar hum industry pe apply karein, to iska yeh matlab hai ke north-based cement plants ki earning power south-based plants se kaafi different ho sakti hai. North of Pakistan ke cement plants naturally imports se protected hain. Yaani ke let's say kal ko agar kabhi Iran se ya Karachi port se cement import ho bhi jata hai, jab tak woh Lahore aur Islamabad pahuchega to uski freight cost hi itni badh gayi hogi ke woh local cement se compete nahi kar payega. This natural protection from imports means ke Pakistan ke jo north ke plants hain woh kabhi bhi apni earning power hamesha ke liye lose nahi kar sakte. Haan yeh ho sakta hai ke kuch saalon ke liye low margins, price war, ya phir low demand ki wajah se unke profits kam ho jayein, but yeh nahi ho sakta ke hamesha ke liye yeh industry shut down pe chali jaye.

South-based plants ki economics thodi different hai. Zyadatar south mein jo expansions hui theen woh yeh soch ke hui theen ke jab CPEC expand hoga to Karachi aur Gwadar mein to bahut zyada development hone wali hai. DG Khan Cement aur Power Cement ne almost 500 million dollar invest kiye the in their cement plants in anticipation ke yahan pe bahut zyada cement ki demand badhne wali hai, but aisa materialize nahi hua. South mein local demand compared to capacity is very low, approximately 40%. But south plants ke paas yeh flexibility hai ke woh apna cement export kar sakte hain. Shipping freight ki economics aisi hain ke shayad Pakistan se agar aapne America cement bhejna ho to uski cost kam ho iske muqable ke agar aapne Karachi se Lahore cement bhejna ho. South-based plants cement export to kar lete hain lekin in exports pe bahut high margins nahi kama paate. Aur iski wajah yeh hai ke global cement industry bahut competitive hai. Long-term basis pe cement export ke margins tabhi increase ho sakte hain agar Pakistan mein imported coal ke muqable mein sasta coal nikal aaye. Kal ko agar Thar coal expand ho jata hai aur yahi cement players Thar coal use karke imported coal ke muqable mein bahut sasta cement bana lenge to aisa ho sakta hai ke export ke margins long-term basis pe increase ho jayein. South plants ke paas jab export ki flexibility hai to phir yeh import se bhi less protected hain. Agar imported cement Iran se ya Karachi port se Pakistan mein aana shuru ho gaya to yeh south-based cement plants ki earnings ko kaafi zyada affect kar sakta hai. To jab bhi aap koi bhi cement ki company analyze karein to apne mind mein yeh zaroor rakhein, the economics of south plants is very different from north-based plants.

Ab hum aapko batayenge ke cement companies ki intrinsic value kya hai. Aapne yeh chart zaroor dekha hoga. Yaani ke agar aapne ek existing facility pe 3 million ton ki capacity add karni ho to yeh aapko taqreeban 180 se 190 million dollar cost karegi. Pakistan stock market mein ek 60 dollar ki cheez 20 dollar pe mil rahi hai, yeh to samajh aata hai. Pakistan mein boom and bust cycles bhi hain, default ka khatra bhi rehta hai, stock market bhi kabhi depressed ho jati hai, but yeh 100 dollar ki kya kahani hai?

Jab bhi koi company expand karti hai aur ek naya cement plant lagati hai to uski cost to similar hoti hai but uski earning power mein bahut zyada difference ho sakta hai. Aur is difference ke chaar main reasons hain. Cement banane ke liye cost-wise two main inputs are electricity and coal. Ek highly efficient plant will use approximately 80 units of electricity aur wahi plant jab 10 se 15 saal purana ho jata hai, woh ek average efficiency ka plant ban jata hai aur tab woh taqreeban 90 se 95 units use karta hai to make one ton of cement. Yeh exact figures aap kisi bhi company ki corporate briefing session mein pooch sakte hain taake aapko pata ho ke aapne jis company mein invest kiya hai woh kitni efficient hai. Dekhne mein to 15 bijli ke unit kuch bhi nahi lagta but aap isko is tarike se sochein ke ek efficient cement company per ton basis pe taqreeban saade teen hazar se leke char hazar profit karti hai aur agar wahi company 15 units extra use karne lag jaye to uska profit taqreeban 1,000 rupay se kam ho sakta hai per ton of cement. Isi wajah se aap sirf yeh mat dekha karein ke company ki capacity kitni hai, yeh bhi dekhein ke uska plant kitni efficient hai. Ek plant jo ke 10 se 15 saal purana hai, woh jab extra units consume karta hai aur uska profit 30% kam hota hai to uski earning power bhi compared to a brand new plant 30% kam ho jati hai. Next aap yeh check karein ke company ki fuel source kya hai yaani ke woh electricity kis cheez se banati hai. Ek company jo ke grid ki electricity use kar rahi hai, uska profit bahut kam hoga compared to a company jo ke solar aur wind se bijli bana rahi hai. Last do saalon mein Lucky Cement ne solar aur wind mein investment karke almost apni 50% of electricity requirement low variable cost units pe le gaye hain. Iske alawa jaise humne aapko shuru mein bataya, cement cannot travel, iska matlab yeh hai ke location is also important. Lastly aap yeh bhi check karein ke company imported coal use karti hai, Afghani coal use karti hai ya local coal use karti hai. Aaj kal inki prices almost equal ho gayi hain but pichle do saalon mein local coal bahut zyada sasta tha.
