Interviewer: Achha Abdul Rehman bhai mujhe ek baat to batayein, yeh jo developments aur news hoti hain aap iske sath updated kaise rehte hain?

ARN: Isme aapko apni daily routine aise rakhni parti hai ke jo bhi news aur developments ho rahi hain aap us se updated rahein. To mein sabse pehle subah uth ke do se teen ghante, aath se nau, Pakistani newspapers parhta hoon jiske business sections mein har tarah ki economy se related news jo hai woh publish ho rahi hoti hai. Aur ek dafa jab aapki achhi understanding ban jaati hai, to aap har cheez ka impact ek company pe easily calculate kar sakte hain. Aur second half of the day mein aap company specific research kar rahe hote hain. Aap company ki annual reports, ya har company ki jab quarterly reports aati hain, to woh detail mein study karte hain. Aur aap yeh continuously dekhte rehte hain ke kaun si company is the best investment for all our investors.
