Speaker 1: Yaar Abdul Rehman bhai mujhe koi ek asan tareeka batayein aisi company dhoondne ka jo meri investment 10 times karde.

ARN: Investment 10 times karne ka sabse asan tareeka hai koi aisi company dhoondhein jo ke apni value khud har saal 25 percent barha rahi ho, to aapki investment khud hi 10 saal mein 10 times ho jayegi. Hum log ek three step process follow karte hain aisi company dhoondne ke liye. Pehle hum uska poora business samajhte hain aur dekhte hain ke is business mein agar 100 rupaay ki investment hoti hai to ye 25 rupaay profit kama sakti hai ke nahi. Phir second step pe hum ye dekhte hain... Second step pe hum ye make sure karte hain ke agar company ke halat bure bhi ho jayein to ye hamein 3 saal mein hamari investment double karke degi ke nahi. To phir humne long term mein bhi make sure karliya ke ye company acchi hai, medium term mein bhi make sure karliya, aur third step mein hum ye dekhte hain ke is company ke last 20 year ke returns kaise hain. Agar teeno step pe hamara 25 percent lock ho jaye phir hum is company mein invest kardete hain.
