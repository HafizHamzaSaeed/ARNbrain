[Published: 2024-07-29]

**ARN:** Shabbir Rehman bhai, mujhe ek baat to batayein, interest rate neeche aane lag gaye hain, cement sector ke liye koi update hai budget mein?

**Shabbir Rehman:** Is budget mein har cement bag pe FED 100 rupay se barha ke 200 ho gayi hai. Toh agar isme sales tax ka impact daal dein, toh ye expected hai ke cement ki bori 120 se 130 mehangi ho jayegi. Jo last two years ka trend hai, wo ye hai ke saari cement ki companies ye cost increase pass on kar deti hain. Cement ki bori mehangi hone ki wajah se ye log sochte hain ke cement ki demand kam ho jayegi, but aisa zaroori nahi hai. Pakistan mein next year mein agar PSDP spend accha hua, aur agriculture sector se bhi profit aane lag gaya logon ko, aur uske sath sath agar urban sector mein bhi thodi boht construction shuru ho gayi, toh log mehanga cement bhi khareed lenge. So is mein main determinant next year ke liye yehi hai ke interest rate kam honge, activity economy mein start hogi thodi boht, aur expected ye hai ke next year cement ki demand theek rahegi, aur companies ke profits already acche hain.
