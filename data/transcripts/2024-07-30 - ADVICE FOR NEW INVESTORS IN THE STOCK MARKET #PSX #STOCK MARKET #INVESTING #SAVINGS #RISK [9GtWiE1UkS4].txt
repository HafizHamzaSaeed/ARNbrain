Interviewer: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, stock market mein naye log aa rahe hain inke liye koi advice to dein.

ARN: Do sabse important galtiyan jo hain jo naye investors aa rahe hain. First yeh hai ke jab naye investors aate hain na woh is cheez ko aise treat karte hain ke ek cheez aaj li das rupay ki, foran usko bara rupay mein bechna hai. Inko chahiye ke yeh business ko samjhein aur yeh jo Pakistan ke top businessmen hain inke saath long-term partners banein. Jo unka return hoga wahi aapka return hoga. Aur aap agar ek IT industry mein kaam karte hain, to aap start karein IT sector mein invest karne se. Aur agar aap power sector mein kaam karte hain, to aap start karein power sector ki companies mein invest karne se. Usme aapki already knowledge hai. Aap yeh na karein ke aap power sector mein kaam karte hain lekin aapne jaake kisi IT sector company mein invest kar diya kyunke uski price badhti ja rahi hai. Aur second, sabse badi galti jo naye investors karte hain woh kya karte hain ke apni net worth ka itna kam percentage invest karte hain ke agar woh double bhi ho jaye, triple bhi ho jaye, to unko koi farq hi nahi padta. To aap apni research pehle sahi tareeqe se karein taake aapke andar confidence aaye, ek framework banayein aur phir ek acchi percentage of your savings stock market mein invest karein.
