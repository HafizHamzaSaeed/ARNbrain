Aaiye aap ko ek bahut hi interesting fact batate hain about Pakistan's power sector. Pakistan mein takreeban 80% of the electricity cost is recovered by bill payments, baaqi jo 20% hai, wo circular debt mein chala jata hai. So, sunne mein toh bahut acha lagta hai ke ek company ko 80% of the payments mil rahi hain, toh iska matlab ye hai ke at least 80% of the portion pe toh dividends aa jayenge. But asal mein aisa nahi hota. First, 30 to 40% of the bills collected goes into fuel payments. Phir, us ke baad jo 25 se 30% portion hota hai, us se plants ki debt repay hoti hain. Because, ye dono cheezein aap delay nahi kar sakte. Us ke baad jo remaining 30% bach jata hai, wo plants ka actual profit hota hai. Aur jab us mein, 20% portion circular debt mein chala jata hai, toh a big portion of the real earnings gets stuck in circular debt. So, next aap kabhi bhi kisi bhi company se sunein, ke 80% of our invoices are being paid, toh aap foran alert ho jaiyega. Ke ho sakta hai ke jo real profits hain, us ka ek good percentage circular debt mein stuck ho raha ho. Kapco ka contract Government of Pakistan ke sath expire ho chuka hai. Even though plant ki life mein abhi bhi das se pandrah saal baaqi hain. Kyunke Pakistan mein already bahut zyada excess capacity maujood hai bijli ki, toh the chances are low ke Kapco ko ek contract extension mile, and specially on high capacity payments. Ye ho sakta hai ke plant ko take-and-pay basis pe extension mil jaye, but take-or-pay basis pe extension milna kaafi mushkil hai. Latest NEPRA petition mein bhi jab Kapco ne apne contract extension ke liye apply kiya tha, toh us mein bhi, a good portion of the capacity was on take-and-pay basis. Contract expiry se pehle, this plant used to get 20 to 25 billion per year capacity payment, aur aaj kal ye plant do se teen arab rupay per year fixed cost apni jeb se de raha hai. Pakistan mein problem ye hai ke expiry ke baad, plant toh aap ki ownership mein rehta hai, but because of single buyer model, aap kisi ko bhi electricity bech nahi sakte. So, future mein, agar single buyer model khatam ho jata hai, toh phir Kapco apni bijli market mein bech kar acha profit kama sakta hai. Government of Pakistan CTBCM pe kaafi kaam kar rahi hai, jis ke through har plant different customers ko apni bijli bech sakega. Ye Kapco ke liye bhi bahut faydemand hoga, aur Hukumat-e-Pakistan ke liye bhi bahut faydemand hoga. Is se capacity payments ka bojh aane wale saalon mein kam ho jayega.
