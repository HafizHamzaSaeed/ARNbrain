Our third reason for not investing in the stock market is, you cannot handle volatility. Stock market mein to aise hi hota hai, kisi din 1000 point upar, to kisi din 2000 point negative. Aur aage bhi aisa hi hota rahega. Agar aap media ki headlines dekh ke ghabra jaate hain, aur apne aap ko control nahi kar paate, aur panic mein aake apne shares sell kar dete hain at the wrong time, then probably, stock market is not for you. Agar ye volatility aap ki health ko affect kar rahi hai, so please don't invest in the stock market. No amount of return is more precious than your health.

Jaise ke hum ne aap ko pehle bhi bataya, ke long term mein stock market ne amazing returns diye hain, much more than real estate and even gold. But is journey mein bhi, last 25 years mein cheh saal aise guzre hain, jis mein aap ko loss experience karna para. For this, my two-point advice would be ke aap quarterly basis pe companies ki reports parhte raha karein, taake aap ko pata ho ke business ki performance kaisi ja rahi hai. And my second advice would be, ke aap headlines parhna chor dein. Main hamesha logon ko kehta hoon, ke ya to aap poora article parhein with the right background knowledge ya phir aap headline bhi na parhein. Ye jo sirf headline parhna hai na, is se aap apna hi nuksaan kar rahe hote hain.

Our fourth reason is, that you are earning more than 30% in your current business. Stock market mein jo top companies listed hain, jinhon ne apne shareholders ko amazing returns diye hain in the last 20 years, unki average takreeban 25% se 30% ki range mein hai in the last 20 years.
