Step number one: Understand the business like owners.

PABC Pakistan ka only aluminum can manufacturer hai. Aap market mein koi bhi canned beverage buy karenge to 90% chance ye hai ke wo can PABC ne banaya hoga. Ye plant 2017 mein Faisalabad mein operational hua tha. It is located in a Special Economic Zone jiski wajah se is company ke paas 10 saal ki income tax exemption hai, aur ye bohot important hai kyunke PABC jo local sales karta hai agar ye tax exemption nahi hoti to phir usko almost 40% income tax dena pdta including super tax. This income tax exemption will expire in 2028.

To fully understand the business, sabse pehle aapko ye estimate pata hona chahiye ke ye plant cost kitne ka karta hai. Ye plant 2017 mein taqreeban 70 to 80 million dollars ka laga tha, aur iske baad last two years mein company ne do minor expansions bhi ki hain jiski cost taqreeban total 25 million dollars thi. Yaani ke, ek total 1.2 billion ka capacity ka plant taqreeban 100 million dollar ka aata hai.

1.2 billion cans is plant ki maximum capacity hai. Because of changeover in designs aur can ke different sizes aur seasonality, iski wajah se ye assume karna theek hoga ke ye plant maximum ek saal mein taqreeban ek billion cans produce kar sakta hai.

Ab thoda aluminum cans ki local demand side dekh lete hain. Pakistan mein taqreeban teen billion single-serve bottles use hoti hain ek saal mein jiska only 10% is aluminum cans. Yaani ke, taqreeban har saal Pakistan mein locally 300 million cans use hote hain. 90% of the single-serve bottles are still plastic in Pakistan.

Main local customers are Pepsi and Coke. Zyadatar log ye sochte hain ke kyunke Pepsi aur Coke itni badi brands hain aur unki bargaining power bhi itni hai to PABC ke to margins bohot kam honge aur Pepsi aur Coke PABC ko 30 se 60 days baad payment karte honge. But cans ki market mein ye cheez sahi nahi hai, aur iski wajah ye hai ke Pakistan mein imported cans pe 30 to 40% duty hai. Isi wajah se PABC import parity price se kom price pe bhi Pepsi Coke ko bech kar accha profit kama leta hai aur iska cash cycle bhi kaafi accha hai.

Export markets mein PABC ki main market Afghanistan hai, and surprisingly Afghanistan mein cans ki demand Pakistan ke mukable mein almost three times hai. Afghanistan mein annually taqreeban 800 million cans use hote hain, aur iski wajah ye hai ke Afghanistan mein the proportion of aluminum cans in the single-serve market is very high.

Ab aayein aapko ek shortcut batate hain jisse aap PABC ke future profits estimate kar sakte hain. Last few years ki company reports padh ke humne ye andaza lagaya hai ke company local cans pe taqreeban dhai cents ka profit karti hai aur export pe taqreeban 1.75 cents ka profit karti hai.

Ye company devaluation se bhi protected hai kyunke ye apna can dollars mein hi bechte hain zyadatar, but the protection is only in terms of pricing. Devaluation ki wajah se jo economy mein slowdown aa jata hai aur uski wajah se jo can sales kam ho jati hain, that affects PABC negatively.

Local sales ka margin 2.5 cents hai aur exports ka taqreeban 1.75. Dekhne mein to ye maamuli sa farq lagta hai but agar aap ghaur se sochein to company is making almost 50% higher profits on local sales.

Afghanistan ki 70% of can demand Kabul se originate karti hai aur PABC Kabul se sabse kareeb can plant hai. Afghanistan export mein ek hi risk hai aur wo ye hai ke kahin Chaman border band na ho jaye. Payment ka risk as such nahi hai kyunke Afghanistan mein bhi PABC multinationals ko hi supply karti hai aur depending on the situation kabhi kabhi to advance payment bhi lene lag jaati hai.

Afghanistan ke alawa PABC Tajikistan, Sri Lanka, Uzbekistan bhi export karti hai but in markets mein profits koi itne zyada nahi hain. Isi wajah se number one determinant of PABC profits is local sales and sales to Afghanistan.

PABC ki sales mein seasonality kaafi zyada hai. Afghanistan aur north of Pakistan mein theek thaak sardi padti hai aur cans ki demand kam ho jati hai. Isi wajah se Q1 and Q4 are weak quarters for the company.

Second step of invest in what you fully understand is value realization.

Value realization isliye zaroori hai ke hum ye company stock market ke alawa kahin aur nahi bech sakte as minority shareholders. Hum is company ki fair value estimate to kar sakte hain but at the end of the day humein ise stock market mein hi sell karna hai, so is wajah se hum is cheez ko bhi kaafi zyada importance dete hain ke aaj se ek se do saal baad is company ki stock market mein kya price ho sakti hai. Is step pe different concepts aa jaate hain jaise ke P ratio, dividend yield, and our concept of moderate market.

Jaise ke aapne dekha hoga, stock exchange mein jo company zyada dividend deti hai uski value realization kaafi acchi ho jati hai, aur iski prime example hai FFC versus EFERT. Engro Fertilizer kyunke apne minority shareholders ko zyada dividend deta hai uski stock price FFC se zyada hai, and this makes sense as well. Minority shareholder to usi cheez se matlab hai na ke usko dividend kitna mil raha hai. Jab usne kisi cheez mein invest kiya hai to wo to ye zaroor dekhega na ke mujhe bank mein cash kitna mil raha hai. So is step mein hum owners ki dividend policy ko kaafi zyada importance dete hain.

PABC ab ek debt-free company hai, but still owners sirf 40 to 50% of earnings payout kar rahe hain. Jaise ke humne apne investing framework mein likha hai, jo owners ki dividend policy hoti hai hum usi ko benchmark bana ke company ki valuation karte hain. Hum ye nahi sochte ke hum share khareed lenge to owners dividend baantna shuru ho jayenge.

Iske alawa aap value realization mein P ratio ko bhi use kar sakte hain. Let's say aaj se do saal baad stock market ka P ratio six hai, to aap conservatively assume kar sakte hain ke PABC ka P ratio at least five to hoga. Har industry mein value realization ka formula thoda different hota hai aur ye aapko us industry ko samajh ke uske shareholders ka behavior dekh ke saari cheezon ko group kar ke ek andaza lagana padta hai.

Jaise ke cement sector ki video mein humne aapko bataya ke cement sector ki value realization is linked to the cost of the plant. In different value realization ke metrics ko use kar ke hum ye andaza lagate hain ke aaj se do saal baad company ki jo earnings hongi uske hisab se stock price aaj se double ho sakti hai ke nahi.

Is step mein hum next year ki demand ko bhi kaafi zyada importance dete hain. Agar next year ki demand thodi weak hai to ho sakta hai stock mein us tareeqe se rally na aaye, lekin agar hum sure hon ke aaj se teen saal baad company ka jo profit hoga uske hisab se share price minimum two se 2.5 to zaroor hogi to phir hum apna 30 to 35% ka return per year lock kar lete hain. Phir humein koi farq nahi padta agar cheh mahine stock na bhi perform karein kyunke humne to teen saal ke liye apna 35% return lock kar liya hai aur kabhi na kabhi is period mein company ki value realization honi zaroori hai.

So first step mein humne poora business detail mein study kiya aur ye andaza lagaya ke going forward in the next five to 10 years ye company 25% pe compound kar sakti hai ke nahi, phir second step mein humne dekha ke do se teen saal ke time horizon pe ye company humein kitna return de rahi hai, aur agar us time pe bhi ye company humein 30% per year return de rahi hai to humne usko bhi lock kar liya.
