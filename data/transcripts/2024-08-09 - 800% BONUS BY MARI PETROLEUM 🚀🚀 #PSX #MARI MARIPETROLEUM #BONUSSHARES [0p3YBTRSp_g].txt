Speaker 1: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, Mari Petroleum ne 800% bonus de diya hai, to iska matlab kya hai, inki price 800% barh jaye gi?

ARN: Nahi nahi, share price 800% nahi barhay gi. Asal mein koi bhi company jab bonus shares deti hai to aik shareholder agar uske paas abhi aik share hai, to jab usko 800% bonus mile ga to uske paas 9 shares aa jayein ge. But agar isko owner's point of view se dekhein to unke liye koi change nahi hota. Owner's point of view se bonus shares ka koi khaas impact nahi hota, but jo log stock market ke through share khareedte hain aur wo minority shareholders hote hain, unke liye itna bara bonus aik value realization event hota hai. Yaani ke aik company agar undervalued chal rahi hai to ye uske liye aik trigger hota hai ke uski asal value saamne aa jaye. Humne historically dekha hai jab bhi itne bare bonus aate hain to stock price mein 20 se le kar 50% tak ki rally ban jati hai. Agar aap is cheez ko aur zyada detail mein understand karna chahte hain aur is company ko bhi samajhna chahte hain to hamari website pe aik one-to-one advisory ka link hai uska form zaroor fill karein.
