Speaker 1: Acha mujhe ek baat to batayein, jo dividend ki announcements aati rehti hain companies ki taraf se, ye iski stock, company ki stock price ko kya effect karti hain?

ARN: Acha iske liye na pehle aap ko samajhna hoga ke investing hai kya. Investing hai ke aaj aap hazar rupay give up kar rahe hain aur aap is liye invest kar rahe hain us ko taake aap ko future mein do hazar rupay milen. So minority shareholders' point of view se dividend announcement jo hai, ek value realization event hota hai. Woh ye sochte hain ke maine is company mein hazar rupay lagaye the, ye company mujhe cash wapas kar rahi hai. Jo banda as minority shareholder invest kar raha hota hai, us ke liye ye ek boht acha trigger hota hai ke company mere sath apne jo profits hain woh share kar rahi hai. Iski downside ye hoti hai ke jo companies ache dividends de rahi hoti hain, us companies mein boht zyada massive under valuation ke chances kam ho jate hain. To agar to ek company ki fair value let's say sau rupay hai, to agar to woh acha dividend de rahi hogi to woh even bear market mein painsath-sattar rupay se neeche nahi jayegi. Lekin jo aisi companies jo let's say dividend nahi de rahin, woh shayad aap ko pachees-tees rupay pe bhi mil jayein aur aap ke paas bari achi opportunity ho ke agar cycle reverse ho to aap us mein 3 to 4x kama sakte hain. To ye kuch features hain jo ke dividend policy se share price pe affect hota hai.
