Speaker 1:
Aik baat to batayein, ye value stocks aur growth stocks mein farq kya hota hai?

ARN:
Main aap ko ek aisa concept samjhata hoon, jis se aap ko khud hi samajh aa jaye ga ke value stocks aur growth stocks mein kya farq hai. Koi bhi company, uski fair value hoti hai, net present value of all its future earnings. Yaani ke aglay paanch se das saal mein ek company ne jo profit banana hai, jo ke us ke shareholders ke saath share hoga, wo uski aaj ki value hoti hai. To jo growth stocks waali companies hoti hain, kyun ke wo grow ho rahi hoti hain aur unki jo total value hoti hai of the company, wo kuch saalon baad us ka contribution ziada hota hai. Is wajah se un ko P/E ratio ziada mil jata hai of current earnings, kyun ke unki jo earnings hain, jo ke teen saal baad, chaar saal baad, un ka bohat ziada contribution hota hai. Asal mein as value investors aap ko ye dekhna hai ke ek company, uski jo total asal value hai, ab wo next year ke profit se ho ya aaj se das saal baad ke profit se ho, aap ne us total value se kam price pe us share ko khareedna hai. To agar aap ko ye concept samajh aa jaye, to har stock jo hai wo value stock hota hai. Ye growth stock aur value stock ek bohat hi bekaar cheez hai.
