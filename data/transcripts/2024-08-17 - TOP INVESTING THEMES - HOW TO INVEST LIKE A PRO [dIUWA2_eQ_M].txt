Agar aapne investing themes samajh liye to aapne risk to reward ratio apne favor mein kar liya. There are six main investing themes in Pakistan Stock Exchange and today we will cover all of them. Aaj hum discuss karenge ke aapne apne portfolio mein different companies ko investing themes ke hisab se kaise distribute karna hai aur sath hi sath hum aap se exact rules share karenge jo hum khud use karte hain in managing our model portfolio. Jab aap apne portfolio ko different investing themes mein distribute kar denge to aapke portfolio ka risk to reward ratio khud hi improve ho jaega aur aapke paas clear rules aa jaenge ke what to expect in the future from different companies in terms of share price volatility and future expected return. Aap apni screen pe humare six investing themes dekh sakte hain. So let's begin with fast growers. Fast growers mein zyada tar local FMCGs, IT companies, retail chains aur upcoming brands aa jati hain. Is investing theme mein zyada tar companies apni early stage par hoti hain. Isi wajah se risk to reward ratio ke hisab se yeh aapko 5 saal mein 10 times returns bhi de sakti hain aur agar inka business model acche tareeqe se replicate nahi hua, to phir yeh -100% return bhi de sakti hain. Is theme ka main rule yeh hai ke yeh companies minimum 2 saal ke liye 30% revenue growth per year or profitability growth per year achieve kar chuki hon. Hum sirf potential ke basis pe invest nahi karte. Even though yeh companies ek early stage pe hain phir bhi hum minimum 2 years ka record chahenge ke yeh companies ne minimum 30% growth achieve ki ho. Aapne sirf numbers ko nahi dekhna, aapne business ki economics samajhni hai aur detail mein jaa ke samajhna hai ke kya yeh business 10 times grow ho sakta hai? Kuch companies jo currently listed hain aur is category mein aati hain, woh hain Image, PABC, Air Link, Systems Limited, Hum Network. Is theme ka ek aur rule yeh hai ke aap jis company mein invest karne ja rahe hain uska market cap 10 se 20 billion ke beech mein hona chahiye ideally aur iski wajah yeh hai ke Pakistan mein 200 billion plus market cap ki companies kafi kam hain aur agar aapne 10 times growth achieve karni hai in the next 5 years to phir aapke paas itna room hona chahiye. 200 billion ke upar companies ka 30% plus pe grow karna kafi mushkil ho jata hai specially agar woh local market mein operate kar rahi hon. So is theme mein aapne make sure karna hai ke agar company local market mein operate karti hai to uska market cap maximum 20 billion ho. Agar to company export karti hai to phir yeh rule apply nahi hota kyun ke global market ka size Pakistan ki market se 1000 times aata hai. Isiliye humne Systems Limited aur PABC jaisi companies ko bhi is category mein include kiya hai. Another rule for this investing theme jo company aap jismein invest karne ja rahe hain uska business capital intensive nahi hona chahiye. Agar to usko growth ke liye ek ke baad ek plant mein invest karne ki zaroorat hai to phir us tareeqe se 30% growth achieve to ho jaegi lekin uske liye jo capital required hoga usmein chances yeh hain ke company baar baar rights issue karegi aur rights issue ki wajah se aapne jo 10x potential is company mein dhoonda tha woh dilute ho jaega. Ab hum aapko batate hain ke is investing theme mein portfolio allocation ke kya rules hain? Initial years mein aap ek company mein maximum 5% invest kar sakte hain. Aur uski wajah yeh hai ke choti companies mein kafi zyada cheezein aapko detail mein study karni hoti hain. To jab tak aap is company ko 2 se 3 saal tak follow nahi kar lenge, aap 5% se zyada invest nahi kar sakte. In 2 se 3 saalon mein aapne do teen cheezon ko kafi detail mein study karna hai. Pehla hai, possibility of off-the-books sales. Agar company off-the-books sales kar rahi hai to phir sponsors ke returns aur minority shareholders ke returns mein kafi farq aa sakta hai aur even though company to grow kar jaye lekin minority shareholders ko shayad fayda na ho. Off-the-books sales check karne ke liye aap uske business ko ko jab study karenge to aapko andaza ho jaega ke off-the-books sales ki possibility kitni hai jaise ke agar koi company kisi multinational ko supply kar rahi hai to phir off-the-books sales ke chance kam hain. Isi business ne agar POS terminal lagaya hua hai on all its retail chains to phir off-the-books sales ke chances kam ho jaate hain. Iske ilawa aap market survey bhi kar sakte hain ke is business ki kya reputation hai. Secondly, aapne sponsors ka vision kafi deeply study karna hai. Aur aapne make sure karna hai ke is company ko adequate attention di ja rahi hai. Bahut dafa ek renowned business group ki ek choti company hoi hai aur investors usmein invest kar dete hain yeh soch ke ke yeh business group to bahut bada hai aur yeh company bhi bahut jald grow kar payegi. To aapne 100% make sure karna hai ke sponsors are 100% committed to this small company kyun ke aisi company mein business economics ke sath sath execution bhi bahut important hai. Ab baat kar lete hain pricing discipline when investing in this theme. Yeh companies bull market mein kafi zyada badh jati hain. Log yeh sochte hain ke yeh company to itni tezi se grow ho rahi hai to iski to valuation bahut zyada honi chahiye. Aur bear market mein yeh companies fully neglected ho jati hain even though woh accha perform kar rahi hoti hain apne business mein. So humare rule yeh hai ke hum aisi kisi bhi company ko maximum wohi P/E pay karte hain jo ke market ka ho but hum yeh P/E forward earnings pe apply karte hain. Forward earnings matlab ke next year mein yeh company kitna profit karne wali hai.

Our second theme is turnarounds. Is theme mein aise businesses aa jate hain jo import parity pe apna product sell karte hain. Global market mein alag alag time pe different products ki shortage chalti rehti hai aur bahut dafa unke margins bahut zyada badh jaate hain. To agar aisi koi company Pakistan mein operate kar rahi ho aur woh apna product import price ke hisab se sell karti ho to global shortage ki wajah se ek do saal ke liye unke profits bahut zyada badh jaate hain. Is category mein zyada tar chemical companies ya imported fertilizers jaise ke FFBL ya phir refineries fall karti hain. Is category ka risk to reward ratio over two years, kyun ke yeh ek medium term play hota hai, is -25% to 500%. Abhi hum aapko explain karenge ke why the risk is only 25% in this category. Aur uski wajah yeh hai ke hum momentum investing kar rahe hain. Hum ek aisi company mein invest karne ja rahe hain jiska next few quarters ka profit pehle se kafi zyada aane wala hai. To kafi kam chances hain ke iski price kam ho jaye. Aaiye aapko example ke sath explain karte hain. EPCL ek aisi company hai jo ke PVC banati hai Pakistan mein. 2020 mein America mein hurricanes ki wajah se do bade producers of PVC, unka plant band ho gaya tha. Aur yeh do producers used to produce 15% of global capacity. Aap is cheez ka andaza aise lagayein ke jab Saudi Arabia 1 million barrel oil ki production cut karta hai jo ke just 1% of global oil production hai to oil price pe kitna zyada impact aa jata hai. To ab sochein duniya mein do producers jo ke 15% produce karte the, agar woh kisi wajah se band ho jayein to uska prices pe kitna impact aayega. Is shutdown ki wajah se PVC margins 400 dollar se takriban 1000 dollar pe chale gaye the aur pehle Engro Polymer ek ton pe takriban 150 se 200 dollar kamata tha aur ab woh 700 800 dollar kamane lag gaya tha jo ke cost to wahi thi na. So is four times of profits jo ke almost ek saal tak chalta raha, company ke profits bahut zyada aa rahe the aur usi time mein company ne dividend bhi pay karna shuru kar diya. Aapko to pata hoga ke PSX mein agar koi company dividend pay karna shuru kar de to uski to value realization bahut acchi ho jati hai. Another example is Descon in 2022. Is case mein Bangladesh ke ek port pe aag lagne ki wajah se Bangladesh se hydrogen peroxide ki imports band ho gayi aur wahi hydrogen peroxide jo ke Bangladesh se 400 dollar ka aata tha woh next best option Korea tha, jahan se 800 dollar ki price thi. So Descon, jo ke locally hydrogen peroxide produce karta hai, woh pehle 50 se 100 dollar kama raha tha per ton but ab woh almost 400 dollar kamane lag gaya tha. Humne ek detailed thread bhi likha hua hai about our investing journey in Descon. Humne kis kis cheezon ka khayal rakha, woh sab bahut detail mein explain ki gayi hain. Hum is thread ka link description mein laga denge. Is investing theme mein aapne sirf global margins ko track nahi karna, aapne sath hi sath yeh bhi samajhna hai ke jab global margins badh rahe hain to uske peeche economics kya hai aur yeh jo rising trend hai isne ek mahina last karna hai ya phir yeh ek se do saal tak continue rahega. Agar to yeh ek se do saal tak continue rehne ke chances hain phir aap is investing theme mein invest kar sakte hain. Is theme ki ek aur example refineries bhi hain. 2022 mein jab Russian war hui thi to diesel margins jo ke takriban 10 12 dollar hote the, matlab ke crude oil se diesel 10 12 dollar mehenga hota tha yeh margins 40 dollar tak chale gaye the. To wahi refineries jo ke diesel pe takriban 5 se 6 dollar per barrel kamati thi, woh 30 30 dollar kamane lag gayi. So is investing theme mein hum local producer ke sath sath global dynamics ko bhi track karte hain on a weekly basis ke wahan pe kya developments ho rahi hain, plants online aa rahe hain, future outlook kya hai, aur yeh margins kab expected hai ke normalize ho jaenge taake humein pata ho ke kab is company ke profits decline hone lag jaenge aur hum us se pehle hi sell kar dein. Turnarounds ko agar aap sahi tareeqe se samajh lein to yeh ek bahut hi sure-shot strategy hai. Yeh bear market mein bhi chalti hai aur bull market mein bhi chalti hai aur iski wajah yeh hai, kyun ke company ke profits almost teen se char guna badh rahe hote hain is investing theme mein. Turnarounds mein maximum time frame do saal ka hota hai, isliye value realization teen se che mahine mein hi start ho jati hai jab company ke acche results aane shuru hote hain. Ek hum aapko advice denge ke agar aap kisi turnaround investing theme mein invest kar rahe hain to aap lower price pe double down bilkul na karein. Yeh momentum investing ki strategy hai. Yahan pe aapko tabhi double down karna chahiye jab aapko confirmation mil rahi ho ke aap jo soch rahe hain, baqi log bhi waise hi soch rahe hain aur company ke results bhi usko confirm kar rahe hain. So is investing theme mein behtar hai ke aap teen mahine baad jab pehla accha result aaye tab double down karein agar aap abhi bhi confident hain ke yeh trend of increased profits will continue for one more year. Iske ilawa, change in dividend policy can also be a value realization event. Iski prime example hai UBL. Early 2023 mein yeh kafi clear ho gaya tha ke bank ki dividend policy kafi change ho gayi hai aur ab yeh minority shareholders ko bahut zyada dividends dene wale hain. Dividend policy ko predict bilkul nahi karna chahiye but agar aapko ek se do quarters mein confirmation mil jaye aur aapko lagta hai ke yeh strategy continue rahegi jo ke usually hota hai because dividend policy sponsors banate hain aur yeh policy kam az kam do se teen saal tak continue rehti hai. To jaise hi aapko confirmation mile after one quarter, aap us company ko deeply study kar lein aur jaise hi second confirmation aaye in the next round of results, aap is company ko apne portfolio mein add kar sakte hain. Do cheezein jisko log turnarounds samajhte hain but humare investing framework ke hisab se yeh turnarounds mein nahi aata. One is, debt to equity conversion and second is, management change. Hum turnarounds mein basically dekh rahe hote hain ke ya to business ki earning power badh gayi hai ya phir change in dividend policy ki wajah se value realization ke chances hain. Iske ilawa humare khayal se koi bhi aisa event jismein management change ho rahi ho ya debt to equity change ho rahi ho, hum us particular company ko turnarounds mein classify nahi karte. Now back to today's video. Our third theme is asset plays, acquisitions, or de-listing. Is investing theme mein basically aisi companies aa jati hain jo ke apni asal value se bahut kam pe trade ho rahi hain. Aur ho sakta hai unki koi wajah bhi ho. Is category mein OGDC, PPL bhi aa jati hai aur woh sari companies bhi aa jati hain jinki ownership change ho rahi hai ya phir woh de-listing ki taraf ja rahi hain. Kyun ke Pakistani stock market mein jo companies hain woh apni real value se bahut kam pe trade ho rahi hain isi wajah se jab bhi koi company bikti hai ya de-listing ki taraf jati hai to woh ek value unlocking event hota. Main characteristic of this theme is ke aapko ek company ke assets bahut kam price mein mil rahe hain aur sath hi sath ek bahut high probability bhi hai ke next one to two years mein yeh apni fair value ke kareeb aa jaenge. Is investing theme mein hum Mohnish Pabrai ka principle Heads I win, tails I do not lose much follow karte hain. In companies ki value already itni kam hoti hai ke inki price mein margin of safety already built-in hota hai. De-listing aur takeovers ke liye hum PSX rules aur SECP rules kafi detail mein padhte hain kyun ke usmein exact likha hota hai ke agar koi company de-list hogi ya uske owners change honge to minority shareholders ko company ne kya offer deni hai. De-listing aur acquisition maximum ek saal mein complete ho jate hain aur is category mein hum sirf tabhi invest karte hain jab public information available hoti hai. Ek confirmation aa gayi hoi hai company ki taraf se ke hum de-listing ya phir company sales karne ki taraf ja rahe hain aur iski wajah yeh hai ke market mein to har waqt rumors chalte hi rehte hain. Yeh company bikne wali hai to yeh company bikne wali hai, yeh company de-listing ka soch rahi hai. Hum sirf tabhi invest karte hain jab humare paas public information ho company ki taraf se. Circular debt related companies, yani ke OGDC, PPL aur power sector mein hum yeh dekhte hain the gap between fair value and current market value kitna hai. Uske sath sath hum is cheez ko bhi kafi zyada importance dete hain the likelihood of circular debt being cleared. Hum sirf yeh nahi dekhte ke company one-third price pe mil rahi hai to kharid lete hain. Hum yeh bhi dekhte hain ke chances kya hain circular debt clear hone ke. Yeh cheez aapko ek example se samajh aa jaegi. Jab hum power sector mein invest kar rahe hote hain, to humein ek confidence hota hai ke aaj nahi to maximum teen saal baad to government ne yeh paise dene hi dene hain kyun ke yeh private investors ke hain. Yahi cheez agar OGDC, PPL mein dekhein to zaroori nahi hai ke government inko ek se do saal mein paise clear kare. So, hum dono cheezon ka dhyan rakhte hain. First is, ke company mein upside kitni hai. And secondly, ke woh chances kya hain ke yeh upside achieve ho jaye. So, aaj humne cover kiye our first three investing themes.
