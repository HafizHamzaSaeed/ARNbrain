Speaker 1: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai paise ikhatay ho gaye hain aur abbu keh rahe hain ke ab plot le lena chahiye, magar main unko samjhana chah raha hoon ke paise hum log stock market mein invest karna chahte hain. Toh please koi tareeqa batayein main unko samjha sakoon.

ARN: Acha no doubt real estate Pakistan ki most popular asset class hai. But aap apne abbu ko dikhayein 25-year chart ke agar aap ne real estate ke achay se achay project jaise ke DHA Phase 6 Lahore mein bhi invest kiya hota, compared to agar aap ne stock market ke index wali companies mein invest kar liya hota, toh aaj aap ki wealth jo hai DHA Phase 6 ke muqablay mein taqreeban double hoti. Aur yeh sirf average index ka return hai, agar aap achi companies dhoond letay toh aap ka return aur bhi zyada ho sakta tha. But lekin aap ke abbu jo baat kar rahe hain woh bhi theek hai, Pakistan mein zyada tar log jab stock market mein aate hain toh woh trading karna shuru ho jaate hain aur gambling karna shuru ho jaate hain jo ke bohat risky hoti hain. Toh agar toh aap ne wahi karna hai, toh phir aap chup chap apne abbu ki baat maan lein aur ek plot mein invest kar dein aur bhool jayein.
