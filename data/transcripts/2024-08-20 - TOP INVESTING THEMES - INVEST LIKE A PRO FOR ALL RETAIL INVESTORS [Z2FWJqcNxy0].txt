Let's start with cyclicals. Cyclicals mein mainly wo companies aa jaati hain jo ke interest rates aur macroeconomics ki wajah se bahut zyada affect ho jaati hain. Waise to high interest rates har company ko negatively affect karta hai, kyunki high interest rate environment mein har company ko jo P/E ratio mil raha hota hai, wo kam ho jata hai. But, cyclicals companies jo hain unko high interest rates mein unke business ki sales aur margins bhi bahut zyada affect ho jaate hain. When investing in cyclicals, hum is cheez ka bahut zyada khayal rakhte hain ke ye industry ya company import se kitni protected hai? Kyunki agar koi aisa product globally bahut sasta available hai aur Pakistan mein us pe bahut zyada duties bhi nahi hain, to ho sakta hai aapko ek company bahut sasti lag rahi ho, but because of that competition, usne apni earning power hamesha ke liye lose kar di hai. Ye phenomenon aapko Pakistan ki steel industry mein kaafi nazar aata hai. Because of cheaper imports from FATA/PATA region, ye companies apne margins bhi sacrifice karti hain aur market share bhi lose kar deti hain. In contrast, there is the north-based cement plants of Pakistan, jo ke naturally hi import se protected hain. Because cement cannot travel. So ye companies long-term mein apni earning power lose nahi kartin. We explained this concept in detail in our cement sector video. Cyclicals mein risk-to-reward ratio -50% se lekar 1000% tak hai. Agar boom cycle aa jaye to kaafi zyada companies over three to four years, 10 times bhi barh jaati hain. Specially, agar starting point pe unki value bahut zyada undervalued ho. So, hamare investing rules ke hisab se cyclicals can maximum be 25% of our investment portfolio, aur ye bhi jab if they are selling at less than 40% of their fair value. If the discount is less than 40% of their fair value, to phir hum usmein maximum 15% invest kar lete hain. A very important rule: we do not invest in highly leveraged cyclicals. Hamara aim ye hota hai ke ye company ye down cycle survive kar jaye. Agar us pe bahut zyada leverage hogi, to ho sakta hai wo permanently bankruptcy ki taraf chali jaye. Agar ek less leveraged company mein invest karke aapko 3-4 times return mil raha hai, to phir itna zyada risk lene ki kya zaroorat hai? Hum banking sector ko bhi cyclicals mein classify karte hain. Shock ho gaye, right? Aaiye aapko explain karte hain. Aaj se dedh saal pehle, Pakistan ka debt-to-GDP almost 80% ho gaya tha. Aur us range mein ja raha tha, jahan pe IMF frontier economies mein debt restructuring karwata hai. Agar ek bank ki book value takriban 200 arab rupay hai, and it is holding 1,000 billion of government bonds, aur agar us pe 15% haircut lag jaye, to after-tax basis pe book value 75 billion se decrease ho jaati hai. Isi liye, 2023 mein banks bahut cheaply available the, because there was a real risk of debt restructuring. Ab debt-to-GDP reduce ho kar almost 70% pe aa gaya hai aur ab Pakistan IMF program mein bhi hai, so this risk has been priced out. So future mein agar Pakistan ka debt-to-GDP close to 80% hua aur IMF program bhi derail ho gaya, to banks will start acting like cyclicals. Now let's talk about stalwarts. A high price can turn a really good company into a really bad investment. Agar kisi company ke liye aapne 20+ P/E ratio pay kar diya, to ho sakta hai us company ke profits to double ho jayein in next three to four years, lekin uska P/E ratio half ho jaye aur aapne jo investment ki hai, us pe 0% return aaye in the next three to four years. In our opinion, only high ROCE companies ke liye aap high P/E ratio pay kar sakte hain. Kyunki us business mein jo high level ki compounding ho rahi hai, uske liye aap aaj high price pay kar rahe hain taake aap uske future profits ka part ban sakein. Ye saari baatein ke is sector mein 20 P/E ratio chalta hai, is sector mein 10 P/E ratio chalta hai, ye sab nonsense hai. Yehi FMCGs 2005 aur 2006 mein, 5 to 6 ya maximum 7 P/E ratio pe trade hoti thin aur us time pe bull market bhi aayi hui thi. So, ye hamara rule hai ke agar kisi bhi company ke liye hum more than 10 P/E ratio pay karenge, to uska ROCE 40% se zyada hona chahiye.
