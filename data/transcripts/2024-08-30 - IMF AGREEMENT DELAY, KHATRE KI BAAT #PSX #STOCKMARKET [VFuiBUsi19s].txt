Speaker: Achha Abdul Rehman bhai, ek mahine se upar ho gaya hai aur ye IMF ka agreement latak gaya hai to ye koi khatre ki baat to nahi hai?

ARN: IMF agreement mein jo do sabse important cheez hoti hain. Ek hota hai tax collection aur doosra hota hai agle kuch saalon ka dollar inflow-outflow, jisko technical language mein external financing gap kehte hain. To abhi Pakistan ko next one year ke liye 2 billion dollar ki aur funding arrange karni hai, jo ke most probably September mein ho jayegi. Aur ye bilkul bhi concerning nahi hai kyunki Pakistan ka jo current account balance chal raha hai vo bilkul balanced hai aur dollar reserves jo hain vo around 9 billion dollars pe hain. Ye 2023 ki tarah nahi hai jab har hafte dollar reserves kam ho rahe hote the aur vo gir gir ke 3 billion dollar pe aa gaye the. To most probably September mein ye IMF agreement bhi ho jayegi aur phir Pakistan ke dollar reserves aur zyada badhne lag jayenge.
