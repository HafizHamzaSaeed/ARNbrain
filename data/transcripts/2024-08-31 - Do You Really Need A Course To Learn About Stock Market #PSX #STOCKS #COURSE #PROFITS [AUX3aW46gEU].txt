Speaker 1: Abdul Rehman bhai ye mujhe batayein ye investing course lene ka koi faida bhi hota hai ke nahi?

Abdul Rehman Najam (ARN): Yeh to depend karta hai ke investing course ke content kya hain aur sikha kon raha hai. Ab yeh koi cement aur steel to hai nahi ke har investing course jo hai woh barabar ho. Agar to kisi investing course mein actual value hai aur woh aapko expert level knowledge jo hai, woh short time mein sikha sakta hai, to bilkul, it's worth it. Aap khud sochein ke aap ek short time mein jab saari skills seekh lenge, to aap future mein jo investment karenge, aapka kitna time bhi bach jayega aur kitne losses se bhi aap bach jayenge. Even Warren Buffett jo hain, woh kehte hain, "The best investment you can make is in yourself" aur khud bhi unhon ne Dale Carnegie ka communication skills ka course liya tha, to improve himself.
