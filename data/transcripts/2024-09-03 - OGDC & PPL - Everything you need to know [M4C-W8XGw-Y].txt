Circular debt ki saari problem kahan se originate hoti hai? Pakistan mein jo gas bills ikathe hote hain, they are much less than the cost of the gas. So iski wajah se profits ka ek hissa hamesha circular debt mein stuck ho jata hai. Pakistan mein salana jo gas use hoti hai uski total cost taqreeban 1500 se 1800 arab rupe hai aur jo bills collect hote hain woh taqreeban 1200 se 1500 arab rupe ikathe hote hain. To yeh jo 200-300 arab ka deficit hai, woh hamesha circular debt mein chala jata hai. Aap soch rahe honge ke 15% profit agar circular debt mein chala bhi jata hai to kya farq padta hai? 85% profit to ho raha hai na? This is wrong. Pakistan mein jo total gas consume hoti hai uska 20 se 25% comes from LNG. But kyunki LNG mehengi hai, 10 se 12 dollar per unit, to in value terms this becomes more than 40%. Aur is payment pe aap bilkul default nahi kar sakte. To jo bhi shortfall hota hai of 15% in bills collection, woh local producers ke upar ek bojh ban jata hai.

Ab mein aapko samajhta hoon ke agar kisi company ka 25 se 30% revenue circular debt mein stuck ho jaye to uska kya impact hota hai. Pakistan mein average local gas ki cost taqreeban 4 dollar hai. Jismein se 12.5% aapko royalty deni hoti hai, 1 dollar aapka taqreeban operational cost hoti hai aur phir aapko 35% income tax bhi dena hota hai. To aapka jo profit hota hai woh taqreeban 1 se 1.5 dollar hota hai. So agar bills collection mein 25% shortfall ho, to aapka saara profit circular debt mein stuck ho jata hai aur in companies ne jo exploration mein investment ki hoti hai, woh bhi recover nahi hoti.
