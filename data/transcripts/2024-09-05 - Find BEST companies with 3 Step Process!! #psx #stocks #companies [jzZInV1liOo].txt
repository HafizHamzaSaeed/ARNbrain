**Speaker 1:** 
Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, yeh jo aap companies nikal lete hain parh ke itni achi, yeh mujhe bhi tareeqa sikhayein zara.

**ARN:** 
Dekhein hamara na three-step process hota hai koi bhi achi company dhoondnay ka. Hum koshish kar rahe hote hain ke aisi company dhoondain jo har saal 25% apni value barhaye taake 10 saal mein hamari investment 10 times ho jaye. To first step yeh hota hai ke hum uska poora business samajhte hain. Understand the business like owners aur yeh dekhte hain ke is company mein capability hai har saal apni 25% value barhane ki. Phir second step pe hum dekhte hain ke stock market mein iski kis value pe trading hogi, to teen saal mein bhi hum yeh lock kar lete hain ke yeh company hamari investment ko double kar degi. Aur third step hota hai ke hamein jab lag raha ho ke yeh company long-term mein bhi achi hai, teen saal mein bhi achi hai, to phir hum check karte hain ke isne last 20 years mein kitna return diya hai. Aur agar pichle 20 saal mein bhi is company ne 25% return diya ho, to phir hum isko apne portfolio mein add kar lete hain. Yeh poora process hum log detail mein explain karenge in our upcoming investing masterclass.
