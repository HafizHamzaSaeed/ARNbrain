Globally ETFs kafi zyada popular hain aur Pakistan mein bhi kafi popular ho rahe hain aur uski wajah ye hai ke Pakistan mein jo mutual funds hain wo taqreeban teen se char percent annual fees lete hain aur jo ETFs hain unki fees sirf aadhe se le ke ek percent hoti hai saal ki. But problem ye hai ke abhi bhi Pakistan mein aisa koi ETF nahi hai jo ke hundred index ki saari companies usme included hon. To I think ETF mein invest karne se behtar option ye hai ke aap apna khud ek ETF bana dein aur jo hundred index mein companies hain unko wahi weightage de ke apna portfolio bana lein. Har che mahine baad usko rebalance kar lein.
