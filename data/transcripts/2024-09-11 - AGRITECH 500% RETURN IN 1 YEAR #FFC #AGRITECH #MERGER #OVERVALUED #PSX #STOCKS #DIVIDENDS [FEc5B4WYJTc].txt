Speaker 1: Achha Abdul Rehman bhai, ye Agri Tech boht ooper ja raha hai. Kahin ye overvalued tou nahi ho gaya?

ARN: Dekhein, jab bhi koi company bikne lag jaye na, tou phir overvalued aur undervalued wali cheezein hain na, uski importance khatam ho jati hai. Aur uski wajah ye hoti hai, ke jo company doosri company ko khareed rahi hoti hai, wo aakhri kuch percentage khareedne ke liye na, koi bhi price degi share ki. Ek ye wajah hoti hai. Doosri wajah ye hoti hai, ke SECP ke rules hain, ke agar koi bhi company bikegi, tou phir jo minority shareholders hain, jinhone stock exchange ke through uske shares liye hue hain, tou jis bhi price pe aapas mein deal hogi, wo price unko bhi milegi. Tou ye cheez aapne Pak Suzuki mein bhi dekhi thi, ke jab tak price announce nahi hoti deal ki, tou ye volatility chalti rehti hai. Tou is case mein, agar tou ye deal 50 rupay pe ho gayi, tou jo log aaj kal invest kar rahe hain is price pe, unka faida ho jayega. Aur agar deal aapas mein 20, 25 rupay pe hui, tou phir unka nuksaan ho jayega.
