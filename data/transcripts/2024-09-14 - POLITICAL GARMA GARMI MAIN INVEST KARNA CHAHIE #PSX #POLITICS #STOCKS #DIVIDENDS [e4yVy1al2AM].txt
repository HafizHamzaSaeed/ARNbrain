Speaker 1: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai ye political garma garmi bari barh gayi hai. To kya stock market mein invest karna chahiye abhi?

ARN: Dekhein abhi na Pakistan mein teen cheezein chal rahi hain. Aik IMF ka agreement bhi thora delay hua wa hai, political garma garmi bhi barh gayi hai, aur teesra Supreme Court mein future Chief Justice kaun hoga ispe bhi bohot uncertainty hai. To in teeno cheezon ki wajah se ho sakta hai agla aik mahina kaafi volatile rahe. Lekin again, aapke paas aik poora investing framework hona chahiye. Aapke paas rules hone chahiye ke maine is volatility ko kaise handle karna hai. Aaj kal hum ye rules saare jo hain apne investing master class mein sabko sikha rahe hain. Aur aik acchi strategy ye ho sakti hai ke agar aapko thori ye volatility tang kar rahi hai, to aap aisi companies mein invested rahein jis mein short term triggers bhi hain aur wo long term mein bhi acchi hain. Aur agar koi aisi company hai aapke portfolio mein jo long term mein to decent hai lekin short term mein us mein aapko koi trigger nahi nazar aa raha, to aap for the time being us mein jo hai exit kar sakte hain aur phir jab ye uncertainties thori kam ho jayengi, IMF agreement ho jayega, to aap phir se us mein invest kar sakte hain.
