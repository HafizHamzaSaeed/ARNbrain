ARN: Accha Adnan bhai, samundar mein se toh bahut tail nikal aya hai. Toh is se kya... Pakistan ka toh bahut bhala ho jaye ga. Toh kya is se OGDCL aur PPL ko bhi fayda ho ga?

Adnan: Mera toh hamesha se ye belief hai ke Pakistan mein number one import substitution jo hai na, woh hai hi oil and gas. Ab Pakistan har saal taqreeban 15 billion dollar ka jo hai, oil aur gas jo hai na, bahar se import karta hai. Toh agar koi choti moti bhi yahan pe discovery ho jaye na, toh Pakistan ka bahut fayda ho jata hai. Lekin abhi ye jo khabrein chal rahi hain ke samundar mein oil nikal aya hai, ye abhi bahut early stage jo hai na uski ek study hui hai, jismein show kiya hai ke shayad idhar koi reserves maujood hon. Ab is mein aap ko bahut zyada detail mein ja ke studies karni parin gi. Foreign companies most probably aayin gi jinke paas ye expertise hoti hain aur woh final decision apni studies se lein gi ke yahan pe oil discovery ho sakti hai ke nahi. Aur agar idhar koi successful discovery ho jati hai future mein, toh Pakistan ka bhi bahut bhala ho ga aur OGDCL, PPL ka bhi bahut fayda ho ga.
