**Interviewer:** Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, sab log keh rahe hain ye Faisal Bank agla Meezan Bank banne ja raha hai, kya ye sach hai?

**ARN:** Dekhein, bank ka kaam kya hota hai? Apne depositors se paise lena aur un logon ko loan dena jin ko zaroorat hai. To bank beech mein jo apna margin rakhta hai na, us se determine hota hai ke wo bank kitna profit kamayega. So, jab bhi ek bank ko dekhna ho ke wo acha hai ya bura hai, to saari game hoti hai ke aapne dekhna hai in dono percentages mein farq kya hai. So, jis bank mein current account zyada honge aur wo apne savings account ke depositors ko kam return dega, uska profit bohot zyada hoga. Agar is metric ko dekhein, to Meezan Bank aur Faisal Bank mein koi comparison hi nahi hai.
