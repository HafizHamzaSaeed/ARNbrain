ARN: Rehan bhai, ye stock market, main do teen mahine se dekh raha hoon ke 65,000 ke kareeb phasi hui hai. To wo jo rally chal rahi thi, wo kya khatam ho gayi hai?

Rehan: Acha waise to stock market ko short-term mein predict karna bohat hi mushkil kaam hota hai, lekin pichle ek se do mahine mein bohat zyada positive developments hui hain. Jis speed se inflation niche aayi hai, ye koi bhi expect nahi kar raha tha aur ab bohat high expectations ho gayi hain ke agle kuch mahine mein interest rate bohat tezi se niche aayenge. So ye stock market ke liye bohat positive development hai. But jo cheez investors ko pareshan kar rahi hai wo hai local politics. And rightly so. Ye jo politics mein jo garma garmi chal rahi hai, jab ye October mein thodi settle down ho jayegi to ye jo saari developments hui hain pichle ek do mahine mein, I think iska impact aana shuru ho jayega aapko. These are my two cents.
