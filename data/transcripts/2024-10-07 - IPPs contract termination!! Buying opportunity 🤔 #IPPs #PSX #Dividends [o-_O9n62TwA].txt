Interviewer: Achha Abdul Rehman bhai, aaj kal yeh IPPs pe bari halchal chal rahi hai, contract termination ki baatein ho rahi hain. To batayenge zara humein kuch is sector ke baare mein?

ARN: Achha power sector mein government chah rahi hai ke paanch aise IPPs hain jin ke plant na poora saal band pade rehte hain. To un se keh rahi hai ke aap ke do teen saal contract ke reh gaye hain, aap ne pehle hi boht paise bana liye hain to aap ka na contract hum terminate kar dete hain. Us ke saath saath 20 se 25 aisi IPPs hain jo ke kam kam chalti hain, teen se chaar mahine chalti hain saal mein. To un se government keh rahi hai ke hum aap ko fixed saal ke paise nahi denge, hum aap ko jab aap bijli humein bechenge, us ke unit ki keemat de denge. So is mein aap ne pehle to analysis yeh karni hai ke woh companies jin ka contract terminate ho sakta hai un ko side pe kar dein. Aur jo companies jin pe government keh rahi hai ke hum aap se take and pay pe agreement kar lenge, un ko analyze karein ke woh kitni price pe bijli banati hain, kyun ke agar woh sasti bijli bana rahi huin aur government un se take and pay pe bijli lene lag jaye, to ho sakta hai un ke profit pehle se bhi ziada barh jayein.
