Dekhein business environment jo hai na, wo har waqt change hoti rehti hai. Kisi saal thora zyada profit ho jata hai, kisi saal thora kam profit ho jata hai. Isi liye jo log past ROI nikaal lete hain na uski koi value nahi hoti. Aapne asal mein nikaalna hai future ROI ke ab is business mein agar main sau rupay invest karunga, to mujhe kitna profit milega. Aur yeh profit ka andaza lagane ke liye na aapko poora business samajhna parta hai. Aapko yeh bhi samajhna parta hai ke yeh company jo product banati hai uski price kis tareeqe se nikalti hai. Phir yeh jo raw material hain, jo khareedte hain, jo cost of production mein contribute karte hain, uski price kis tareeqe se decide hoti hai. Jab aap yeh saari cheezein samajh lete hain, to aap ek andaza laga lete hain ke yeh company future mein kitna profit kamayegi. Aur phir aap future ROI jo hai, wo calculate kar sakte hain. Har industry ka, har company ka ROI kis tareeqe se nikaalte hain, yeh hum apne investing masterclass mein aaj kal sabko sikha rahe hain. To jab aap yeh calculate kar lete hain, to aap future ki top companies bhi pick kar lete hain.
