Interviewer: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, EFERT to pehle har dafa aath aath rupey ka dividend de deta tha. Pichle do quarter mein sirf inhone do rupey ka quarter diya hai, to ye kya hua hai?

ARN: Acha pichle quarter mein to asal mein inka jo plant tha main plant, wo refurbishment pe gaya hua tha is wajah se kam earnings aayi thin aur kam dividend aayin thin. Is dafa jo hai, agar aap overall dekhein pure nau mahine mein, to abhi bhi EFERT ki jo 100% dividend payout policy hai, wo intact hai. Dekhein aage is company ne abhi next year mein, jo gas ka pressure enhancement facility hai us mein kaafi zyada investment karni hai, to itna boht zyada debt lena bhi company ke liye acha nahi hai. Aur jaise humne pehle bataya, ke 9-month basis pe abhi bhi 100% payout ki policy jo hai, wo intact hai. Pichle teen mahine mein thori si urea ki sale kam bhi hui thi, lekin jaise humne aapko pehle bhi explain kiya hai, ke Pakistan mein pure saal mein jitna bhi urea banati hai company, uske bikne ke boht ache chances hote hain, to ye cheez jo hai, aage ja ke recover ho jati hai. Company ki earnings kaafi achi aayi hain, aur abhi bhi ye company jo hai, wo retail investors ki bari achi ek choice hai.
