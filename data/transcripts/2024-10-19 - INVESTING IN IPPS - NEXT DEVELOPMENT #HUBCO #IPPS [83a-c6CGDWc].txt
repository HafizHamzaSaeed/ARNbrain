[Published: 2024-10-19]

ARN: Ghazwan Bhai, IPPs mein bohot nuksan ho gaya already to please humein batayein yeh government ki agli move kya hogi?

Ghazwan Bhai: Acha, ab pehli cheez investors ko sabse pehle to ab yeh apne dimaag mein daal lena chahiye ke ab jo government baat kar rahi ho na usko bohot ghaur se sunein kyunki ek dafa unhone jo pehle keh rahe the woh karke dikha diya hai. So ab government jo hai woh yeh keh rahi hai ke ab hum next 18 IPPs hain jo ke 1994 aur 2002 policy mein aate hain unse hum takreeban per unit sawa se dedh rupay jo hai woh capacity payment kam karayeinge. Ab yeh takreeban sawa sau aur dedh sau arab rupay ke beech ki amount ban jati hai. Aur jaise ke pichli deal mein hua hai, yeh jo total amount hoti hai, kuch portion iska future capacity payment kam karke liya jata hai aur kuch portion jo hai jo purane paise dene hote hain government ne, usme bhi haircut lagta hai. So iske jo dono angles hain woh main aapko explain kar deta hoon. Agar aapne yeh andaza lagana hai ke ek particular company ko purani payment pe kitna haircut lagega, to aap iska reference Muhammad Ali report se le sakte hain. Aur agar aapne yeh dekhna hai ke future mein agar government ne in plants ko take-and-pay pe shift kar diya, to phir aap jis keemat pe bijli bana rahe hain, woh bohot important ho jati hai. To jo plant sasti bijli bana rahe honge, woh zyada profit kamayeinge aur jo mehangi bijli bana rahe honge, woh kam profit kamayeinge.
