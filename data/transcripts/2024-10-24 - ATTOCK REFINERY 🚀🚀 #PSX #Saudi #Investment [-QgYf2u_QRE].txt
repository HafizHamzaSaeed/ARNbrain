**Speaker 1:** Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, pehle to ye Saudi Arabia ye 10 billion dollar ki refinery laga rahe thay. Ab sunne mein aa raha hai ke ye Attock Refinery khareed rahe hain. Share price bhi kafi barhi hai, to ye kya ho raha hai ye? Kuch details batayenge aap humein?

**Abdul Rehman Najam (ARN):** Ji bilkul, market mein bohat zyada rumors chal rahe hain ke Attock Refinery mein Saudi investors ka interest hai. Abhi Saudi delegation bhi aaya tha to bohat zyada log keh rahe hain ke pehle inka jaise plan tha ek new refinery lagane ka, but ab ye plan thoda change ho gaya hai aur ye interested hain ke Attock Refinery ko upgrade kar lein. Attock Refinery jo hai, wo Pakistan ki sab se north refinery hai. To Pakistan mein zyada tar jo crude oil nikalta hai wo north se nikalta hai aur wo zyada tar Attock Refinery mein hi jo hai refine hota hai. Aur ye bohat bara advantage hai, is mein aap sari financing cost se bhi bach jate hain aur jitna bhi dollar upar neeche hota hai, uska bhi impact bohat zyada kam aata hai. To agar in sari cheezon ko dekhein to no doubt Attock Refinery Pakistan ki number one refinery hai, inki balance sheet pe bohat zyada cash bhi hai, lekin again ye sirf rumors hain. To jo bhi log is company mein invest kar rahe hain, ya to is ko poore tareeqe se study kar lein aur thoda sa wait karein ke jab company khud confirm kar degi ke is ki ownership change hone ja rahi hai, phir I think ek behtar time hoga ke aap is mein invest kar sakein.
