ARN: Acha Chaudhry bhai ye pharma sector ka profit ek dam se bohot ziada barh gaya hai, ye kya hua hai exactly?

Chaudhry: Acha pharma sector mein do teen main cheezein jo hain change hui hain. First of all to April mein government ne jo non-essential medicine thi na, usko deregulate kar diya. To ye jo saari medicines thin, inki price kaafi ziada barhi hai. Kisi ki 10% kisi ki 20% aur aapko na ye samajhna hoga ke kisi product ki agar 10% price barhti hai, iska ye matlab nahi hai ke profit 10% barhega. Aap sochein ek cheez 100 rupay ki thi aur usko banane ka kharcha tha let's say 80 rupay, to aapka profit hota tha 20 rupay. Lekin agar uski keemat 110 rupay ho jayegi to aapka profit 50% barh jayega. So ek ye sabse important cheez hui hai. Uske ilawa agar aap pichle saal se compare karenge to pura ek saal ho gaya hai koi bhi devaluation nahi hui aur jo pharmaceutical ke raw material hote hain, jo bahar se aate hain, unki price dollars mein bhi neeche aa rahi hai. To ye jo teen factor hain, ye teeno combine kar ke aapko aisa lagega ke pharma sector ki jo companies hain, unke profit pichle saal ke mukable mein bohot ziada barh gaye hain.
