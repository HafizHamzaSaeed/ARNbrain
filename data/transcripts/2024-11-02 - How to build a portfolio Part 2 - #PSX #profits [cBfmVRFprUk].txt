Third theme hai oil and gas sector. Kyunke IMF ne kaha hai ke gas circular debt bilkul nahi barhni chahiye. Toh is mein jo ache options hain, woh hai OGDC aur Mari. Dono companies jo hain, growth companies bhi hain. Mari jo hai, abhi aap ne dekha hoga Waziristan mein ek nayi discovery ki hai, aur OGDC bhi jo hai, unka bhi internal target hai ke unhone apni production ko 50,000 barrels of oil pe leke jana hai agle kuch saalo mein.

Aur fourth theme hai pharma sector aur FMCG sector. So is mein woh products hote hain jo aap daily use karte hain, toh is mein achi companies aa jati hain Colgate bhi hai, Nestle bhi hai. Pharma ke andar aisi companies mein jayein jin ki jo pharma ki brands hain, woh aap ke samne roz nazar aati hain. Aur woh ek tarah se pharmaceutical ke bajaye ek FMCG product ban rahi hain. Toh yeh theme jo hai, yeh bhi aap ko long term mein bohot ache returns de sakta hai.

Toh aap apne portfolio ko four themes mein divide kar lein aur agar aap ko har theme mein aap do stocks bhi le sakte hain aur agar aap ko ek company doosri se bohot achi lag rahi hai, toh aap ek stock bhi le sakte hain har theme mein. Aur is se aap apna portfolio bana sakte hain.
