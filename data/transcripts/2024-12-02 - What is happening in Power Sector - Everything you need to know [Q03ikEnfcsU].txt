Take or pay ka matlab hota hai ke main aap se bijli khareedun ya na khareedun, main aap ko ek fixed rent diya karunga saal ka, aur usi mein se aap ne apna munafa karna hai. Take and pay hota hai ke ab main aap se ek unit ki price decide karke per unit aap ko paise diya karunga. So aise arrangement mein, jaise ke aap khud bhi soch sakte hain, jo plant sasti bijli bana raha hoga, Government uss se unit bhi zyada khareedegi aur uska profit badh bhi sakta hai. For example, ek plant hai jo ke 35 rupay ki bijli bana raha hai, uss se toh Government kahegi mujhe toh aap se khareedna hi nahi hai, toh shayad uska ek bhi unit sale na ho. Lekin ek doosra plant hai jo ke let's say 10 rupay ki bijli bana raha hai, Government khushi khushi uss se 20 rupay mein agar bijli khareed le, toh uska profit toh pehle ki capacity payment se bhi zyada ho sakta hai. So aap ko ye samajhna hai ke take and pay mein variable cost, jis pe aap bijli bana rahe hain, yaani ke aap ka fuel kitna sasta hai, iski bohot importance hai. Rough rough aap ko bata dete hain ke furnace oil taqreeban 30 se 35 rupay ki bijli bana raha hai aaj ke rate ke hisab se. LNG ke power plants bhi taqreeban 30 rupay fi unit bijli bana rahe hain, aur jo local gas pe plant hain, woh taqreeban 15 rupay ki bijli bana rahe hain. Ek 200 megawatt ka power plant agar 70% capacity pe chale throughout the year, toh woh taqreeban ek arab unit bana leta hai. Toh ab aap khud hi andaza laga sakte hain har power plant ke liye ke agar Government uss se bijli khareed rahi hogi aur unko per unit let's say paanch rupay munafa ho raha hai, toh unka total profit saal ka kya hoga. Variable cost low hone ke do faide hain. Ek toh aap ka per unit profit bhi zyada hota hai, aur doosra Government ke chances bhi zyada hote hain ke woh aap se zyada se zyada unit khareedein. So ye aap ne samajh liya ke take and pay mein future earning power of the company kis tariqe se affect ho sakti hai in 18 IPPs ki.
