First step is ROIC yani ke Return on Invested Capital. Yeh metric humein quality of the business aur us ki earning power batata hai. Aasaan alfaaz mein agar Return on Invested Capital ko samajhna ho to aap sochein ek business hai ab woh jahan bhi pahunch gaya hai, agar us mein hum 100 rupaye ki investment karein ge to us 100 rupaye pe mujhe kya return mile ga? Is mein saari cheezein included hoti hain ke plant ki cost kya hai, working capital kitna required hai, aur ek normal saal mein yeh company us investment pe kitna return kamati hai. Long-term mein company ka jo ROIC hota hai, wahi us ki share price CAGR determine karta hai. Yani ke agar ek business mein investment kar ke acha return aa raha hoga aur woh cheez aap compound karte rahein for many years to jo value create hogi us investment se, that will be so huge ke aap ki jo investment hai, woh 10x, 20x even 100x ho jaye gi.

Ek example ke saath aap ko explain kar dete hain, Lucky Cement ki example le lete hain. Aaj se takreeban 15-20 saal pehle, Lucky Cement ke paas takreeban 1-2 million ton ki capacity hoti thi. But us cement plant se woh log jo bhi profit generate karte the us ko reinvest karte the, ek naya cement plant lagate the, aur aaj un ki capacity takreeban 15 million ton hai. Is ke ilawa aur bhi bohot zyada businesses mein unhon ne invest kiya hai aur woh is wajah se kiya hai kyunke cement business ka ROIC was decent.

Long-term mein agar koi company bohot high ROIC pe compound ho rahi ho to phir initial years mein us ki value undervalued ho ya thodi si apni price se upar ho, woh bilkul farq nahi karta. Aap khud sochein, agar ek 1 million ton ka plant us time pe 5 arab, let's say, us ki fair value thi, agar woh 7 arab pe bhi trade ho raha tha, but agar us ka ROIC high hai to woh future mein itne zyada plants lagane wala hai us profit se ke us, jo new plants hain, un ki value hundreds of billions mein hogi. So is liye jo base effect hota hai, yani ke aaj agar thodi si price upar bhi hai, agar ek company mein high ROIC hoga to woh phir bhi aap ko acha return de ga long-term mein.

Jaise ke hum ne aap ko pehle bataya, ROIC calculate karne liye sab se pehle aap ko capital expenditure pata hona chahiye, phir working capital cycle pata hona chahiye, us ke ilawa duty structure pata hona chahiye, us company ki earning power pata honi chahiye, aur earning power ke liye aap ko us ke saare raw materials aur us ke jo main products hain us ki pricing ka bhi pata hona chahiye taake aap ek acha estimate laga sakein ke yeh company future mein kitna profit kamaye gi.

Ek aur example se aap ko samjhate hain, banking industry ki example le lete hain. Ek bank hai jis ka ROIC let's say 35% hai aur doosra bank hai let's say us ka ROIC 15% hai. Agar 15% wala bank aaj aap ko thoda sa sasta mil bhi raha hai price to book ke hisab se, but jo 35% wala bank hai, woh agle 5-10 saalon mein itni zyada compounding karne wala hai ke overall returns aap ko us mein zyada mil sakte hain kyunke us ka ROIC high hai.

Second step is Value Realization. Ab yeh jo step hai, yeh capture karta hai ke company abhi undervalued hai ya overvalued hai. Is mein aap ne yeh dekhna hota hai ke aaj se do se teen saal baad aap ne andaza lagana hai business poora samajh ke ke is company ki fair value takreeban kya hogi. Us ke liye aap pehle andaza lagayein ge ke is company ka do se teen saal baad profit kitna hoga aur phir yeh kis PE ratio pe trade kar raha hoga. So jab aap do se teen saal ke level pe kisi bhi company ki target price calculate karein ge aur aaj agar woh aap ko bohot sasta mil raha hoga to that means it is really undervalued. Do se teen saal is liye bohot important hai kyunke yeh ek aisa time hai jis mein market jo hai apne aap ko correct kar leti hai. Bull market bhi bohot zyada time nahi chalti aur bear market bhi bohot zyada time nahi chalti. Do se teen saalon mein ache chances hote hain ke ek company apni fair value pe zaroor trade kare gi. So yeh jo hamara second step hai, is mein bhi aap 25 se 30% return per year lock kar sakte hain. Agar aap ki target price two times se zyada hai of current price to phir aap ne teen saal ke level pe bhi 25% return per year lock kar liye, jo ke multi-bagger returns hain.

Value Realization ka concept bhi aap ko ek example se samjha dete hain aur example le lete hain Indus Motors ki. Us se aap andaza laga sakte hain ke us time pe company kis price pe trade kar rahi hogi aur agar aaj ki price us se half ya us se bhi zyada kam hai to aap ne do se teen saal ke level pe bhi ache returns lock kar liye.

Multi-bagger kya hai? Multi-bagger consistently ek aisa business hai jo har saal apni value takreeban 25 se 30% badhaye taake aap ki investment jo hai 10x, 25x, 50x even 100x ho jaye. But this 25 to 30% per year can come from different sources aur is ke liye aap ko business samajhna pade ga. Bohot zyada log sochte hain ke ek company hai woh to already 2 billion dollar ki ho gayi hai aur ab agar mein sochun ke yeh future ka multi-bagger ho sakta hai ke nahi to phir to is ko 50 billion dollar ke market cap pe jana pade ga aur Pakistan mein to aisa hone wala nahi hai, to phir yeh company multi-bagger nahi ho sakti. Aap bilkul ghalat soch rahe hain. 25 to 30% per year return ka jo benchmark hai, woh do teen different cheezon se aata hai, us mein dividends bhi included ho sakte hain, us mein Pakistan mein jo har saal 11% inflation hoti hai us ki bhi contribution hoti hai, and third thing is, woh company jo expansions kar rahi hoti hai ya phir different industries, new industries mein ja ke investment kar rahi hoti hai.

So depending on the industry, aap in teen metrics ko breakdown kar sakte hain. Let's say fertilizer ki example le lete hain. Ab fertilizer mein humein pata hai ke expansion bohot kam hoti hai lekin woh companies 12 se 15% har saal dividends deti hain. So already hamare paas 12 se 15% return aa gaya, is ke ilawa inflation bhi hoti hai. So zaroori nahi hai ek company ko multi-bagger hone ke liye bohot zyada expansions aur volumetric growth ki requirement ho aur is ki ek aur example hai Millat Tractors. Millat Tractors aaj bhi utne hi tractors bechti hai takreeban jo woh 10 saal pehle bechti thi lekin har tractor pe jo woh profit kamati hai, woh bohot zyada increase ho gaya hai. So multi-bagger broken down into 25 to 30% per year can come from different sources.

Ek aur example le lete hain, cement industry ki agar aap example lein ge to wahan pe to dividends bohot zyada nahi hote aur inflation bhi cement bag ki prices mein takreeban 6 se 7% per year hai, to cement company ko multi-bagger banne ke liye bohot zyada volumetric growth yani ke bohot zyada naye plants mein investment ki zaroorat hogi. So ek cement plant ko 25% per year apni value badhane ke liye agar inflation se sirf 6% aa raha hai to baqi 18% us ko most probably volumetric growth se kamana pade ga. 18% volumetric growth ka matlab hota hai ke har 4 saal baad aap apni capacity double karein.

Banking sector mein multi-baggers ko aap is tareeqe se breakdown kar sakte hain ke let's say ek bank on average 10% dividend deta hai to baqi 15% ya 20% jo requirement hai to become a multi-bagger, woh phir deposits growth se poori honi chahiye.
