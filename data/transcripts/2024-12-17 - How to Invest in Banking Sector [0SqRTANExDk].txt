Is cheez ko samajhne ke do tareeqe hain. First is ke kisi bhi company ki fair value hoti hai ke us ne future mein jitne bhi profit kamane hain, us ki aaj ki net present value. Aur is ka jo easy version hai, wo ye hai ke banks P/E ratio pe trade ho rahe hote hain aur dividend yield pe trade ho rahe hote hain. To agar let's say bank ka koi dividend pehle Rs. 30 deta tha, ab us ki earning kam ho gayi hain aur wo Rs. 25 dividend de raha hai. But jo interest rate kam hone ki wajah se dividend yield kam ho jaati hai, us se us ki share price bohot upar chali jaati hai. Similarly, agar ek bank pehle Rs. 50 kama raha tha aur us ko 4 P/E ratio mil raha tha, but ab wo Rs. 45 kama raha hai lekin us ko 8 P/E ratio mil raha hai, to aap khud hi soch sakte hain ke us ke stock ki price kitni upar chali jayegi.

Second and very important aspect is, jab interest rate kam hote hain, to Pakistan ka debt-to-GDP bhi kam ho jata hai aur is se local debt restructuring ke chances bilkul zero ho-jaate hain. Jaise humne aap ko pehle explain kiya ke ek banking sector mein agar ek bank ka apna capital Rs. 100 hai, us ne Rs. 1,000 ke loan diye hue hain, Rs. 1,500 ke loan diye hue hain, to similarly agar Government of Pakistan ki debt unsustainable ho jaye, to wo bank ko kehti hai ke aap ko main ne Rs. 100 dene the, but ab main Rs. 90 doongi. To jo losses ka impact hota hai on banking sector, wo bohot zyada severe hota hai. Kam interest rate hone ki wajah se debt sustainability Pakistan ki bohot zyada acchi ho jaati hai aur ye jo risk hota hai, ye bhi khatam ho jata hai. Isi wajah se banking sector ke andar jo ye risk aaya hota hai, ye bhi eliminate ho jata hai, is se bhi share price bohot upar chali jaati hai. Roughly roughly, ek bank agar us ka market cap taqreeban 200 arab rupay hai, us ne Government of Pakistan ko 1,000 arab rupay ka loan diya hota hai. To agar koi bhi debt-to-GDP high ho jaye us ki wajah se risk aa jaye, to ye easily ek possibility hoti hai ke us 1,000 arab mein se 100 arab to milega hi nahi, to aap khud hi dekh sakte hain ke ek bank jis ki value 200 arab hai, agar us ko 100 arab kam wapas milenge, to us ki market cap jo hai, wo half ho sakti hai. Jab interest rates low hote hain, to ye possibility khatam ho jaati hai.
