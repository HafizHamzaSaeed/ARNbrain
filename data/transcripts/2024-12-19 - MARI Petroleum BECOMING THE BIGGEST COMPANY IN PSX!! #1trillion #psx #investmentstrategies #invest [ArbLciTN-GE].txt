Interviewer: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, ye suna hai koi Mari Petroleum ki koi nayi discovery aa gayi hai. Woh aapko kya lagta hai ke PSX ki sabse badi company toh already ban chuki hai, toh ek trillion se do trillion pe ja rahi hai market cap iska?

ARN: Ji bilkul, market mein na ye kaafi rumors chal rahe hain ke Mari Petroleum ne koi bahut badi discovery kar li hai. Lekin Mari field jo hai woh already Pakistan mein sabse zyada gas produce karti hai ek field se. Toh fundamentally ye sochna ke ye jo 50% 60% ki rally aayi hai, itni koi badi field ki discovery ho sakti hai jo ke 50% of the value contribute kare jo ke aaj Mari field kar rahi hai, ye bahut hi naamumkin cheez hai. But phir bhi agar koi discovery hui, hum aapko update kar denge. Major jo iski rally chal rahi hai na, woh I think isse related hai ke jo high ROIC companies hoti hain na, unka PE ratio bhi high hota hai. Aur is company mein PE ratio ki re-adjustment ho rahi hai. Pehle bhi ye 10-12 PE ratio pe trade ho raha tha aur ab bhi is rally ke baad jo hai ye 10-12 PE ratio pe pahunch gaya hai. Aur company ki long-term economics bhi bahut achhi hai. Ispe hum ek bahut hi detailed video bhi bana rahe hain, ek do din mein release ho jayegi. Usme humne is company ko full detail mein cover kiya hai aur humne usme bataya bhi hai ke kaise humne isme pichle 18 mahine mein invest karke 5 to 6 times profit kamaya hai.
