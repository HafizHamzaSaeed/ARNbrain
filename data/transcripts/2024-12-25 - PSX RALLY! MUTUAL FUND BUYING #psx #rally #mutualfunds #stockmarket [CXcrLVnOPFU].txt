**ARN:** Acha Chaudhry Noman bhai, yeh market mein bohot rally aa gayi hai, mutual funds se kaafi buying kar rahe hain, toh yeh kya ho raha hai market mein, kuch batayenge humein?

**Chaudhry Noman:** Market mein jo recent explosive rally chal rahi hai, iske peeche mutual funds ki buying hai. Acha, main aapko thoda sa na history batata hoon iski. Aapko bilkul samajh aa jayega ke kya ho raha hai. 2017 mein Pakistan mein mutual funds ke paas total investment takreeban 800 billion ki hoti thi aur woh takreeban aadhi equity mein hoti thi, aadhi jo hai fixed income mein hoti thi. Ab uske baad jo pichle saat-aath saal rahe hain, usme interest rates bhi high rahe hain aur koi lamba time nahi aaya jis mein equities ne na bohot acha perform kiya ho. Aaj ki soorat-e-haal agar hum dekhein, toh woh jo 800 billion ka total number hai, woh 3,000 arab pe pahunch gaya hai aur sirf ek mahine pehle tak 8% sirf equity mein tha, yaani ke dhaa-i-sau billion rupay takreeban. Aur ab inho ne jab buying shuru ki hai, abhi bhi yeh sirf 10-11% pe hi pahunche hain, toh aap dekhein kitni badi rally aa gayi hai. 2023 aur 2024 ka jo first half tha, usme log kehte the ke yeh jo equities ki rally chal rahi hai, yeh zyada time nahi chalegi aur humein toh bhai fixed 20% mil raha hai toh humein kya zaroorat hai shift hone ki. Par ab pichle mahine se mutual funds ka jo return hai, woh 10% pe aa gaya hai aur Pakistan mein 10% return kuch nahi hota. Is liye aapne khud bhi dekha hoga fixed income ke representative bhi aapko message kar rahe hain ke aap apne investment ka 20 se 30% PSX mein invest kar dein. Aur ab yeh bohot tezi se jo hai shifting hogi aur yeh percentage agle kuch mahine mein at least 20 se 25% pe pahunchegi. Equities mein jo investment aayegi, woh takreeban 500 billion ki additional aa sakti hai agle kuch mahine mein. Isi wajah se next few months mein agar koi major shock nahi aaya, ek bohot hi explosive kism ki rally continue rahegi.
