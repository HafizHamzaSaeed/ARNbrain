2023 ki tarah, 2024 bhi Pakistan stock market ne apne naam kar liya aur 2024 mein investors ne taqreeban 80% return kamaya. Jo most popular asset class hai Pakistan mein yani ke real estate, woh again jo hai 2023 ki tarah usne bohot struggle kiya. On average taqreeban minus 7 se le ke minus 10% jo hai us mein logon ko loss hua. Gold ne bhi 2024 mein decent 25 se 27% return diye hain aur jin logon ne bank accounts mein apni savings kar rahe the unhon ne bhi taqreeban 17 se le ke 22% return kamaya. 2025 mein bhi jo asset classes hain Pakistan mein, un mein similar trend continue rehne ke chances hain. Jin logon ne banks mein savings ki hui thi un ke returns kafi zyada kam ho jayenge aur yeh after tax jo hai 10% se bhi kam ho jayenge next year mein, yeh expectations hain. Stock exchange ka positive trend jo hai woh continue rehne ke bohot zyada chances hain aur kyun ke government ek naya law le kar aa rahi hai jis se jo hai aap ne jitni apni wealth show ki hogi apne tax return mein us se 1.3 times ki value ki hi aap property khareed sakte hain. To is se jo hai aisa lag raha hai ke real estate ke jo challenges chal rahe hain, woh continue rahenge 2025 mein. 2024 ke asset class ke returns pe hum detailed video jo hai hamare channel pe soon aane wali hai, jis mein hum 2025 ki kya expectations hain aur yeh sab kyun ho raha hai, yeh aap ko detail mein explain karenge.
