Adnan: Main aap ko na do teen themes batata hoon, jiske andar jo hai na bumper profits expected hain. Aur agar aap thoda closely follow kar rahe hain, toh aap ko pata hai ke aaj kal kaunse cement player jo hain woh bumper profits kama rahe hain, toh unke liye bhi bahut zyada bumper results jo hain woh expected hain. Iske ilawa pharmaceuticals jo hain, unka jo trend hai of increasing profits, woh is dafa bhi continue rahega. Specially, jo do teen companies hum log dekh rahe hain, unho ne mazeed apne products ki price increase ki hai, toh wahan bhi bahut zyada profits increase ki expectations hain.
