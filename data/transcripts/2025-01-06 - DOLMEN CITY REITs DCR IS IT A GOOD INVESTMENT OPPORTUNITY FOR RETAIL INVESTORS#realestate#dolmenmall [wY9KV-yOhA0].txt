Interviewer: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, Lahore mein to ab Dolmen Mall khul gaya hai. Pair rakhne ki wahan jagah nahi hai, itna bara hai wo. To phir kya kehte hain, khareedein Dolmen ke shares?

ARN: Nahi, ye ek bohat bari misconception hai. Log sochte hain ke Dolmen Mall Lahore mein khula hai to jo listed Dolmen REIT hai hum us mein invest karein ge to hamein us se fayda hoga. Jo Dolmen REIT listed hai us mein sirf Karachi ka mall aur us ke sath jo office building hai, wo aati hai. Is Lahore ke Dolmen khulne se us company pe koi impact nahi aaye ga. Future mein ho sakta hai ke ye jo Lahore mein Dolmen khula hai, is ki ek separate REIT aaye. To jab wo aaye gi aap us mein invest kar sakte hain. Ye jo trend shuru ho gaya hai ab stock exchange pe REITs listed hone ka, ye bohat hi positive trend hai. Is mein jo hai har investor individual files lene ke bajaye ya koi mehangi property ke liye paise jama karne ke bajaye directly is mein equal partner ban jata hai. Aur Dolmen group jo hai un ka record bhi bohat acha hai. Pichle das-pandra saalon mein har saal taqreeban aap ka jo rent hai, wo 10% se barhta rehta hai aur company ke profits bhi jo hain, wo 10% se barhte rehte hain. To aap ko long-term mein ye company ek decent return de sakti hai compared to jo aap khud ek property khareedein, phir us ko rent out karein, phir us ke saare kharche bardasht karein, to ye ek bohat acha option hai for all retail investors.
