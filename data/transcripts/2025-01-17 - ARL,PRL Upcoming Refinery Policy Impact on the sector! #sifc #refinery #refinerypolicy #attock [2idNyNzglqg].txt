**ARN**: Acha Chaudhry Rizwan bhai hum ye refinery policies ka do teen paanch saal se sun rahe hain, to ab to SIFC bhi involve ho gayi hai, do teen meetings ho gayi hain do teen mahine se. To ab kya aap ko lagta hai ye resolve ho jayegi?

**Chaudhry Rizwan**: Bilkul SIFC ka jo agenda hota hai har mahine ka us pe ye pichle do teen mahine se aa rahi hai aur thodi si is pe developments bhi hui hain, but abhi bhi bahut saari cheezein hain jo ke challenges hain. FBR ka tax collection shortfall jo hai wo abhi bhi kaafi zyada hai to zaroori nahi hai ke ye bahut jaldi approve ho jaye abhi isme kaafi zyada challenges hain. Is ke ilawa agar aap petroleum minister ka interview bhi sunenge recent, to wo bhi koi itne zyada inclined nahi lag rahe ke mazeed refineries ke liye jo hai mazeed incentives create kiye jayein. Pakistan refinery jo hai wo already policy mein change aaye na aaye wo apni investment jo hai kar rahe hain aur wo almost 2 billion dollar jo invest karenge. Attock refinery aur National refinery jab ye policy mein thode bahut changes ho jayenge to wo 500 million dollar each jo hai apni refinery up-gradation mein invest karenge aur jo Cnergyico hai wo almost 1 billion dollar jo hai wo invest karegi. Ab in saari companies ko agar dekhein to sirf Attock refinery ke paas hi cash available hai. So baqi jitni bhi teenon refineries hain jab ye refinery policy mein thode changes aa jayenge aur aaj se ek do saal baad jab ye capex karna hoga to bahut ache chances hain ke ye teenon jo hai kisi na kisi tareeqe se right issue mein jayengi.
