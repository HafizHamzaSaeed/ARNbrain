Speaker 1: Abdul Rehman bhai, Mari ka phir result aa raha hai Monday ko, to phir aa raha hai 800% bonus?

ARN: Ji Monday ko Mari Energies ka half-yearly result hai. 800% bonus ke to bohat kam chances hain. Wo ek bohat hi lambay time baad jo hai ek event hota hai aur wo already ho chuka hai is saal mein. Is dafa half-yearly result mein dividend zaroor aayega. Latest development ye hai ke Pakistan mein jo bohat purani gas fields hain na, un pe ab royalty jo hai 12.5% ke bajaye 27.5% apply hogi. Aur Mari field pe ye mid-November se apply hogi to is dafa second quarter ki earning jo hai, first quarter ke muqable mein thodi si kam hongi. Company ki policy hai ke 6 mahine mein jitna bhi profit hota hai, uska approximately 50% jo hai wo cash dividend mein de dete hain. Aur agar going forward is company ko dekhein, to ek aur inki KPK mein field hai jo ke jaise hi uski production start hogi, ho sakta hai 3 mahine baad ho, ho sakta hai 6 mahine baad ho, us se jo iski saal ki earnings hain, abhi taqreeban 60 rupees hain, wo badh ke koi taqreeban 70 se 75 rupees per share ho jayengi. Aur ye kaafi acchi company hai, high ROIC company hai aur ye usually bull markets mein 10 plus P/E ratio pe trade hoti hai.
