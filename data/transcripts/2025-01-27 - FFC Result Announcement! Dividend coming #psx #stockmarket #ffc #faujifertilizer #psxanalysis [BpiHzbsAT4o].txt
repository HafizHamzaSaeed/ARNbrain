**Speaker 1:** Achha Abdul Rehman bhai, pehle bhi FFC ne first quarter mein bhi dividend nahi diya, toh phir ab aa raha hai 25 rupay ka dividend jo sab log keh rahe hain?

**ARN:** Dividend exact kitna aana hai, yeh toh phir on the day hi pata chalta hai, board of directors hi decide karte hain. Toh, is cheez ke chances hain ke dono quarters ka dividend mila ke aa jaye. Lekin again, yeh us din on the day, jo board of directors hain woh decide karenge.
