To aaiye start karte hain with business number one, cement business. Cement business ka number one rule: cement cannot travel. Cement un chand commodities mein se hai jiska finished product ki value per ton uske raw material se kaafi kam hoti hai. Cement ka raw material coal takreeban aajkal 30,000 rupay per ton se leke 40,000 rupay per ton tak chal raha hai, but jo cement hai uski ex-factory price jo hai wo takreeban 15,000 rupay hai. Aur logistic costs ka to aapko pata hi hoga. Aap ek lakh rupay per ton ki value ko truck ke through bhejein ya das lakh rupay per ton ki value ki cheez ko truck ke through bhejein, jo kiraya hai wo 3,000 se 10,000 rupay per ton hi hai Pakistan mein, depending aapne kis jagah se kis shehar tak ye cheez pahonchani hai. So kyunke cement cannot travel, uski jo logistic transport ki cost hai wo bahut zyada hai compared to its actual value, is mein bahut zyada importance hoti hai ke aapka plant kis jagah pe located hai. Aur isi wajah se north-based cement plants jo hain uski economics, south-based cement plants se kaafi zyada different hain aur ye aapas mein compete bhi nahi karte. Isi concept of 'cement cannot travel' ko agar extend karein, to phir hum ye bhi is se deduce kar sakte hain ke ye industry import se protected hai. Agar kabhi China se imported cement aane lag jayega aur wo Karachi port pe aayega, to usko north mein pahonchte pahonchte hi uski cost itni zyada barh jayegi ke wo north ke cement plant se compete nahi kar sakta.
