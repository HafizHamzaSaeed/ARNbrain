**ARN**: Achha Adnan bhai, Sazgar ke ache numbers aa gaye hain, 2000 units sold ho gaye hain. Company bhi sahi lag rahi hai, to ye aap kya kehte hain, le leni chahiye ye wali company?

**Adnan**: Dekhiye iske liye sabse pehle aapko samajhna hoga year end phenomenon. Jab bhi ye jo aakhri do mahine hote hain, November aur December, usme log kya karte hain ke unko naye saal ki gaadi ka jo model hai wo chahiye hota hai, to wo apna jo purchase decision hai usko thoda sa delay kar dete hain taake unko naye saal ka jo model hai wo mil jaye aur kal ko jab wo do teen saal baad is gaadi ko sale karenge, thodi si behtar price mil jaayegi. So ye jo year end ke mahine hote hain, isme aapne sale number ki bajaye production number actual mein dekhna hota hai. Koi bhi jo auto sector ki company hai wo advance mein gaadi bana ke nahi rakhti, jab unke paas orders hote hain tabhi wo saari production karte hain. November ke mahine mein bhi Sazgar ne almost 900 se 1000 units produce kiye the, but kuch logon ne kaha ke hum isko January mein jo hai receive karenge, is wajah se jo sales thi wo 500 ki thi. Aur abhi bhi jo 2000 ka number aaya hai, most probably December mein jo hai 400 se 500 logon ne apni delivery li hogi, baaki 1500 gaadiyan jo hain wo logon ne January mein delivery li hogi. So abhi jo result aa raha hai, October, November aur December ka, usme agar aap combine karein to taqreeban 2000 gaadiyan jo hain wo sale hui hain jo ke pichle quarter ke muqable mein thodi si kam hain. To thoda sa EPS most probably company ka pichle quarter ke muqable mein kam aayega. But I think jo dividend policy hai of rupees 10 every quarter, wo jo hai continue rahegi.
