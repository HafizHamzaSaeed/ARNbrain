ARN: Acha Junaid bhai, ye government ne phir bank se baat kar li hai aur 1000 arab ka loan bhi milne wala hai to keh rahe hain IPPs ki circular debt khatam ho jayegi to kya ye baat sach hai?

Junaid: Dekhein pehle bhi humne aapko bataya hai ke ye wali jo government hai, with the collaboration of military establishment, inka boht zyada focus hai is cheez pe ke jo Pakistan mein bijli ki qeemat hai na wo kam ki jaye. To jaise hi pehli dafa jab IPPs ke contract jo hain unho ne terminate kiye thay, uske baad humne aapko bataya tha ke ab ye jo bhi kaam karne jayein na to usko boht seriously lijiyega. Ab sirf ek hi cheez hai jo ke future koi bhi power sector se related news aati hai na, jo usko rok sakti hai aur wo hoti hai ke IMF jo hai na wo agree na kare. To abhi jo hai government ne plan banaya hai ke hum banks se 1250 billion rupees borrow karein ge KIBOR -1 pe aur us se jo hai power sector ki saari jo circular debt hai usko retire kar dein ge aur is se jo aapke bills mein PHL surcharge lag kar aata hai, wo actual mein circular debt ki aap interest payment kar rahe hote hain. To ab unho ne plan kiya hai ke ab is surcharge ko thoda sa kam kar dein ge aur 5 saal tak jo surcharge collect hoga us se is loan ki repayment bhi ho jayegi aur uska interest bhi khatam ho jayega.
