**Interviewer:** Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, ek do din se market phir upar ja rahi hai, toh phir aa gayi bull market dobara se wapis?

**ARN:** Acha bull market jo hai woh kahin nahi gayi thi, lekin aapne dekha hoga ke bull market mein bhi thode thode do do teen teen mahine ke slots aate hain, jahan pe market thodi si cool down ho jati hai, thodi si side ways ho jati hai, toh yeh bhi bull market ka hi part hota hai. Toh ek tarah se pichle dedh saal se bull market chal rahi hai. Agar aap is bull market ko thoda sa study karenge, July 2023 se toh aapko samajh aayegi ke jab bhi market jo hai, 30-40% badhti hai, toh phir woh do se teen mahine ke liye side ways ho jati hai. Aur jo sabse important cheez thi jiski wajah se log bhi thoda sa hesitant ho rahe the, woh tha ke IMF review mein kya hoga? Toh is hafte se jo hai IMF ki team ke saath negotiations bhi start ho gayi hain, aur IMF negotiations mein US ke saath Pakistan ke relations kis tareeqay ke chal rahe hain, woh bahut important hote hain. Aur jo December end ke results the, woh bhi sab companies ke kafi ache aa gaye hain. Kuch companies ke reh gaye hain, toh ek tarah se profitability side se bhi thode logon ko jo ho raha tha ke profit increase honge ke nahi, toh wahan bhi achi news hai, aur ek tarah se ab ache chances hain ke momentum phir se start ho jayega.
