Speaker 1: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, bari hype thi Barkat ki IPO ki, oversubscribe bhi ho gaya hai. Lock pe lock lagaye ja rahe hain ab, to le lena chahiye apne portfolio mein?

ARN: Ji Barkat Friesland ka kafi successful IPO hua hai aur company bhi kafi achi hai. Inka jo international partner hai, wo inko kafi zyada jo hai, faida dega future mein. Pakistan mein bhi jab bhi kisi company ke paas aap jaate hain to aap batate hain ke mera jo international partner hai, wo duniya ki bohat bari company hai, to local market increase karne mein bhi aap ko faida hota hai jab aap ke paas ek naya product hota hai. Aur agar aap export karne ja rahe hote hain, tab bhi aap ko is collaboration ka bohat zyada faida hota hai. To company ke growth prospects jo hain, wo kafi zyada ache hain. Aur jab ye thode chote size ke IPOs hote hain jin mein jo hai 1 billion se kam raise ho raha hota hai, to bohat zyada logon ka jab interest aa jaye, to is tarah se oversubscription bhi ho jati hai aur phir jab uski trading start hoti hai, to demand zyada hone ki wajah se uski price kafi zyada upar jaane ke chances hote hain. To ye to Barkat Friesland ki jo positive sides thin, wo maine aap ko explain kar dein. Inki jo thodi si negative sides hain, wo ye hain ke company itna innovative product bhi lekar aayi hai, itne ache inke customer bhi hain, itna acha time period bhi dete hain unko payment karne ka do se teen mahine ka, phir bhi, even though ye SEZ mein located hain, income tax bhi inpe apply nahi hota, phir bhi jo inke net margins hain wo 6 to 7% ki range mein hain, to ye bohat zyada concerning hai.
