Speaker 1: Acha Abdur Rehman bhai, sab log keh rahe thay ke interest rate neeche aa jayein ge, ye sab har jagah ye khabar thi ke interest rate neeche aa rahe hain. Inflation tou kam ho gayi hai magar State Bank ne interest rate kam hi nahi kiya, ye kya hua?

ARN: Jaise ke aapne State Bank ki statement mein bhi parha hoga, do teen factors thay jiski wajah se State Bank ne jo hai thoda sa cautious approach liya hai. Aur ye cautious approach aapne jab bhi dekha hoga jab inflation neeche aane lag gayi thi, 10-11 percent pe aa gayi thi lekin interest rate jo hain wo 22 percent pe hi rokay hue thay. Tou State Bank jo hai thoda sa cautious raha hai, unho ne do teen factors jo highlight kiye hain wo ye hai ke ramzan mein thodi si inflation barhne ke chances hotay hain aur doosra important factor ye hai ke jaise jaise interest rate neeche aaye hain tou ab aahista aahista imports barh rahe hain. Tou State Bank ye bhi chah raha hai ke aisa na ho ke current account deficit jo hai wo phir se barhne lag jaye aur phir jo saari mehnat ki hai pichle ek se do saalon mein wo saari jo hai wo zaya ho jaye. So, ye tou analysis ho gayi is particular monetary policy ki. But going forward, interest rate cuts ke abhi kaafi zyada expectations hain aur agar aapko future inflation predict karni ho Pakistan mein, uske do main determinants hotay hain, ek hota hai ke dollar parity kya chal rahi hai aur ek jo hai global oil prices jo hain unka trend kis taraf hai. Aur interest rates ki jo downward trajectory thi, wo next monetary policy se phir se start ho jayegi.
