**ARN**: Muzammil bhai, aaj kal phir se dollar upar jaane lagay hain, matlab thora thora barh raha hai. Current account deficit bhi aa gaya hai, to kahin ye phir se to nahi 400 pe jaane laga?

**Muzammil**: Nahi nahi, bilkul koi isme ghabrane ki baat nahi hai. Last few months mein thore se imports barhe hain, lekin uske saath saath ek bohat hi blockbuster event hua hai Pakistan mein ke February ke mahine mein 3.1 billion dollar ke remittances aa gaye hain. February jo hai, doosre mahino se agar aap compare karein, to isme do se teen din kam hote hain. To hamesha jo hai February mein 200 se 300 million dollar ke jo remittances hain, wo kam aate hain, lekin is dafa jo hai 3.1 billion dollar ke jo February mein remittances aaye hain, iska matlab hai ke aglay jo ek do mahine hain, jisme Eid bhi aa jaati hai, to ho sakta hai ye remittance ka jo figure hai, ye 3.3, 3.4 billion dollar tak bhi chala jaaye. So again, agar aap analyze karenge, dollar ko agar aapne future mein predict karna ho to sabse important cheez hoti hai inflows aur outflows. So inflows jo hain, usme remittances kaafi zyada increase ho rahe hain, exports bhi achhi trajectory pe ja rahe hain aur imports bhi jo hai, koi out of control nahi hue. To ye jo parity hai, ye most probably yahan pe stable hi rahegi. Aur iske saath saath State Bank ne jaise bataya hai ke is saal ab jo char mahine reh gaye hain, usme sirf unho ne 3 billion dollars ki repayments karni hain aur abhi jaise ye IMF ka jo review hai, ye complete hoga aur climate financing Pakistan ko mil jayegi, to 2.2 billion dollar to yahin se aa jayenge.
