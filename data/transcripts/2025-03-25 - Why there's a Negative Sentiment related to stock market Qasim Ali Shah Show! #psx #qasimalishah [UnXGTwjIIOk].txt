Interviewer: Main ye poochna chahunga aapse, Abdul Rehman Najam Sahab, ye Pakistan mein stock exchange ke hawaale se negativity kyun hai?

Abdul Rehman Najam (ARN): Achha dekhein, har cheez ki ek reality hoti hai aur ek uski story hoti hai. To Pakistan stock exchange ki na reality kaafi achhi hai. Story shaayad isliye thodi si buri hai kyunki mujhe bhi yaad hai jab main university se graduate hua tha, 2015 ki baat hai, us time pe mere saare doston ne account khulwaya tha. Us time pe bhi bull market chal rahi thi. To jaise ek famous saying hai na, "Every failure is an orphan and every successful story has many fathers." To pichhle jo chaar paanch saal guzre the, agar aap pichhle dedh saal ko nikaal dein, to stock market ke returns itne achhe nahi the. Pakistan mein jo boom bust cycle hote hain na, to ek tarah se bust cycle chal raha tha. To slowly slowly logon ka rujuan doosri taraf chala jaata hai. But agar aapne long-term wealth creation karni hai, us mein utaar chadhaao aata rehta hai, lekin jab aap 25 saal ki cheez dekhte hain na, to phir ye saari cheezein khatam ho jaati hain ke jaise koi cheez do saal ke liye bahut zyada badh jaati hai, phir bilkul hi baith jaati hai. Koi cheez ek saal mein bahut badh jaati hai, lekin 25 saal mein actual mein aapko na wealth creation karni hoti hai. Yaani ek business ki value 25 saal mein aise hi nahi bahut upar chali jaati. Us ko koi plant lagaana hai, koi profit kamaana hai, phir ek aur plant lagaana hai. To actual mein usne apni value badhaayi hai, tabhi stock exchange ne 25 saal mein itne achhe returns diye hain.

Interviewer: Bilkul theek hai.
