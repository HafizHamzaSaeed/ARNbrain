ARN: Dekhein stock market bullish chal rahi ho ya bearish chal rahi ho, uska ek company pe zaroor impact hota hai, lekin us ke sath-sath us company ka khud ka business kitna accha ja raha hai na, woh bhi boht important hota hai in determining a company's stock price. So EPCL ke case mein do major negative developments hui hain, jiski wajah se iski jo earning power hai, woh thodi si down ho gayi hai. Sab se important yeh hai ke is sector mein aapki jo main cost hoti hai na, woh hoti hai bijli ki cost aur yeh company banati thi gas ke power plant se bijli. Toh pehle gas ka rate aaj se takreeban do saal pehle barah sau rupee per MMBTU hota tha, aur ab woh chaar hazar bhi cross kar gaya hai. Toh iski wajah se is company ka jo saare margins hote the, woh increase in electricity cost mein, jo hai, woh distribute ho gaye hain. Dusra important reason ke yeh ek import parity business hai, yani ke yeh log jo cheez banate hain, uski price jo hai woh internationally determine hoti hai, aur internationally bhi jo PVC ke margins hain, woh koi itne khaas nahi hain because of global outlook.
