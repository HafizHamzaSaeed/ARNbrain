Assalamualaikum everyone. Aaj stock market kafi zyada down hai almost minus 5% pe chal rahi hai. But isme jo hai fundamentally koi bhi change nahi hai. Sirf ek cheez ho sakti hai ke is tarah ki jab koi global level pe shock aati hai na to kuch percentage of investors jo hain vo safety ki taraf chale jaate hain. Bas unko kehte hain hume is time pe jo hai is asset class me invested hona hi nahi hai. Vo acchi development ho ya buri development ho, usko analyze analyze kiye bagair bas unko hota hai flight to safety. Isme kuch foreign investors bhi ho sakte hain. Unko hai ke hum apne jo saare high risk assets, jo foreign funds hote hain na unke liye, eh, ye jo frontier markets hote hain ye high risk assets hote hain. To jab koi crisis aata hai vo sabse pehle unko sell kar dete hain. Ho sakta hai unhone apni US treasury bills me percentage badhani ho ya unhone gold khareedna ho, vo bhi ek safe asset samjha jata hai. To sirf wahi buyers jo hain vo ek tarah se, even though kuch mulko ko is se faida bhi hoga aur Pakistan un me se ek hi lag raha hai lekin unki poori thinking ke hisab se unko flight to safety karni hoti hai. Aur maybe ho sakta hai usme fir is cheez ko anticipate karte hue aise kuch log locally bhi hote hain jo mutual funds ke through invest karte hain aur vo redemption daal dete hain aur fir funds ko aage jo hai usko execute karna hota hai. To koi bhi aisi cheez nahi hai jisse aapko pareshan hona chahiye. Sirf ek risk hai is saari development ka ke Pakistan jo dollar me karze leta hai na, other than IMF, jo commercial banks aur idhar udhar se jo milta hai, vo thoda sa mushkil ho jata hai lena jab is tarah ki situation chal rahi hoti hai duniya me. Lekin aaj kal waise hi Pakistan ko koi itne khaas mil nahi rahe commercial loans. Jab tak inki credit rating B me nahi jayegi, jo ke saal ke aakhir me jaake kahi hoga, tab tak waise hi koi bank jo hai itna interest rate nahi hai. Aur baaki saari developments jo hain vo Pakistan ke liye positive hain. Oil price aur saari cheezen, doosri commodities bhi. To bilkul kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi hai, do se teen din me ye saari cheezen jo hain vo smooth out ho jayengi. Thank you.
