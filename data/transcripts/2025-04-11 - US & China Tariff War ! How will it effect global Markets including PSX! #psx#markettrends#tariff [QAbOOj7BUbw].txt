**Speaker 1:** Yaar chalti phirti mandi, 100 100 percent ke tariff lag rahe hain, kabhi US kuch karne lag jata hai, kabhi China kuch karne lag jata hai. Is sab ka follow up karte karte mera to dimaag band hone laga hai, ek to ye roz ek news aane lag jati hai ki tariff lag gaya. To is sab ke baare mein kuch batayein, market pe iska kya impact aayega?

**ARN:** Acha is mein aap ne dekha hoga ke bohot ziada analysis hoti hai ki itne tariffs lag jayenge to itni global GDP jo hai kam ho jayegi, us ke hisab se oil bhi neeche aa raha hai. Lekin ab jis range mein tariffs chale gaye hain na, ab ye ek, do bandon ki ladai ban gayi hai. 84% China ne apply kar diya hai, 104% USA ne apply kar diya hai. Ye jo tariffs lagenge, us ki wajah se har mulk mein jo demand hai wo kam ho jayegi aur company ke profits kam ho jayenge. Aur stock market ki jo valuation hai wo company ke profits ke sath chalti hai. Ab jo next step hai na is war mein, wo ye hai ke Europe kis tareeqe se behave karta hai. Agar to Europe bhi USA ke sath, China ke sath behave karne lag gaya, to ye war aur intensify ho jayegi. Is ke sath sath USA aur China ab kya next step lenge, wo bhi aap ne dekhna hoga. Ab jo next steps ho sakte hain, abhi jo war thi wo sirf trade war ho rahi thi. Services ki bhi ek war hoti hai. USA ek bohot bada services ka exporter hai, to China us ko bhi target kar sakta hai. Is ke sath sath trillions of dollars ki investment China se USA ke T-bills mein bhi jati hai. China us ko bhi ban kar sakta hai. To agar to ye services war aur capital flows ki taraf baat gayi, to duniya jo hai bilkul ek Armageddon mein chali jayegi.
