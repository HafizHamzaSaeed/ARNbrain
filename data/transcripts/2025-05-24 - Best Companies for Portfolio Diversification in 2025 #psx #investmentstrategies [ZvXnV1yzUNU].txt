**Host:** Ziada companies mein invest karna faidamand hota hai ya aapko lagta hai ke jo company upar ja rahi hai usi ke andar paise bas laga dein us time pe saare?

**ARN:** Dekhein ye jo upar aur neeche jaane wali cheez hoti hai na, iske liye aapko phir knowledge chahiye hogi. Ye bhi aap gather kar sakte hain slowly slowly. Lekin jo shuru kar raha hai na, usko 20 companies jo hain na top ki usmein apna portfolio divide kar dena chahiye.

**Host:** Ye jo sense develop karna hai ke kaunsi company upar ja rahi hai, kaun-kaunsa stock upar ja raha hai, mujhe kahan invest karna hai, agar 1 se 10 tak isko ratio dein, matlab 1 is easiest aur 10 jo hai wo hardest hai, to ye kitna mushkil kaam hai?

**ARN:** Ye alag alag depend karta hai company company pe. Dekhein jab aapne kisi bhi company mein sahi tareeqe se usko samajhna hai na, to aapne uske owner ke barabar knowledge haasil karni hai. Warren Buffett ne ye kiya hua hai. Warren Buffett agar aaj itna wealth creation kiya hai apne liye bhi aur apne investor ke liye bhi, to uska reason ye hai ke wo oil and gas ko bhi samajhta hai, wo gaadiyon ke business ko bhi samajhta hai, wo har business ko samajhta hai aur phir dekhta hai ke aaj ki soorat-e-haal ke hisab se kaunsa accha hai. Kaunsi company jo hai future mein ziada growth lekar aayegi. To uski understanding owners ki tarah hoti hai aur uske paas option hota hai. Wo kabhi kisi ke sath partner ban sakta hai, kabhi kisi ke sath ban jata hai.
