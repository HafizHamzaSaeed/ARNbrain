rich versus poor mindset mein basic farq hai hi ek. rich mindset hai na, wo earn karega, usme se ek good amount save karega, aur sirf usko save nahi karega, takiye ke neeche nahi rakh dega, ya is tarah nahi karega ke bank mein ja ke rakh diya current account mein. Wo usko foran ek aise asset mein convert karega jo ke inflation ke sath grow hota hai. Duniya mein har jagah inflation hoti hai. Pakistan mein to boht ziyada hoti hai. Pakistan mein devaluation bhi ziyada hoti hai. To aapko aisi asset class choose karni hai jo ke aapko long-term mein achhe returns de. First step, ek good amount save karein, luxuries se door ho jayein. Luxuries... iske liye na ek famous Indian investor hain wo kehte hain ke "your income should feed your investments." Yani ke pehle income aaye, usi waqt luxuries pe nahi jana, pehle investments pe jana hai. Usme se save karna hai, usko investment mein convert karna hai, wo grow hogi over time, phir ye jo passive income aa rahi hai na investments se, that should feed your luxuries.
