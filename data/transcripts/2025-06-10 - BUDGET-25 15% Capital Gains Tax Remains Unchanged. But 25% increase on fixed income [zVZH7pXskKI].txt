Ek mashhoor kahawat hai, aapne bhi zaroor suni hogi, ke agar aap mujhe koi faida nahi de sakte, toh mera jo competitor hai na, usko nuksaan pahuncha do. Aur yahi story hai Budget 2025. Budget 2025 mein stocks ka dividend aur capital gain tax toh 15% pe hi maintain kiya gaya, lekin jo fixed income aur jo log bank mein save karte hain, us pe tax rate jo hai woh 20 se 25% tak barha diya gaya. Yaani ke ab toh hukoomat bhi aapko keh rahi hai ke aapne stock market mein investment karni hai. Agle saal agar aapne bank mein paise rakhe hue hain ya aap fixed income mutual funds mein invest karte hain, toh return taqreeban 9 se 10% hoga, aur after tax yeh taqreeban 7% ban jayega. Aur doosri taraf, stock market aap dekh hi rahe hain pichle do saalon se kitna accha return de rahi hai, toh yeh jo aap peeche bull dekh rahe hain, yeh most probably agle saal bhi roar karti jayegi.
