[Published: 2025-06-10]

Budget 2025 ki sab se bari khabar. Salaried individuals ki real take home income aglay saal ek billion dollar se barhne ja rahi hai. Agar aapki monthly salary ek lakh rupaye hai tou aapko tax reduce honay ki wajah se dhai hazar rupaye izafi income milegi. Agar aapki salary do lakh rupaye par mahina hai tou tax reduce honay ki wajah se aapko che hazar rupaye extra milenge. Har saal Pakistan mein salary taqreeban 10% barhti hai aur almost chaar saal baad jo hai ye pehli dafa hua hai ke jo inflation rate hai wo 10% se bhi kam hai. In dono cheezon ko agar combine karlein tou aglay saal Pakistan salaried individuals ki spending power jo hai wo ek billion dollar se increase ho jaye gi aur is se urban economy flourish karegi.
