[Published: 2025-06-11]

Cement sector ke liye Budget 2025 bohot zyada positive hai. Sabse pehle coal pe jo 5% duty thi, usko maintain kiya gaya hai aur iske sath sath mazeed koi FED increase nahi ki gayi. Cement ki asal demand aati hai government projects se aur real estate se. Federal PSDP to thoda sa kam hai lekin jo provincial PSDP hai, woh 3 trillion se bhi zyada hai jo ke bohot bada positive hai. Aur real estate pe to aapne dekh hi liya hoga ke jo purchase pe taxes hain, unko 1.5% se kam kar diya gaya hai. So pichle aik saal mein to waise hi cement ki sale itni ho nahi rahi thi, ab thodi bohot real estate mein activity shuru hogi, government ke projects shuru honge to sale jo hai, agle saal mein increase karegi. Globally coal jo hai, woh sasta hota ja raha hai. Pichle do teen mahino mein cement ke bag ki price bhi badh gayi hai taqreeban dedh sau rupay se aur jo electricity ke rate hain, woh bhi taqreeban paanch rupay se kam ho gaye hain. To outlook for cement sector is very positive. Cement sector mein kaunsi company sabse best hai, uske liye aap humare channel pe aik detailed video dekhein about this sector.
