[Published: 2025-06-12]

Steel sector ke liye bhi budget 2025 bohot positive hai. Saat saal ki koshishon ke baad finally FATA PATA pe reduced sales tax wali problem jo hai, woh solve ho gayi. FATA PATA mein yeh jo fake factories lagi hui thin, yeh bagair sales tax ke jo hai sariya import kar leti thin aur poore Pakistan mein bechti thin. Ab kyun ke yeh khatam ho gaya hai, toh ab steel ki jo companies hain, woh zyada volumes bhi bech payengi aur price bhi shayad thodi si increase ho jaye.

Cost side pe jaise ke government ne bataya hai ke humne 500 se zyada categories pe RD jo hai zero kar di hai, toh most probably scrap import pe jo 5% RD thi, woh bhi abolish ho gayi hai. Aur iske saath saath 2% additional customs duty thi, woh bhi khatam ho gayi hai. Pichle do teen mahino mein globally scrap ki qeemat bhi kam ho gayi hai aur iske saath saath bijli bhi 5 se 7 rupay per unit jo hai sasti ho gayi hai. Yeh bhi steel sector ke liye positive cheezein hain.

PSDP bhi bohot positive hai. Zyada tar log jo hai federal PSDP pe focus karte hain lekin asal construction aur infrastructure projects toh provinces karte hain, aur provinces ka PSDP budget almost 3000 arab rupay hai.

So overall steel sector ki outlook kaafi acchi lag rahi hai. Sirf ek cheez hai jiska aapne khayal rakhna hai aur woh yeh hai ke agle kuch dino mein government jo hai RDs announce karegi ke kis kis category pe kam karne ja rahi hai. Toh usmein ho sakta hai ke imported steel jo hai uspe bhi RD jo hai, jo ke currently 30% hai, yeh bohot zyada reduce ho jaye. Toh isse jo hai aapne alert rehna hai.
