[Published: 2025-06-15]

Auto sector ke liye budget 2025 kaisa tha? Sab ke mind mein yahi sawal chal raha hai aur jawab ye hai ke auto sector ka asal budget to abhi aaya hi nahi hai.

Number one issue jo Pakistan mein chal raha hai, jo ke Pakistan ne IMF ke sath agree bhi kiya hai aur jo decide karega ke Pakistan mein future mein used cars chalengi ya jo locally assembled cars chalengi, wo ye hai ke Pakistan used gaariyon ke limit aur RD kya decide karta hai.

RD yaani ke regulatory duty imported cars pe bohot zyada kam hone ja rahi hai aur iska indiyah Finance Bill ke salient features mein bhi bata diya gaya hai. Lekin jo RDs hoti hain, wo budget ke Finance Bill ke bajaye jo SRO aata hai jo ke agle kuch dino mein aayega, uske through jo hai, wo communicate kiya jata hai. Budget speech mein Finance Minister ne bhi keh diya hai ke 5 saal mein Pakistan mein saari regulatory duties jo hain, wo khatam ho jayengi.

So, budget 2025 jo hai, wo auto sector ke liye negative hai aur ye kitna negative hai, ye humein agle kuch din mein pata chal jayega jab ye SRO aayega related to regulatory duties. Abhi tak ki jo information aayi hai, usmein jo 850 cc se kam gaariyan thi, unpe pehle 12.5% sales tax hota tha, usko badha ke 18% kar diya gaya hai, jis se choti gaariyan jo hain, takreeban ek se dedh lakh rupay mehengi ho jayengi.

Aur iske ilawa jitni bhi engine se chalne wali gaariyan hain, aur ismein hybrids bhi included hain, inpe jo hai, 1 se lekar 3% tak ki levy apply kar di gayi hai. 1300 cc se kam gaariyon pe 1%, 1300 se 1800 cc pe 2%, aur 1800 cc se upar gaariyon pe 3%.

So, local gaariyan to awaam ke liye mehengi hone ja rahi hain aur auto sector ke liye bhi negative hai. Ab dekhte hain ke government regulatory duty imported gaariyon pe kitni kam karti hai. Waise to budget ke salient features mein bata diya hai ke maximum RD 50% hi hogi lekin phir bhi chalo kuch der intezar kar lete hain.
