[Published: 2025-06-18]

Budget 2025 ka salaried individuals ko relief dene ke baad number one qadam aur woh yeh hai ke ineligible persons jo hai, Pakistan mein gadi, property, aur bank account jo hai unko kholne mein dushwari hogi. Yani ke non-filer category ko jarr se khatam kar diya gaya hai. Ineligible person woh hai jisne return file nahi ki ya phir agar usne jo last return file ki thi usme jo usne wealth show ki thi us se zyada woh purchase karne ja raha hai. To ineligible persons jo hain woh 850cc se zyada gadi bhi nahi khareed sakte, Hajj aur Umrah ke ilawa foreign travel bhi nahi kar sakte, real estate ki purchase jo hai ek limit se zyada jo government abhi announce karegi woh bhi nahi kar sakte, aur is ke sath sath unko apna bank account operate karne mein bhi dushwari hogi. Zyadatar log sochte hain ke Pakistan mein to filers hain hi nahi, aur ineligible persons ko jab yeh sab allow nahi hoga to phir banking deposits bhi kam ho jayenge, real estate transactions bhi kam ho jayengi, auto sector pe bhi jo hai pressure aa jayega. But aisa bilkul nahi hai. Pichle do teen saalon mein Pakistan mein jo filers hain woh 75 lakh tak pahunch gaye hain. Aap kisi bhi badi auto company se baat karein to kehte hain hamare zyadatar gaadiyan jo purchase karte hain woh filers hi hote hain. To kuch log zaroor hain jinko dushwari hogi yeh sab economic transactions karne mein, and rightly so. I think it's time ke ab Pakistan jo hai woh documented economy ki taraf jaye aur jo log apni wealth sahi tareeqe se show nahi karte unko punish kare.
