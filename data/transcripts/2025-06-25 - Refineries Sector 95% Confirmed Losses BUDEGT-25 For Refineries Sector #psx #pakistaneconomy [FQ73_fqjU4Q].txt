[Published: 2025-06-25]

Woh kehte hain na, ek haath se do, aur doosre haath se waapis le lo. Yehi hua refinery sector ke saath Budget 2025 mein. Budget se pehle to SIFC ke support ke saath aur IFEM ka problem bhi solve ho gaya, lekin budget mein na hi sales tax ka problem solve hua, aur na hi refinery policy se related jo road blocks thi, woh solve huin. Aur sone pe suhaga, jo bada duzz mila refinery sector ko, woh ye hai ke agle kuch dinon mein furnace oil pe pachattar hazaar rupay per ton se bhi zyada jo hai, woh levy apply hone ja rahi hai. Pichle kuch mahino mein jab se jo hai captive gas jo hai mehangi hui hai, zyada tar industries jo hain, woh gas ke bajaye furnace oil se bijli bana rahi thin. Aur ab kyunki ye levy apply ho gayi hai, to is se bijli ka jo unit hai, woh tees rupay se uth kar pentalis rupay pe chala jayega, aur ab koi bhi furnace oil istemaal nahi karega. Refineries agar furnace oil locally bech dein, to unka bohat kam nuksaan hota hai, aur agar ab unko export karna parega, to massive losses honge. Is development se agle saal mein refinery sector ke losses jo hain, woh 95% confirm ho gaye hain.
