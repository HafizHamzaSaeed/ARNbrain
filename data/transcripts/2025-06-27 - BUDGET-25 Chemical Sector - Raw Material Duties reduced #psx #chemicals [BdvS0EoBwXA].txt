[Published: 2025-06-27]

Chemical sector ke liye budget 2025 khatta meetha raha. Ek taraf se to faida mila ke raw material ki duties jo thin wo kam ho gayin, on average 3 se 5 percent. Lekin doosri taraf, wo jo product banate the, uski duty bhi kam ho gayi. So net net, chemical sector ke liye budget negative tha. Lekin chemical sector ki khoobsurti ye hai ke kuch companies hain jo ke aise chemicals banati hain ke unko transport karna bada mushkil hota hai. To agar us pe import duties kam ho bhi jati hain, phir bhi wo import nahi honge. Jaise ke gases aur caustic soda. Lekin jo doosre chemicals hain, jaise ke PVC aur PTA, jin ko Engro Polymer aur Lotte Chemicals banate hain, un ke liye ye budget jo hai wo negative hai. Is ke ilawa, chemical sector jo hai wo bahut zyada electricity use karta hai, to aap ne har saal jo bijli ki qeematein revise hoti hain, us pe kadi nazar rakhni hai, jiski announcement agle 15 dino mein aayegi.
