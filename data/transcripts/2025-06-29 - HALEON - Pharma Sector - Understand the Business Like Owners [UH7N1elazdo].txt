Start karte hain ek baray hi important investing concept se. Aur is se aapko samajh aayega ke business mein kabhi kabhi choti choti developments bhi ho rahi hoti hain, but unka impact itna bara hota hai ke company ke jo profits hain wo do se teen guna barh jaate hain. For example, let's say ek company hai, wo 100 rupay ki sale karti hai aur uski jo cost of production hai wo takreeban 95 rupay hai. To inka profit jo hai wo 5 rupay hota hai. But do choti choti developments hoti hain jis se jo product price hai wo 105 rupay pe chali jaati hai aur jo cost of production hai wo 90 rupay pe aa jaati hai. So is company ka jo new profit hai wo 15 rupay ho jayega, pichle profit se teen guna zyada. Aur aapko to pata hai company ka jo share price hoti hai wo direct link hoti hai profit se, to most probably is company ka share bhi teen guna barh jayega sirf is choti choti developments ki wajah se.

2023 mein Panadol ki ek tablet 3 rupay 16 paise ki hoti thi, aur aaj ye takreeban saaray teen rupay ki hai. Aur isi time mein isko banane ka jo sabse important ingredient paracetamol hota hai, uski bhi jo cost hai wo kam ho gayi hai. In do developments ka profit pe impact itna huge tha ke 2024 mein 2023 ke muqable mein Haleon ke profits jo hain, almost paanch guna barh gaye. Aur isi wajah se Haleon ka jo share hai wo pichle do saalon mein 5x barh gaya hai. So is concept ko aapne dhyan se samajhna hai. Future mein agar let's say Panadol ki price 20-30 paise barh jaati hai, aur jo paracetamol hai wo thora sa aur sasta ho jata hai kyunki oil price neeche aa rahi hai, to company ke jo profits hain is impact ki wajah se 50-60 percent bhi barh sakte hain, and vice versa.

Yehi faida hai investing mein agar aap "understand the business like owners" ka concept jo hai wo follow karte hain. So pichle do saalon mein oil jo hai wo kaafi sasta ho gaya, uski wajah se pharmaceutical raw materials saste ho gaye. Aur isi time mein medicine ki prices jo hain, non-essentials ki, wo de-regulate ho gayin, to medicines ki prices bhi 10-15 percent se barh gayin. Aur inhi do saalon mein Pakistan ke rupay ki de-valuation bhi nahi hui, to ye jo teen developments huin in teeno ne aisa impact kiya ke jo pharma sector ke profits thay wo 300 se 500 percent barh gaye.

Agle ek saal mein complete hone wala ye project Haleon ko is qaabil bana dega ke wo duniya bhar mein low cost Panadol jo hai wo Pakistan ke shehar Jamshoro mein banegi. Currently, Haleon Panadol jo hai khud nahi banata, balkeh toll manufacturing use kar ke of GSK aur Pharma Tek isko bahar se banwata hai. To yaqeenan is in-sourcing project se cost saving bhi hogi. Aur ye jo technology lagne ja rahi hai, ye purani technology se aur zyada efficient hai, yani ke do type of cost saving hongi. Ek aapka toll bachega aur ek aapki technology zyada efficient hai to wahan se bhi cost saving hogi. Hamare andaze ke mutabiq is project se takreeban 1.5 se 2 billion har saal Haleon ko cost saving hogi.

Aur saath saath is project se Panadol aur CAC ki production capacity jo hai wo bhi barh jayegi. So ye project jo hai takreeban Rs. 4 billion mein lag raha hai aur after tax basis pe Haleon ko takreeban 1 billion rupees ki saving kar ke de raha hai. To is project ka ROIC jo hai wo takreeban 25% ban jata hai. Aur isme sirf cost saving hai. Future mein jab Panadol ki demand increase hogi, CAC ki demand increase hogi ya phir company export karegi, to wahan se jo earnings aayengi wo isme included nahi hain.

Ye to baat ho gayi is project ke ROIC ki. Generally bhi pharma sector mein, kyunki Pakistan mein APIs import hote hain aur usko finish medicine mein convert kiya jata hai, to ye jo plants hote hain ye itne zyada costly nahi hote. Aur overall bhi is sector ka jo ROIC hai wo kaafi acha hai. Aur MNCs ka to isme added advantage hai, kyunki MNCs ke jo principals hote hain wo unko raw material jo hai wo do se teen mahine ke credit pe provide kar dete hain. So multinational pharmaceutical company jo hai, uske paas ye advantage hota hai ke agar usne kisi product ki sale barhani hai ya koi naya product lekar aana hai market mein, to usme usko working capital ki jo investment hai wo nahi karni parti.

Business ki duniya mein cash cycle ki importance bahut zyada hai. Aapne khud dekha hoga ke duniya ki jitni bhi top brands hain jaise ke Apple, Amazon, wo apne customer se cash pehle le leti hain aur jo product hai wo baad mein deliver karti hain aur jo supplier unko sell kar raha hota hai usko bhi payment do se teen kabhi kabhi to cheh mahine baad karti hain.

Multinational pharmaceutical companies ke paas ek aur mazeed advantage hai, aur wo ye hai ke ab kyunki Pakistan mein non-essential medicines ki prices jo hain wo de-regulate ho gayi hain, to aap apna koi bhi global pharmaceutical product jo hai wo Pakistan mein launch kar sakte hain apni marzi ki price pe. So agle kuch saalon mein ye multinational brands jo hain, ye kaafi zyada apne products jo hain wo Pakistan mein lekar aayenge. Jaise ke Centrum already aa rahi hai, phir Panadol ke new versions aa rahe hain, Glaxo bhi bahut zyada medicines jo hain wo apne international portfolio se yahan lekar aa raha hai.

Haleon ke case mein company ke plan hain Panadol ke new versions aur Centrum ke ilawa Robitussin, jo ke ek cough medicine hai usko bhi launch karne ka. Aur agar aap inka global portfolio dekhhein ge, to ek T-Day naam ki medicine bhi inki kaafi zyada mashhoor hai. To ho sakta hai aane wale saalon mein ye medicine bhi jo hai Pakistan mein wo le aayein.

Aaiye ab mein aapko ek bari important cheez batata hoon about new versions of Panadol. Pakistan mein jo essential aur non-essential ki categorization hoti hai, aur ye World Bank ka tareeqa hai categorize karne ka jisko Pakistan follow karta hai, uske rules ye hain ke agar aapki medicine mein koi ek bhi agar API hoga jo non-essential hai, to wo poori medicine jo hai wo non-essential category mein aa jaati hai. Jaise ke paracetamol jo API hai wo essential hai, lekin Panadol Extra jo hai kyunki usme thori si caffeine maujood hoti hai aur caffeine non-essential hai, to Panadol Extra jo hai wo non-essential category mein aa jaati hai. Aur agar aapka product non-essential category mein aa gaya, to phir aap apni marzi ki jo price uski set kar sakte hain market conditions ko dekh ke. So isme samajhne wali baat ye hai ke per tablet profit jo hai of Panadol's new versions is way higher than normal Panadol.

Ye jo saaray teen rupay ki Panadol aap khareedte hain, ye company takreeban dhai se paune teen rupay mein distributor ko bechti hai, aur iski cost of production takreeban dedh se paune do rupay ke beech mein hai. Yani ke company har tablet pe takreeban ek rupaya profit karti hai. Aur ab aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain Panadol Ultra, Panadol Night, in sab ki prices jo hain wo takreeban average 10 rupay per tablet hain. Theek hai maan lete hain ke inko produce karne mein shayad thora sa extra kharcha bhi hota ho, let's say 3 rupay cost karti hai. Phir bhi aap dekhein per tablet jo profit hai wo takreeban chaar se paanch rupay hai. So aapko ye bhi samajhna hoga ke ye jo new versions hain, bhale ye overall sale mein paanch se das percent contribute karein ge aaj se do-teen saal baad, lekin kyunki inka per tablet profit bahut zyada hai compared to normal Panadol, to is se bhi company ki overall profits jo hain, substantially increase ho sakte hain.

So pharma sector ke liye aapne product prices ko bhi monitor karna hai, unki jo cost of production hai unke raw materials, unpe duties, unki cost usko bhi monitor karna hai, aur saath saath companies jo new products lekar aa rahi hain, unko bhi dekhte rehna hai.
