[Published: 2025-07-01]

Fertilizer sector ke liye budget 2025 jo hai wo thoda sa positive tha lekin asal budget abhi aana baqi hai. Even though Pakistan ne IMF ke sath commitment ki hui thi ke hum fertilizers pe aur pesticides pe 5% FED jo hai wo mazeed barhaen ge, lekin Prime Minister ne IMF ko convince kar liya ke is saal hum ye nahi kar sakte. Aur dealers se khabrein aa rahi hain ke jo urea ki sales hain is mahine mein wo bhi kaafi achhi ja rahi hain aur ab kyunke FED apply nahi hui to bag bhi mazeed mehanga nahi hoga yani ke agriculture sector mein koi aisa massive change nahi aaya. To ye to side ho gayi taxes ki, dusri important cheez hoti hai cost of production aur wahan aayega asal budget. Agle 15 se 20 dino mein Pakistan mein gas prices jo hain wo revise hongi aur isme phir aapne dekhna hoga ke kis player ki gas price ko increase kiya jata hai, kisko decrease kiya jata hai aur kisko maintain kiya jata hai. To jo asal budget hai fertilizer sector ka wo agle 15 dino mein aayega with the announcement of gas prices. Aur agar isme status quo rehta hai to yaad rakhiyega sabse achhi company wo hai, uski sale sabse zyada hogi jiska bag sabse sasta hai kyunke agar urea ki demand kam hoti hai, agriculture struggle kar raha hai to log prefer karenge ke main us se khareed leta hoon jiska bag 200-300 rupay sasta hai.
