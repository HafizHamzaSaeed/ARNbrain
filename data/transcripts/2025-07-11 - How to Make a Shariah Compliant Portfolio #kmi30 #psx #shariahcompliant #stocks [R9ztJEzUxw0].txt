Speaker: Stock market mein Shariah compliant investing karna chah raha hoon lekin kuch samajh nahi aa raha kaise start karoon.

Abdul Rehman Najam (ARN): Aaiye aapko ek minute mein sab kuch samjha dete hain. Sabse pehle aapne jana hai PSX ki website pe, top five brokers ki list dekhni hai aur kisi ek mein jidhar se aapko sabse achi service mil rahi ho, foran asaan account khulwa lena hai. Ab aapka account khul gaya hai. Step two ye hai ke aapne again PSX ki website pe jana hai, KMI 30 ki list nikaalni hai, ismein top Shariah compliant companies saari aa jati hain aur aapne apne portfolio ko is list ke hisab se divide kar dena hai. Let's say aap ek lakh ka portfolio bana rahe hain aur Meezan Bank KMI 30 mein nine percent hai, to aapne no hazar ke Meezan Bank le lene hain. Isi tareeqe se Engro Holdings aapne das hazar ke le lene hain. Aise karte karte jab aap top fifteen companies ke shares buy kar lenge, to aapke ninety thousand already invest ho gaye hain aur ab jo das hazar bach gaye hain woh aap top ten companies mein ek ek hazar aur invest kar dein. Aur ab har mahine aapke paas jab panch hazar das hazar aayenge, uske sath bhi aapne yehi karna hai. Let's say aap saari companies ek mahine mein nahi le pa rahe, to aap pehle mahine mein top three companies le lein, agle mahine mein next three companies le lein, aur kuch mahino baad jo hai aapka pura cycle complete ho jayega. Ye karne ke baad aapne sirf relax karna hai, koi trading nahi karni aur ab aap bhi Pakistan ke top business owners ki tarah earn karna shuru kar denge. Jo KMI 30 ka index jitna return lekar aayega, aapko bhi utna hi profit hoga.
