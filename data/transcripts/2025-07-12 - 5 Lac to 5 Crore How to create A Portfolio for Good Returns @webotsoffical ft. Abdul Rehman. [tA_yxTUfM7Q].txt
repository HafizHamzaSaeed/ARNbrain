Interviewer: Numan, ye batayein ke ek bande ke paas paanch lakh rupya hai. Wo stock exchange mein invest karna chahta hai. Wo apna portfolio kis tarah se build kare, kin companies mein wo paise dale?

ARN: Dekhein ek to humne jaise aapko tarika bataya ke aap wo average ke sath chalenge na usse bada accha result aa jata hai. Lekin logon ka bhi bada shauk hota hai ke main bhi acchi company dhoondo, koi next Meezan Bank jo hai wo main bhi usme invest karoon. To let's say aapne faisla kiya hai ke main na paanch companies maine ek ek lakh sabme dalna hai aur phir dekhte hain kaun si acchi perform karti hai main usko follow bhi karta rahoonga. Ab isme thoda sa aapko jo hai na aise har cheh mahine baad thoda sa time lagana padega. To iska best tarika hai aap na jo top 15 se 20 companies hain na jinhone pichle 20 saalon mein bada accha return diya hai. Un sabko jaake study karein. Aur dekhein ke jis company mein aapko lag raha hai na ke aage bhi same potential hai yani ke wo jo uski jo do teen cheezein hain na jo usko multibagger banati hain wo change nahi hui. Usme aap invest kar sakein. Aur ye jo 15-20 companies aap khud research karenge bahut mushkil hai, aap hamari jo hai YouTube channel pe jayein 'Abdul Rehman Najam' se channel search karein. Almost top 15 companies jo hain jisme Meezan Bank bhi aa jata hai, Millat Tractors bhi aa jata hai, Engro Fertilizer aa jata hai, Fauji Fertilizer bhi aa jata hai. Almost 20-25 minute ki videos bani hui hain, usme jo teen chaar cheezein hain na jinhone usko multibagger banaya hai. Wo jo hai humne aapko explain kiya hua hai. Phir usme jis se bhi aap zyada connect karein, aapko lagta hai ke meri ye top five sabse acchi hain, main uska portfolio bana leta hoon, usme se choose karein.
