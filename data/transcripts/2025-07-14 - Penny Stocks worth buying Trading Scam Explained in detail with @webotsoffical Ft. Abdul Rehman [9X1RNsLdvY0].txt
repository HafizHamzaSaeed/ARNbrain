ARN: Penny stocks pe to nahi jana chahiye, right?

Guest: Bilkul bhi nahi jana chahiye, penny stocks pe na aapki cost bhi zyada hoti hai, commission bhi zyada hota hai. Aur ye saari jo movies hain, even Hollywood, Bollywood, ye sab trading dikhati hain. Logon ko na pichle das saalon mein content hi aisa dikhaya hai ke na humne trading karni hai. Trading kar ke jo hai na main ek dam se apni zindagi badal sakta hoon. Lekin zindagi aap hamesha badlenge apni job se. Aur jo job se saving hogi na us se aap wealth creation karenge stock market mein. Yaani ke aap koshish karein aap apne kaam mein achhe hon, promotion lein aur jaise jaise zyada zyada saving karenge aap, aap jo hai das saal, pandra saal mein hi itni investment ikatthi kar lenge ke phir aapko job karne ki zaroorat nahi.
