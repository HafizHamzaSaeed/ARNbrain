Stocks mein invest karna ek zero sum game hai. Jab tak koi bechega nahi, loss nahi karega, to kisi aur ka faida nahi ho sakta. Bilkul ghalat. Isi tarah ki ahmakana baatein jo hain, aam logon ka nuksan kara deti hain. Businesses ki growth jo hai aap sab ke samne hai. Meezan Bank ki aaj se das saal pehle paanch sau pachas branches hoti theen, aur aaj hazaar branches se bhi zyada hain. To zahir hai phir profit to barhein ge hi na, to phir ye zero sum game kaise hui? Time ke sath sath, businesses apni wealth ko compound karte hain. Aise thodi Meezan Bank ne pichle das saalon mein Apple se zyada returns diye hain in dollar terms. Zero sum game hoti hai trading, kyunke short term mein wealth creation nahi ho sakti. Isi liye trading nahi karni, investing karni hai, aur karni kaise hai, asan alfaaz mein maine samjhaya hua hai is video mein.
