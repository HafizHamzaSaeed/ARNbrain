Stock market to ameeron ka game hai. Jab tak zyada paise nahi honge to paise se paisa kaisa banega? Ab mere paas to sirf pachas hazar rupay paray hain, mere liye to yeh sab hai hi nahi.

Bilkul ghalat. Duniya bhar mein stock exchange ek aisa platform hai jo ke har bande ke liye hota hai. Har koi isme invest kar ke wealth creation kar sakta hai aur yeh actually status quo ko torta hai. Stock market aapko platform provide karta hai kisi aise bande ko jisne let's say abhi abhi apni pehli job lagi hai, paanch se das hazar rupay har mahine save kar sakta hai, to usko opportunity hoti hai ke woh Pakistan ke top business owners ke saath equal partners ban sakta hai. Ji haan, aap bhi stock market mein invest kar ke Pakistan ke top business owners ki tarah earn kar sakte hain. Aur duniya bhar mein bhi jitne bhi famous investors hain jinhone stock market ke through jo hai apni wealth create ki hai, un sabki bahut hi humble beginnings hain. Aap India ke Rakesh Jhunjhunwala dekh lain, aap America ke Warren Buffett ko dekh lain, sabki bahut humble beginnings hain aur unhone apni skills se yeh wealth creation ki hai.
