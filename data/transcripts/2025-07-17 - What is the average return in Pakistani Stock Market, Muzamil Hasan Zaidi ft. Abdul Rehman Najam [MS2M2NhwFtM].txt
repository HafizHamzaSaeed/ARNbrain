ARN: Average return kya milta hai Pakistan mein?

Guest: 18 to 20%. Yeh ek tarah se aap keh sakte hain, ek large average company ka hi return hai aur yeh bara jo hai na Pakistan ke case mein fit bhi baithta hai. Globally bhi aap dekhenge na to real returns jo hain stock market ke koi 5 to 7% hote hain per year aur Pakistan mein koi takriban 10, 11% inflation hoti hai to us cheez ko mila lein to yeh takriban 18 se 19% ban jate hain. Kuch aisi companies hoti hain ke agar let's say cost 5% barhi hai na, woh 7% price barha deti hain yani ke unka product aisa hota hai ke woh agar logon ko price barha ke bhi bechein ge, log phir bhi khareedein ge, iski itni value hai. Iski example aap is tarah se dekh sakte hain let's say pharmaceutical companies hain. Pakistan mein agar devaluation hoti hai, us time pe thode se returns kam ho jate hain lekin jab bhi cheezein normalize hoti hain, har investor phir sochta hai ke yeh jo plant laga hua hai, jo ke dollars mein import hota hai, iski aaj ki value kya hai? To devaluation bhi over the long term, jaise cheezein normalize hoti hain, woh is return ko upar le jati hai aur 50% of the companies mein achhi pricing power hoti hai, is wajah se yeh log itne achhe returns kama sakte hain.
