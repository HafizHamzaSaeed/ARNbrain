Takreeban 6 lakh bijli ke unit lagte hain to mine one Bitcoin. To agar aapka best case bhi aaj ye hai ke aap 9 cents ki bijli de sakein. Jab aapne ye capacity payment wala system banaya hua hai na, to aapne fixed payment sabko deni hai. To jab aapne fixed payment sabko deni hai, sabse pehle aap us se bijli banayenge na, jiski variable cost sabse zyada hai, kyunki ek cheez to aapki fixed ho gayi. To sabse pehle aap kis se bijli banate hain? Paani se. Us ki variable cost zero hai. 30% aapne usi se bana li. Phir aap aate hain solar, wind aur jo local gas hai jo ke sasti hai, us se aap takreeban 5-7 rupay average cost pe bana lete hain. Phir aapki baari aati hai local coal ki, wo bhi 8-9 rupay variable pe bana leta hai. To is wajah se is basket ki jo price hai na, wo 5 hai. Agar aap isko increase karenge, aapne let's say jo next marginal unit banana hai, wo aaj bhi jo stand by pe plant band hain, wo hain imported coal. 15 rupay ki takreeban unki cost hai. Thoda sa aap usme sara distribution ka kharcha dalenge, to aap 20 rupay se sasti bijli agar aap denge to bakiyon ki cost badh jayegi. To is wajah se, let's say, 15 rupay ki aap Bitcoin mining ko bijli de rahe hain, aap wahi jo hai textile exporters ko de dein, EV mein de dein. To aapka jo best case bhi hai na, jo power minister hain wo jab EVs ki baat karte hain, saari cheezon ki baat karte hain, to kehte hain hum 9 cents pe bijli lekar aayenge. To agar aap Bitcoin mining liye 9 cents ki bijli denge, ya chalein aapne 8 ki bhi de di, tab bhi wo viable nahi hai.
