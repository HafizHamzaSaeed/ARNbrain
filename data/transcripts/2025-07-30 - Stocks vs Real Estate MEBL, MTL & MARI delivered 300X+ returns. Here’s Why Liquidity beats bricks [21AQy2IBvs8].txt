Ghor se suno zara. Aaj mein tumhe bataunga ke real estate mein kitna paisa hai. DHA Phase 6 Lahore, 10 lakh ki file 2001 mein agar khareedi hoti, aaj 5 crore ki hai. 50X Profit. Bahria Town Lahore, 1992 mein 2 lakh rupay ki file mil rahi thi, aaj sava crore rupay ki hai. 60X Profit. Purani baatein chhoro, aaj ki baat batata hoon. DHA Islamabad, 2008 mein 7 lakh ki file thi, aaj 80 lakh ki hai. 11X Profit. Ab zara lambi saans le lo, kyunke ab mein batane laga hoon ke stocks mein kitna profit hota hai. Meezan Bank, same time 2002 mein 10 lakh rupay lagaye hote, to aaj 30 crore rupay ho jate. 300X Profit. Isi tareeqay se Millat Tractor 10 lakh rupay lagaye hote, aaj 40 crore ho jate. 400X Profit. Aur ab zara dil thaam lo, kyunke Mari Petroleum ne profit diya hai 800X. 10 lakh rupay aaj 80 crore ho jate. Average KSE 100 Index hi 2002 se aaj tak jo hai, 100X profit de chuka hai apne investors ko. Aur real estate ki top examples jo hain, wo 100X tak mushkil se pohanch rahi hain. To ab ya to comments mein mujhe bata do koi aisi example jisne 500X profit diya ho real estate mein, ya phir chup chap maan lo ke stocks mein real estate se zyada profit hota hai.
