**Speaker 2:** Wo investor jo abhi dekh rahe hain ke yaar stock market upar ja rahi hai, chakkay lag rahe hain, to ye point hai main iske andar main bhi ghus jaon?

**ARN:** Stock market na ek spring ki tarah behave karti hai. To kehte hain ye spring jo hai na kabhi kabhi jab log depressed honge, mulk ke halat acche nahi honge, to full jo hai na isko squeeze kar diya jayega. Phir ye apni normal position pe wapis aayega, isne wapis aana hai kyunki jo business hai na wo average har saal apna profit badhayega hi badhayega. Thoda sa understand karein aapne jis cheez mein invest kiya hai na wo apne time ke sath value badha raha hai ke nahi badha raha. Agar to wo badha raha hai, to phir aapko usko jaldi nahi bechna chahiye. Wo bahut zyada compound hota hai. Jaise abhi bhi log puchte hain ke agle do saal mein aapko kya lag raha hai ke US behtar hai ya phir Pakistan behtar hai. To jab tak Pakistan ke halat acche hain, idhar ke jo returns hote hain, 30 se 35% average in bull market, usko koi bhi beat nahi kar sakta.
