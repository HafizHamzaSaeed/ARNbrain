Aap sab ki bohot zyada requests aane ke baad jo hai humne ek bohot hi special video banayi hai on National Foods. Aur yeh hamare YouTube channel pe kal jo hai woh live ho jayegi. Yeh ek bohot hi special video hai, kyunki is mein humne practical application show ki hai rich versus poor mindset. Ab National Foods ke jo products hain, ketchup, masala, hum sab ne use kiye hain, lekin 1% bhi log nahi honge jinho ne is ke share mein investment kar ke compounding kar li. Recently company ne apni new Faisalabad factory jo hai, woh bhi start kar di hai, aur yeh special economic zone mein hai, jis mein aap ko 10 saal tak tax bhi pay nahi karna hota. Is ke elawa, kuch hi Pakistani companies hoti hain jo international level pe business karti hain. Is company ne bhi Canada mein cash and carry ka ek bohot hi zabardast business establish kar liya hai. Yeh saari cheezein, aur bhi bohot kuch hum log detail mein cover karenge in a detailed video on National Foods. Aur yeh hamare YouTube channel pe kal live ho jayegi.
