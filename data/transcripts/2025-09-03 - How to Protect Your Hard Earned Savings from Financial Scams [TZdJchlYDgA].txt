Dunya bhar mein wealth creation ke do models hote hain. Ek hota hai scammers ka model, aur ek hota hai entrepreneurs ka model. Scammers ka model ye hota hai ke wo logon ko laalach dikha kar ek farzi company create karte hain. Ab wo real estate se munsalik bhi ho sakti hai, wo koi platform bhi ho sakta hai, but us ka maqsad ye hota hai ke logon ko sabz baagh dikha kar, laalach dikha kar un ka paisa lootna. 30 din mein paisa double, 5% monthly rent, ye sab scammers ke model hain. Scammers ke model mein back end pe to kuch hota nahi hai, koi product ya service to provide karni nahi hai, to in ka poora focus hota hai ke marketing pe spend kiya jaye. Aur isi wajah se ye jo scammers hote hain, ye kisi na kisi platform se jo affiliated hote hain, ye phir in ko buhat zyada commission bhi pay karte hain.

Ek aam business mein agar aap dekhenge, koi agar product ya service hai us ki price agar 100 rupay hai, to jo entrepreneur hota hai us ka profit taqreeban 20 se 25 rupay hota hai. To agar koi affiliate us ke saath kaam kar bhi raha hota hai, to wo us ko maximum 3, 4, 5% commission deta hai. Lekin, jo scammers ka model hai, us mein to poora maqsad hi yehi hai ke logon ko product, service bech kar 10-15% profit nahi kamana, poore paise hi hadap lene hain, to ye apne affiliates ko, ya jo in ke saath associated log hote hain jo in ko promote kar rahe hote hain, un ko 30% tak bhi commission dete hain.

Dunya mein wealth creation ka doosra model hota hai entrepreneurs ka model. Aaiye entrepreneurs ke model ko bhi thoda sa samajhte hain, aur phir jab aap in dono models mein farq karna seekh jayenge, to aap ke mind mein khud hi teen-chaar steps ban ke aa jayenge jo ke aap ko future scams se protect karenge. Entrepreneurs ke wealth creation model ki foundation hoti hai ke wo apne customers ke liye wealth create karte hain. Is model mein let's say, agar un ke customer ne 100 rupay ka faida kiya, to phir wo un customers se 10 se 15 rupay fees bhi charge kar lete hain.

Dunya bhar ke mashhoor entrepreneurs, Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, dunya ke number one investor Warren Buffett, sab is model ko follow karte hain. Jeff Bezos aur Elon Musk ne agar 100 billion dollars ki wealth create ki hai, to unhon ne jo product aur jo company create ki hai, in companies ne aur in ke products ne logon ko dunya bhar mein 1 trillion dollars se bhi zyada ka faida ponchaya hai. Aap khud soch sakte hain, dunya mein jo let's say EVs launch keen Tesla ne, to us se dunya bhar mein kitni zyada fuel saving ho rahi hai. Isi tareeqe se Amazon ki services se dunya bhar mein logon ko kitna faida ho raha hai, aur jo logon ka faida ho raha hai, us ka kuch percentage jo hai wo phir in entrepreneurs ki wealth creation ho jati hai.

Isi tareeqe se jab Warren Buffett ne portfolio management services start keen 1956 mein, to unhon ne logon se kaha ke main aap ko market se jo extra return kama ke doonga, us ki 25% meri fees hogi. To jo aap ko extra profit mil raha hai, us ki fees main charge karoonga. So pehla tareeqa scammers se bachne ka: agar let's say, koi bhi aap ko aa ke YouTube pe ya kisi aur platform pe aap ko kehta hai ke maine thode se time mein hi 10 million dollars ki wealth jo hai na wo accumulate kar li hai, aur aap ko mehengi-mehengi gaadiyan dikha raha hai, foreign travel kar raha hai, foreign properties buy kar raha hai, aur keh raha hai ke maine 10 million dollars ki wealth jo hai thode se time mein accumulate kar li hai, to pehli cheez aap ke mind mein aani chahiye jo ke aap ko farq batayegi ke ye entrepreneur hai ya phir scammer hai, to aap sochein agar ye keh raha hai maine 10 million dollars ki wealth create ki hai, to is ki jo community hai, jo is ke customers hain, unhon ne kam-as-kam 100 million dollars ki wealth yaani ke 30 arab rupay jo hain wo kamaye honge.

To pehla aap ne question ye karna hai ke koi bhi agar aap ko wealth show-off kare, to aap kahein ke aap ki community mein jo 10 times value create hui thi wo kahan hai? Wo to hamein nazar nahi aa rahi. Aur agar aap ko ye wealth creation nazar na aaye, aur Pakistan mein bhi buhat mushkil hota hai. Pakistan ke GDP mein agar koi particular group 100 million dollars ki bhi wealth create kar le to wo aap ko kahin na kahin nazar zaroor aati hai. Agar aap ko ye wealth creation in ki community mein nazar na aaye, to aap ne foran alert ho jana hai, aur aap ne foran sochna hai ke ye jo log hain, ye logon ke paise lootne ke business mein hain. So pehla step, aap ne entrepreneurs aur scammers mein aise differentiate karna hai ke aap ne un ki personal wealth creation nahi dekhni, aap ne un ki community mein dekhna hai ke wealth creation hui hai ke nahi.

Second step to protect yourself from financial scams, aur wo ye hai ke aap ne hamesha SECP registered company se hi deal karna hai. Third, most important point, aur wo ye hai ke aap ne kabhi bhi kisi ke personal account mein apne funds jo ke investment purposes ke liye hote hain wo transfer karne hi nahi hain. Buhat zyada log jo hain wo aaj kal apni services online sell kar rahe hote hain, kuch ki registered companies bhi bhi hoti hain, aur kuch log buhat accha kaam bhi kar rahe hote hain, lekin phir bhi aap ne sirf un ko un ki fees transfer karni hai. Custody of your funds hamesha aap ke apne account mein rehni chahiye.
