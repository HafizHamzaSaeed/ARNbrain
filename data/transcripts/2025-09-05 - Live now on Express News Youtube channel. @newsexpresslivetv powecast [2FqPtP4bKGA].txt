ARN: Aaj ek aam aadmi ne investment karni hai, wo kahan kare?

Guest: Agar thodi si koshish karke paanch hazar rupay bacha lega na aur usko har mahine stock market ke shares kharidta rahega average company ke jo top businesses hote hain, to ye jo paanch-paanch hazar ki har mahine investment ho rahi hai, ye paanch crore tak pahunch jayegi.

ARN: Is is waqt kis sector mein sarmaya kari kare?

Guest: Cement sector jo hai usme aap dekh sakte hain, kaun si company jo hai jiska accha track record hai. Aap fertilizer sector mein bhi invest kar sakte hain.

ARN: Ye batayein ye tip kya hoti hai?

Guest: Tip bhi aap samjhein trading ka extra leverage version.

ARN: Accha investor ko tip ki zarurat nahi hoti?

Guest: Investor apna kaam khud karta hai aur wo long term partnership karta hai. Usko tip ki kya zarurat.

ARN: Aap batayein ye Pakistan ke liye ye jo hai na stock market ke king hain paanch, naam batayein?

Guest: Pehle hote the, to ab king koi bhi nahi hai. Ab king hai investor.

ARN: Investment mein halt ka option nahi hai?

Guest: Investment mein kaise halt ka option hoga? To maine aapko bataya jis din stock market band hui thi, us din bhi aapne investment ki hoti, to aaj bhi aap das guna pe khade hote.
