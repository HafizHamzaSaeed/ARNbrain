ARN community ke young members ke liye special message. Aaj mein aapko teen cheezein bataunga jo ke maine apne pichle 10 se 15 saalon ke experience mein seekhi aur aap agar inko adopt kar lenge to aapka financial future jo hai woh secure ho jayega. 

First: lifestyle inflation. Abhi abhi aap ki job lagi hogi ya abhi aap apni university khatam kar rahe hain aur bahut jaldi aap jo hai earn karna start karenge to aapne delayed gratification jo hai woh practice karni hai. Agar aapki income ek lakh rupaye hai to aapne bas sochna hai ke meri income 80,000 hai aur uske hisab se hi apne lifestyle ko adjust karna hai. 

Doosri important cheez: aapne koi bhi high interest rate debt nahi leni. Bahut zyada log young hote hain aur is debt trap mein phans jaate hain aur 10 15 saal baad unko samajh aati hai jab woh constantly interest jo hai woh pay karte rehte hain but us debt se bahar nahi nikal paate. So Pakistan mein bahut zyada loan sharks jo hain woh ghoom rahi hoti hain, koi bhi aapko koi bhi karza offer kare aapne foran jo hai usko no bol dena hai. 

Aur thirdly, aapne 80/20 wealth rule follow karna hai aur choti choti amounts jama karke over some period of time aap kaise jo hai ek passive income create kar sakte hain woh humne is video mein explain kiya hua hai. So agar aapko apni job pe ek lakh ka bonus mile, aapne usse jaake naya phone nahi khareedna. Aapne jo hai us paison ko invest karna hai aur jab aapka portfolio let's say 20 lakh rupaye tak ho jayega aur aapko har saal jo hai 1.5 lakh 2 lakh ka dividend aa raha hoga, fir aap un paison se jo hai woh phone khareed sakte hain. So pehle income ko save karna hai, usko compound karna hai aur fir jab usse dividends aur returns aayenge, usko fir apni luxuries pe spend karna hai.
