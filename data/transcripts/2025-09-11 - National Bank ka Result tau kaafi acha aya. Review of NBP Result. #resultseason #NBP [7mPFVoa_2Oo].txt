National Bank ka result to kafi acha tha. Deposit bhi kafi zyada increase hue hain. Profit bhi increase ho rahe hain. Lekin dividend nahi de rahe, iski kya wajah hai? Dividend na dene ki wajah ye hai ke jo do banks hain, Bank of Punjab aur National Bank, inke Articles of Association mein Government of Pakistan inko allow hi nahi karti ke aap saal ke beech mein dividend pay kar sakein. Lekin Bank of Punjab ne 2024 ke aakhir mein in rules ko amend karwa liya tha. Isi wajah se unhone half year mein dividend announce kar diya aur National Bank jo hai, wo saal ke aakhir mein dividend announce karega. Hamare estimates ke hisab se National Bank ke profits per share jo hain, takreeban 38 se 40 rupees tak chale jayenge 2025 ke liye aur kafi zyada chances hain ke ye 22 se 25 rupees dividend announce kar dein, kyunki last year unhone 18 rupees ka dividend to already announce kar diya tha. National Bank mein deposit growth bhi kafi achi hui hai, current account ki percentage bhi barh gayi hai aur aapko pata hoga ke inhone 2000 arab rupees Government of Pakistan ko 14% rate pe lock kiye hue hain for the next 1.5 years jiski wajah se inke profits ache aa rahe hain.
