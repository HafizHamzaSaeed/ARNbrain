[Published: 2025-09-16]

2024 ka best performing sector, pharma sector aisa lagta hai 2025 mein bujh bujh sa gaya hai. Pehle results aate thay, upper lock pe upper lock lagte thay. Profits bhi jo hai, kaafi zyada increase ho jaate thay, kyunki Government of Pakistan ne medicines ki price ko de-regulate kar diya tha 2024 mein. But, is baar aap dekhein, company ke results aa rahe hain. Highnoon ne bhi result announce kar diya hai, AGP ne bhi announce kar diya hai, Glaxo ne bhi announce kar diya hai. Aur jo sales hain, woh koi khaas increase ho nahi rahi. Aur bohot saari companies mein to sales decline bhi hui hain. Aur iske sath sath khabrein bhi aa rahi hain ke government soch rahi hai ke medicine ki jo prices hain, unko kisi tareeqe se control kiya jaye. Is sab declining trend mein ek do companies hain jinhone bohot achhe results announce kiye hain, jismein se ek hai Haleon, jiski sale bhi bohot zyada increase hui hai aur profit bhi pichle quarter ke muqable mein kaafi zyada increase hue hain. Aur iska reason hai inka star product CAC, jiski pichle kuch mahino pehle price bhi increase hui thi aur iska season bhi chal raha hai jis mein iski sale bhi zyada hoti hai. Aur is medicine ka aapko pata hoga koi khaas competitor bhi nahi hai. So, one-time impact jo pharma sector ki saari companies pe aana tha, I think woh to aa gaya hai. Ab future mein, jis company ke paas pricing power aur jiski medicine achhi hogi, wahi jo hai future growth lekar aayega. Pharma sector ke results aapko kaise lage, aapne hamein comments mein batana hai. Apni favourite company kaun si hai, yeh bhi batana hai. Aur aapko kya lagta hai ke FY26 pharma sector ke liye kaisa hoga, yeh aap humse comments mein share karein.
