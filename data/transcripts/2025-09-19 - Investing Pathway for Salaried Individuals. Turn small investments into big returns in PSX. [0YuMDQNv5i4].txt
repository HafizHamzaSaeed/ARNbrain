ARN: Bilkul main aap ko aik pathway bhi batata hoon, jo har banda jo hai woh follow kar sakta hai. ARN: Ji, bilkul. To asaan account mein aap log agar das lakh tak investment karte hain na, to aap se kuch nahi mangein ge. Aap bas jo hai investment kar sakte hain, koi source of income nahi chahiye. To hum ne aik cheez start ki, kyunke jo sara seekha hua tha na ke kis tareeqay se aik aam aadmi bhi is se faida kar sakta hai, woh ye hota hai ke aap jitni bhi salary earn karte hain na, us ka thora sa hissa, do hazar rupay, paanch hazar rupay har mahine jo hai na stock market mein lagate hain. Aur ab aap jab agle saalon mein jaise thori thori investment karte rahein ge, apni saving karte rahein ge, woh itne ache tareeqay se compound ho jaye gi ke aap ki jo next generation ho gi na, us ko kaam karne ki, same kaam karne ki zaroorat nahi ho gi.
