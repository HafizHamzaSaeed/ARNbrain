Warren Buffett ke agar three steps mein conclude karun na, jo maine unke saari principles padhe hain. First is woh kehte hain, woh kaam karein jismein aapko waqai mein maza aa raha hai, kyun ke usme na aapke expert banne ke chances bohat zyada hote hain. He says, "make your passion your work." Aur agar isko financial freedom se link karein, to aapki jab jaldi promotions hoti hain, aap jo earn kar rahe hote hain, aapki earning power badh jaati hai. Jitni zyada earning power hogi, utna zyada aap save karenge. Theek hai? Phir doosra unka important principle hai delayed gratification. Usmein woh phir kehte hain ke agar aapko ek dafa compounding samajh aa jaye, to phir aap log jo hai, har mahine, let's say agar hum Dirham mein baat karein, ek hazar Dirham save karne lag jayenge. Aap yeh nahi sochenge ke abhi main save karunga, mujhe to bohat saari cheezein give up karni pad rahi hain, lekin jab aapko ek poora pathway nazar aa jata hai compounding ka, to aap delayed gratification karte hain, kuch savings jo hain unko accumulate karte hain aur apne paison ko lagate hain ke woh ab hamare liye paisa banayein. Second principle se samajh aaya ke delayed gratification kitni zaroori hai. Agar aap aaj sau dollar earn kar rahe hain, to usme se 20-25 aapne save karna hi karna hai. First earning potential badhane ka tareeqa bhi bata diya, saving karne ka bhi tareeqa bata diya. 

Phir third unka principle hai ke asset class jo best hai na poori duniya mein, woh hai stocks, kyun ke stocks ke peeche yeh sab nahi hai ke chart upar ja raha hai, chart neeche ja raha hai, uske peeche actual businesses hain. Actual businesses hamesha compound honge, growth hogi. Log unse poochte hain na aap gold kyun nahi khareedte? Kehte hain yaar duniya mein jitna bhi gold hai na woh ek bade room mein aa jayega, aur main 10 saal baad us room mein aaunga na tab bhi woh waisa hi hoga. Lekin agar main America ke top 10 businesses le loon aur main 10 saal baad aaun, to woh size mein taqreeban 50% ya 100% tak badh gaye honge, to woh compound karenge. So, third principle mein woh batate hain ke ab jo aapne apne savings ko kaam pe lagana hai, woh kahan pe lagana hai, aur usko karne ke poore tareeqe batayein.
