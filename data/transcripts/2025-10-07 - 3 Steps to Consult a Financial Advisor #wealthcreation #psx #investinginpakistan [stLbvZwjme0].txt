ARN: Acha financial advisor hire karne ke rules. To pehla hota hai ke aap unki baat aapko samajh aati hai ke nahi. Yani ke unki strategy jo hai wo safe hai ke nahi. Wo businesses ko kitna detail mein understand karte hain. Teen char saal mein to koi leverage kar ke bhi acha return kama sakta hai, lekin end mein usne jo hai wo kharab ho jaega. Phir track record bhi dekhein thoda. Agar koi bahut naya hai to ek do saal ka dekh lein. To jo first wala step hai us pe thoda zyada focus karein ke inki strategy kya hai. Phir uske baad uski fees ko bhi dekhein. Fees bhi kam honi chahiye, after fees returns bhi ache hone chahiye. Lekin jab aapki 50 lakh, 75 lakh ki savings ho jayein, to phir aapko ek acha financial advisor jo hai na wo hire karna chahiye, kyunki aage phir aapki amount jo hai crores mein bhi jaegi aur wo aapko affect karegi. So ek person hona chahiye jo ke aapko sara guide bhi kare, conviction bhi de, information bhi de taake aap ghalat decision na lein.

Speaker 2: Lekin aap kahin ke traders ke paas chale gaye to phir to bahut masla ho jaega. Traders, lekin aapne khud seekh liya hoga itna ke aap trading us tarah se nahi karenge.
