Assalam-o-Alaikum everyone, and the wait is finally over, aur Pakistan ki first ever TEDx Talk on how to achieve financial freedom is now live. Hazaron log is important topic pe ye talk attend karna chahte the, aur organizers ko aapne bahut zyada calls bhi ki, but because of the limited tickets and seating capacity, they were unable to accommodate you. But ab ye jo video hai, ye YouTube pe upload ho gayi hai aur is 18-minute talk mein humne bahut important cheezein discuss ki. Kaise Pakistan mein aap reh kar jo hai, financial freedom achieve kar sakte hain. Financial freedom hota kya hai? Compounding ko explain kiya, aur kaise explain kiya ke financial freedom is for everyone. Yaani ke aaj aap sab jo hain, jo apni salary earn karte hain, aap kaise jo hai, compounding kar ke aur apni investments kar ke ek aisa portfolio bana sakte hain, jo ke kuch saalon baad aapko jo har mahine profit dega, wo aapki salary se bhi zyada hoga. Aur phir aapne financial freedom jo hai, wo achieve kar liya hoga. Maine to personally is talk ko bahut zyada enjoy kiya, and I am sure aap bhi bahut zyada enjoy karenge. So aapne karna kya hai? Aapne YouTube pe search karna hai, 'TED Abdul Rehman Najam' aur is video ko dekh kar aapne apni financial freedom achieve karne ki journey jo hai, wo foran se plan kar leni hai. Aur phir aapne humein comments mein zaroor batana hai jo aapne apna plan banaya hai on achieving financial freedom.
