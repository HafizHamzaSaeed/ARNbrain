Maine financial freedom achieve kar liya hai aur ab aapki baari hai. Assalam-o-Alaikum. Mera naam hai Abdul Rehman Najam aur aaj main aapke sath milke Pakistan ki pehli financial freedom movement start karne ja raha hoon. Jis mein hum 10 lakh Pakistaniyon ko financial freedom achieve karwayenge. Financial freedom hai kya? Financial freedom itni important cheez hai ke jab aap ye apni life mein achieve kar lenge to aap ki zindagi hi badal jayegi. Maine jab financial freedom achieve kiya to maine ye feel kiya ke meri zindagi se paison ki tension jo hai na, woh zero ho gayi. Aur uske sath sath mere paas jo time tha usme main apni marzi ka kaam, jiska mujhe shauk tha, and more importantly, main woh kaam un logon ke sath kar sakta tha jinke sath kaam karna main enjoy karta tha. Financial freedom is for everyone, aur agar aap soch rahe hain ke ye financial freedom achieve karne ke liye saving, investment, bahut hi mushkil kaam hai, to abhi recently humne Pakistan ki first TEDx Talk on how to achieve financial freedom deliver ki. Aur ismein maine aapko bataya ke woh kaun sa best time hota hai jab aapko ye journey start karni chahiye, aur woh chaar easy steps jo ke aap bhi follow karke financial freedom achieve kar sakte hain. Agar aapne rich versus poor mindset samajh liya, aur financial compounding kaise karte hain, isko samajh liya, to phir aapko financial freedom achieve karne se koi bhi nahi rok sakta. Aur main aapko personally invite karna chahunga to join me on this movement of financial freedom aur aaiye milke aap aur main 10 lakh Pakistaniyon ki zindagi badalte hain.
