**Interviewer**: Acha ye jo stock market hai ye kisi shakhs ke liye passive income ka source bhi ban sakta hai?

**ARN**: 100% ban sakta hai. Jo log young hain, isko thora sa samajhte hain, passionate hain, wo hamare sath mil ke ye 10 lakh logon wala jo mission hai hamara usko achieve karein. 4,000 logon ne already register kar liya hai toh aap bhi usmein apna naam, number likh lein toh hamari jo bhi usmein planning hogi hum aapke sath share... Maine khud apni life mein kyun ke kabhi kahin naukri nahi ki. Jab maine investing ke baare mein parhna shuru kiya toh main sochta hota tha naukri kar ke seekh lete hain. Jo apne heroes thay, Warren Buffett, unke baare mein bhi parha toh maine maine socha ke main khud jaldi seekh sakta hoon. Toh hamesha se meri ek dream thi jab bhi main is level pe pahunch jaoon toh ek financial freedom ki movement zaroor chalani hai.

**Interviewer**: Abdul Rehman bhai, aap ab ye jo baar baar word use kar rahe hain financial freedom, ye financial freedom ko sunne se logon ke zehan mein aa raha hai ke ye hum paise kamayenge. Financial freedom kehte kisko hain?

**ARN**: Even main bhi agar apne aap se poochun na ke mere liye financial freedom kya hai toh wo kuch saal pehle kuch aur tha, abhi kuch aur hai. Jaise mujhe yaad hai jab maine nayi nayi investing shuru ki thi na toh mere liye financial freedom ka goal hota tha ye hai ke chalo main independent ho jaoon. Toh independence jo hai na financial freedom ka ek bara important pillar hai. Financial freedom hai ke agle char se panch saal ke jo expenses hain na wo bilkul secure hone chahiye.
