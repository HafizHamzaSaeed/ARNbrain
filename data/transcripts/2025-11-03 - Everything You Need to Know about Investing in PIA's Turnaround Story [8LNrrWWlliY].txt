Agar aap airline industry ko samajhna chahte hain toh teen cheezein sabse zyada matter karti hain: load factor, RASK aur CASK. Yeh teen metrics decide karte hain ke airline profit kama rahi hai ya har flight ke saath paise waste ho rahe hain. Globally top airlines ka load factor 82 se 85 percent hota hai, yaani ke 100 seats mein se kam az kam 82 seats filled hoti hain on average. Profitability tabhi aati hai jab RASK yaani ke revenue per available seat kilometer zyada ho aur CASK yaani ke cost per available seat kilometer kam ho. RASK aur CASK bohat hi important terms hain aur inko samajhne ka aasan tareeqa aise hai ke aapka plane jab fly karta hai toh har kilometer pe har seat pe aap takreeban 8 cents kama rahe hain, yaani ke yeh aapka revenue hai. Aur isi ke saath saath, jab aapka plane fly kar raha hai toh har seat pe har kilometer pe aap 6 cents kharch kar rahe hain, yaani ke cost ya phir CASK. Globally RASK ki average hota hai 8 se 10 cents, jabke CASK hota hai 5 aur 6 cents ke beech mein. Airlines jaise ke Emirates, Qatar Airways aur Turkish Airlines isi efficiency ke masters hain. Modern fleet, smart route planning aur tight cost control inka forte hai. Inka margin hota hai 2 se 4 cents per available seat kilometer. Yaani ke per seat har kilometer fly karke ye average 2 se 4 cents profit karte hain. Ab aa jaate hain PIA par aur saath saath samajh lete hain RASK aur CASK ko calculate karne ka aasan tareeqa. RASK ke liye aapne total revenue ko divide karna hai total seats available and total kilometers traveled se. PIA ka total revenue 2021 mein tha 86 billion rupees. Toh is number ko aapne available seat kilometer se divide karein toh answer aata hai 11 rupay 22 paise yaani ke 2021 ke dollar ke rate ke hisab se takreeban 7 cents. Aur isi tarah CASK calculate karte hain operating cost ko divide karke total seats and total distance traveled se. Again 2021 mein operating costs thi 101 arab rupay aur CASK ban gaya 13 rupay 18 paise ya phir 8.23 cents. Yaani ke har kilometer par PIA kama raha hai 7 cents aur kharch kar raha hai 8.23 cents. Result: har kilometer jo ke PIA udd raha hai aur har seat pe 1.22 cents ka nuksan. Jaise ke aapko pehle bataya, global airlines industry ka yahi spread positive 2 se 3 cents hota hai. So RASK aur CASK itne comprehensive metrics hain ke yeh aapko bata dete hain PIA ke loss karne ka reason jo ke hai overstaffing (400 se zyada employees per aircraft), purani fleet jisse cost barh jaati hain, limited flying hours, aur professionally is airline ko run na karna, government ki involvement. Ye sab milkar PIA ke cost structure ko distort kar dete hain aur PIA ke CASK ko increase karte hain. Jahaan duniya ki airlines ek kilometer fly karke profit kama rahi hoti hain, wahin PIA ek kilometer fly karke loss kar rahi hoti hai. Yakeen nahi aata yeh wahi airline hai jisne 1962 mein woh kar dikhaya jo duniya ne socha bhi nahi tha. London se Karachi tak ki us waqt ki fastest flight ka world record banaya tha, sirf 6 ghante 43 minutes mein aur 51 seconds. Aur aaj dekhein kya haalat hai. PIA ke losses sirf numbers nahi hain, ek kahani hai inefficiency ki aur mismanagement ki. 2020 se 2024 tak European Union aur UK ne complete ban laga diya tha. Nateeja: 30% jo profitable routes thhe jahan se accha profit aata tha, London, Manchester, Birmingham, direct flights, sab khatam. Sirf in bans ki wajah se PIA ko har saal takreeban 40 arab rupay ka revenue nuksan uthana pada. Aur inefficiency ki baat karein, toh har aircraft ke liye humne rakhe hue hain 427 employees. Zara compare kijiye. Turkish Airline mein hain per aircraft 94 employees. Jabke Emirates mein 231 staff per aircraft hain. Matlab hamare case mein staff double aur efficiency half. Aapke liye ek aur shocking fact. Auditor General of Pakistan ke mutabiq PIA ne pichle saalon mein 1,118 flights aisi operate ki hain jahan poori flight mein sirf ek passenger maujud tha. Poora plane, crew, fuel, maintenance sabka kharcha aur earning almost zero. Yeh thi peak of mismanagement jisne PIA ko barbad karke rakh diya.
