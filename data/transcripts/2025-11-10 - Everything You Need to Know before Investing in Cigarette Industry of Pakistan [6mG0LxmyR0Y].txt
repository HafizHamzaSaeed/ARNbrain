Ek waqt tha, cigarette sirf ek aadat thi, phir bani ek controversy. Laws badhe, taxes badhe, par industry ruki nahi. Aur aaj bhi cigarette industry Pakistan ke money-making sectors mein shamil hai. Aur isi industry mein listed companies ki share price pichle kuch saalon mein bhi aur longer term mein bhi, agar aap dekhein to amazing compounding karke di hai apne investors ko. Aur inhi investors ko saath hi saath khoob dividends bhi enjoy karwaye hain.

Dekhna ab ye hai ke aage bhi kya ye momentum barkarar rehta hai? Kyun ke jo fully tax-compliant companies hain is sector mein, unka market share waqt ke saath-saath neeche aa raha hai. Itna criticism, itne negative health effects, aur itne taxes ke bawajood bhi ye industry itni unstoppable kyun hai? Marketing se manufacturing tak har cheez ek art ban chuki hai, jahan har tick ek strategy hai aur har puff ek profit. Ye wo business hai jahan branding bhi science hai aur loyalty ek legacy.

Agar hum is industry ke total market size ki baat karein, to ye koi ek hazaar arab rupaye plus hai. Aur is waqt Pakistan ki jo teen listed cigarette companies ki value hai, wo almost 500 arab rupaye tak pahunch gayi hai, jo ke aaj se sirf do saal pehle sirf 180 arab rupaye hoti thi. Ek mulk jahan mehangai bhi badh rahi hai, berozgari aur ghurbaat bhi badh rahi hai, wahan cigarette ek aisi industry hai jiske profits pe zyada farq nahi pad raha. Har saal iska scale badhta ja raha hai. Aisa kya hai is industry mein?

Pakistan ki abaadi lagbhag 25 crore hai, jismein se adult population taqreeban 13 se 14 crore hai, aur almost do crore adults Pakistan mein active smokers hain. Matlab har saat adults mein se ek Pakistani smoking karta hai. Aur zahir hai male population mein almost har chaar mein se ek individual. WHO ke mutabiq, ye aadat har saal 1,60,000 zindagiyon ko nuksaandeh bimariyon ke zariye nuksaan pahunchati hai. Lekin irony ye hai jis cheez ne health system ko ghutno pe la khada kiya hai, wahi government ke khazane ko bhi bhar rahi hai. Cigarette companies ne 2024 mein government ke liye 200 arab rupaye se zyada ka tax revenue generate kiya. Phir bhi ye kahani yahan khatam nahi hoi. Estimate ye hai ke Pakistan mein bechi jaane wali cigarettes ka lagbhag 58% aaj bhi smuggled ya non-tax paid hai. Ye wo parallel economy hai jo tax-compliant companies se bhi zyada tez raftari se badh rahi hai. Yahan almost 200 se 300 arab rupaye ki tax evasion bhi hoti hai.

Cigarette sirf ek aadat nahi, ek industry hai, ek empire hai. Aaj mein aap ko detail mein explain karunga ke ye sab kaise hota hai. Tobacco industry ka Pakistan mein future kya hai? Kya ye ek export powerhouse banne ja raha hai ya phir ek slow death ke route pe hai? Aur ye ek aakhri puff hai jo aap enjoy kar sakte hain. Chaliye shuru karte hain basics se.

Tobacco ki kahani hazaaron saal purani hai. Sabse pehle ise Native Americans ne use kiya spiritual aur medical purposes ke liye. Lekin jab 16th century mein Europeans ne tobacco ko dekha, to duniya mein ek naya daur shuru ho gaya—the age of global trade. Tobacco ne North America ko ek trading powerhouse bana diya. Isne agriculture, packaging, aur export industries ko janam diya. 1880s mein cigarette-making machines ne ek nayi duniya khol di. Ab ek din mein lakhon cigarettes banti thi. Tobacco industry ne mass production ka concept introduce kiya, jo baad mein har industry ne follow bhi kiya. Advertising aur branding ka era shuru ho gaya. Marlboro, Dunhill, Lucky Strike—ye sirf brands nahi, cultural icons ban gaye.

Pakistan mein tobacco industry ka safar 1947 ke baad shuru hua, aur aaj ye mulk ki sabse high tax-contributing industries mein se ek hai. Aaj Pakistan mein 50 se zyada factories hain jo ke directly tobacco manufacturing se judi hui hain, aur lakhon log is industry se directly aur indirectly rozgar hasil karte hain. Sirf ye industry itni zyada tax leakages ke baad bhi 200 arab rupaye se zyada taxes aur revenue contribute karti hai government ke khazane mein. Pakistan ke Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ke zyada tar ilaqe aur Punjab ke kuch ilaqe bhi tobacco cultivation par depend karte hain. Har saal hazaaron farmers apni tobacco crops is industry ko bechte hain.

Ab dekhte hain Pakistan mein existing cigarette brands aur inke business ko owners ki tarah understand karte hain. Yahan local brands bhi hain aur international brands bhi. Legitimate cigarettes ke market share ki agar baat karein, to Tobacco Economics Working Paper ke survey report ke mutabiq, 2012 mein Pakistan Tobacco Company ka market share taqreeban 55% tha, lekin latest report ke mutabiq, June 2025 tak isi company Pakistan Tobacco ka share jo hai wo badhkar 70% tak chala gaya. Pakistan Tobacco ke brands ki agar baat karein to aap in bohot saaron ko recognize bhi karte honge, ismein Gold Leaf, Capstan aur Dunhill jaise famous brand aa jaate hain. Aur isi tarah ek local tobacco company jo listed hai, Khyber Tobacco, jo ke kabhi sirf teen se chaar percent ka market share rakhti thi, aaj wo bhi das percent se zyada ka share rakhti hai. Khyber Tobacco ke famous brands mein Kissan aur Gold Street jaise brands aa jaate hain, jo ke saste hone ki wajah se apne market share bhi badhate ja rahe hain. In dono companies ka market share increase hone ka matlab hai ke koi company apna market share lose bhi kar rahi hogi, aur wo hai Philip Morris, jiska market share 2012 mein taqreeban 33% hota tha aur June 2025 tak gir ke sirf 13% reh gaya. Philip Morris ke famous brands ki baat karein to mashoor-e-zamana Marlboro, Morven aur Red & White aate hain.

Yaad rahe ke ye jo humne aap se market share jo hai aap ke saath share kiye hain, ye listed companies ya jo tax-compliant companies hote hain unke market share hain. Smuggled aur non-tax paid brands iske alawa hain. Aur in brands ka market share overall market mein badhta ja raha hai. Agar hum dekhein to Pakistan mein har saal average taqreeban 80 billion sticks consume hoti hain. Latest figures ke hisab se ismein se taqreeban sirf 34 billion sticks yaani ke aadhi se bhi kam tax-paid hain, aur baqi saari smuggled ya non-tax paid hain. Jab ke 2013 mein yehi tax-paid sticks ki sales jo thi wo 67 billion sticks tak hua karti thi.

Isliye sabse pehle samajh lete hain cigarettes pe tax structure kya hai, kyun ke saari game hai hi yahan. Aur isi se ye business aap owners ki tarah samajh payenge. Taxes badhte ja rahe hain lekin cigarette ab bhi sasti hai, kyun? Kyun ke asli game manufacturing floors aur smuggled packets ke beech mein kheli ja rahi hai. To isko kuch yun samjhein ke Pakistan mein total 413 cigarette brands hain, jinmein se taqreeban 286 brands smuggled ya non-tax paid hain. Aur interestingly 332 brands aise hain jo ke 160 rupaye se kam price pe bik rahe hain market mein. Jab ke government ki taraf se jo tax structure hai, usmein kam se kam tax hi ek packet pe 160 rupaye hai. To ye tax se kam price pe bhi kaise bech sakte hain? Ye to khulla khulla nazar aa raha hai ke companies jo hai wo tax avoid kar rahi hain. Ab aap ko kuch samajh aane laga hoga ke smuggled aur non-tax paid ke popular hone ki wajah kya hai. Sasta maal kisko nahi pasand.

Ab yehi sab cigarette brands ko government ne three categories mein divide kiya hai tax purposes ke liye. Tier-1 ya premium cigarettes category mein Gold Leaf, Marlboro, Benson & Hedges aur Dunhill jaisi brands aa jaati hain, jin ke ek pack ki price taqreeban 500 se 550 rupaye hai, aur ismein se taqreeban 300 rupaye jo hai wo taxes mein chala jata hai. Tier-2 ki agar baat karein to usmein Capstan, Morven aur Gold Flake jaise brand aa jaate hain, jis ki price around 250 se 300 rupaye hai. Aur Tier-3 mein Kissan, Parliament aur Olympic jaise brands aa jaate hain, jo ke market mein taqreeban 150 se 200 ki range mein bikte hain. Aur inhi ke darmiyan kuch brands hain jo ke non-tax paid hain aur saste bik rahe hain. Aur ab agar aap aaj se koi paanch saal pehle dekhein, to yehi taxes ek cigarette pack ki price ka taqreeban 25 se 30 percent hote the, aur aaj jo fully tax-paid companies hain usmein ye 50 percent se bhi zyada hain taxes.

Jaise jaise tax badh rahe hain, log saste non-tax paid aur smuggled cigarettes ki taraf move kar rahe hain. Lekin phir bhi Pakistan Tobacco Company, being the largest listed company, kam cigarettes bech kar bhi zyada profit kama rahi hai. Profit before tax per stick last five years mein kaise upar ja raha hai, ye aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain. Ab kyun ke almost 60% cigarettes ki sale illicit market mein hoti hai, to agar government inpe sakhti karke apne tax bracket mein le aati hai, to government ka tax revenue cigarette se hi almost 300 arab rupaye se badhkar 600 arab rupaye tak pahunch sakta hai. Aur isi shadow industry ki teh mein chupi itni badi opportunity ko catch kiya ja sakta hai.

Cost structure breakdown par agar hum aayein to, total cost mein se 40 se 45% hissa raw material ka hota hai. Is raw material mein tobacco itself, filter, paper aur packaging shamil hain. Jab ke manufacturing aur marketing ka portion 10 se 15% contribute karta hai, ismein factory cost, advertising aur distribution jaise tamam kharche shamil hain. Ab baat karte hain main raw material ki sourcing ki. Ye sab Pakistan se hi provide kiya ja raha hai. Pakistan mein tobacco ki total production taqreeban 140 million kg hai, jisme se domestic tobacco companies ki demand taqreeban 80 million kg ki hai.

Aaiye ab hum product economics ki baat karte hain. Agar hum tax ki baat karein, to Pakistan Tobacco ki annual report ke mutabiq har ek cigarette par lagbhag 8 rupaye tax shamil hota hai. Ye total tax do hisson se milkar banta hai. Pehla FED, jo ke average 6 rupaye 40 paise per stick hai, yaani ke ek pack par taqreeban 128 rupaye. Aur dusra tax hai GST jiska average hissa ban jata hai taqreeban 1 rupaye 60 paise per stick ya phir 32 rupaye per pack.

Aur aaiye ab dekhte hain Pakistan Tobacco ke kharchon aur munafon ko. Manufacturing aur cost of sales Pakistan Tobacco ke liye taqreeban 2 rupaye 25 paise per stick ban jaati hain. Selling aur distribution ki baat karein to ye cost sirf 25 paise per stick hai. Iske baad manufacturer ka gross margin ban jata hai taqreeban 2 rupaye per stick, aur agar saare additional costs aur taxes hata dein to net profit ban jata hai taqreeban 1 rupaye per stick. Ab aap sochiye ye Pakistan ki number one company hai in tobacco sector. Har stick par saare kharchon ke baad, saare taxes pay karne ke baad ye taqreeban 1 rupaye kama rahe hain. Aur aap tax evasion karke 8 se 10 rupaye per stick jo hai wo easily bacha sakte hain. Ye hai asal economics is business ki.

Baat sirf cigarettes tak nahi rahi. Ab waqt badalne ke saath-saath is industry ka rukh bhi badal raha hai. Nicotine pouches, e-cigarettes, vapes, aur heated tobacco products aap ne khud bhi dekhe honge market mein aane shuru ho gaye hain. Investors aur entrepreneurs is alternative smoking products ki taraf bhaag rahe hain. Future trends ko dekhte hue regulations sakht ho rahi hain, lekin demand ab bhi unstoppable hai. Log nicotine ko chhod nahi rahe, bas shift kar rahe hain—traditional se tech-driven aur nicotine pouches ki taraf. Aur isi ko dekhte hue Pakistan Tobacco Company ne apne modern brand Velo ke zariye ek naya aur bold safar shuru kar liya hai. Sirf paanch saal pehle introduce hone wala Velo aaj Pakistan Tobacco ke total revenue ka 4 to 5% already ban chuka hai aur management ke mutabiq is saal break-even bhi achieve kar lega.

Pakistan Tobacco ne Made in Pakistan 3.0 export initiative bhi launch kar diya hai, jiske tehat Velo ko Japan, Gulf countries, aur Europe jaise international markets mein bhi export kiya ja raha hai. Company ki latest reports ke mutabiq export sales 10 arab rupaye tak already pahunch gayi hain jahan sabse tez growth Velo segment hi dikha raha hai. Ab Pakistan Tobacco Company apna product saat countries ko export kar rahi hai aur Bahrain ke liye bhi pehli shipment tayyar khadi hai. Iske saath-saath company ke future plans mein ye bhi shamil hai ke France aur Peru jaisi markets ko bhi target kiya jaye.

Ab agar export potential par aa jayein jo ke is industry ka major trigger hai, fiscal year 2024 aur 25 mein Pakistan ke tobacco exports mein zabardast izafa dekha gaya. Inki value 64 million dollars se badhkar 166 million dollars tak pahunch gayi, yaani ke almost 150% se bhi zyada ki phenomenal growth. Tobacco exports volumes mein bhi kaafi tezi se badh rahe hain, 43,000 metric tons se ye 1,04,000 metric tons tak already pahunch gaye hain. Aur jab Gulf countries jaisi markets tobacco import kar rahi hain to in companies ke liye ye bhi ek new growth achieve karne ki golden window open ho rahi hai. Aur isi cheez ka fayda uthane ke liye bhi Khyber Tobacco jaisi company ne bhi tobacco exports ki volume increase karna start kar di hain, aur company ki recent annual report ke mutabiq company ne ek saal mein almost 25 million dollars ki export already kar di hain.

Chaliye ab hum aap ko dikhate hain ke is sector ki largest company ki outlook kaisi lag rahi hai aur agle saal ke revenues aur profit kya expected hain? Pakistan Tobacco Company ki agar 2025 ke estimates ki baat karein to inka revenue ho jayega taqreeban 350 arab rupaye, jisme taxes minus ho jayenge aur cost of production jab minus ho jayegi to gross profit ban jayenge taqreeban 67 billion rupees, aur income tax pay karne ke baad ye net profit ho jayega taqreeban 32 billion rupees.

Aur iske saath-saath is sector mein ab government ki rook-thaam start hone ke aasaar nazar aa rahe hain. Illicit tobacco trade par ab game badal rahi hai, crackdown shuru hone ki baatein bhi ho rahi hain. Aur agar black market companies pe crackdown sahi se ho gaya to legitimate companies ki sales aur profits skyrocket kar sakte hain. Yaad rakhein, tax evasion ki wajah se in companies ki jo sales hain in sticks per year kitni zyada decline ho gayi hain pichle paanch saalon mein aur agar ye crackdown sahi se start ho gaya to ye wapas upward trend par chali jayengi.
