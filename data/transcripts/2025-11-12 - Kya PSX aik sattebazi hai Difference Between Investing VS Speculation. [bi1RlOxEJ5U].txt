Log isko use karte hain ke koi khabar dekhi aur andaza lagate hain ke is khabar se ye barh jaega, main khareed leta hoon main bech deta hoon. To koi bhi cheez mein invest karna without knowing ke wo kya hai, kisi ke kehne pe le lena ke aapke kisi dost ne kaha ke yaar maine ek share liya tha mujhe usme bada profit ho gaya tum bhi le lo.
