Speaker 1: Acha mujhe bhai jaan is mein scam ka batayein detail se, ke scam kya kya hai, kis kis chizon se bachna hai, kahan kahan jana hai, kahan nahi jana?

ARN: Scam jo hai na financial scam kisi bhi cheez mein ho sakta hai. Pehla ye hai ke zyada tar jo scammers hote hain na aap ko lalach dete hain. To koi bhi aap ko mahine ka 2% se zyada kahe, to bohat zyada chances hote hain ke woh scam kar raha hai. Ab bohat zyada log sikhate bhi hain, forex bhi sikhate hain, doosri cheezein bhi sikhate hain. Aap seekhne ki fees dein, apni jo investment hai na woh apne control mein rakhein. Koi company kitni bhi barri ho, jab tak woh SECP se registered nahi ho gi, aap ne jo hai na us company ke sath deal nahi karna. Phir aap ne jo scammers hote hain na un ke content ko hamesha un ki knowledge pe judge karna hai, ye nahi judge karna ke woh dikha kya rahe hain. To success stories pe bhi hamesha nazar rakhein, apni sirf fees transfer karein, sarmaya kabhi bhi transfer na karein, aur 2% rule jo hai na, woh bhi yaad rakhein. Is se agar aap ye cheezein kar lein ge na, الحمد لله aap ka koi aap ke sath koi scam nahi ho ga.
