To start karte hain deposit franchise se. Deposit franchise kehte hain un banks ko jinke paas depositors khud chal ke aate hain apna paisa deposit कराने. In banks ki har jagah branches bhi hoti hain, ATMs bhi hoti hain. Koi bhi kaam ho, asaani se ho jata hai, aapko zyada door nahi jaana parta aur bare bank mein log safe bhi feel karte hain ke mera deposit, jo meri savings hain, wo bilkul 100% secured hain. In deposit franchises ke liye apne deposits ko increase karna relatively easy hota hai. Khud jo customer chal kar aa raha hota hai inke paas. Aur phir jab customer khud chal kar aata hai to bank ke liye bhi easy ho jata hai ke unko convince karna ke aap current account khulwa lein, specially agar interest rates bohat zyada high level pe na hon. Agar aapko current account ki importance bank ke point of view se nahi pata, to isko aise samajh lein ke jaise aaj kal 11% interest rate chal raha hai aur bank jo hai government ko loan de ya kisi company ko loan de to wo 11 aur 12% ke beech mein loan jo hai wo de deta hai. To ek dafa jab bank ke paas deposit aa gaye to us pe unhone aage loan de ke 11 se 12% hi kamana hai, lekin agar current account hoga to uski cost hogi 0% aur agar saving account hoga to uski cost hogi 7 se 10%. To yaani ke aap jo spread kama rahe hain on that deposit, wo agar current account ke deposit honge to wo 11 se 12% hoga, aur agar saving account ke deposit honge to wo spread jo hissa aapka hota hai wo 2 se 4% hoga sirf. Yaani ke current account pe jo spread hai wo saving account ke muqable mein almost aapko teen guna profit de raha hai. So zyada current account hone ka matlab hai less cost of deposits and high profits and ultimately high return on invested capital. High current account percentage aur high ROIC yaani ke ek bank ka multibagger hona in mein bara gehra taaluq hai. Jin banks ke paas current accounts zyada hote hain unka ROIC normally 25% ya isse above hi hota hai. Jaise ke Meezan Bank, UBL, in dono banks mein almost 50% deposits jo hain wo current accounts hain. Ab ROIC ki baat ho hi rahi hai, to aapko ye bhi batate hain ke asal mein ye hota kya hai? Return on Invested Capital. Jaise ke naam se zaahir ho raha hai ke company kitna profit generate karti hai apne capital required to run the business pe, yaani ke agar business mein main ₹100 invest karunga to us pe mujhe kitna profit milega ek saal mein. So banking business ko samajhne ke liye aapne agar sochna hai ke ek bank chalane ke liye aapko kitna paisa required hai aur us pe kitna return aayega, to State Bank ki requirements aur jo aapne apne banking softwares aur infrastructure mein investment karni hoti hai us poore system ko chalane ke liye, to agar aapke bank mein let's say 1,000 arab ke deposits hain to ye jo investment hogi jo ki aap State Bank mein jama karayenge aur apna jo poora infrastructure banayenge, ye ban jayegi taqreeban 100 arab rupaye. Ab ek average bank is 100 arab rupaye ki investment pe har saal taqreeban 10 se 15 arab rupaye profit kamata hai. Aur agar ek efficient bank ho jiska high ROIC ho to shayad wo isi 100 arab rupaye pe 30 arab rupaye bhi kama le. Aur phir ye 30% annual compounding wali machine jab saalon saal chalti hai yaani ke depositors aate rehte hain to ye ek natural multibagger ban jata hai. Aaiye aapko asaan words aur numbers use karke ek average bank aur ek top bank mein farq samjhate hain. Aur yahan aapne meri baat ghaur se sunni hai, main batane laga hoon aapko banking sector ka secret sauce, yaani ke aapne future ke multibagger banks jo hain wo kis tareeqe se pick karne hain. So ab aap sochein do banks hain, dono ke paas 1,000 arab rupaye ke deposits hain aur interest rate chal raha hai 11%. Ek jo average bank hai uska current account percentage hai 25%, yaani ke uske paas 250 arab rupaye current account mein hain aur jo doosra efficient bank hai uske paas 500 arab rupaye current account mein pare hue hain. To jaise maine aapko pehle samjhaya ke current account mein agar aapke paas paise pare hote hain to aapka us percentage of deposits pe jo spread hota hai wo 11 se 12% hota hai aur jo savings accounts hote hain usme 2 se 4% hota hai. To bank A jo ke average bank hai uska spread on total deposits jo ki aap average se nikalenge wo ban jayega 4.5% yaani ke 45 billion rupees. Aur jo efficient bank hai jisme current account ki percentage zyada hai, unka average spread ban jayega 7% yaani ke 70 arab rupaye. Iske baad aa jaati hai non-interest income. Isme card ki fees ya jo bank remittance lekar aa raha hota hai uski fees ya aap doosri jaise LC open karte hain uski fees, to usme ek average bank aur ek top bank mein bohat zyada farq nahi hota, to hum assume kar lete hain dono banks jo hain isme taqreeban 15 arab rupaye kama rahe hain. Phir high ROIC achieve karne ke liye aapko apne expenses ko bhi control karna hota hai. To ek jo top bank hota hai wo apne deposits ka taqreeban 3% har saal operating expenses mein spend karta hai. Yaani ke efficient bank ne spend kar liye 30 arab rupaye aur jo ek average bank hoga wo apni cost control jo hai usko nahi kar pata aur wo spend karta hai 35 se 40 arab rupaye. Phir aa jaati hai provisioning. Ye wo hota hai ke aapne kisi company ko loan diya aur kisi wajah se aapko wo loan waapas nahi mila. To isme aapne jo saari apni chhaan been karni hoti hai loan dene se pehle ke ye loan mujhe waapas milega ke nahi, uski quality yahan pe reflect hoti hai. Ek achha bank jiska high ROIC hota hai wo taqreeban agar uske paas 1,000 arab rupaye ke deposits honge usme se agar wo 500 arab rupaye ke agar loan dega to sirf 5 arab rupaye aise honge jo ke waapas nahi aayenge. Wahin ek average bank jo hai 10 arab rupaye aise loan de dega jo ke shayad waapas na aayein. To in saari cheezon ko agar aap accumulate karein, to ek efficient bank jo hai wo profit before tax kama lega 50 arab rupaye aur ek average bank jo hai wo sirf 15 arab rupaye kamayega. Aur phir is pe jo hai aapne 50% Pakistan mein tax bhi dena hota hai banking sector mein, to profit after tax efficient bank ka ho gaya 25 arab rupaye aur average bank ka ho gaya 7.5 se 10 arab rupaye. Aur investment required to run the business dono ki same hai, 100 arab rupaye. To ROIC efficient bank ka ho gaya 25% aur ek average bank ka ho gaya roughly 8 se 10%. Iske saath saath high ROIC hone ka ek aur faida bhi hota hai aur wo ye hota kyunke logon ko pata hota hai ye bari zabardast compounding machine hai, isme hum agar partner banenge to kal ko hume bohat zyada compounding milegi, to ye jo high ROIC banks hote hain inko jo P/E ratio allocate karti hai stock market, wo bhi thodi si zyada hoti hai. Jaise ke aapne dekha hoga ke Meezan Bank 8 se 9 P/E ratio pe chal raha hota hai aur ek average bank jo hai wo 6 se 7 P/E ratio pe chal raha hota hai, uski wajah yahi hoti hai ke log kehte hain ke aaj agar hum isme investment karenge, future mein compounding zyada hogi to aaj hum thodi si zyada price bhi pay kar denge to ye hamare liye behtar hai. First is deposit growth. So interest rate zyada hon ya interest rate kam hon, jaise jaise waqt guzarta rehta hai economy mein inflation hoti rehti hai, logon ke paas jo amount hoti hai paiso ki wo barhti rehti hai, to deposit growth bhi barhti rehti hai. Iske saath doosra reason hota hai ke jaise hum ek high interest rate environment se low interest rate environment mein aa rahe hote hain, to jab interest rate high hote hain to bank ne kuch portion apna investments ka long-term government ko qarz diya hota hai.
