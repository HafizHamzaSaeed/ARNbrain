Sab se pehle samajhte hain ke mining industry ki business economics kaise kaam karti hain. Dekhein, mining industry mein do types ke mine hote hain: tier-1 and tier-2. Dono extraction hi karte hain, dono copper-gold hi produce karte hain, lekin business economics ka farq zameen-aasman ka hota hai. Aur is farq ki wajah se Reko Diq jaisa project duniya ke tier-1 assets mein count hota hai. Sab se important hota hai ore ka difference. Tier-1 ka copper grade 0.6% se 0.7% hota hai. Jabke tier-2 ka grade is se half hota hai. Matlab ek tonne ore nikal ke tier-1 mine mein aapko almost double metal milega. Ek tonne mein se aapko 65 grams copper milega aur tier-2 mine mein aapko 35 grams copper milega. Aur aapko to pata hai value to metal ki hi hoti hai, mitti ki to koi value hoti nahi hai. To revenue per tonne tier-1 mine ka double ho jata hai.

Aur phir aa jata hai ke ek tonne ore uthane ke liye aapko kitni mitti move karni padti hai. Is ko kehte hain strip ratio. Tier-1 mines ka strip ratio aam taur par 0.5 to 1 ratio hota hai. Aur Reko Diq ka yeh world class level 0.47 to 1 hai. Tier-2 mines ka strip ratio 3 is to 1 hota hai. Matlab ke tier-2 mines mein aapko ek tonne ore tak pahunchne ke liye teen tonne waste jo hai woh remove karna padta hai. Aur tier-1 mines mein aapko is se cheh guna kam waste remove karna padta hai. Aur aapko to pata hai yeh jo saari costs hoti hain diesel ki, explosives ki, fleet ki, fuel ki, yeh sab tier-2 mines ke liye kaafi zyada badh jati hain kyunki unko mitti zyada remove karni hoti hai.

Yahan par ek misconception clear karna bahut zaroori hai. Strip ratio six times hone ka matlab yeh nahi ke total cost six times ho jati hai. Waste movement total mining cost ka 25 se 30% contribute karta hai. Do aur important factors jo tier-1 mine ko aur zyada favor karte hain, ek to scale, jab production zyada hoti hai to economies of scale ki wajah se aapki fixed cost per tonne neeche aa jati hai. Aur jitna zyada metal content hota hai aapki ore mein, utna zyada aapka processing mein loss kam hota hai. To yeh saari cheezon tier-1 mine ko bahut hi ek amazing project bana deti hain. Aur isi wajah se poori duniya mein jo tier-1 mines hain, us ke peeche investors jo hain bahut zyada interest show karte rehte hain.

In sab cheezon ko agar combine karein, to aaj ke rate ke hisab se tier-1 mine ka revenue per tonne ho jata hai 70 dollars per tonne. Aur tier-2 mine ka taqreeban 40 se 45 dollars per tonne. Aur costs tier-1 mine ki taqreeban 25 dollar per tonne aur tier-2 mine ki 30 se 35 dollar per tonne. To agar aap business owner hain, to aap khud dekhenge ke profit per tonne mein aap dekhein zameen-aasman ka farq hai. Tier-1 mine ka hai 45 dollars per tonne aur tier-2 mine 10 se 15 dollars per tonne. Ab agar hum assume karein ke tier-1 mine ko banane ke liye double investment bhi chahiye hoti hai, to phir bhi uska jo profit hai per tonne, woh four times hai. Aise hi thodi tier-1 mines ke peeche poori duniya ki investment peeche-peeche bhaag rahi hoti hai.

Ab hum baat karte hain Reko Diq ke phase 1 ke ROIC ki. Jis se aapko idea hoga ke gold-copper mining Pakistan mein kitna accha business ho sakta hai, doosri companies ke liye bhi. Current gold aur copper price aur jo phase 1 ke production targets hain, unko use kar ke revenue jo hai ek saal mein 3 billion dollar se bhi zyada cross kar jata hai. Is production pe 5% royalty directly Government of Balochistan ko milegi, jabke 1% net smelter return Balochistan ke special development fund ke liye allocate kiya jayega. Yeh amount province ki socio-economic uplift projects mein use kiya jayega. So total royalty amount approx 200 million dollars ban jati hai.

Agar cost side pe aayein, to AISC jo ke ek mining industry ki term hai jis ko kehte hain all-in sustaining costs, jis mein production se related saari cost cover ho jati hain, woh roughly tier-1 mines ke liye, gold ke liye per tonne taqreeban 1500 dollar hoti hain. Aur copper ke liye roughly 5 se 6 hazaar dollar per tonne hoti hain. So operating cost roughly 1.7 se 1.8 billion dollars ban jayengi. Yaani ke operational level par project roughly 1.4 se 1.5 billion dollars earn karega. Aur jaise aapne news mein bhi padha hoga, phase 1 mein Reko Diq mein estimated investment 7 billion dollars ki hai. Aur is investment par nearly 20% dollarized ROIC ban jata hai. Aur phase 2 mein yeh production double hogi, but additional investment 7 billion dollar se kam hogi. So overall ROIC project ka at current gold-copper prices bahut zyada attractive hai, even in dollar terms. Isi liye, jo company bhi mining business mein investment karne ka plan share karti hai stock market mein, investors is announcement ko positively lete hain, kyunki unko pata hai ke future mein yeh project shareholders ko kaafi zyada benefit dega.

Aap khud sochein Pakistan ki stock market mein sab se bada issue hai boom-bust cycles. To jab yeh project phase 1 mein taqreeban 3 billion dollar ke exports karega aur phase 2 mein jaane ke baad har saal 5 billion dollar ke export kiya karega, aur is ke saath-saath shayad jo doosre Pakistan ke bade conglomerates hain, woh bhi apna mining business jo hai develop kar lein, to combine kar ke mining sector se agar 5 to 7 billion dollars ki exports hone lag gayi, to Pakistan ka boom-bust cycle jo hai, woh bahut zyada improve ho sakta hai.

Agar aap pichle 10-15 saal, jis mein yeh boom-bust cycle jo hai bahut zyada relevant raha hai, uska aap average current account deficit nikal kar dekhenge to woh teen se char billion dollar ya maximum paanch billion dollar banta hai. Aur agar Pakistan apne exports ko paanch cheh billion dollar se is new sector ko explore kar ke increase kar le, to aapki economy ki viability badh jati hai. Jab exports zyada honge, to mazeed dollars available honge to import aur phir us ki wajah se economic growth bhi badh jayegi. Jab exports zyada honge, to phir currency pe bhi kam pressure aayega. Aur PKR kam devalue hoga, to phir inflation bhi kam hoga. Aur inflation kam hone ka matlab hai ke interest rates bhi kam level par zyada der tak reh sakte hain.

Aur aapko to pata hoga Warren Buffett ka famous quote hai ke "Interest rates are like gravity for stocks". Agar interest rates Pakistan mein sustainable level pe neeche aa jayein, to phir Pakistan ki stock market ko bhi 12, 13, 14 PE easily mil sakti hain. Jab interest rates ghirte hain, economy ka multiplier effect kick in ho jata hai. Housing revive hoti hai, cement, steel demand grow karti hai. Autos ki sale pick up ho jati hai. Banks ki loan growth increase hoti hai. Aur industrial profitability improve ho jati hai kyunki aapko financing cost bhi kam deni hoti hai. Macro stability ka direct impact PSX ki valuations par hota hai. Lower interest rates is equals to higher market-wide PE multiples jo ke har company ko milte hain.

Aaiye aapko ek bahut hi interesting fact batata hun. Warren Buffett poori zindagi gold mines se door rahe. Unhone hamesha kaha ke mining business bahut unpredictable hai. Lekin jaise hi copper global electrification cycle ka backbone bana, to Berkshire Hathaway ne 2020 mein Barrick Gold mein investment ki. Unka thesis bahut simple tha, "When a mine has a long life, predictable cash flows, it becomes a toll road." Yaani ke agar ek tier-1 mine ho jis ki cost bhi kam ho aur woh jo product mine kar rahi hai uski demand bhi increase ho rahi ho, to phir woh ek road ki tarah ban jata hai jis pe log safar karte rehte hain aur kiraya bharte rehte hain.
