So baat kuch yun hai ke 250 se 300 rupees per liter wala petrol, diesel bechne par OMC ko sirf sat se aath rupees commission milta hai. To isliye pehle briefly samajhte hain ke Pakistan mein petrol, diesel ki price jo Government of Pakistan decide karti hai, uska formula kya hai. Yeh import-based pricing hoti hai aur sabse important factor hota hai ke unhi pichle 15 dinon mein diesel aur petrol, jo uska Euro 5 version hai, uski international market mein kya price chal rahi hai. Us mein phir freight add kiya jata hai aur phir usko interbank dollar rate se multiply karke rupees mein ek value nikali jati hai per liter. Phir uspe duties, taxes, jis mein PDL (Petroleum Development Levy), climate levy, jo pichle budget mein apply hui thi, aur doosri import duties add ki jati hain. Uske baad phir IFEM add kiya jata hai. IFEM ek aisi cheez hai jo ke make sure karta hai ke Karachi mein jo petrol ki price hai aur jo Khyber mein hai, un dono mein jo difference hota hai transport ka, woh jo hai, ek tarah se saari companies contribute karein aur poore Pakistan mein petrol, diesel ki price jo hai, woh unified rahe. So Government of Pakistan kya karti hai, similar prices rakhne ke liye? Ke poore mulk mein petrol, diesel ya crude oil ko transport karne ka jitna bhi kharcha hota hai, us sab ko combine karti hai aur usko total liters se divide karti hai aur woh jitna bhi banta hai, paanch rupees, che rupees per liter, woh har company usko collect karti hai from the customer, aur phir woh ek pool mein chala jata hai. Aur us pool mein jisne jitna transport ka kharcha kiya hota hai, usko reimbursement mil jati hai. Iske baad phir aa jata hai OMC margin aur phir aa jata hai dealer margin, jo ke aath rupees ke aas pass hote hain. So ab jab aap ne yeh poora formula samajh liya to aap ne khud already sochna shuru kar diya hoga ke is mein do jagahon pe masle aa sakte hain. Aur dono jo hain, import se related hain. And now it is time for some expert insights about OMCs or petrol pump franchise business. Sabse pehle concept aur yeh aap ne apne dimaag mein bitha lena hai. Aap ne kabhi bhi aankhein band karke P/E ratio use nahi karna is industry mein. Aur uski wajah yeh hai ke jaise maine aap ko samjhaya ke 280 rupees ka ek jo liter hota hai, us mein mushkil se company ka profit before tax jo hai, woh teen rupees hota hai, yani ke ek percent. So jab aap 1% margin par taqreeban kaam kar rahe hote hain, uske baad aap ki pricing international prices se linked hai aur teen se char percent to har 15 din mein price ki volatility chalti rehti hai. To kabhi agar aap ne let's say sasta petrol manga ke rakha hua تھا aur kisi wajah se internationally petrol, diesel ki price thodi si badh gayi aur Government of Pakistan ne bhi 15 din baad uski price badha di, aap ke paas jo sasti inventory padi hui thi, uspe aap ko aisa lagega ke per liter profit jo hai, jo ke teen rupees profit before tax ke aas pass hona chahiye, woh che, sat rupees ho gaya hai. Aap ki bila wajah without any sustainable change aap ka jo profit hai, woh double aane lag jayega thode time ke liye. Aur isi tareeqe se agar trend jo hai, woh downward ho jaye, to jo teen rupees profit before tax per liter hota hai, woh kabhi kabhi ek ya kabhi kabhi negative bhi ho jata hai. Isko kehte hain inventory gains, inventory losses. To aap ne P/E ratio tabhi use karna hai jab aap ki earnings mein inventory gains aur inventory losses na hon. Yani ke kisi aise saal mein jis mein petrol, diesel ki price agar internationally stable rahi hogi to phir woh company ki real earnings hain. Uspe aap P/E ratio bilkul use kar sakte hain. Kisi bhi saal mein agar petrol ki price upward hai, to aap P/E ratio use karenge to aap ko woh bohot zyada fair value degi kyunke profits exaggerated hain.

Doosri important cheez aur again aap ne isko link karna hai ke yeh companies 1% se 1.5% net margin pe chal rahi hoti hain.
