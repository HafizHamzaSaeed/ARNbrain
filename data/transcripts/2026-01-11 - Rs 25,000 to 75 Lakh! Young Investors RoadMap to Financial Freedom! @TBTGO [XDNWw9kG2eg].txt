Second jo log thode young hain to har mahine aap ne 20,000 rupay save kiye. Sahi hai? Doosre saal mein aap ki jab 10% salary badhi, aap ne 20,000 ko 22,000 kar diya. Theek hai? To aap ne total next 25 years mein, chalein isko 25,000 kar lete hain, saal ke 3 lakh rupay for 25 years. Aap ne jo second year mein save kiya uski value to zyada hai, lekin uski asal buying power to 25,000 hi hai. To aap ne 25 saalon mein 75 lakh rupay jo hai na uski real investment ki. Ab jab ye 18% pe compound hoga, so iski final value banegi 25 crore rupay. 25 years mein aap ki 15 times inflation ho jayegi. To aap 25 crore ko divide kar dein 15 se. Ye aap ke ban gaye taqreeban paune do crore. Aap ne save kitna kiya? 75 lakh. Isne aap ki real earning power ko bhi 2 times se zyada badha diya.
