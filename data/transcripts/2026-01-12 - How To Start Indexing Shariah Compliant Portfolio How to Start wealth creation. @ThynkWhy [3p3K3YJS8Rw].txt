Aur us ka tareeqa hota hai index investing. Index investing hoti hai ke main apna time nahi lagaunga, main jo duniya bhar ki ya Pakistan ki top companies hain na, us mein sab ke saath thoda thoda partner ban jaunga. Let's say log Shariah-compliant investing karna chah rahe hain, us ka ek index hai KMI 30. Us ke andar Pakistan ki top 30 Shariah-compliant companies jo hain na wo hain. Jo sab se badi company hogi us ko 10%, phir doosri ko 8% diya gaya hai. To aap bhi us ke hisab se jo top 15 companies hain na wo portfolio bana lo. Matlab aap ke paas let's say lakh rupay aaye, aap ne jo number 1 company thi us mein 10,000 laga diye, jo number 2 company thi us mein 9,000 laga diye, aap ka poora portfolio ban gaya. Ab aap ne is mein koi research nahi ki, koi aap ko khabar padhne ki zaroorat nahi hai, kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Ye businesses grow hote rahenge, aap ki bhi investment saath saath grow hoti rahegi.
