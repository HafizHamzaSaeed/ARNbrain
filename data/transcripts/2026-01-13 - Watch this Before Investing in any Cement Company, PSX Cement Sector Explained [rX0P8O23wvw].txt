Zara sochiye, jis farsh par hum chalte hain, jis makaan mein hum rehte hain, jis sadak par hum travel karte hain aur jis flyover se hum roz guzarte hain, in sab ka ek hi DNA hai: cement. Yeh sirf ek building material nahi, yeh Pakistan ki economy ki foundation hai. Ek aisi industry jiska size Rs 1 trillion se bhi zyada hai. Aur jahan har ek cement bag ke peeche hai: fuel, power aur profit ka ek network, jiska hissa aap bhi ban sakte hain.

Aur sath hi sath, yeh bhi sochein ke is poori industry ka aadha hissa sirf teen companies ke paas hai: Lucky Cement, Bestway Cement, aur Fauji Cement. Yeh hai Pakistan ki cement oligopoly, jahan power concentrate karti hai kuch hi haathon mein. Aur yeh woh industry hai jahan se long term growth companies paida hoti hain.

Lucky Cement, ek aisi company jisne apne owners aur shareholders ko pichle decades mein kaafi zyada jo hai profits kama ke diye hain. Sirf 1992 mein start hone wali yeh company aaj Pakistan ki one of the biggest giants ban chuki hai, aur company ki value hai more than 700 billion rupees. Yeh proof hai ke Pakistan ki volatile market mein bhi agar strong fundamentals aur long-term patience ke sath cement jaise sector mein bhi amazing long-term returns ban jaate hain.

Cement ki demand ek cheez pe kaafi zyada depend karti hai, aur woh hai interest rates. Aur aapne abhi dekha hoga thode din pehle ke State Bank of Pakistan ne interest rates phir se cut karne shuru kar diye hain. Jab interest rates badhte hain to construction ruk jaati hai. Aur jag interest rates girtay hain to economy dobara revive hoti hai. 2023 mein jab policy rate 22% tak chala gaya tha to cement demand crash kar gayi. Factories 40% capacity pe chal rahi thin, projects delay hue, aur poora sector ek freeze state mein chala gaya.

Aaj Pakistan ke paas 82 million tons ki total cement capacity hai, lekin pichle do saalon mein local demand thi 35 se 38 million tons ke aas paas. Lekin jaise hi interest rates ne neeche aana shuru kiya, inflation cool down hui, rupee stabilize hua, investor sentiment improve kar gaya aur last teen se chaar mahinon se cement demand double-digit growth dikha rahi hai. Local demand in North of Pakistan pichle do se teen saalon ke baad monthly basis pe 3 million tons cross kar chuki hai. Result? Aayein start karte hain. Cement industry ko samajhne ke liye humein sabse pehle yeh samajhna hoga ke cement banta kaise hai, kyunke jab tak hum is process ko andar tak nahi jaanenge, tab tak hum is business ko owners ki tarah samajh hi nahi payenge. Cement ka safar start hota hai ek cheez se jiska naam hai clinker. Yeh ek intermediate product hai jo limestone, clay aur gypsum ko mix karke high temperature kilns mein roast karke banaya jata hai. Har 1 ton cement banane ke liye lagta hai 1.4 to 1.6 tons of limestone aur heat pahonchti hai 1400 degree tak. Is process ke baad clinker ko grind karke fine powder banaya jata hai.

Aur wahi powder hum sab ke gharon, buildings aur bridges ki foundation banta hai. Ek ton cement banane ke liye 80 se 90 units bijli aur 130 se 140 kg coal bhi lagta hai. Yani yahan game raw material ke sath sath energy efficiency aur fuel optimization ki bhi hai. Cement banana more or less sab companies ka same process hota hai, aur finished product bhi same quality ka hota hai, to is commodity ki price ek particular location mein lagbhag har company ki similar hoti hai, market mein bag price ka farq jo hai woh roughly Rs. 20 se 30 se zyada nahi hota.

Isi cheez ko samajhte hue koi bhi company apni marzi se price set nahi kar sakti. To agar koi company price control nahi kar sakti, phir profit kaise badhaye jaate hain from one company to another? Iska sirf ek hi tareeqa hai aur woh hai cost control. Jo company apna fuel mix yaani ke sasta coal aur power source yaani ke sasti bijli efficiently manage kar leti hai wahi company apna cost structure control mein rakh pati hai jiska result hota hai extra profit for investors. On average ek cement company har ton cement par Rs. 3,000 per ton profit before tax kama leti hai yaani ke per bag yeh ban jata hai roughly Rs. 150. Lekin is profit ka sabse bada driver hai energy mix. Ek ton cement banane ke liye jaise maine aapko bataya taqreeban 80 units electricity chahiye hoti hai. Ab agar ek company grid ki bijli use kar rahi hai jiski cost hai taqreeban 30 se 35 rupaye per unit, to energy expense kaafi zyada badh jata hai. Lekin agar kisi company ke paas hai sasta coal ka power plant jisse ek unit banta hai 18 se 20 rupaye ka to phir is company ki kaafi zyada saving ho jati hai. 80 units ko agar aap per unit Rs. 14 ki saving se multiply karein to yeh ban jaate hain taqreeban 1200 se 1300 rupaye per ton of cement. Yeh saving direct translate hoti hai profitability mein aur company ka per ton profit jo hai woh 3000 se badh ke 4000 tak pahonch jata hai yaani ke 30% extra profit agar aapke paas sasti bijli hai. Isi tarah coal ki source aur quality bhi cost mein important role play karti hai. Local coal milta hai taqreeban 30 se 35 hazar rupaye per ton, jabke Afghan coal 38 se 42 hazar per ton yaani ke 5 se 6 hazar rupaye per ton ka difference, jo agar aap use karein 130 se 140 kg coal chahiye hota hai to isse bhi har ton cement banane mein 700 se 800 rupaye per ton ka farq aa jata hai aur again iska matlab hota hai 25% extra profit.

Lekin yeh cost saving har company utilize nahi kar sakti kyunke har company ka plant design thoda thoda different hota hai, koi plant max 30% local coal use kar sakta hai aur koi plant 80 se 90% bhi use kar lete hain. Ab humne power aur fuel ke cost dynamics ko to samajh liya ke kaise yeh do factors ek cement company ke profits per ton ko doosri company se 50% se bhi zyada increase kar sakte hain.

Ek aur factor jo ke aapke profits ko market leader bana sakta hai, woh hai different provinces mein limestone mining pe different royalties. KPK mein limestone royalty hai Rs. 350 per ton aur 1.4 ton istemal hota hai ek ton cement banane ke liye yaani ke jo royalty hai uski cost ho gayi Rs. 500 per ton of cement, jabke Punjab mein yeh royalty 6% of selling price ke aas paas hai jo ke ban jati hai roughly 1,000 se 1,100 rupaye per ton, to idhar aap ko Rs. 500 ka extra cost jo hai Punjab mein padh jata hai.

Ab aap apni factory ki location ko change to kar nahi sakte aur na hi aap KPK based companies jo hain unko rok sakte hain ke woh North of Punjab mein aake sale na karein, to aapko yeh cost jo hai woh absorb karni padti hai. So, teen cheezein maine aapko batayein aapne note karni hain jo ke important hain in determining earning power of a cement company: pehla coal, doosra bijli and third royalty on limestone.

Pakistan ki cement industry economy ka ek vital pillar hai, yeh sirf construction ke liye nahi balkay infrastructure aur employment generation ke liye bhi backbone ka kaam karti hai. Cement industry ka GDP contribution taqreeban 1 se 1.5% hai aur indirectly yeh lakhon logon ko rozgar bhi deti hai. Mining, logistics aur retail se lekar exports tak, industry do zones mein divided hai: North Zone, jismein aata hai KPK aur Punjab jahan demand mainly local projects se driven hai, aur South Zone jo exports par depend karta hai. Lekin ek fundamental baat har company ke liye same hai: cement cannot travel. Yeh heavy aur low value product hai jiski freight cost 250 se 300 kilometer ke baad is profit margin ko hi erode kar sakti hai. Isiliye har company ka focus apni regional market mein hi hota hai. So, cement industry ka simple rule hai: sell where you produce, kyunke isko transport karne ki cost per ton itni zyada hoti hai compared to its value per ton ke aap bahut zyada door tak isko travel nahi karwa sakte. Aayein ek aur interesting fact aapke sath share karta hoon about the cement industry.

Agar hum time mein thoda peeche chalein to 2012 mein aap dekhein ek 50 kg cement ka jo bag tha, uski price mein se agar aap saare taxes wagera hata dein to iski price banti thi taqreeban Rs. 300, aur pichle 13 saalon mein Pakistan mein average inflation taqreeban 10 se 11% ki rahi hai. Agar us time se lekar ab tak sirf inflation ke hisab se price badhti to aaj cement ki bag excluding taxes ho jati Rs. 1200, lekin aaj ground reality yeh hai ke cement bag ki price excluding taxes hai Rs. 800 se Rs. 850, so iska matlab hai companies ne over the years investments karke costs ko control kiya hai aur apne profits ko bhi secure kiya hai. Isse yeh samajh aata hai ke cement companies ne apni pricing power banaye rakhi hai matlab cost badhne ke bawajood bhi unhone apne margins protect kiye hain. Yeh sector ki strength dikhata hai ke chahe business capital-intensive hai aur demand upar neeche hoti rehti hai lekin phir bhi cement companies apni profitability long term mein sustain kar sakti hain. Agle kuch saalon mein Pakistan ki cement industry ke liye kuch naye aur interesting developments dekhne ko mil rahe hain jo yeh signal dete hain ke sector sirf construction material tak limited nahi rahega balkay ek multi-dimensional industrial player banne ki taraf ja raha hai.
