To jitni bhi ye financial literacy hai na, iska maqsad ye nahi hai ke maine billionaire banna hai. Iska 99% logon ke liye maqsad ye hota hai ke wo thodi thodi savings jo hai, wo save karein, usko sahi jagah lagayein aur ek time aisa aaye, 10, 15 saalon baad, aapka jo savings hai na, usse jo return aa raha hota hai, wo aapki salary se zyada ho. Aur phir aap azaad ho jaate hain, usko kehte hain financial freedom. Ab jo hai meri umar jo hai taqreeban 40 saal hai, mere paas jo hai acche resources bhi hain, main apni life enjoy bhi kar sakta hoon. Kuch log enjoyment ki taraf chale jaate hain, early retirement le lete hain, kuch log jo apne kaam ko bahut zyada pasand karte hote hain na, wo apna business start kar lete hain usi niche mein. Aur wo unko ek safety hoti hai ke mere paas jo hai na saari jo important cheezein hain life ki, wo secured hain, finances secured hain, aur ab main risk leta hoon, aur us mein bahut zyada log ke phir successful hone ke chances badh jaate hain.
