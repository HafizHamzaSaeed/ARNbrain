Speaker 1: Kitne paise hon kam se kam ke aap is stock market mein kahin laga saken?

ARN: Ab to itne zyada Pakistan mein brokerage offices hain, 5,000 se bhi aapka account khul jata hai. Google pe jayenge, likhenge SIP calculator. Systematic Investment Plan. Yani ke main na faisla kar lun, maine har mahine jo hai na 5,000 invest kar dene hain. To yeh agar aap karte hain na, to iska bhi Pakistan mein jo 18% ka profit hai na har saal, us poori cheez ko machine ko agar chalane lagein na, to result yeh nikalta hai, aap jitne hazar rupaye har mahine isme daal rahe honge stocks par, aur har saal let's say aapki salary barhi 10% se, aapne us amount ko 10% se barha diya har saal. To jitne hazar per mahine honge na, 25 saal baad jab aapki next generation barhi hogi, utne crore honge aapke paas.
