Speaker 1: Agar kisi ne account kholna ho farz karein, iske liye kya kuch chahiye hota hai aur kaise account khol sakte hain? Kya broker jo choose karein, uska bhi koi meyar hona chahiye?

ARN: Broker ka bilkul dhyan rakhna chahiye kyunki ek tarah se wo samjhein aapka custodian hai. Dekhein, iska sabse asaan tareeqa hai ke aap stock exchange ki website pe jayein. Unhone wahan pe likhe hue hain top brokers. Agar aapki investment 10 lakh se kam hogi, to wo aap se bahut zyada document nahi maangein ge. Ye 2022 se shuru hua hai, isko kehte hain asaan account. To 10 lakh tak to sirf shanakhti card pe account khul jata hai. Agar aap is se zyada investment karein ge, to phir wo aap se source of income maangein ge, usme aap apni salary slip bhi de sakte hain, tax return bhi de sakte hain. Is tarah se phir koi limit nahi, aap jitni bhi investment karna chahein, easily kar sakte hain.
