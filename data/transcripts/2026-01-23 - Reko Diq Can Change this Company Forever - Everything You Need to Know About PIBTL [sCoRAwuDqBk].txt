Ports aur terminal ke business ko samajhne ke liye aap ko business ki economics ko samajhna hoga. Ye business economies of scale ke sath operate hota hai. PIBT ka business model high fixed cost model hai. Terminal banane ki cost, equipments, conveyors, unloaders, jetty, salaries, ye sab fixed hoti hain. Chahe aap 2 million ton ek saal mein coal handle karein ya phir 10 million tons. Ye costs more or less constant hi rahengi. Isiliye jitna zyada cargo aayega utni behtar aur effectively fixed cost absorb ho jati hai. PIBT ki coal handling capacity 12 million ton hai aur aap ko shayad pata ho ke ye terminal 300 million dollar ki investment thi 2015 mein. Chalein aap ko exact numbers se business ki economics samjhate hain. PIBT on average har ton coal ke import par 7.5 dollar charge karta hai, jisme se takreeban 35% yaani ke takreeban dhai dollar jo hai woh Port Qasim ki royalty hoti hai. So royalty ke baad PIBT ko roughly har ton par 4.8 dollars per ton revenue milta hai. PIBT ki fixed cost jisme loans pe karza bhi shamil hai, takreeban har saal 15 million dollar hoti hain aur variable cost takreeban 1 dollar per ton. Jaise maine aap ko pehle explain kiya ke ye ek high fixed cost model hai. Yaani ke saari game hai hi usage ki. 4 million ton tak to company ki fixed cost hi cover hoti hain. Aur phir agar coal imports 8 million ton ho jayein to company ka cash flow before tax takreeban 15 million dollar ya phir 4 billion rupees tak chala jata hai. Isko aap aise bhi samajh sakte hain aur main khud bhi ye shortcut use karta hoon ke 4 million ton pe cash flow break-even ho gaya. Aur har mazeed coal import par company ko 4 dollar ki income hogi before income tax. To agar import 6 million ton hue to company ka cash flow before income tax 8 million dollar ho gaya. Aur agar coal import 8 million tons hui ya 10 million tons hui to cash flow before income tax 16 million dollars aur 24 million dollars tak bhi ja sakta hai. Ye hai value us contract ko samajhne ki jo PIBT aur Government of Pakistan ke beech mein hua hua hai aur isko samajhne ke baad profits predict karna kitna simple ho jata hai. Afghanistan border close hone ke baad, KPK ke cement plants aur kuch Punjab-based cement plants jaise ke Kohat, Cherat, Bestway, Lucky Cement aur Fauji Cement ab Afghan coal se imported coal par shift ho rahe hain. Ye sab north-based plants ek saal mein takreeban 25 million ton cement produce karte hain jiske liye teen se saade teen million ton coal required hota hai. So agar agle saal bhi Afghanistan border aise hi open-close hota raha, to easily 1.5 million ton imported coal ki demand badh sakti hai. Pakistan ke south players bhi aaj kal bahut zyada cement export kar rahe hain, jaisa ke aap ne news mein bhi dekha hoga, aur ye sab south-based plants imported coal pe hi chalte hain. Cement industry ke baad, next major coal import hota hai power plants ke liye. Power sector mein next cheapest bijli banane wale power plants jo abhi idle hain, woh hain coal ke power plants, balki imported coal ke power plants. Aur jaise Government of Pakistan incremental bijli package lekar aayi hai agle teen saalon ke liye aur isse electricity demand increase hui, aur jo LNG cargoes ki import karne ki Government of Pakistan ne deal ki hai Qatar se, usse jab LNG se kam bijli banegi to coal ke power plants zyada use honge. But isme ek aur angle bhi hai. Almost sab coal power plants ke paas apni jetty bhi hai coal import karne ke liye aur sirf monsoon season mein ye PIBT ke through coal import karte hain. To shayad agar coal ke power plants zyada bhi chalein to PIBT ko utna zyada fayda na ho.

Aap ne dekha hoga ke recently Reco Diq mining company ne PIBT ke terminal ko Reco Diq project ke gold aur copper concentrates ki export ke liye most preferred terminal declare kar diya hai. PIBT ke terminal ke through Reco Diq har saal 2 billion dollar worth ke gold aur copper export karega. Iske sath sath, Reco Diq mining company PIBT ke terminal ko ready karne ke liye 150 million dollars ki mazeed investment bhi karne ja rahi hai jisse terminal mazeed efficient ban jayega aur storage capacity bhi enhance ho jayegi. PIBT Reco Diq ke first phase mein 8 lakh tons concentrates handle karega, jabke second phase mein 1.8 million tons handle karega aur ye sab 2028 onwards mein expected hai. PIBT ne Reco Diq export concentrate ki per ton fee ya charges abhi tak disclose nahi kiye. Isiliye precisely bataya nahi ja sakta ke iska PIBT ka profit pe impact 2028-2029 onwards kitna hoga. Aur aakhir mein main aap ko batata chaloon ke PIBT 30 years ke liye Port Qasim Authority ke sath Build, Operate and Transfer basis par terminal operate karta hai.
