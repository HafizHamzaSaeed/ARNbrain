Toh shuru karte hain apne pehle concept se, jo hai "Understand the business like owners". Bank ka business toh hai hi logon se paise deposits karwana aur aage loans de dena aur beech mein jitna margin bachta hai woh bank ka profit hota hai. Ab jo loans bank ne dene hain, woh toh interest rate ke aas-paas hi hote hain, aap Government of Pakistan ko dein ya kisi individual ko dein, toh us loan par aapko 10 se 12% ke kareeb hi return aa raha hota hai. Aur agar jo bank deposits leta hai from individual or companies, woh agar current accounts hon toh poora 10 se 12% bank ka profit ho jata hai. Lekin agar yahi deposits savings accounts mein hon aur bank ko un savings accounts par 8 se 9% per saal customer ko profit dena pare, toh is bank ka profit sirf 3% bach jata hai. Kahan 12% aur kahan 3%. Isko mazeed behtar tareeqe se numbers mein understand karne ke liye, let's suppose do banks hain. Dono ke paas 1000 arab rupay ke deposits hain. Aur ek ke current accounts 15% hain yani ke 150 arab rupay aur doosre ke 25% hain yani ke 250 arab rupay. Aur interest rate aaj kal ke hisab se takreeban 10% chal raha hai. Baqi sab cheezein agar same bhi rakhein, sirf jo 100 arab rupay ke extra current accounts hain, us par jab bank ko apne customer ko 8 to 9% return nahi dena jo ke savings accounts mein dena padta hai, us se saal ka 8 se 9 arab rupay ki cost saving ho jati hai. Aur profits sirf 10% current accounts extra hone ki wajah se almost double ho jate hain. Aur Bank of Punjab isi journey pe hai. 15% current accounts jo ke aaj se ek se do saal pehle hote the, inhone agle ek se do saalon mein target rakha hai ke is percentage ko humne 30% tak lekar jaana hai. Aur 23 se 24% tak already pahunch bhi gaye hain. Ye ek major shift hota hai, jaise maine aapko numbers ke saath explain kiya. Ek baar current account mix improve agar ho jaye toh woh bank ka permanent competitive advantage ban jata hai. Aur yahi current accounts ka zyada hona ROIC ko bhi behtar bana deta hai.

So, ab aapko samajh aa gaya hoga ke current account percentage ek bank ke liye kitni important cheez hoti hai aur iska major impact hota hai on profits. Minimum Deposit Rate, MDR, jo bank rate pay karta hai apne depositors ko on savings account, 2024 tak ye rate State Bank ki taraf se decided tha ke jo bhi interest rates chal rahe honge, us se 1.5% kam MDR hoga. So, 2024 mein jab interest rate 17 to 22% ki range mein the aur tezi se neeche aa rahe the, aur sabko pata tha ke interest rate neeche aane hain bahut tezi se, toh hua yun ke interest rate on paper hota tha 22%, magar kyunke sabko pata tha ke ye interest rate bahut jaldi neeche aa jana hai, toh banks asal mein loan de rahe the 15 to 16% ki range mein. Aur MDR tha 20.5%, 22% minus 1.5, toh ismein banks ko on average loss ho raha tha agar unke paas savings account the. Phir, January 2024, ye regulation tamaam conventional banks ke liye remove kar di gayi. Isi wajah se ab sabhi conventional banks ke paas ye opportunity hai ke woh apne depositors ke saath kam rates bhi negotiate kar sakte hain. Aur Bank of Punjab ke paas toh jaise aapne dekha kafi zyada savings account the, cost of deposits ke reduce hone ki wajah se Bank of Punjab ka net interest income ekdam significantly improve ho gaya. Ye koi ek baar ka effect nahi tha, ye long term profitability effect kehlata hai. Spread mein minor si improvement bhi earnings ki base ko permanently improve kar deti hai.

Bank of Punjab ki majority shareholding ki baat karein toh Punjab Government around 57% shares of the bank own karti hai. Bank ke paas is time recent financial reports ke mutabik takreeban 1850 arab rupay ke deposits hain jo ke overall industry ka 5 to 6% share ban jata hai. Aur inmein se takreeban 380 arab rupay current accounts mein hain. Bank ki management recently apne corporate briefing session mein openly target kar chuki hai ke woh end of 2026 tak apne deposits ko 2.3 to 2.4 trillion tak lekar jaane ka plan rakhte hain. Aur current account deposits ki baat karein toh total deposit ka 25% tak yani ke takreeban 550 to 600 billion rupees ka target hai 2026 ke end ka. Bank of Punjab ka deposit engine ab sustainable ho chuka hai, current account mix grow kar raha hai aur 2.3 trillion ka target practically achievable lag raha hai.

Aaiye hum assume karte hain ke 2026 mein average interest rate 10% rahega. Aur Bank of Punjab ke deposits average 2.2 trillion rupees aur current accounts 25% tak pahunch jayein ge, toh is hisab se Bank of Punjab ki net interest income 2026 mein roughly 85 billion rupees ke around ho sakti hai. Aur phir non-interest income, jo bank services ke against fee charge karta hai, bahar se remittances aa rahe hote hain, credit card fees, un sab ko milayein toh woh roughly 2026 mein 25 billion ke around ho sakti hain. Aur 2026 mein Bank of Punjab ke operating expenses roughly ho jayein ge 60 to 65 billion rupees aur profit before tax 40 billion ke aas-paas ho sakta hai. Jaise ke aap jaante hain ke banking sector ka jo tax rate hai, woh har saal 1% se neeche aa raha hai, toh 2026 mein 52% tax rate jab apply hoga, toh 2026 ka profit after tax ban jayega roughly 20 billion rupees. Aur agar usko per share pe convert karein, toh ye ban jaate hain roughly 6 se 6.5 rupees per share.

Fair value ki baat karein toh Pakistan ka banking sector normally jab achhi market chal rahi hoti hai toh 8 to 9 P/E multiples par trade karta hai. Jo banks zyada clean, zyada stable aur jinke current accounts heavy hote hain, woh higher P/E ratios bhi enjoy karte hain. Bank of Punjab ka new profile dekh kar expected hai ke P/E koi 7 to 8 ki range mein ho sakta hai. Agar EPS 6 to 6.5 ki range mein assume karein aur normalized P/E ratio use karein, toh Bank of Punjab ki fair value 2026 mein 45 to 50 ki range mein ho sakti hai. Iske saath-saath dividend bhi future valuation mein crucial role play karta hai, jaise maine aapko shuru mein bataya. Bank of Punjab ki historical payout ratios dekhein toh expected hai ke 2026 mein 3.5 se 4 rupay ka dividend aa sakta hai.

Pehle Bank of Punjab sirf annual dividend pay karta tha. Kyunke iske memorandum of association mein allowed nahi tha semi-annual ya quarterly dividend. Lekin 2024 mein ye amendment ho gayi hai, aur ab bank koshish kar raha hai, communicate bhi kar raha hai, ke hum aahista aahista semi-annual aur quarterly dividend pe jayein ge. Iske saath-saath 2024 ka final dividend bhi aane wala hai, aur bahut zyada log iska wait bhi kar rahe hain. Hamari expectation ke hisab se ye dividend jo hai, 1.5 to 2 rupees per share ki range mein ho sakta hai.
