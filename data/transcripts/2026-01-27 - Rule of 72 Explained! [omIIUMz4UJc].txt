Finance mein na ek hota hai rule of 72. Ziyada tar log sochte hain main 100 rupay laga raha hoon, aur woh na samjhein chaar saal mein agar double hote hain paise, toh woh sochte hain aath saal baad yeh ho jayenge three times. Lekin asal mein rule of 72 hota hai, ke jo saal ka profit aa raha hai na, us ko agar aap 72 se divide karein, toh aap ko pata chal jayega kitne saal mein paise double honge. Ziyada tar log sochte hain agar 20% profit aa raha hai toh paanch saal mein paise double honge, aise nahi hota. Agar aap ka 18% bhi profit aa raha hai na, rule of 72 aap ko kehta hai ke aap ke paise chaar saal mein double honge, kyun ke woh compound hote rehte hain. Toh 25% agar kisi company ka har saal ka profit ho, toh woh teen saal mein aap ke paise double kar dete hain. Toh is tarah ki companies hain maujood Pakistan mein, kaafi saari hain.
