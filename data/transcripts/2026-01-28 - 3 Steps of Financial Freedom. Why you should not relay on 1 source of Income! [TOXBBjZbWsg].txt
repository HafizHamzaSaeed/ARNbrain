First step of financial freedom hai ke aap ke paas na ek emergency fund hona chahiye. Kehte hain ke business karna risky hai, lekin duniya mein sab se zyada risky kaam hai ek particular job pe rely karna jo ke kabhi bhi ja sakti hai. So first step hai ke aap ke paas na takreeban six months aap ki jo salary hai, woh aap ke paas saved honi chahiye. Phir us ke baad alag steps aate hain. Aap let's say ek passion project start karna chahte hain. Aap ne dekha is mein na mujhe apne teen chaar saal invest karne honge, so us bande ke liye phir financial freedom ki definition hoti hai ke mere paas paanch saal ke expenses jo hain na, woh kahin invested hon, taake mujhe passive income aati rahe aur mein apne essential expenses wahan se dekhta rahoon. Phir retirement mein aap sochte hain ke ab maine apni poori zindagi guzar li, ab mere paas itni investments honi chahiye ke meri jo passive income aa rahi hai na, woh mein jab tak zinda rehta hoon, woh mujhe jo hai sahara de. So yeh different aspects hote hain financial freedom ke.
