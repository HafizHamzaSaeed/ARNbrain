Aaiye start karte hain. Rebar ya phir sariya banane ke liye do major costs hoti hain, scrap aur electricity. Scrap primary raw material hai aur quantity terms mein ek ton scrap se taqreeban 0.9 se 0.95 ton rebar ban jati hai. Scrap ki international price around 350 dollar aajkal chal rahi hai aur jab aap ismein freight aur duties add kar lete hain to Pakistan mein landed cost scrap ki taqreeban ek lakh panch hazar rupee per ton ban jati hai. Doosri taraf sabse badi cost power consumption ya phir bijli hai. Ek ton rebar banane ke liye 700 se 800 unit bijli ke chahiye hote hain aur agar aap grid se bijli, jiska rate taqreeban 34 rupee se 37 rupee per unit hai, to ek ton sariya banane ke liye aap ki sirf power cost jo hai wo 27 se 28 hazar rupee per ton ban jati hai. So is tarah scrap aur power milkar production cost ka almost 75% se bhi zyada hissa contribute karte hain. Iske baad depreciation, salaries, aur overheads aate hain aur industry typically 8 se 10% operating margin par operate kar rahi hoti hai. Yani ke agar local rebar ki price dhai lakh rupee chal rahi hai, to operating profit taqreeban 20,000 ke aas-paas hote hain. Ab isko ROIC ke framework mein samajhte hain. Ek char lakh ton per year capacity ka steel plant lagane ke liye rolling mill plus furnace ki cost currency depreciation ke baad taqreeban 12 se 13 arab rupay tak pahonch chuki hai. Iske ilawa working capital, jo aapne do se teen mahine ka dealers ko credit dena hota hai, wo bhi bada component hota hai aur sath hi company ko scrap ki inventory bhi hold karni padti hai. Agar plant let's say 60% utilization pe bhi chal raha hai, yani ke saal ke do lakh chalis hazar ton bana raha hai, to aap ko har time pe 40,000 se 60,000 ton scrap hold karna padta hai jiski value taqreeban 5 arab rupay ban jati hai, to aapko ye working capital bhi chahiye hota hai is business ko operate karne ke liye. To total capital required to run the business roughly 18 se 20 arab rupay tak pahonch jata hai. Operating profit agar 20,000 rupee per ton assume karein aur after tax jo ke 12 se 13 hazar rupee ho jata hai, to annual profit roughly is plant ka teen arab rupee ban jata hai aur ROIC ho gaya around 15%.
