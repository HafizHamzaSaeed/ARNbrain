financial freedom ke outcomes bohot zyada ho sakte hain har kisi ke alag ho sakte hain. lekin us ke peeche jo do pillars khade hue hain, ek hai independence aur doosra hai purpose. jab aapko independence milega apni job se ya aapko let's say aap koi business karte hain. aap ka ek do clients aise hain ke wahi aapka business chalate hain lekin aapko maza nahi aata un ke sath kaam karne mein. bas job type feeling aati hai ke roz mujhe jaa ke apne ghante dene hain, baat bhi mujhse sahi tareeqe se nahi karte, payments time pe nahi aatin. to aap ke paas independence nahi hai, even though aapka business apna hai. jab aap ek dafa independence achieve kar lete hain phir aap sochte hain ke mera duniya mein aane ka na koi maqsad tha aur woh zaroori nahi hai ke aap ne duniya badalni ho. us ka maqsad yeh bhi ho sakta hai ke aap ne apni family ko protect karna hai, un ko ek acchi life deni hai, apni next generation ko accha bada karna hai taake woh koi cheez create karein. first ek independence achieve karenge to phir hi aap apne purpose pe kaam kar sakte hain. aur is ke liye hum logon ne financial freedom movement bhi Pakistan ki first start ki hai.
