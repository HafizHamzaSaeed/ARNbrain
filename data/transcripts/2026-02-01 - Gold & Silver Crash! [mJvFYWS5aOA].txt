Assalamualaikum everyone. Umeed hai sab khairiat se honge. Last week kafi zyada volatile raha financial markets mein to maine socha hamare saare members ke liye ek guidance aur kis tareeqe se aise time period ko counter karne ka best tareeqa kya hota hai, uspe ek voice note jo hai wo sabke saath share karte hain. Aap sab ne dekha hoga kal parso kis tareeqe se gold aur silver ne crash kiya. Pichle hafte mein stocks mein bhi thodi boht volatility rahi. Pehle thoda sa decline bhi aaya but recovery bhi boht zyada fast thi. Aur January ka mahina still ek bullish strong month close hua PSX ke liye. To in saari cheezon ko specially jo new investors hote hain ya jo purane bhi hote hain, kafi time se kar rahe hote hain lekin bilkul confuse ho jaate hain is tarah ka koi events hota hai. Headlines jis tareeqe ki aati hain, wo bhi koi help nahi kartiin balki aur bande ko confuse kar deti hain. To ismein maine bhi apne experience mein seekha hai aur sab ko bhi main ye advice karta hoon ke aapne frameworks aur systems ke hisab se sochna hai. Har cheez ka ek system hota hai, ek framework hota hai. Jaise ke PSX ka ek bull market framework hai. Ab uske andar ye nahi hota ke ek din ya kisi ek mahine agar koi decline aa gaya to iska matlab hai ke wo framework toot gaya hai. Kuch cheezein hoti hain jo us framework ko banati hain aur jab tak wo cheezein theek rehti hain, is tarah ki agar koi volatility bhi aati hai to wo aapko ek tarah se tang kar rahi hoti hai, aapko challenge kar rahi hoti hai, aapko test kar rahi hoti hai ke aap jo hai bas ek novice se investor hain ya waqayi mein aapke paas ek poora framework ek system hai jisko lekar aap phir invest kar rahe hain. To agar aap dekhein pichle do saalon se ek poora hum logo ne lecture bhi share kiya hua hai apne YouTube channel pe ke PSX ka bull market framework hai kya, usmein kaun kaun si cheezein hoti hain. Wo aap aaj bhi dekhenge aaj bhi wo intact hai aur abhi kuch din pehle hi ek podcast pe bhi ispe detailed discussion hui thi ke 2026 ka outlook kaisa hai PSX ka. To jab tak wo saari cheezein intact hoti hain, ye saare jo events hote hain ye aapko shake karne ke liye hote hain. Ye aapko ghabrahat dene ke liye hote hain, aapko test kar rahe hote hain. Aap khud sochein ke duniya bhar mein even developed markets mein bhi jo log invest kar rahe hote hain, usmein se 95% log speculators hote hain. Sirf 5% log value investors hote hain jo ke systems ke saath invest kar rahe hote hain. To agar to jaise itni massive koi bull market chal bhi rahi hoti hai wo gold mein ho ya wo PSX mein ho ya kisi aur asset class mein ho, agar to itna easy ho aur usmein roz hi thoda thoda increase aata rahe to har koi isse faida utha le. Lekin har koi faida nahi utha sakta kyunki uske paas systems nahi hote. To PSX ka framework jo hai humne apne YouTube channel pe share kiya hua hai, recently ek podcast mein bhi share kiya hai. Isi tareeqe se gold ka bhi ek framework hai. Gold ka bhi ek poora framework hai jo ke aapko batata hai ke abhi ismein sahi time hai invest karne ka ke nahi aur ye maine recently ek Yasir Janjua ke podcast mein bhi jo hai share kiya tha. Phir abhi recently Aftab Iqbal saab ke saath ek podcast kiya tha, usmein bhi share kiya tha ke kya cheezein hain jo ke gold ko bullish banati hain, ab bas aapne wohi check karna hota hai ke abhi tak agar wo intact hain to phir ye drop, ye increase ye sab jo hai, ye sirf aapko jo hai ek tarah se ignore karna hota hai, absorb karna hota hai. Jab tak wo saara framework intact rehta hai, to aapko bhi in cheezon mein invest karte rehna chahiye. To lesson ye hai ke panic bilkul nahi karna. Panic karne se saare decisions kharab honge aur main aapko bataya hai ke 5% log hi value investors hote hain, 95% log bas aise hi panic ke saath chal rahe hote hain. Kabhi unko faida ho jata hai, kabhi unko nuksaan ho jata hai. Aur is 5% category mein aane ke liye aapko system seekhne hote hain aur ye itne mushkil nahi hote. Jo do teen maine references diye hain, aap wohi dekhenge, usse hi aapke systems boht zyada strong ho jayenge. Ye systems aapko batate hain ke koi ek asset class agle ek do saal ke liye bullish rahegi aur aapne ek do saal ke hisab se best investments karni hoti hain. Ek do hafton ke baare mein nahi sochna hota. Phir ab thodi si baat kar lete hain agle ek do hafton ka jo main theme hoga, US aur Iran ki situation. Ab uska bhi jo system hai jab ye event bilkul as it is thode mahine pehle Iran aur Israel war pe hua tha to humne ek voice note share kiya tha ke is type of event ka kya framework hota hai. To wo aapko bilkul usko review karein aur dekhein ke usko aapne kis tareeqe se tackle karna hai. Again, wo jo 5% category hai value investors ki wahan aane ki koshish karein. Aur isko dekhein sahi tareeqe se ke Pakistan ka impact oil price increase hone ki wajah se kya hota hai. US President ka kya record hai ab to unko almost do saal ho gaye hain, to agar koi bhi war hoti hai to wo lambi chalti hai ya jaldi khatam ho jati hai, usmein aapko saari cheezein kaise, kis tareeqe se manage karni chahiye. Iske alawa phir ek aur boht ghabrahat jo logo ko deti hai ke India aur Pakistan ki war hui thi aur India poori tayari mein hai ke phir se kabhi is tarah ka koi event ho, to iska bhi ek poora framework hai. Aur wo abhi recently jaise maine bataya Aftab Iqbal saab ke saath jo podcast hua, usmein humne detail discussion ki. To bottom line ye hai ke in saare events ke apne apne frameworks hote hain aur aapne jo hai wo framework seekhne hote hain. Jab aap wo framework seekhenge, aapki ghabrahat bhi kam ho jayegi, aap acche decisions lenge aur aap ek value investor ban jayenge. Abhi ye saari jo volatility chal rahi hoti hai, aage bhi boht zyada opportunities aayengi. Jo agar aap sochte hain ke pichle ek do saal mein boht acche returns aaye, aage aayenge ke nahi, aage bhi isi tareeqe se opportunities aati rahengi lekin opportunities ka faida wohi uthata hai jiske frameworks strong hote hain. To aaj ka mera aapko ye message hoga ke aap apne systems aur frameworks jo hain, usko strong karein aur ye jo volatility ka period aata hai aur waise bhi apni financial literacy ko improve karne ke liye, acchi investments karne ke liye future ke liye, jab aap ye systems seekh jayenge, aap khud, aapki ghabrahat bhi kam ho jayegi aur aap khud dekhenge aap ek acche investor ban jayenge. Thank you.
