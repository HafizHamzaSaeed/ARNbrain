Boht zyada log na start kar dete hain, let's say unko kisi ne kaha ke aapko na emergency fund hona zaroori hai usse financial freedom achieve ho jayega. Aur do teen mahine guzarte hain, koi aur banda unko milta hai unka dost, wo kehta hai ke nahi nahi aapke paas to jo hai das saal ke paise hone chahiye, wo fir confuse ho jate hain. Aap mujh se agar poochenge na ke aap paise save karna chahte hain, financial freedom achieve karna chahte hain, sab kahenge yes. Fir aap kahenge aap kuch kar rahe hain financial freedom achieve karne ke liye, usme bhi almost 95% log yes karenge. Fir jab third question aata hai na ke aap list down karein ke aap kya kya kar rahe hain, wo boht zyada logon ka blank hota hai. To har koi karna chahta hai, thoda boht kuch kar bhi raha hota hai, lekin jo usko actual guidance nahi mil rahi hoti na uski wajah se, wo jo goal hota hai wo achieve nahi hota. So is movement ka poora maqsad hi yehi hai ke logon ko ek detailed breakdown mile ke financial freedom achieve kar ke aapki productivity boht zyada barh jati hai. Jab ek dafa aapke sar ke upar se stress nikal jaye to aap khud sochein, aap kitna zyada productively nayi cheezein create kar sakte hain, apna business create kar sakte hain, ya agar aap life enjoy karna chahte hain, aap wo bhi kar sakte hain.
