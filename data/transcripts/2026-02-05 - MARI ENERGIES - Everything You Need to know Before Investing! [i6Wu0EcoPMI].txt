And 90% of this gas is used to make fertilizer. Jis ki wajah se Pakistan mein sasta urea farmers ko milta hai aur Pakistan ki food security maintain rehti hai. Mari Field ki gas pricing two-tier structure par based hai jo company ki overall economics ko naturally ek growth company mein convert kar deti hai. 525 mmscfd tak ki production Tier 1 mein shamil hoti hai, jabki Tier 2 wo additional production hai jo 525 mmscfd se upar hoti hai. Tier 1 production pe Mari Energies ko purani low gas pricing milti hai, jabki Tier 2 par latest petroleum policy ki higher price apply hoti hai. Aur ye kuch aise hua ki 2013 aur 2014 mein jab new PML-N ki government aayi, to unhone dekha ki Pakistan mein bijli aur gas ki bohot zyada shortage chal rahi hai, load shedding ho rahi hai, industry band ho rahi hai. To unhone dekha ki ek option hai ki aap baahar se LNG import kar lein jo ke 10 se 12 dollar per mmbtu aati thi. Aur ek hai ki existing local companies ko hi incentivize kiya jaye aur unse kaha jaye ki agar aap apni bhale purani fields hon, us mein aap uski gas ki production increase karenge, to hum aap ko latest petroleum policy ke hisab se 4.5 se 6 dollar ke beech mein aap ko price de denge. Aur obviously, ye bilkul theek policy thi, kyunki baahar ki LNG ke muqable mein local gas jo hai, wo kaafi zyada sasti pad rahi thi. So, isi tareeqe se us waqt Mari gas field taqreeban 500 mmscfd produce karti thi, to Tier 2, 525 mmscfd se upar ki production pe Mari field ko latest policy ke hisab se price milne lag gayi gas ki. Tier 1, Tier 2 ko to aapne samajh liya, lekin iska impact kaisa hai? Aaiye main aap ko is policy ka company ke profits par impact kya hota hai, samajhata hoon. Tier 1 mein gas ki price hai taqreeban 1.5 dollar per mmbtu, jabki operating cost is field ki on average taqreeban 0.5 dollar per mmbtu hai. Kyunki Mari Field ek bohot hi purani field hai, isliye royalty pichle saal se 12.5% se barha kar 27.5% already ho chuki hai. Jiska Tier 1 gas par impact taqreeban royalty ka ban jata hai 0.4 dollars per unit, jabki income tax taqreeban 0.3 dollars ban jata hai. So, Tier 1 production se net profit per mmbtu roughly 0.3 dollars ban jata hai. Aur asal value creation Tier 2 production se hoti hai. Tier 2 gas par Mari Energies ko 5 to 6 dollars per mmbtu milte hain, jabki operating cost yahan wahi 0.5 dollars rehti hai. Ye ek critical point hai. Cost similar hai, lekin price multiple times zyada. 27.5% royalty Tier 2 gas par taqreeban 1.6 dollars per unit ban jati hai aur income tax roughly 1.5 dollars per unit. In sab ke baad, Tier 2 gas production ka net profit ban jata hai roughly 2.3 dollars per unit. Comparison bilkul clear hai. Tier 1 profit per unit 0.3 dollars, Tier 2 profit per unit 2.3 dollars. Gas price to Tier 1 aur Tier 2 mein 3 to 4 times ka farq hai, lekin profit per unit 7 to 8 times. Yaani jitna profit 500 mmscfd ki production par hota stood, wo profit jo hai double ho gaya jab company ne 600 ki production achieve ki, kyunki per unit profit bohot zyada hai. Aur aaj company 800 se 850 mmscfd production kar rahi hai. Isi policy ki wajah se, jaise jaise company grow kar rahi hai, earnings aur zyada speed se badh rahi hain. Ye hai Mari Energies ki last 10 years ki amazing growth ka main reason, two-tiered pricing system. Do mazeed reasons jo Mari field ko Pakistan ki number one gas field banate hain, economically, wo hain superior field economics aur cash-paying customers. Mari field ki average drilling depth sirf 1,000 se 1,200 meter hai, jabki local industry ki average taqreeban 3,000 meter hai, jis se cost aur investment needed in drilling dono low rehti hain. Aur phir exploration success ratio, 60 to 70% hai versus industry average of 35%. Yaani ke kam depth pe gas bhi mil jati hai, aur average ke muqable mein double chances hain of finding gas, aur oil and gas mein to aap ko pata hai major investment hai hi drilling ki. Aur second important reason hai, company ke paas superior customers hain aur strong cash flow discipline. PPL aur OGDC ke receivables aapne dekha hoga ke almost 500-600 arab rupay tak pahonch gaye hain, jabki Mari ke receivables negligible hain. Kyunki in ke primary customers fertilizer sector se hain, jahan demand stable hoti hai aur payments timely ho jati hain. Isee liye, Mari Energies circular debt ki daldal se abhi tak bachi hui hai. Aur aage bhi jo latest developments hui hain, mazeed fertilizer plants Mari Energies ke customers banne ja rahe hain, us se aisa lag raha hai ke future mein bhi is company ko circular debt se related problems jo hain, wo kaafi kam hongi.
