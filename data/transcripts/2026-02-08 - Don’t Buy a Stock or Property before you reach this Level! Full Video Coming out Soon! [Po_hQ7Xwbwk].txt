Aap samjhein do lakh rupay ke expenses aapko required hain ek mahine ke. Kuch logon ke liye ye current expenses bhi honge aur kuch logon ke liye aaj se teen char saal panch saal baad ka ek estimated expense bhi ho sakta hai. Pacchis lakh rupay ki saving jo hai na wo aapko level one pe le aayegi. Ab agar aapki isse zyada expenses hain ya kam hain, aap isko adjust kar sakte hain. Is level mein aapne investing ke baare mein bilkul nahi sochna. Koi zaroorat nahi hai kisi share purchase karne ki ya koi property khareedne ki, kyunki pehle aapne security lekar aani hai apne finances mein aur apne mind ke liye taake aap apni job pe sahi tareeqe se focus kar sakein. Is stage pe jo hai insurance ek badi important cheez ho sakti hai. Kyunki aap jo cheez chah rahe hain wo hai ke ek mera mind jo hai na secure ho jaye, to health insurance, life insurance taake aapki family protected rahe, ye bade ache financial products ho
