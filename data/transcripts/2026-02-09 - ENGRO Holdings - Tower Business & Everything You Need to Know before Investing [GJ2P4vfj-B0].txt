Engro ka tower business asal mein real estate jaisa hai, farq sirf itna hai ke ye zameen ya buildings rent par dene ke bajaye telecom towers rent par dete hain. Engro Enfrashare towers ki tameer, unki power supply, security aur maintenance par badi initial investment karte hain aur phir mobile network operators jaise ke Jazz, Zong, in sab ko long-term contracts par ye infrastructure rent pe de dete hain. Mobile companies apna signal dene wala equipment khud laati hain, jabke Engro sirf passive infrastructure provide karta hai. Is business ki beauty ye hai ke ek tower ka kharcha ek dafa mein ho jata hai lekin usi tower se rent saalon saal tak aata rehta hai aur har saal incremental growth bhi hoti rehti hai rent mein. Ab baat karte hain us mashhoor 560 million dollar deal ki, jis mein Engro Connect ne Jazz ki tower subsidiary Deodar ko acquire kiya. Deodar ke taqreeban 10,500 towers Engro Enfrashare ke control mein aa gaye. Is acquisition se pehle Engro Enfrashare ke paas already taqreeban 4,000 towers maujood the, aur Deodar ke towers add hone ke baad total tower count taqreeban 14,500 ke kareeb pahunch gaya hai. Tower business mein sabse bada profit ka driver tenancy ratio hota hai, yaani ek tower par average kitne tenants maujood hain. Is waqt Engro Enfrashare ka average tenancy ratio around 1.2 hai. Tower ki construction, power aur security ka kharcha pehle hi lag chuka hota hai, isiliye agar ek hi tower par doosra ya teesra tenant aa jaye to additional revenue mostly seedha profit mein convert ho jata hai. Isiliye kehte hain ke tower business mein tenancy ratio badhao aur profit khud ba khud badh jayega. Pakistan mein abhi tenancy ratio global average jo ke 2.2 hai, us se kaafi zyada kam hai, jis se ye pata chalta hai ke abhi bhi bahut growth ka margin hai. Aaye ab is business aur is deal ka ROI calculate karne ki koshish karte hain. On average, agar aap ek complete telecom tower lagane jayenge jis mein active radio equipment, spectrum aur network system bhi shamil hon to iski cost 2 lakh dollar se aadha million dollar tak bhi chali jaati hai, lekin kyunki Engro Enfrashare sirf passive infrastructure own karta hai, isliye ek tower ki cost taqreeban 60,000 dollars ke aas paas hoti hai yaani ke roughly 2 crore rupaye. Isi tarah agar revenue ki baat ki jaye to sirf infrastructure leasing se ek tower se mahana taqreeban dhaaee lakh rupaye rent aa jata hai, agar tower sirf ek tenant ko leased ho to salana revenue ban gaya taqreeban 30 lakh rupaye. Is business mein depreciation ke elawa aur agar aapne bank se koi loan nahi liya hua to as such koi major expense nahi hota, isiliye maine aapko kaha hai ke ye real estate business ki tarah hota hai. So pre-tax ROI is business ka ban jata hai roughly 15% ki range mein. Ab do cheezein jo is ROI ko increase kar sakti hain, wo hain ek to electricity costs kam karne ke liye maximum solar panels install karna, aur doosra ek hi tower par doosra customer add karna. Agle kuch saalon mein agar Engro ka tenancy ratio 1.2 se 1.5, 1.6 ki range tak pahunch gaya to ROI is deal ka automatically increase ho jayega. Asal farq ye hai ke Telenor aur PTCL ki deal ek poori telecom company ki deal hai, jis mein customers, spectrum licenses aur network equipment sab shamil tha, jabke Engro Enfrashare ki deal sirf infrastructure ki deal hai, isiliye is business ko revenue per user ke bajaye tenancy ratio aur per tower economics se judge karna chahiye.
