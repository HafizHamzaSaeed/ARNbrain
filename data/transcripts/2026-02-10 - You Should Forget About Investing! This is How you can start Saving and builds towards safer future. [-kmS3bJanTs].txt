Ab level one wahan aata hai jahan aapne ek emergency fund create kar liya. Ab emergency fund alag alag logon ki alag alag definition hai. Meri definition hai 12 months of expenses. To jab aapke paas itni saving ho jayegi, phir behtari aayegi zindagi mein, sense of security aayegi. Yeh hoga ke chalo koi aisi emergency aayi, meri job chali gayi, to mere paas chheh mahine ke expenses plus one big emergency ka fund maujood hai.
