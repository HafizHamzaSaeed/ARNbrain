Bohat saare log asal mein financial stability ko isliye nahi pohanch paate kyunke unke paas na clarity nahi hoti. Yeh masla hai clarity ka. Aap ke paas plan nahi hota.

Yeh jo financial freedom hai na, yeh agar koi bhi insaan samajhta hai ke woh akayla achieve kar sakta hai, toh woh apne aap ko bilkul dhokay ke andar rakha hua hai.

Jab tak aap ka partner aap ke saath is mission mein nahi hai, aap kuch bhi kar lein, aap is mission mein kaamyab nahi ho sakte.

Log samajhte hain budgeting ya to koi bohat ghareeb logon ke liye... main to jab itne paise bana leta hoon ke mere thode paise bach bhi jaate hain, to budgeting mere liye nahi hai. Halanke aisa bilkul nahi hai. Budgeting is for everyone.
