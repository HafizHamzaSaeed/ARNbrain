Delayed gratification se related aik bohat achi cheez batata hoon. Aap sab ne suna hoga marshmallow experiment. Kuch saal ke bachay jinko ye sab kuch nahi pata hota hai ke aage life mein kya hona hai, unke sath aik poora experiment kiya gaya. Unke samne rakhe gaye marshmallow. Aur unko bataya gaya ke agar aap abhi marshmallow nahi khayenge na, to thore time baad aapko do milenge. Jin bachon ne wo resist kar liya, wo zindagi mein aage ja ke kamyab log ban gaye. Yaani ke unki percentage of success jo thi wo bohat zyada thi compared to jin logon ne kaha ke nahi nahi, aik mil raha hai, kal kisne dekha hai, pata nahi jo batane wala hai wo dega bhi ke nahi to abhi le lete hain jo mil raha hai. To matlab ye itni jo hai na, delayed gratification itni powerful skill hai. Is level pe aapne delayed gratification, invest in yourself aur communication skills, bas! Iske ilawa koi zindagi nahi hai abhi. Zindagi jo hai wo baad mein khud hi improve ho jayegi jab aap ye achieve kar len.
