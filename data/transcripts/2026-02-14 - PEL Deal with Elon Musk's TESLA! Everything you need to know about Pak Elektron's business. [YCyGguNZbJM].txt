Sab se pehle Pak Elektron Limited ke business ko samajhte hain. Company basically do major segments mein operate karti hai. Ek taraf home appliances ka business hai jis mein refrigerators, air conditioners, deep freezers aur doosri home appliances shamil hain jo company ki total sales ka taqreeban 60% contribute karti hain. Aur is segment ke top-selling products hain ACs aur refrigerators. Aadhi se zyada sale appliances ki refrigerators aur deep freezers ke through aati hai. Har panchwa fridge ya refrigerator Pakistan mein PEL ka sale hota hai. Aur Pakistan mein har daswa AC bhi PEL brand ka hota hai. Doosri taraf hai power infrastructure ka segment jis mein energy meters, transformers, switchgears aur electrical components aate hain. Aur yeh segment taqreeban 40% sales generate karta hai. Yeh segment kaafi diverse hai, chand hazar rupay ke energy meter se lekar karoron rupay ke transformers is segment mein aa jaate hain. Large power transformers mein PEL ka market share bahut zyada hai. Is segment ko yeh dominate karte hain aur 90% tak inka market share chala jata hai.

Aaiye ab in dono businesses ki economics samajhte hain. Appliances ka business asal mein dealers ke shop system par run karta hai. Zyada tar dealers apni shops par borrowed inventory rakhte hain yaani saman company se credit par uthaya jata hai. Normal economic cycle mein dealers do se dhai mahine ke andar company ko payment kar dete hain. Lekin jab cycle slow hota hai, logon ki purchasing power kam hoti hai, jaise ke 2022 ke baad hua, to yahi payment cycle char se panch mahine tak stretch ho jata hai. Is ka seedha asar company ke cash flows par padta hai kyunke raw material aur operational kharche foran pay karne hote hain jabke cash late mil raha hota hai. Aur last few years mein jab import restrictions lagni shuru hueen aur sath-sath devaluation ki wajah se imported kits ki prices sharply barh gaeen aur appliances ki prices mein dramatic change aa gaya, so 2023 aur 2024 aise saal ho gaye jahan price increase ki wajah se sales kam ho gaeen aur yeh payment cycle company ke against chala gaya.

Aur inhi saalon mein Pakistan ne apni history ke highest interest rates experience kiye. 2021 mein interest rate 7% tha jo ke barhte-barhte 2024 tak 22% tak pahunch gaya. So dealers se payment bhi der se milti thi aur us ke upar bank charges teen guna ho gaye. Yahan ek important baat samjhana zaroori hai. Is dauran PEL ke gross margins itne bure nahi the. Product bechne par company theek paise kama rahi thi, lekin jo sara profit tha woh finance cost ki wajah se nuksaan mein badal raha tha. Isi stage par management ko realize hua ke char se panch mahine ka credit cycle sustainable nahi hai. Interest rate to pata nahi kab neeche aayenge, business model mein dealers credit ko kam karna parega. Isi liye company ne working capital management pe focus karna shuru kiya. Aur aaj situation kaafi had tak improve ho chuki hai. Working capital cycle char se panch mahine se reduce ho kar do se teen mahine ke darmiyan aa chuka hai. Is ke sath-sath interest rate bhi 22% se gir kar saade 10% tak aa gaye hain. In dono cheezon ka combined effect yeh hua ke PEL ki finance cost aadhi se bhi kam reh gayi hai.

Appliances manufacturing mein steel ek key input hai aur steel prices mein commodity super cycle khatam hone ke baad jo decline aaya hai, us ne cost pressure ko kaafi had tak kam kar diya hai jis se gross margins bhi behtar ho rahe hain. Is ke sath-sath PEL ne localization strategy par kaam shuru kiya hai. Management ke mutabiq top-selling products mein refrigerators ke almost 50% components aur ACs aur ovens ke 10 to 15% components ab locally manufacture ho rahe hain jis se dollar devaluation ka impact kam ho jayega.

Aur phir aati hai woh development jo is poori kahani ko local turnaround se aage le jati hai. Management ke mutabiq PEL ne 2025 mein Tesla ke sath agreement sign kiya hai jis mein company 50 million dollars worth yaani ke 14 billion rupees ke power appliances khas taur par distribution transformers export karegi in 2026. Aur is ke sath-sath company mazeed international buyers ko bhi explore kar rahi hai USA mein taake apne global footprint aur export base ko aur expand kiya ja sake.

Company ki management expect kar rahi hai ke home appliances ki market agle saal jaise-jaise economic recovery hogi, last year ki tarah around 20% recover karegi. Aur PEL brand ki home appliances segment ki growth bhi management around 25% tak expect kar rahi hai.

Ab baat karte hain power segment ki aur zyada tar investors sirf home appliances business ko dekh kar PEL ko judge karte hain jab ke jaise main aap ko abhi bataya, asal development aaj kal power appliances ke segment mein ho rahi hain. Power appliances wali side par PEL ka energy meter business agle saal mein do guna tak grow karne ka strong chance rakhta hai. Jahan company ki energy meter sales taqreeban rupees 4.5 billion se barh kar 9 billion tak ja sakti hai. Mainly is liye ke ab tamam DISCOs ko existing single phase meters ko replace karna hoga aur unhein two phase, AMR aur three phase, AMR smart meters install karne ka mandate mil chuka hai. Is change ka seedha impact yeh hoga ke simple conventional meters ki jagah automated meter reading aur smart meter technology ki demand bahut tezi se barhegi, jo traditional metering se zyada advanced, accurate aur theft reduction mein effective hote hain.

Power segment ke exports angle ko dekhein to PEL ki management ke targets hain ke 2026 mein 50 se 100 million dollar ki exports ki jayein. In sab assumptions ko dekhte hue ab agar hum PEL ki calendar year 2026 ki projections ki baat karein to revenue, exports aur local sales ko mila kar around 74 to 75 billion rupees tak expect kiya ja sakta hai. Is revenue ke muqable mein cost of sales almost 55 billion rupees estimate ki ja rahi hain jis mein raw material, manufacturing aur energy cost shamil hain. Is ke baad PEL ka gross margin taqreeban 20 billion rupees ke aas-paas ban jata hai aur management ka kehna hai ke USA exports pe margins kaafi achhe hain. Is ke baad phir aa jata hai selling aur distribution expenses jo dealers, logistics aur marketing se link hote hain, taqreeban 4 billion rupees tak estimate kiye ja rahe hain. Is ke sath-sath admin expenses jo management ki salaries, offices aur daily operations se related hote hain, almost 3 to 3.5 billion rupees rehne ki expectations hain. Finance cost jo interest rates ke neeche aane aur better cash flows ki wajah se sirf roughly 2 se 2.5 billion rupees ke kareeb ho sakte hain. Aur is mein ek mazeed positive development hui hai aur woh yeh hai ke Government of Pakistan ne exporters ke liye interest rate ko 4.5% kar diya hai.

Ab aap soch rahe honge ke how a small company from Pakistan is exporting to Tesla? Iska jawab hai ke aap sab ko China aur US ki trade war ka to pata hoga jis ke baad duniya bhar mein tariffs wali kahani shuru hui. Is trade war ka asar sirf China aur America tak limited nahi raha, balki slowly-slowly global supply chains aur exporting economies bhi iski lapet mein aa gaeen. Aaj situation yeh hai ke jab global buyers power appliances source karte hain to woh sirf price nahi balki tariffs, delivery times aur supply reliability ko bhi bahut zyada importance dete hain. Yahin par Pakistan aur khas taur par PEL ko ek clear advantage mil jata hai. Pakistan se power appliances export karne par tariffs comparatively kam hain, specially jab aap ise compare karein Vietnam aur China se. Iska seedha matlab yeh hai ke Pakistani suppliers international buyers ke liye zyada cost-effective ban jate hain. Is ke sath-sath PEL ka delivery time bahut competitive hai. Jahan bahut se international suppliers ko transformer ke orders complete karne mein 18 se 24 mahine ka time lag raha hai, wahin PEL ka average delivery time taqreeban chhay se nau mahine hai. Global buyers jis mein America ke buyers bhi aa jate hain, unke liye yeh delivery time bahut matter karta hai kyunke utilities aur power companies ke projects time-sensitive hote hain. Isi wajah se aaj PEL ko international market mein ek preferred supplier ke taur par dekha ja raha hai.

Ab aap soch rahe honge ke China, Vietnam in jagahon pe to itna bada infrastructure hai to inke delivery times jo hain woh itne zyada kyun hain? Aur Pakistan ke delivery times kyun kam hain? Aur USA se itni zyada transformers ki ek dam se demand kaise aa gayi? To iska answer hai ke duniya bhar mein jo electricity grid hai, woh transform ho raha hai because of new renewable energies. Jaise-jaise electricity grid mein solar aur wind energy incorporate ho rahi hai to jo transformers hote hain unko upgrade karne ki zaroorat hai. Aur ab aap khud sochein ke America mein upgradation ka jo safar hai, woh kitne saalon tak chalta rahega. Sab utilities ko apne transformers ko upgrade karna hoga apni services ko maintain karne ke liye. Aur jab yeh companies apne in transformers ke order lekar duniya mein jaati hain, India, China, to unko pata chalta hai ke un mulkon mein bhi yeh same trend chal raha hai. Wahan pe bhi jo electricity grid hai, woh renewables ko incorporate karne ke liye un companies ki order book local orders se already full hain. To is wajah se unka delivery time jo hai woh 18 se 24 mahine tak gaya hua hai.

Transformer business ki capacity expansion ke hawale se ek aur important development yeh hai ke company is waqt ek mazeed US certification ke milne ka intezar kar rahi hai. Jaise hi yeh certification mil jaati hai, PEL ko United States ki government-owned companies ko directly products supply karne ka right mil jayega. Abhi tak company sirf USA ke private customers ko sell kar rahi hai. Lekin yeh certification milne ke baad government utilities aur public sector ke large, long-term contracts ka rasta khul jayega. Isi wajah se company ne is certification ke milne ke basis pe 2026 mein 50 se 100 million dollar ka export target rakha hua hai. Aur exports ka ek aur bada benefit yeh hai company ke liye ke kuch revenue foreign currency mein generate hona shuru ho jayega jo local economic cycles aur currency volatility ke muqable mein PEL ko thodi stability aur protection farahm karega.
