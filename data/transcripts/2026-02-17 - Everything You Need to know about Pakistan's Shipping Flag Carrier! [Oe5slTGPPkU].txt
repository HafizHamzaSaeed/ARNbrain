2022 mein jab Corona virus ke baad economies reopen huin aur logon ne stock piling shuru ki, to us time pe excess demand aur too little supply ki wajah se freight rates spike kar gaye. Aur phir thoda hi arsa guzra tha ke Russia Ukraine war ki wajah se global oil tankers shortage ho gayi aur oil tankers ke per day rates double ho gaye. Asal mein hua kuch yun ke jab Russian oil aur Russian fleets par Western sanctions lage to Russia ne apna oil India, China aur baqi Russian allies ko supply karna shuru kar diya. Commodity to same transport ho rahi thi lekin usne jitna travel karna tha, jisko kehte hain ton miles, wo bahut zyada barh gaye. Aap isko yun samjhein ke Russia se Europe agar crude oil supply kiya jaye Baltic Sea ke through, 5 se 10 din lagte hain, lekin agar wahi same oil India ko supply kiya jaye South China Sea ke through to usme 25 se 35 din lagte hain. To commodity to wahi same thi lekin distance usko three times travel karna parh raha tha. Isse ships ki supply kam ho gayi aur demand zyada thi, iski wajah se freight rates spike kar gaye.

Ab aap soch rahe honge ke log double rates par ships ko kyun charter kar rahe hain? Itni kya zaroorat hai mehanga freight pay karne ki? Uska wajah ye hai ke agar aap dekhein, ek oil jo aap buy karte hain, ek barrel jiski cost takreeban $60 se $70 hoti hai, us pe aap freight normal situation mein $5 pay karte hain. To agar aapko double freight bhi pay karna pare, $10 per barrel, lekin agar aapko oil pe $20 se $30 jo hai, discount mil raha ho, to all-in-all ye jo saare oil importing countries hain, India, China, inko extra freight mila kar bhi sasta oil apne mulk tak pohanch raha tha. Iske saath saath, Russian ships par sanctions lagne ki wajah se takreeban 5 to 10% global ships waise hi system se out ho gayin, jisne rates ko mazeed barha diya.

Shipping companies ka business kaafi cyclical hota hai. Ismein freight rates jo ke kaafi volatile hote hain, geopolitical factors, sanctions par depend karte hain, lekin ultimate deciding factor hota hai distance traveled by ships, na ke poori duniya mein oil kitna istemal ho raha hai. Oil ka use agar kam bhi ho lekin agar uska distance travel barh jayega oil tankers ka to freight rates upar chale jayenge. To aajkal jaise USA sabko force kar raha hai ke humse directly oil buy karein, to aap khud sochein isse distance travel pe kitna zyada affect aa sakta hai. Asian economies, European economies abhi sab jo hai apne qareeb kisi source se oil khareedte hain. Ab agar unko America se khareedna parega to distance travel mein kitna zyada increase aa jayega.

So trade patterns change hone ki wajah se shipping mein distance travel jo hai jisko ton miles kehte hain, wo barh bhi sakta hai jisse freight rates upar chale jaate hain and vice versa. So shipping business door se kaafi volatile lagta hai, lekin agar aap distance travel pe focus karein to everything makes sense. Aur jisko ye business samajh aa jaye, uske liye ye ek opportunity hai. Shipping companies ka ROIC, freight rates par bahut zyada depend karta hai.

Aaye aapko ye ek example se explain karte hain. Let's say ek 10 saal purana Panamax tanker, jiski capacity maximum 80,000 tons tak ki hoti hai, almost $20 million cost karta hai. Aur let's say ye ship saal mein 330 days operate karta hai aur ship ke annual operating expenses almost $5 million ke hote hain jismein fuel, crew ki salaries, insurance, maintenance sab cover ho jata hai. Ab let's say freight rates normalize hain, around $20,000 per day. To saal ka revenue 330 operating days ke hisab se ho gaya almost $6.6 million. Aur operating expenses $5 million ke hain, to agar hum ROIC nikalein to wo banega almost 8% ka. But ab let's say freight rates apne all-time high par hain, around $45,000 per day. To saal ka revenue ho gaya almost $15 million. Hum kehte hain ke chalein operating expenses thode se barh bhi gaye, $5 million se $6 million tak pohanch gaye. To is case mein shipping company ka profit ek saal mein $9 to $10 million ho jayega aur ROIC almost 50%.

Shipping ka business ek global business hai, ships just like commodities operate karti hain. Inke freight rates, fuel, maintenance, dry docking sabhi almost same hi hota hai kyunki har ship jis destination pe hoti hai wahin se fuel khareedna hota hai aur port charges same hi pay karne hote hain. Pakistan mein low salaries aur khud ki workshop hone se PNSC ki cost global shipping companies ke muqable mein is segment mein thodi si kam hoti hai lekin Pakistan mein high interest rates bhi hote hain, jiski wajah se yahan local companies ko disadvantage hai. Jaise aapko pata hoga ke new ships khareedte waqt industry mein 80% tak jo shipping ki cost hoti hai ship khareedne ki, usko bank se borrow bhi kiya jata hai. Is wajah se high interest rate Pakistani industry ke liye ek disadvantage hai. So salaries ka advantage aur interest rate ka disadvantage, ye ek doosre ko ek tareeqe se balance kar dete hain.

Ab PNSC kyunki state-owned hai, isi wajah se ye company Pakistan ka almost 95% oil import lekar aati hai. Aur yehi wajah hai ke ye company almost 100% utilization par chalti hai. Inko fixed contracts mile hue hain, first right inke paas hai ke Pakistan ne jo bhi oil import karna hai, wo isi company ke through aayega. To aapki jo ships hain, wo ek tarah se unko ek long-term contract mila hua hai aur aapko per day rate jo hai, wo wahi milta hai jo international market mein chal raha hota hai. Iske ilawa corporate tax rate Pakistan mein baqi mulkon ke muqable mein kaafi zyada hai jo ke ek disadvantage hai, but shipping policy 2020, 2021 mein income tax rates kaafi low hain. Pakistan Merchant Marine Policy ke mutabiq, shipping companies just $1 tax pay karengi har gross registered ton par. To profit jitna marzi zyada ho jaye, kisi bhi freight rates ke increase hone ki wajah se, jo tax ki liabilities hain, wo Pakistan mein shipping policy ke under fixed hain. Ye factor shipping business ko Pakistan mein competitive bana deta hai.

PNSC ki privatization ho ya aapki khud ki investment, valuation ek aisi cheez hai jisko ignore kiya nahi ja sakta. PNSC ek shipping company hai, iski valuation PE ratio par ho nahi sakti, jaise ke mein aapko bataya ke freight rates bahut zyada volatile hote hain aur ye upar neeche hone ki wajah se profit jo hai kabhi bahut zyada ho jata hai, kabhi bahut zyada kam ho jata hai. So mere hisab se iski valuation asset-based zyada accurate hai. Means company ke assets ki value assess ki jaye aur ek value derive ki jaye jo ke company ki intrinsic value hogi. To chaliye is aspect ko thoda sa discuss karte hain.

PNSC ke paas almost 12 ships ka fleet hai, jismein 7 oil tankers hain aur 5 bulk carriers, jin ki average age already 19 years ho chuki hai. Jab bhi koi ship 18 se 20 saal ki age ko pohanchti hai, to uski value scrap plus 5 se 8 saal ki remaining life aur un saalon ki earning power yaani ke ek premium pe trade hoti hai. Ships ko almost $400 per Light Displacement Tonnage par value kiya jata hai aur ye wo rate hota hai jispe ship-breaking industry ek purani ship ko khareed leti hai aur usko scrap karke usse phir se iron bana leti hai. LDT wo weight hota hai jo ship ka khud ka weight hota hai, na ke wo weight jo wo product carry karta hai. Mostly ships ka LDT un ships ke Deadweight Tonnage ka almost 30% ke around hota hai. Aur PNSC ke case mein takreeban agar hum 30% assume karein, to PNSC ke 12 ships ke LDT ko add karein aur ek reasonable value assign karein of $400 to $450, to in ships ki scrap value almost 35 to 40 billion rupees ki range mein ban jati hai. Ab yaad rakhein isme humne jo next few years ki earning power hai aur jo premium wala aspect hota hai, wo add nahi kiya.

Iske ilawa PNSC ke paas 14,000 square yards yaani ke 28 kanals ki investment property bhi hai, jiski value roughly 5 billion rupees ke aas-paas hai. Aur agar hum jo company ke paas cash hai aur jo thodi bahut liabilities hain, unko adjust kar lein, to is company ki ships ki scrap value plus cash minus liabilities ki value roughly 110 se 120 arab rupees ke darmiyan ban jati hai, jisko agar hum per share basis pe convert karein to wo ban jati hai roughly 550 to 600 rupees per share. So ye to ho gayi asset-based valuation. Iske saath saath agar is company ki privatization hoti hai, to obviously agle 5 se 6 saalon ka jo freights ka outlook hoga, uske hisab se bhi jo iske investors honge, wo isko exact value assign karenge. Aur agar koi private buyer PNSC ko buy karta hai, to wo is value par premium pay karega, kyunki PNSC national flag carrier hai, iske paas oil import ke fixed contracts bhi hain aur Pakistan ki akeli shipping company hai isliye ports par PNSC ki ships ko preference bhi di jati hai. So ye to humne baat kar li asset-based valuation ki. Ab likelihood of privatization pe bhi baat kar lete hain. Even though kuch ministers aur kuch Pakistan ke top businessmen ne is company ko privatize karne ke bare mein kuch comments kiye hain, lekin abhi tak ki latest privatization list ke hisab se ye company jo hai, privatization list par maujood nahi hai. Asal mein, purane zamane se shipping business aisa hai ke ship ki apni value, aur agar usko aap compare karein ke wo jo saman carry kar rahi hoti hai, to saman ki value kabhi kabhi to 1,000 times hoti hai of the ship's value. To is wajah se kabhi bhi ship agar kisi aisi jagah se guzar rahi ho jahan pe pirates hote hain ya koi unforeseen event ho jaye, to koi bhi shipping company us product ko jo carry kar rahi hoti hai, uski liability apne sar pe nahi leti. Isi wajah se har ship jo hai, ek different company ke under register ki jati hai taake agar usme koi unforeseen event ho jaye to ek to wo insurance se covered ho aur us products ki liability jo wo carry kar rahi hai, doosri company ya company ki doosri ships jo hai, usko affect na kare. To is wajah se PNSC ne bhi apni har individual ship jo hai as a separate company register ki hui hai. Aur is wajah se aapko consolidated results pe zyada focus karna chahiye jismein wo saare companies jo different ships own karti hain, wo consolidated basis pe nazar aati hain.

Ab chalte hain PNSC ke expansion plans pe, PNSC ne recently apne teen Aframax, jinke naam the Karachi, Lahore aur Quetta ko sale kiya hai for almost 13.2 billion rupees. Iske ilawa PNSC mazeed apne do tankers ko sale karna chahta hai aur inko new Aframax tankers ke saath replace karna chahta hai. Ye PNSC ke strategic growth plans ka hissa hai. PNSC na sirf apne purane ships ko new ships se replace karna chahta hai, balki uske ilawa mazeed new ships bhi order karna chahta hai. Aur iske peeche ek hi reason hai, aur wo hai International Maritime Organization ke strict regulations. Aur jan aapko ye regulations samajh aayenge, to aap kahenge ke waqai purani ships to sirf chand saalon ki hi mehman hain. 2030 ke baad, shipping companies ko apni greenhouse gases ki emissions par control karna hoga aur 2035 tak GHG ki ye emissions mein 30% ki reduction karna hogi, aur 2040 tak almost 65% reduction karni hogi. Iske ilawa ships ki energy efficiency ko improve karna hoga. Ek old ship almost 30 se 40 tons Marine Gas Oil consume karti hai compared to a new ship jo ke almost 30 tons fuel consume karti hai per day. To ye savings aapki costs ko bhi affect karti hain, aapki GHG emissions ko bhi affect karti hain aur future mein jab IMO is pe penalties bhi lagayega to ye aapki profitability ko bahut zyada hit karegi. Isliye new ships ki price, old ships ke muqable mein kaafi zyada hoti hai. Iske ilawa old fleet ko regular dry docking ki bhi zaroorat hoti hai. Dry docking means ship ko sea se bahar nikal ke uske hull yaani ke bottom part, propeller, rudder, sea valves aur structure ko check kiya jata hai aur damage parts ko repair kiya jata hai. Ships ki dry docking har 5 saal baad hoti hai. Lekin agar fleet old ho, to aksar immediate emergency dry docking bhi karni parh jati hai. Iske ilawa new ships ki dry docking ki cost takreeban 1 se 2 million dollar hoti hai jab ke used ships mein yehi cost 3 se 5 million dollar bhi ho jati hai. To is wajah se purani ships ko operate karna costly hota hai aur agar freight rates thode se kam ho gaye, to purani ships to ghaate ka sauda ban jati hain. Yehi wajah hai ke shipping companies old fleet ko new ke saath replace kar rahi hain. Aur PNSC bhi apne aap ko isi industry trend ke saath adjust kar raha hai. PNSC new ships jo procure kar rahe hain, wo almost less than 5 years old honge. Iska maqsad hai IMO ke regulation par poora utarna. PNSC ne apni history mein aaj tak kabhi koi new ship buy nahi ki, kyunki Pakistan mein interest rate itna zyada hota hai to hamesha wo koshish karte hain ke thodi purani ship mil jaye taake uski cost jo hai wo kam rahe. Lekin ab wo bhi plan kar rahe hain ke hum new ships bhi buy karenge because of the IMO regulations.
