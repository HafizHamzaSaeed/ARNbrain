ARN: Assalam-o-Alaikum everyone, umeed hai sab khairiyat se honge. Aaj se kuch din pehle humne jo Pakistan ki sabse pehli Financial Freedom Movement start ki thi, jiska maqsad ye tha ke 10 lakh Pakistaniyon ko financial freedom achieve karwana, uska pehla physical event humne Lahore mein conduct kiya. Humein pehle hi andaza tha ke Pakistan mein financial stress logon ki zindagi mein bohot zyada hai, aur financial freedom har koi achieve karna chahta hai, aur aap ke turnout ne is event pe is cheez ko sabit kar diya.

Mere saath saath Ammar Siddiqui, Saqib Azhar, Qasim Ali Shah, aur bhi humare bohot se doston ne financial freedom se related aap ko bohot zyada guidance di taake aap apni zindagi mein financial stress ko kam kar sakein aur acche decisions le sakein jis se aap ka lifestyle aur aap ki zindagi improve ho. Maine personally is topic pe jab baat ki to maine financial freedom ke chaar different levels jo hote hain, wo aap ko bataye aur bataya ke aap jis bhi level pe abhi currently hain, next level tak jaane ke liye aap ke liye best path kya ho sakta hai.

To ye bohot hi informative humara ek event tha jiska humne socha ke aap ke saath bhi share karte hain taake aap bhi bohot acche tareeqe se financial freedom achieve kar sakein. Aur ye sirf shuruat hai, ye humara pehla physical event tha. Next hum aayenge Karachi, aur aap humari Financial Freedom Movement ke liye foran register kar lein taake humara event jaise hi Karachi mein ho, aap ko pehle se pata chal jaye. To is session ki recording aap enjoy karein aur humein comments mein batayega ke aap financial freedom ki journey pe kahan khade hain, aur is video se aap ko kaun si cheez sabse acchi lagi? To aaiye start karte hain.
