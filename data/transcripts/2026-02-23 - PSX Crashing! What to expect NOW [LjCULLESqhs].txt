السلام علیکم everyone. This is Abdul Rehman aur aaj 23rd February takriban sawa aath baje ka Pakistan mein time hai aur ye mein video record kar raha hoon for all PSX investors.

Pichle kuch dino mein aap ne headlines suni hongi, PSX mein 10 billion dollars ki market jo hai, correct ho gayi, logo ka nuksaan ho gaya.

Iske sath sath global jo scenario chal raha hai usme bhi kafi volatility hai.

Aur pichle ek mahine mein kafi zada more than 100, 200 million dollars se zada ki foreign investors jo hote hain, Pakistan ki stock market mein invest karte hain wo bohot aggressively selling kar rahe hain.

To ye to hain sare facts jo aap ne sune honge, but analysis kya hai? Analysis mein aap se aaj share karunga, aur mein apni analysis teen points ke andar aap ko summarize kar ke bataunga. First, mein aap ko historical trend, recent historical trend jo pichle do teen saal se chal raha hai, do points mein aap ko aise bataunga jo ke is bull market ki ek characteristic hai aur wo ek dafa fir se repeat ho rahi hai, aur us se aap ko samajh ayega ke ye opportunity hai ya ye risk hai. Phir mein aap ko bataunga ke pichle ek mahine ke jo events ho rahe hain us se bull market framework pe koi impact aa raha hai ya nahi aa raha, aur kuch historical reference bhi dunga ke ye aisa pehle bhi hua hai aur phir uske hisab se aap ko kya karna chahiye.

So, sab se pehle hum shuru karte hain, mein aap ko do points aise bataunga jo ke aap ne apne hamesha mind mein rakhne hain. Aap pichle do se dahi saal ka data dekhein. Do trends hain. Ek hai ke jab bhi PSX ki market jo hai, wo thori si barhti hai, usme acchi khasi rally aati hai, phir uske andar ek correction aati hai, jis ko profit taking bhi kehte hain. Obviously stock market agar straight line mein upar jati hoti to har koi is se faida utha leta. To ye kabhi thori zada barhti hai, phir thori si correction hoti hai, phir phir se barhti hai aur phir se thori si correction hoti hai. To trend ye hai ke stock market pehle barhti hai phir wo jahan tak pahonchti hai takriban 10 se 12% ki ek correction aati hai. Ab mein aap ko isko events mein batata hoon.

Sab se pehla event jab Pakistan ka IMF ke saath agreement ho gaya, July 2023 mein, to ek rally upward bani aur ye takriban 6 mahine last ki. Phir, February 2024 mein jab elections hue aur election ki night pe jab jo results aa rahe the aur agle din bohot zada uncertainty phel gayi to stock market jo hai ek dum se bohot zada correct hui aur takriban 65,000 se ye almost 56,000-57,000 pe aa gayi thi. Lekin jaise hi cheezein jo hain settle down huin, foran ek aur rally bani. To ye aap ne dekha 10 se 15% ki ek correction aa gayi.

Phir next humne same cheez dekhi jab September-October 2024 mein humara index takriban 85,000 pe pahonch gaya, us waqt supreme court mein ek naya change aaya aur us waqt ki uncertainty ki wajah se market jo hai usne phir se correction li, wo 85,000 se takriban 78,000 pe aa gayi. Lekin jaise hi uncertainty khatam hui, us time mein poori political parties ki meetings bhi ho rahi thein, sab kuch aap ko yaad hoga, mujhe yaad karane ki zaroorat nahi hai, lekin phir ek aur rally bani aur hum 78,000 se le kar ek lakh 20,000 tak gaye.

Phir ek new world order jo hai wo set hua, US ka election hua, Donald Trump president ban rahe the aur bohot zada uncertainty thi ke Pakistan mein kis political party ko jo hai US se support milega, aur phir us uncertainty ke saath saath jo hai Pakistan aur India ki jung bhi ho gayi, aur is time mein humne phir se ek correction dekhi, again wahi 10 se 12% wali range mein, ek lakh 20,000 se hum log takriban jo hai ek lakh paanch hazaar pe aaye, lekin kyun ke bull market framework on tha, phir se ek rally bani jo ke 170,000 tak gayi, aur phir thori si uncertainty aayi aur aaj phir, aaj se thode din pehle jab hum 190,000 pe pahonche, to phir ab thori si uncertainty chal rahi hai aur phir se wo jo 10-12% ki correction hai, wo aap ki nerves ko test kar rahi hai. To pehla point jo mein ne aap ko bataya ke bull market mein rallies aati hain aur phir jab correction aati hai to data humein show kar raha hai ke ye 10 se 12% ki range mein hoti hai, aur aaj bhi bilkul aise hi chal raha hai. To aap ko is se ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi hai, this is nothing new. Ye cheez pehle bhi repeat ho chuki hai aur jo itni acchi bull market chal rahi hai ye bilkul uska jo framework hai us se fully intact hai.

Doosra ek aur tareeqa ye hota hai ke jo recent rally aayi ho, yaani ke jo recent up leg aayi hai PSX mein, uski takriban 25 se 30% correction ho jati hai. Aur ye hum dekh rahe hain pehle 40,000 se hum 65 tak gaye to jo 25,000 points barhe the, election ke time pe usme takriban jo hai saath aath hazaar ki correction aa gayi. So isi tareeqe se hum recent leg dekhein, jo ke takriban 155,000 se le kar 190,000 tak gayi to usme bhi correction aa rahi hai. To aap dono tareeqon se dekhein to bull market framework jo hai wo fully intact hai.

Ye cheezein determine nahi kartiin ke 10 se 15% correction se bull market khatam ho jati hai balki actual mein aap ne dekhna hota hai ke aaj kal Pakistan mein best asset class kaun si hai? To aap bank mein paise rakhne ka dekhein, interest rate kitne kam ho gaye hain, tax rate kitne zada hain bank mein agar aap paise rakhte hain, real estate abhi tak dead hai. Iske ilawa aap doosri asset classes dekh lein. Jo best opportunity hai wo abhi bhi stock market mein hai, retail investors bhi naye account khulwa rahe hain aur individual investors jis ko high net worth individuals bhi kehte hain wo khud personal portfolios ke through aur mutual funds ke through bhi abhi bhi jo hai aggressively buying kar rahe hain.

Aur jo pichle ek mahine ke events hain, wo uska number one factor agar geopolitics dekhein to wo Iran, US ki jo uncertainty chal rahi hai us se related hai, aur doosra hai ke Pakistan mein jo foreign portfolios hote hain jo stock market mein investment karte hain, unhone almost 200 million dollars ki selling kar di hai. But acchi baat ye hai ke in investors ka size jo hai ab bohot kam hai, aur Pakistan ke jo mutual funds hain, unhone jo stocks mein investment ki hui hai wo ab almost 2 billion dollars ke aas paas pahonch chuki hai. To ye jitni bhi selling kar rahe hain, doosre local individuals aur mutual funds jo hain bade asaani se usko absorb karte ja rahe hain, aur badi khushi se jo hai wo aage aage ho ke pichle ek do hafte mein buying kar rahe hain.

Foreign investors ka lens jo hai cheezon ko dekhne ka unke paas bohot zada asset classes hoti hain, pichle ek saal ka trend bhi ye hai ke wo Pakistan ke saath saath India, Vietnam aur bhi bohot sari stock markets hain wahan se exit le rahe hain aur US mein investment kar rahe hain. US mein interest rates bhi barh gaye hain to wahan pe bhi thori si reallocation chal rahi hai. Aur aap ko yaad hoga Trump tariffs ke time pe mein ne aap ko poora explain kiya tha ke Trump tariffs se jaise global economy slow down hoti hai aur commodities sasti hoti hain to Pakistan ko is se faida ho jata hai. Lekin foreigners ke liye ye ek negative cheez hoti hai kyun ke jab uncertainty aati hai wo flight to safety karte hain aur US bonds ki taraf jaate hain. To us din bhi mujhe yaad hai jis din pehli dafa Trump tariffs announce hue the aur PSX jo hai usme market halt hua tha to the lower side kyun ke foreigners jo hain wo saari exit kar rahe the, halanki ye development jo thi wo Pakistan ke liye positive thi, Pakistan ke bull market framework ke liye.

To aaj bhi saari soorat-e-hal ko dekhte hue, interest rate ko dekhte hue, companies ke results ko dekhte hue, aur jaise jaise ye Iran-US ki jo uncertainty hai wo bhi ek nazar aa raha hai ke ek taraf jo hai settle down ho rahi hai. To aaj jo situation hai uske hisab se data bhi humein show kar raha hai ke humein bilkul ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Jo correction aani zaroori hoti hai kabhi uski koi wajah banti hai, kabhi elections wajah bante hain, kabhi supreme court wajah banti hai, kabhi Iran aur US ki jo short war pehle bhi hui thi June 2025 mein, kabhi wo wajah banti hai, kabhi foreign selling wajah ban jati hai. Wajah har waqt alag hogi lekin result same hoga. Aap ne apne framework ko strong karna hai aur is time mein do tareeqe hote hain markets jo aap ko tang karti hain. Ek hota hai bohot deep correction lete hain aur bohot short time mein, jaise COVID mein hua tha. Waisa hi almost jo hai aaj kal ho raha hai. Ek se do hafte mein hi 10% ki correction aa gayi hai. Aisi situation mein market ka structure humein indicate kar raha hai ke zada chances hain ke market V-shape recovery legi. Aur jo doosra tareeqa hota hai jo market aap ko test karti hai wo hoti hai ke wo bohot zada time ke liye side-ways ho jati hai, aur ye jo hai wo January se le kar May 2025 mein hua tha. Wo aap ko time se bhi jo hai time aap ko test karti hai ke aap itne time tak side-ways movement bardasht kar sakte hain, aap ki conviction ko challenge karti hai. Doosra tareeqa hota hai ke wo bohot sharp correction leti hai aur aap sochte hain ke itne zada log roz roz sell kar rahe hain to mujhe bhi bech dena chahiye, kal jo hai price aur niche chali jayegi.

To is dafa market structure humein bata raha hai ke ye doosri type se market aap ko test kar rahi hai aur zada tar in cheezon ka result hota hai ek V-shape recovery. Jitni tezi se markets niche jati hain, utni tezi se hi rebound kartiin hain. To bohot acche chances hain aur ye meri prediction bhi hai ke agle kuch hi dino mein jaise hi jis speed se correction hui hai usi speed se jo hai hum ek recovery bhi dekhenge, aur ye aap ko khud market ke jis tarah se ek oversold level pe aa jati hai wahan pe hum khade hain, aur aap khud dekhenge ke agle kuch dino mein hum ek correction bhi dekhenge, phir aage cheezein jo hain develop hongi, companies ke jaise results aayenge uske hisab se jo hai share prices move karti rehti hain. To is time mein aap ne bilkul ghabrana nahi hai, jo log new start kiya hai abhi wo bhi jis conviction ke saath start kiya tha usko review karein, jo log pehle se invested hain, wo jis wajah se unhone bull market framework mein unki conviction build hui thi usko review karein, wo agar aaj bhi intact hai to aap ko bhi jo hai invested rehna chahiye. Thode dino ki baat hoti hai market aap ko test karti hai, aur ye saari uncertainty jo hai wo with time settle down ho jati hai.
