To log jab ye numbers sochte hain, to unko ye nahi samajh aati ke ye jo teen teen char char percent ka farq hai, ye long term mein kitna zyada difference create kar deta hai, aur wo sochte hain ke thoda sa to farq hai, to kisi mein bhi kar lete hain, kya farq padta hai? Lekin, ye jo do do teen teen percent ka farq hota hai, isko aap rule of 72 se samjhein. Rule of 72 ek bohot zabardast financial term hai, jismein jab aap 72 ko ek saal ke profit se divide karte hain, to aapke paas aa jata hai, ke ye investment meri investment ko, kitne saalon mein double karke degi. To 18% mein aapke char saal mein paise double hote hain. 15% pe aapke paanch saal mein paise double hote hain. Aur agar 10% ho, to phir saat saal mein paise double hote hain. Ab aap khud sochein ke 20 saal mein, agar aap har char saal mein paise double kar rahe honge, to aapke paanch dafa double honge, yaani ke 32 times. 2 raise to the power 5 is 32 times. Pakistan mein ETFs ko samajhne ke liye sabse pehle liquidity ka concept clear karna zaroori hai. Liquidity ka simple matlab hai, ke aap jab chahein apni investment ko cash mein convert kar sakein. Isko samajhne ke liye, ek ghar yaani ke real estate, aur stock market ki misal dekhte hain. Agar aapko apna koi real estate ka plot bechna ho, to mahino lag jate hain, aur sahi buyer dhoondne mein aapko price bhi negotiate karni padti hai. Ye ek illiquid asset hai. Lekin agar aapke paas kisi blue chip company ke shares hain, to aap kisi bhi din market price par bech kar isko cash mein convert kar sakte hain, kyunki buyers available hote hain. Ye ek liquid asset hai. Pakistan ki ETFs unfortunately, ghar ki tarah thoda zyada behave kar rahi hain, aur major stocks ki tarah kam. Specially agar aapka portfolio size accha hai. Jab hum actual numbers dekhte hain, to reality samajh aa jati hai. Agar hum Mahana ETF ko dekhein, jo ke Pakistan ki sabse badi ETF hai, jiska assets under management 1 billion se zyada hai, iski daily traded value sirf ₹1 crore hai. Isi tareeqe se agar hum Meezan ETF aur UBL ETF ka value traded roz ka dekhein, to wo bhi ₹1 crore ke aas paas hi hai. Ab compare kijiye top stocks, jinki daily trading jo hai, wo 10 se 20 crore tak bhi chali jati hai. Ab aap sochein ke agar aapki investment size zyada hai, aur aapko ₹5 crore jo hai, wo invest karne hain ya nikalne hain, to ye ETFs mein problem create karega. Aur ab hum liquidity problems ka directly visible effect, bid-ask spread ko samajhte hain. Jo Stock Exchange par har waqt do prices hoti hain. Ek bid price jo buyers dene ko tayyar hain, aur ek ask price jo sellers maang rahe hain. In dono ka difference spread kehlata hai. Agar kisi bhi ETF ki actual value let's say ₹100 hai, aur liquidity kam hai, to bid price ₹99 aur ask price ₹101 ho sakti hai. Matlab agar aap fauran khareedein, acchi quantity mein, to ₹101 dene padenge, aur fauran bechein, to sirf ₹99 milenge. Ye 2% ka difference instant loss sirf transaction ki wajah se aapki investment ki performance ko hurt kar sakta hai. So, ye understand karna critical hai, kyunki bohot se investors ye cost dekhte hi nahi hain, initially. Har ETF ko apne index ko follow karna hota hai. Jab index change hota hai, to ETF ko bhi wahi changes implement karni padti hain. Ye changes do tareeqon se aati hain. Pehla rebalancing, jo quarterly ya semi-annually hota hai, jismein existing companies ke weights adjust kiye jate hain. Doosra reconstitution, jismein purani companies nikal jati hain aur new add hoti hain. Har dafa jab ye hota hai, to ETF manager ko multiple trades execute karne padte hain. Purani companies bechni aur nayi khareedni padti hain. Is process mein do costs aati hain. Pehli brokerage fees, jo har trade par lagti hai, doosri taxes jo buying, selling par applicable hoti hain. So, ye hai ETFs ki Pakistan mein kahani. Ab ye batayein ke decision kaise karein? Sabse pehle to aaj ETFs ki position ye hai Pakistan mein. Sirf retail investors hi is mein invest kar sakte hain, liquidity ko dekhte hue. Uske baad agar aapko kisi ETF ki exact allocation pasand hai, to ye ek sensible choice hai. So, Pakistan ke ETFs ko dekhte hue, conclusion ye hai ke retail investor ke liye jo rupees 10 lakh tak invest kar rahe hain, unke liye ETFs aur wo ETFs, jiske portfolio se aap mutmaeen hain, ek accha option hai.
