Automobile industry mein aisi dynamics hain ke ek se do saal mein hi ek company jo hai bahut chhoti se bahut badi ho sakti hai. To agar koi chhota sa player bhi ho, aur usne kisi se partnership kar li baahar, jiski gaadi ki price bahut acchi hai, features bahut acche hain, to wo thodi si investment karke apne plant mein bahut zyada gaadiyan bech sakta hai. NCPL aur Nishat Power, ye jo new company aa rahi hai jismein JAECOO OMODA hai, uska 33% each own karte hain. Trend ye hai ke Chinese gaadiyan hain sasti. Yaani ke agar aap Toyota ka per car profit dekhenge, to wo 1 crore ki gaadi pe after tax 6-7 lakh rupay hota hai. Lekin Chinese gaadiyon pe wo almost 10 lakh hota hai. To yaani ke aapke ek gaadi pe profit bhi zyada hai, aur aap acchi price rakh sakte hain to wo bikti bhi zyada hai. J5 bhi launch ho gayi hai, aur jo J7 pehle launch hui thi, uski bhi koi 3-4 hazaar ki booking hui hui hai. Wo almost 1 crore ki gaadi hai. To agar agle ek saal mein 4000 gaadiyan bhi bikti hain, jo ke already almost book ho gayi hain, aur har gaadi pe 10 lakh rupay profit ho gaya, to 4 arab rupay is company ne kama liye. Ye jo company hai na, wo hai Next Gen Auto. Phir NCPL aur Nishat Power jo hai Next Gen Auto ko 33% own karte hain each. To 4 arab mein se takreeban 1.5 arab ya sawa arab jo hai in dono company mein aa jayega.
