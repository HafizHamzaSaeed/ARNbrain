Speaker 1: Jo aap, portfolio mein kisi stock ki weightage assign karte hain, wo kin factors pe hai? Kya wo upside pe hai, volatility pe hai, liquidity based hai?

ARN: Portfolio mein percentage jo teen pillars hain na, investing framework ke, uske hisab se hi hoti hai. Sabse pehle aap dekhte hain upside kitni hai. Kaam sara udhar se hi shuru hota hai. Aap sabse pehle dekhte hain ke iski reasonable market mein jo bhi us company ki ek tarah se reputation hai, usko log kitna highly rate karte hain, aur end mein usko aapne jab number milana hota hai to aapne sochna hota hai ke isko kya P/E ratio milega. Kuch companies ko investors high P/E ratio pe bhi kharidte hain kyunki wo high ROIC ki companies hoti hain. To ek dafa jab aapne future business samajh ke agle ek do saal ka profit kar liya aur uske hisab se dekha ke ye company ki fair price itni honi chahiye. Phir jo restrictions hoti hain na, wo aa jati hain. Agar mujhe tier-one company, tier-one company jismein buying selling bhi zyada hoti hai, group bhi strong hai, koi negative reputation bhi attached nahi hai, to phir you can go as high as 25% as well. Ya shayad 30 bhi, depending on kitni acchi upside.
