**Interviewer:** Hamari society mein usually jo riwaj yahi hota hai ke jitni earnings hain, savings hain, wo decisions mard hazraat lete hain. Isiliye ya to khawateen hain hi nahi ya bahut thori hain. Khawateen ko financially literate kaise hona chahiye aur kyun hona chahiye?

**ARN:** Jo mard hazraat sun rahe hain, unki shayad sabse badi soch ya dream ye hogi ke mere jo bacche hain, wo financially literate ho jayein. Unko samajh aa jaye ke achhi investment kahan karni hai, fazool kharchi nahi karni. The second reason, which is even more powerful jiski wajah se financial literacy women mein, poore ghar ko uska faida hota hai, wo ye hai ke agar koi mother financially literate hogi to us ghar ke bacche financially literate hone ke chances das guna badh jaate hain. To uske liye jo hai, women financial literacy apne ghar mein jo hai na usko improve karne ki jo hai na bohot zyada zaroorat hai. To jo bhi aapke ghar mein women hain, agar wo in cheezon mein jo hai aage badh ke participate karna chahti hain, unko aur encourage karein, aur agar nahi karna chahti, to unko thoda thoda jo hai exposure dein.
