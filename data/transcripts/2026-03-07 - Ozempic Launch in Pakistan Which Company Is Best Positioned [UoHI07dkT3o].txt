Sabse pehle hum baat karenge ROIC ki. Kisi bhi biopharma plant ka ROIC samajhne ke liye teen cheezon ki badi importance hai. Agar hum Pakistan ki real examples dekhein, to BF Biosciences ka case bahut relevant hai. BF Biosciences ne Bagó Group, Argentina ke saath milkar ye facility 2006 mein start ki, jo initially 15,000 injections per day ki facility thi. Company ne recently Biotech Line II expansion ke liye 4 billion rupees invest kiye hain, jiske baad unki daily capacity 15,000 injections se barh kar 65,000 injections tak ho gayi hai. So agar ROIC estimate karne ki koshish karein, to sabse pehle unit economics ko samajhte hain. BF Biosciences semaglutide ke teen variants banate hain, aur inki average selling price around rupees 1500 per injection hai. Ek injection banane ke liye API, formulation, excipients, device, packaging, cold chain aur distribution sab mila kar average production cost taqreeban 750 rupees per dose ho jati hai, jo gross basis par company ko 50% margin earn kar ke deti hai, aur ye 50% ka gross margin industry ke participants confirm bhi karte hain. So agar ye plant 50% capacity par operate kare, to company annually 8 million doses produce kar legi jinki annual sales roughly 12 billion rupees tak pohnch jati hai. Distribution, administrative, aur production cost nikalne ke baad operating level par taqreeban 3 to 3.5 billion rupees ka profit generate ho jata hai, jo after-tax taqreeban 2 to 2.5 billion rupees reh jata hai. Is poore business ko chalane ke liye agar hum PKR 4 billion plant mein invest karein, aur saath saath 2 billion rupees ka working capital bhi assume karein, to roughly 6 billion ki investment par almost 35% ROIC achieve kiya ja sakta hai agar aapne 50% utilization achieve kar li.
