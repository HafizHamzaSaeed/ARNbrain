Kya aapko pata hai ke Pakistan ki ek cement company pichle do se teen salon se USA cement export kar rahi hai? Aur yehi company 2023 mein losses kar rahi thi aur 2025 mein massive profits karke ek zabardast turnaround story ban chuki hai. Main baat kar raha hoon DG Khan Cement ki, retail investors of Pakistan ki favorite, aur cement sector ki acchi outlook bhi is company ko tailwinds provide kar rahi hai. In saare aspects ko aur jo company ne recent announcement ki hai ke hum ek aur cement ka plant jo hai wo lagane ja rahe hain aur usme almost 200 million dollar ki investment karne lage hain agle kuch salon mein, in sab aspects ko cover karne ke liye ek detailed video on DG Khan Cement aap sab ki request pe hamare main YouTube channel pe live ho gayi hai. Ab aap ne kya karna hai? Aapne is video ko hamare YouTube channel pe ja kar dekhna hai, isme humne FY26 aur 2027 ki outlook bhi share ki hai company se related. Aur aapne humein comments mein batana hai ke aapko kya lagta hai ke 2026 aur 2027 mein cement sector ki growth jo hai wo continue rahegi aur kya ye sector stock market mein accha perform karta rahega?
