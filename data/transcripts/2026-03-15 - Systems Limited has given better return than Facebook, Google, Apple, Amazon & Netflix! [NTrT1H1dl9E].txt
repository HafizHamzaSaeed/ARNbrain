Ziyadatar log dekhte hain ke technology jis tarah se boom aya hai pichle 10-15 saalon mein, to yeh jo America ki five big companies hain, is ko FAANG bhi kehte hain. Yeh matlab ke flagship hain pichle das saalon ke jinhon ne sab se zyada profit diya hai. Ab aap in sab ka record dekhenge 1 January 2006 se, poore das saal ho gaye hain ab. To in sab companies ne kisi ne ek dollar ko 8 dollars kiya hai, kisi ne 9 dollars kiya hai, kisi ne 10 dollars kiya hai. Aur yeh saari woh examples hain jis ka ek acronym ban gaya hai, ke yeh stock market ke sab se ache performers hain pichle das saal ke. Aur Pakistan mein bhi ek IT company hai, woh hai Systems Limited. Same arse mein isi company ne jo hai aap ke 1 dollar ko 11 dollars par pohanchaya, yaani ke jo top five examples hain America ki jis ko log quote karte hain, us ko Pakistan ki ek IT company jo hai sab ko beat karti hai, dollar terms mein.
