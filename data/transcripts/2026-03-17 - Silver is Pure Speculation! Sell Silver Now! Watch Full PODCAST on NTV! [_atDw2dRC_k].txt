**ARN:** Silver bas aese hi hai, matlab...

**Interviewer:** Bila-wajah hai aap ka khayal hai?

**ARN:** Silver achanak badha na. Silver ki jitni bhi rally hai na is ke peechay koi fundamental nahi hota. Ye bas gold ko follow karta hai kabhi kabhi, kabhi kabhi nahi karta. To silver to intahai speculative element hai. Agar kisi ko apni safety mein invest karna hai usko lagta hai de-dollarization ho rahi hai, to usko gold ki taraf jana chahiye, silver ki taraf to kisi ko jana bhi nahi chahiye. Kuch nahi aap uska bata sakte. Aur uska koi trend nahi hai matlab jis ke basis pe aap investment karein. Gold phir bhi ek investment hoti hai, silver jo hai pure speculation hoti hai. To jab ye thodi profit taking hoti hai iske sath sath jo silver bahut zyada badha, aur China ne iski trading jo hai na wo band kar di apne mulk mein. To is tarah ki jab steps leti hain hukumatain, to ye temporary taur pe neechay bhi aa jati hain.
