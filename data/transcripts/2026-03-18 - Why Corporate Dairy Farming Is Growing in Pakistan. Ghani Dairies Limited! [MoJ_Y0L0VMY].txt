Pakistan duniya ka fourth largest milk producer hai. Reports ke mutabiq Pakistan har saal almost 72 million metric ton yaani ke almost 70 arab liter milk produce karta hai, jismein se almost 60% buffalo milk aur 40% cows produce karti hain. Pakistan ke bade shehar jaise Lahore, Karachi, annually almost derh arab liter milk akele consume karte hain. Pakistan mein milk packaged aur loose, donon tareeqon se sale hota hai. Almost 92 to 94% milk jo sale hota hai, wo loose milk hai aur packaged milk ka market share sirf 6 se 8% hai. So aap dekh sakte hain ke under the right policy environment, standardized packaged milk industry kitni grow ho sakti hai, kitna potential hai. Agar hum cost side se shuru karein, to ek high-yielding Dutch ya Australian Holstein heifer almost PKR 8 lakh cost karta hai ya 3,000 dollars. Aur let's say aap ne 100 animals ka farm set up kiya aur five-month pregnant heifers import kiye, to is scale par initial livestock ki investment roughly 7 se 8 crore ke aas paas ban sakti hai. Actual costs location aur market conditions ke mutabiq change ho sakti hain. Pregnant heifers ko aapko four months mazeed feed karna hoga, unki milking start hone se pehle. Heifers apne weight ka almost 3 se 4% feed daily consume karte hain. Ek average heifer almost 600 se 700 kg weigh karti hai aur almost 25 kg feed consume karti hai daily, jismein 10 se 12 kg almost dry matter hota hai, jaise ke soybean meal, corn gluten, etc. So agar hum feed ki cost calculate karein, to wo almost Rs. 2,500 daily ban jaati hai. So four months ke liye ek heifer ki feed cost taqreeban 2.5 se 3 lakh tak jaa sakti hai depending on feed quality. Operational expenses jaise electricity aur staff cost location ke hisab se vary karti hain, jo average case mein around 1 lakh per animal tak ho sakti hain. Iske sath-sath heifers ko rakhne ke liye land bhi chahiye hogi. 100 heifers ko rakhne ke liye roughly 8,000 square feet area required hota hai. Aur baqi infrastructure jaise shed, milking parlor, etc. sab aa jaate hain, to ye around 2.5 crore ki investment hoti hai. Agar hum ek broad scenario dekhein, to 100 animals ke scale par total capital requirement 14 se 16 crore ki range mein aa sakti hai. Aur ek average heifer ek lactation cycle mein around 10,000 liters aapko milk produce karke degi, lekin actual output genetics, feed quality aur herd management par depend karta hai. Aur isi ke mutabiq next five years mein, five cycles aapko ek animal se total around 50,000 liters milk mil jayega agar health aur productivity stable rahe. So multiple lactation cycles ke dauran, cumulative production is estimate ke around ho sakti hai. Lekin time ke sath-sath, specially fourth year ke baad, ye yield naturally decline ho jaati hai. So hypothetically, har year aapko 100 animals se 1 million liters ke around milk milega aur Rs. 150 se 160 per liter ke hisab se agar aap iski price assume karein, aapka revenue ho gaya 150 se 160 million. Aur feed plus farm expenses estimate karein agar using the same formula of around Rs. 1 lakh per animal per month, to aapke expenses ho gaye taqreeban 120 million, jaise ke aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain. So ye aapne samajh liya ke revenue potential largely milk pricing structure, regional demand aur procurement contracts par depend karta hai. Iske sath-sath feed aur operational costs bhi thodi bohot fluctuate karti rehti hain, isliye net margins har farm ke liye thode se different ho sakte hain. Aaiye aap se ek aur important cheez share karte hain. Ek cow ki milking life five years ki hoti hai aur un paanch saalon mein five animals bhi degi. Corporate dairy farms mainly artificial sexed semen techniques female calf ki birth probability ko increase karne ke liye use karte hain. In female calves ko future herd replacement ya sale ke liye use kiya jaa sakta hai. So ye jo five years mein aapke paas five animals aate hain, ye ek additional cash flow ka source bhi ban sakte hain. So ab aapko dairy business owners ki tarah samajh aa gaya hai. And now it is time for some expert insights about dairy industry. Sabse pehle packaged milk par 18% sales tax ke importance ko samajhna hai, kyunki ye government ki policy har kuch saal baad badalti rehti hai aur ye is industry ke liye make or break hai. Jaise maine aapko bataya ke 92 se 94% industry jo hai, wo informal sector mein hai, wo koi sales tax pay nahi karti, wo koi income tax pay nahi karti, aur jo baqi industry hai jo ke formal sector mein hai, usko 18% sales tax bhi pay karna padta hai aur apne profits pe income tax bhi pay karna padta hai. Aur obviously, doodh ek packaged aur ek khula doodh, un donon mein jo difference hai, wo ek hadd se zyada badh nahi sakta, warna log jo hain, khule doodh ki taraf shift hone lag jayenge. To jab bhi ye government ka sales tax packaged milk pe apply hota hai, to ek unka per liter jo profit hota hai, wo bhi kam hone lag jata hai, aur unka market share bhi kam hone lag jata hai. To ye ek double whammy hoti hai is industry ke liye. Sales bhi kam hone ke chances hote hain, aur uske sath-sath profit per liter bhi kam hone ke chances hote hain. Baqi industries mein bhi Pakistan mein formal aur informal sector hota hai, lekin kyunki unke raw materials imported hote hain, to aapko port pe to sales tax pay karna hi padta hai, to 18 ke bajaye 8 se leke 12% tak sales tax informal sector bhi indirectly de raha hota hai. To jo farq hota hai between formal and informal sector, wo kaafi zyada kam hota hai. Lekin milk industry mein kyunki koi import nahi hota, sab kuch local hota hai, to ye jo 18% ka difference aur phir uske upar income tax, ye farq bohot zyada hai. Pichle kuch budgets mein ye topic bohot zyada limelight mein rehta hai, aur bohot zyada pressure bhi hota hai government ke upar ke is sales tax ko kam kiya jaye, kyunki milk ek essential commodity hai. So next budget mein agar is percentage ko kam kiya jata hai, to wo is industry ke liye ek positive kadam hoga. Iske elawa dairy companies apne farm ke paas fertile land bhi buy karte hain, taake wo wahan par in-house feed grow kar sakein. Feed ki shortage, quality aur badhti prices ki wajah se farms ab in-house fodder ki taraf move kar rahe hain. Ek acre land almost itna fodder ya feed grow kar sakti hai ke around teen se chaar cows ke liye enough ho. Ghani Dairies ke paas almost 54 acre land hai. Aur inke mutabiq, ye land itni hai ke Ghani Dairies almost 10 se 20% tak fodder ki need in-house fulfill kar sakti hai. Ab aate hain dairy companies ki income statement ki taraf. Aksar logon ko inki financial statements ki samajh nahi aati. Aur ye aapko baqi normal companies se kaafi different lagti hongi. Aaiye income statement ko samajhte hain aur dekhte hain ke inko padhne ka sahi tareeqa kya hai. Agar hum Ghani Dairies ke FY25 ke results ko dekhein, to ismein gross profit se pehle teen heads aate hain. First hai Revenue from contract with customers. Ye aapko batata hai ke Ghani Dairies ne kitna milk apne customers ko sale kiya, jaise Nestle, IRC, aur Fauji Foods ko. Second item hai Gain on initial recognition of milk. Accounting rules mein, in-house produce hone wala milk as part of revenue recognize hota hai, but ye item phir aage chal ke Cost of sales mein minus bhi ho jata hai. Means iska net effect zero hai, aap isko, in short, ignore bhi kar sakte hain, kyunki ye item revenue mein pehle add aur phir cost of sales mein deduct bhi hota hai. Third item kaafi interesting hai. Ye unrealized gain hota hai immature calves ki age badhne se related, kyunki aap non-milking animals ki feed ki cost expenses mein add kar rahe hain, to phir jaise-jaise ye animals bade ho rahe hote hain, unki value bhi badh rahi hoti hai. To wo gain in value yahan pe aa jata hai. Herd size increase hona aur jaise-jaise immature animals ki age badhti hai, unki value badhti hai, to ye company ki asset base ko increase karte hain. Aur ye yahan pe is head mein reflect kiya jata hai. But cash profit ki calculation mein isko aap ignore kar sakte hain. Aap table mein dekh sakte hain ke kaise one month old female cow ki price over the next few months increase hoti hai. So kabhi bhi kisi bhi quarter mein agar ye gain bohot zyada ho, to ye normalized profits nahi hain. Phir aapne us quarter ki earnings pe PE ratio apply nahi karna.
