Interviewer: Acha foreign currency ke andar bhi bohat ziada log invest kar letay hain, koi ruble le letay hain, koi jo dollar le letay hain, koi yuan le letay hain, euros khareed letay hain. To us ke andar bhi hai koi potential ya nahi?

ARN: Nahi, iska to bohat bura record hai. Matlab aap ye jo char, panch option hotay hain na, agar us mein dollar ko bhi daal dein, kisi ne aap ke paise das guna kiye hain bank mein paise rakhay hotay pachees saalon mein, kisi ne pachaas guna kiye hain, stock market ne bohat ziada kiye hain. To dollar mein agar aap ne rakha hota to wo pachaas, sath rupay se le ke abhi tak sirf do sau assi ka hua hai. Sirf paanch guna paise kiye hain, to ye to aap ka ek tarah se nuksaan ho raha hai. Sirf us time pe jab thora sa devaluation ka risk lag raha ho aur aap ko lag raha ho ke idhar jo hai dollar reserves bohat ziada kam ho gaye hain, us waqt sirf agar kisi ke paas ho, bhai dollar agar koi exporter hai ya bahar earn karta hai to behtar hota hai ke thora time ke liye aap us ko rupee mein convert na karein. Long-term investment mein to currencies ka role hai hi nahi.
