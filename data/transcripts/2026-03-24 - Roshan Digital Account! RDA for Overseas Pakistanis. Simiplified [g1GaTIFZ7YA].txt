Interviewer: Jo Pakistan se baahar baithe hain Pakistani, woh kaise investment karein? Woh kya us mulk ke andar karein jahan pe reh rahe hain ya woh bhi Pakistan ke andar investment kar sakte hain?

ARN: Nahi, overseas Pakistanis ka toh Pakistan ki economy mein bohat zyada role hai, remittances itne zyada bhejte hain aur mere hisab se toh mohabbat bhi woh Pakistan se zyada karte hain. Toh 2020 mein, us waqt ke State Bank ke governor hote hain, Reza Baqir. Unhone ek system design kiya tha poora, Roshan Digital Account. Uske through overseas Pakistani bhi investment kar sakte hain. Bilkul koi risk nahi hai ke aapke koi paise le ke bhaag jayega, jaise aap real estate mein invest karte hain, kabhi kabhi broker paise le ke bhaag jata hai, plot idhar udhar ho jata hai...

Interviewer: Plot par qabza ho jata hai.

ARN: Baahar baith ke aapka account khulega, aapko aane ki zaroorat nahi hai, koi sign karne ke liye Pakistan aane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aapke jo shares honge, uska custodian aapka brokerage house nahi hoga, CDC hoga. CDC hukumat ka idara hai toh custody point of view se aap secure hain, technology bhi poori secure hai aur government ne aap se waada kiya hua hai ke jab bhi aap wapis le ke jana chahenge foreign currency, aap le ke ja sakte hain.
