Sabse pehle samajhte hain ke flat steel kis tarah banaya jata hai. Shuru mein HRC yaani ke hot rolled coil ko clean kiya jata hai taake us par lagi oxide layer remove ho jaye. Uske baad coil ko 80 to 100 degrees tak pre-heat kiya jata hai. Ye heating steel ko melt nahi karti sirf moisture remove karti hai aur rolling ke liye material ko stable banati hai. Phir HRC ko re-rolling mill mein dala jata hai jahan heavy rollers uski thickness kam karte hain. Room temperature par pressure apply karke steel ko thin aur smooth banaya jata hai. Isi process ke baad final product cold rolled coil yaani ke CRC ready ho jata hai. CRC, HRC ke muqable mein zyada smooth, strong aur precise thickness ka hota hai. Isliye ye automobiles, home appliances jaise ke fridge, washing machine, panels aur transformers mein use kiya jata hai. Aam taur par CRC ki value 500 se 550 dollars per ton ke aas paas hoti hai. CRC ki mazeed processing ke baad bohat se value-added products bante hain jaise ke galvanized aur color-coated steel. Galvanized steel banane ke liye CRC ko molten zinc mein dip kiya jata hai jisse us par zinc ki protective layer lag jati hai jo use rust se bachati hai. Galvanized iron sheet ko roofing, pipes aur construction mein use kiya jata hai. Color-coated steel mein galvanized sheet par primer aur paint coat lagaya jata hai aur color-coated steel sheets appliances aur decorative panels mein use hoti hain. So is business mein HRC main raw material hai aur total production cost ka sabse bada hissa bhi hai aur overall cost ka 70 to 80% ke around contribute karta hai. Flat steel companies ki profitability asal mein HRC aur CRC ke darmiyan price spread par depend karti hai kyunki unka core business HRC ko process karke CRC, galvanized aur color-coated steel banana hai. Agar prices ki baat karein to HRC ki international price aam taur par $450 se $470 per ton ki range mein rehti hai, jabke CRC, HRC se around 70 to 80 dollars upar trade karta hai. Isi wajah se flat steel business mein invested capital sirf plant cost tak limit nahi hota balki working capital bhi meaningful role play karta hai. Agar spread compress ho jaye to margins naturally tight ho jate hain. And now it's time for some expert insights when investing in flat steel companies. Steel ki demand mein location bohat important role play karti hai. Aisha Steel aur International Steel dono ke plants Karachi mein located hain. Karachi mein hone ki wajah se companies local demand aur exports dono ka fayda utha leti hain. Port ke zariye USA, Europe aur Canada tak easily exports kar sakti hain without any additional freight cost. Aaiye ek aur positive development aap se share karte hain aur wo hai galvalume par duties ka lagna. Market mein pehle importers China se galvalume import karke Pakistan mein dump karte the, jisse local companies ki pricing par negative effect hota tha. Galvalume asal mein ek type ka coated steel hai jismein zinc aur aluminum ka mixture hota hai. Ye coating steel ko rust aur corrosion se bachati hai aur iski life normal galvanized steel se zyada hoti hai. Lekin ab National Tariff Commission of Pakistan ne different companies par galvalume import ke liye 40% tak tariffs impose kar diye hain. Specific countries aur un par lagaye gaye tariffs ki detail aap apni screen par dekh sakte hain. FY25 mein total overall Pakistan ki local flat steel ki demand to increase hui, lekin galat imports aur exemptions ki wajah se importers ne 50 to 60% market share capture kar liya tha, jo ab aahista aahista local companies ko wapas mil raha hai. Ek aur important factor hai IMF ka duty protection plan. International Monetary Fund Pakistan mein tariff rationalization push kar raha hai, jisse import duties gradually kam ki ja sakti hain aur iski reflection aapne pichle budget mein bhi dekhi hogi. Filhaal finished flat steel par taqreeban 31% duty hai jo local manufacturers ko strong protection deti hai jaise maine aapko pehle numbers mein bhi explain kiya. Kyunki imported material mehnga padta hai aur local companies international price se upar bhi comfortably sell kar sakti hain. Agar future mein tariff rationalization implement hoti hai next few years mein aur ye duties reduce hoti hain, to pricing dynamics change ho sakti hain, jiske natije mein imported steel ki landed cost kaafi kam ho sakti hai aur ise market mein competition barh jayega. Iska seedha pressure local companies ki pricing par padega. Aur most probably local companies ko apni prices adjust karni padengi jisse pricing dynamics aur margins par pressure aa sakta hai jo companies ki earnings aur profitability ko impact kar sakti hain. Aaiye aap ko duty protection se related ek important cheez samjhta hoon. Duty protection hai to taqreeban 30%, lekin agar aap sochein commodity super cycle chal raha ho aur CRC ki price kuch arse ke liye 800 dollars per ton ho jaye, to aapko taqreeban 240 dollars per ton ki duty protection mil jati hai. Lekin wahi CRC agar kabhi low commodity cycle mein 400 dollars ka ho jaye to ye duty protection jo hai wo 120 dollars per ton tak chali jati hai. To jitna zyada CRC mehnga hota hai utna zyada duty protection ka impact jo hai wo local companies enjoy karti hain. To aapne is business ko samajhne mein ek aur cheez analyze kar li ke CRC ki price kya chal rahi hai, iski bhi bohat zyada importance hai.
