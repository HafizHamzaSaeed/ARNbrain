ARN: Agar aap stock market ko ek dukan samjhein na, toh shayad duniya ki ek wahid dukan hogi ke jis mein jab sale lagti hai na, toh log dukan se bahar aane lagte hain. Speaker 2: Acha, jis tarah trading mein hota hai ke aap raaton raat ameer bhi ho sakte hain aur apna saara sarmaya dubo bhi sakte hain. Kya ye market crash kar sakti hai jis tarah trading mein ho jata hai?

ARN: Acha, is mein pehle toh ye jo market crash wali term hai na, ye bohot hi loose tareeqe se use ki jaati hai headlines mein bhi, news channels pe bhi. Aap sochein aap ke paas ek plot hai, sahi hai? Ab wo plot let's say ek crore rupay ka tha. Kisi din aap ko aa ke agar kisi ne us ki offer 97 lakh rupees de di, toh aap kahein ge yaar theek hai, ek crore aur 97 lakh mein kitna ko farq hai. Lekin stock market mein jab hamara index dedh lakh pe hota hai aur us mein paanch hazar point kisi wajah se ek din gir jaate hain, jo ke wahi same amount banti hai 3% ke around, toh headlines lag jati hain 'crash ho gaya'. Jab bhi is tarah ki situation ho na, toh teen cheezon ka aap ko khayal rakhna chahiye. Ek toh aap ne jis company mein invest kiya hua hai us ko aap ne kaise follow karna hai, doosra aap ne na bull market aur bear market ke beech mein farq samajhna hai. Kabhi agar bull market chal rahi hai, ache returns aa rahe hain aur koi market thodi si neeche aa jaati hai, toh wo ek correction hoti hai. Bohot zyada stock market badhti hai, phir log thodi si profit taking karte hain, wo thodi si neeche aa jaati hai, us ne phir se badhna hota hai. Lekin jab bear market hoti hai toh wo trend jo hai continue rehta hai, aur teesra angle hota hai phir emotional.
