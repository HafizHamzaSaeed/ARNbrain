Assalam-o-Alaikum. Aaj se kuch din pehle maine ek badi zabardast book jo hai, woh parhni shuru ki aur main kaafi isko enjoy bhi kar raha hoon. Maine kaha aaj aapse share karoon. Bade hi mere ek kareebi dost aur bade pyare dost ne mujhe recommend ki thi aaj se kuch mahine pehle, aur ab mujhe finally time mila isko parhne ka. Yeh book hai, What I Learned About Investing from Darwin.

Bohot hi zabardast book hai, aur isme basically, maqsad hai apni investing ko mazaid kaise improve kar sakte hain. Jo hum choti choti galtiyan kar, kar dete hain investing mein, usko mazaid improve kaise kiya ja sakta hai, bade acche tareeqe se isko explain kiya gaya hai. Toh aap mein se agar kisi ne yeh book parhi hui hai, toh zaroor comments mein share karein ke aapko is book mein kya cheez acchi lagi, aur jin log... aur jin logon ne isko abhi tak nahi parha, aur woh investing ke liye kaafi zyada passionate hain, apni reading list mein isko add kar lein.
