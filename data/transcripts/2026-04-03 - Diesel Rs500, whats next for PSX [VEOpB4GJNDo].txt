But yeh thi first order thinking. Second order thinking yeh hai ke in fuel par subsidy dena jo hai macroeconomic stability aapki usko kharab kar deta hai. Aur aap yeh zyada lambe arse tak nahi de sakte, aapke reserves bhi khatam ho jayein ge aur aapka budget bhi bilkul out ho jayega. Isi wajah se meri apni bhi analysis thi aur aaj market ne bhi usko second kiya ke is se PSX ki outlook behtar ho jati hai.
