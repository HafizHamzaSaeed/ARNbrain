Auto sector mein har din nayi gaariyan launch ho rahi hain. Kabhi koi company jo hai apni gaari launch kar rahi hai, kabhi luxury gaari aa rahi hai, kabhi mid-size SUV launch ho rahi hai. Lekin ek aur sector aisa hai ke wahan pe ek clear company jo hai woh market ko dominate kar rahi hai. Agar aap trucks ki industry ko detail mein analyze karein, toh Isuzu ke trucks bhi bohot popular hain. Aur Pakistan mein axle load lagne ke baad, pichle do saalon mein trucks ki sale gaariyon se zyada speed se barh rahi hai. Pakistan economy mein aage mining ke bhi bohot acche prospects nazar aa rahe hain. So, in sab developments ko dekhte hue, truck segment ko cover karne ke liye, hum logon ne YouTube channel pe ek detailed video banayi hai, jismein humne truck segment ki number one market share wali company, Ghandhara Industries ko bhi detail mein cover kiya hai. Is company ne latest announcement bhi ki hai ke ab ye Pakistan mein local bus manufacturing bhi start kar rahe hain, jis aspect ko bhi humne detail mein discuss kiya hai. Aapne ye video humare YouTube channel pe jaa kar dekhni hai, aur humein comments mein batana hai, ke Ghandhara Industries jaise Isuzu mein market lead karta hai, usi tareeqe se kya ye buses mein bhi agle saal mein ek dominant player ban jayeingi?
