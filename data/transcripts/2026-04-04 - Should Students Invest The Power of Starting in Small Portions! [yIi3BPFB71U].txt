Abdul Rehman Najam (ARN): Kis age pe aapko invest kar dena chahiye? Aur student life ke andar jahan pe aapke paas bilkul thore se paise hote hain, kya aap recommend karte hain ke bhai ek ek hazar nikalo, do do hazar... jo faltu mein idhar udhar kharche karte ho, uske bajaye agar stock mein invest karein?

Guest: Bilkul 100% investing ka aur financial literacy ka ek aur bahut important rule hai: "The best time to start was yesterday and the second best time is today." To maine ek example batayi ke char saal mein aapke paise jo hain wo double ho jate hain. To agar let's say hum har mahine 20, 20 hazar nahi bhi kar rahe, hum 2000 kar lete hain. To aapne wahi 10-year ka jo plan hai agar aap 20,000 ke bajaye 2000 pe karenge to bhi 10 saal baad aapke paas almost jo hai 10 lakh rupay ho jayenge. Ek tarah se student life aapne khatam ki, apni university khatam ki, do do hazar rupay aap jo hai har mahine save kar rahe the lekin ab aapke paas 10 lakh rupay honge. Aap apna koi business start kar sakte hain ya aap jo hai kisi aur sheher mein jaake aapko opportunity mili hai lekin aapko jo hai thore se initial expenses karne ki zaroorat hai wahan pe, to wo sara ek aapke paas ek tarah se fund available hoga.
