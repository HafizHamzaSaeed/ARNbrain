Fatima Fertilizer Limited do bade industrial houses Fatima Group aur Arif Habib Group ka ek joint venture hai. Fertilizer Policy 2001 ne is company ki buniyaad rakhi. Government ne dekha ke Pakistan ki fertilizer demand jo hai woh badhti ja rahi hai aur existing plants is growing demand ko meet karne ke liye nakaafi hain. To naye investors ko attract karne ke liye policy ne new entrants ko concessional rate par gas provide ki for 10 years. Fatima Fertilizer ne isi mauqe ka faayda uthaya aur teen products: Urea, CAN, aur NP ke saath ek full-spectrum fertilizer plant mein investment ki. Ek policy, do strong groups, teen products, ye hai Fatima ki foundation.

So pehli important baat. Fatima Fertilizer sirf Urea par dependent nahi hai. Ye CAN aur NP mein bhi market leader hai. Ye baat choti lagti hai lekin business economics mein bohat important hai. Ab main aapko ek ek karke in teenon fertilizer products ko owners ki tarah explain karta hoon.

Urea Pakistan ka sab se zyaada istemaal hone wala nitrogen fertilizer hai. Jiski annual demand 6 se lekar 6.5, kabhi 6.7 million metric tons hai. Industry estimates ke mutabiq Fatima ka share roughly 14% se lekar 18% ke kareeb hai. Lekin Pakistan ka sab se mashhoor fertilizer, Urea banta kaise hai? Aaiye aaj main aapko bohat simple alfaaz mein samjhata hoon. Reports ke mutabiq ek ton Urea banane ke liye total takreeban 27 se 28 gas ke units lagte hain. Gas cost of production ka almost 60 to 70% hissa hai, jo directly profits ko affect karta hai.

Pakistan mein Urea ki production capacity bhi around 6.5 se lekar 7 million tons hai. Yaani is demand environment mein local production utilization generally high rehti hai. Maamooli imports sirf tab hote hain jab seasonal demand high ho ya phir koi plant jaise turnaround ya maintenance pe chala jaaye. Aur yehi cheez Fatima jaise local producers ke liye structural advantage ban jaati hai.

Ab sochein agar kabhi gas price badh jaaye to kya hoga? Inke margins tab bhi zyaada affect nahi hote. Aap soch rahe honge aisa kyun? Kyunki local Urea ab bhi imported Urea ke muqaable mein sasta hai. Commodity markets mein local aur imported Urea mein average 2,500 se 3,000 per bag ka price difference rehti hai. Aur ab to ye difference mazed badh jayega global oil shock ki wajah se kyunki duniya mein gas mehangi ho rahi hai lekin local gas ke rates more or less fixed hain. So price bhi kam hai versus imports aur supply bhi demand ke baraabar hai. Isliye agar kabhi gas price let's say badh jaati hai to industry structure ki wajah se is increased cost ko product pricing mein pass on kar diya jata hai. Ye ek natural pricing shield hai jo sirf local producers ko milti hai. Isi wajah se jab Pakistan mein devaluation crisis bhi aaye, tab bhi profits aur dividends fertilizer sector ke negatively affect nahi hue.

Ab baat karte hain doosre product Calcium Ammonium Nitrate ki. Ye ek balanced aur Urea se kam nitrogen content wala fertilizer hai. Is ko banane ka process aur iski production cost bhi Urea se similar hai kyunki iska bhi main raw material natural gas hi hai. Per ton CAN banane ke liye takreeban 15 gas units lagte hain along with calcium. Pakistan mein sirf Fatima hi CAN banata hai. Jiski wajah se inhe koi direct local competition face nahi karna padta. Farmers is product ko Urea se ek discount pe khareedte hain kyunki is mein kam nitrogen content hai. So raw materials bhi local aur pricing bhi local, Urea se linked hai, is structure ki wajah se is product ke margins bhi relatively stable rehte hain.

Ab baat karte hain Fatima ke premium product NP, Nitrogen Phosphate ki, jo ke DAP yaani ke Di-ammonium Phosphate ki prices se linked hai. Iska main raw material natural gas aur Phosphate Rock hai. Per ton NP banane ke liye takreeban 12 units of gas aur 0.7 tons of Phosphate Rock lagta hai. Phosphate Rock import hota hai so agar imported Phosphate ki prices badh jayein to NP ki production cost bhi badh jayegi. Lekin saath hi DAP bhi global market mein mehanga ho jata hai. Aur NP Pakistan mein DAP ki prices se discount par chalta hai kyunki ye dono Phosphorus fertilizers hain. DAP ki pricing international market se set hoti hai Pakistan mein kyunki aaj bhi DAP import hota hai. Aur NP kyunki isse linked hai, globally oil shock ki wajah se agar NP mehanga ho to Fatima Fertilizer ke margins par positive impact hone ke chances hote hain jaise ke 2021 aur 2022 mein hue during the commodity super cycle.

Fatima Fertilizer ki intrinsic value samajhne ke liye iske manufacturing plants ki detail ko samajhna bohat zaroori hai. Fatima Fertilizer ke teen manufacturing plants hain. Main plant Sadiqabad, Rahim Yar Khan mein hai. Ye Urea, CAN aur NP teeno banata hai. Is plant ki capacity 5 lakh ton Urea, 4 lakh 70 thousand ton CAN aur 3 lakh 60 thousand metric ton NP hai annually. Is plant ko 110 MMCFD dedicated gas, Mari gas field se low rate par milti hai. Jabke February 2024 mein reported policy revisions ke baad kai fertilizer companies ke liye gas ka rate Rs. 580 se badh kar almost Rs. 1600 per MMBTU ho gaya tha lekin tab bhi FFC aur Fatima ka jo ye wala plant hai iske liye gas ka rate 580 per MMBTU hi raha kyunki inke contracts direct Mari Petroleum se hain, SNGPL ya SSGC se nahi. Ye ek bada important point hai.

Doosra plant Fatima Fertilizer ka, jo ke Multan, Punjab mein hai, ye bhi teen products Urea, CAN aur NP banata hai. Annual capacity 92,000 metric ton Urea, 4 lakh 50 thousand metric ton CAN, aur takreeban 3 lakh metric ton NP hai. Ye plant bhi Mari gas network se connected hai lekin ise gas SNGPL ki pipeline ke zariye PP12 petroleum policy ke mutabiq milti hai, jiss ka rate abhi takreeban $5.6 hai. Jisko rupees mein convert karein to ye wahi takreeban 1600 rupees per MMBTU ban jata hai.

Third aur last plant, Chichoki Mallian, Sheikhupura mein hai. Yahan sirf Urea banta hai. Jiski annual capacity almost 4.5 lakh metric ton hai. Ye plant SNGPL network par hai aur imported RLNG par chalta hai. Gas price aur supply issue ki wajah se ye plant kabhi kabhaar band bhi ho jata hai lekin ye masla ab future ke liye hal ho chuka hai, Mari se new gas allocation approval ke baad jiski hum aage detail mein baat bhi karenge.

Pichle kuch saalon mein management ne apne capital ko diversify karna shuru kar diya tha, jiss ka asar company ki other income mein bhi nazar aata hai. 23 December 2025 Pakistan ki economy ke liye ek historic din tha aur takreeban 20 saalon mein hone wali Pakistan ki sab se badi privatization deal hui. Jiss mein Fatima Fertilizer bhi shaamil thi. Reports ke mutabiq PIA ke paas 18 planes hain aur fleet ko agle 4 saalon mein 38 se 40 aircraft tak expand karne ka plan discuss kiya ja raha hai aur passengers ki taadaad bhi 4 million se badh kar roughly 7 million tak estimate ki ja rahi hai. Ab agar mining sector ki taraf aayein to Fatima Fertilizer ka 33.3% stake National Resources Limited mein bhi hai. Arif Habib Group ki associated company Javedan Corporation Limited Karachi mein Naya Nazimabad jaisa large-scale real estate development project chala rahi hai, jiss ki wajah se Fatima Fertilizer ko real estate ka bhi indirect exposure milta hai. Iske ilawa direct investment ke taur par Fatima Fertilizer ke shareholders ne 22 September 2023 ki AGM mein Rupees 7.66 billion ki REIT investments ko approve kiya jiss se Arif Habib Dolmen REIT Management investments manage kar rahi hai. Pichle kaafi saalon se Sheikhupura plant Fatima ka sab se problematic child raha hai. Wajah: Ye plant SNGPL network pe tha jahan gas supply kabhi bhi band ho jaati thi. Rate baar baar badhta tha aur har quarter ECC se approval lena padta tha. Basically jab Urea demand kam thi aur government inko tabhi operate karti hai jab zaroorat hoti thi. Ab picture badal rahi hai. Urea ki demand teen char saal se 6.5 million tak aa rahi hai. Aur isi ko dekhte hue Government of Pakistan ne Mari field ke Ghazi aur Shawal gas discoveries se seedha Fatima Fert Limited ke Sheikhupura plant ko dedicated gas allocation de di hai jiss se 52 MMscfd process gas direct milegi, matlab stable supply woh bhi OGRA regulated rate par.

Expert perspective se ye development Sheikhupura plant ke liye ek important structural change ke taur par dekhi ja rahi hai aur ye structural shift Sheikhupura plant ki long-term viability ko significantly improve karne ka potential rakhti hai. Is gas supply ka infrastructure jaise ke pipelines install karne mein takreeban dedh se do saal lagenge. Iske saath ek aur important development Fatima ki corporate restructuring ki hai. Pehle Fatima ke teeno plants ek hi company ke under operate kar rahe the lekin ab is structure ko separate kar diya gaya hai. July 2024 mein Sheikhupura plant ko alag karke Fatima Fert Limited bana diya gaya, jo Fatima Fertilizer ki wholly-owned subsidiary hai. Aur phir January 2025 mein Multan plant ka demerger initiate hua jise November 2025 mein Lahore High Court ne approve kar diya. Is approval ke baad Multan plant ko Pakarab Fertilizers Limited mein transfer kiya ja sakta hai. Is restructuring se investors ko zyaada transparency milegi aur har subsidiary ki performance ko independently evaluate kiya ja sakega aur shayad future mein potential strategic options jaise IPO bhi consider kiya ja sakta hai different companies ke liye kyunki Arif Habib Group ka stock market se kaafi gehra taaluq hai. In dono developments, new gas allocation aur corporate restructuring ko agar saath dekha jaye, to Fatima ek leaner, more efficient, aur structurally stronger company ban kar saamne aa raha hai. Stable aur relatively sasti gas supply se production cost kam ho sakti hain, margins improve ho sakte hain, aur plants apni full capacity par operate kar sakte hain.
