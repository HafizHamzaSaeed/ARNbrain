**ARN**: Company acchi hoti hai, uska business theek ja raha hota hai, usme value hoti hai, wo bear market mein bhi aapko profit de jati hai. To ye jo poora do saal ka arsa tha ye bear market ka tha, index jo hamara neeche aa raha tha. Lekin is company ne almost 100% profit mujhe kama ke diya.

**Interviewer**: Aapne khud aaj tak sabse zyada profit kis trade mein banaya?

**ARN**: Ek aisi example share karna chahunga jo meri thodi se shuru ke dino ki thi aur wo mere close to heart bhi hai kyunki usme learning bahut zyada thi. Stock market kyunki anticipatory hoti hai wo har din jo hai na crash karti ja rahi thi 2020 ke shuru mein. To ek company mein maine invest kiya hua tha Nishat Power. Ab, ye jaise IPPs ko log samajhte hain Pakistan mein ke inke fixed contract hote hain government ke sath inko fixed paise milne hote hain ye chalein ya na chalein. To baqi saari companies neeche ja rahi thi, cement ki company neeche ja rahi thi, maine kaha chalo theek hai samajh aata hai ke shayad cement ka business jo hai wo band ho jaye ek do mahine ke liye, losses hone lag jayein. Lekin iska to fixed contract hai government ke sath, isko kya farq parta hai? Wohi emotions sabke andar aaye hue the aur roz usme jo hai na lower lock lagte ja rahe the. Ek din price jo hai na bahut hi attractive ho gayi, mujhe abhi bhi yaad hai 25 lakh rupaye ki usme investment ki thi. Jaise hi cheezein thodi behtar hui to wo badhna shuru ho gaya lekin maine usko foran sell nahi kiya. Jo hai maine poora system samjha Pakistan ka ke bijli ka kya system hai, companies ko kis tareeqe se paise milte hain, kaun si company kaun se fuel se bijli banati hai aur 2022 mein ja ke jab maine usko sell kiya almost double price pe, isko khareeda tha maine koi 20 rupaye ki average pe lekin jab sell kiya to ye 35 se 40 rupaye ka tha aur us arse mein dividend bhi isne kaafi zyada diye the.
