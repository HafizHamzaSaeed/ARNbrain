**Interviewer:** Magar abhi jo ek recession ki baat ki ja rahi hai ke given state of hum thode se arse ke liye bhi agar control ruk jaye, us ka ek ripple effect wo jo recession hamesha US se trigger hota hai, aap ko lagta hai ke wo Pakistan mein translate karega?

**ARN:** Acha, interesting baat ye hai ke jab global recession aata hai na, to wo Pakistan ke liye positive hota hai. Aur wo hota is wajah se hai ke abhi to war ki wajah se oil mehnga hua wa hai, but agar ye cheez global recession ki taraf chali jati hai, aur saare jo duniya ke bade mulk hain un mein production jo hai commodities ki kam hone lag jati hai to oil kam use hoga. To eventually, jab ye war premium oil price mein se nikal jayega, to oil price jo hai neeche aa jati hai. Pakistan pe positive impact is tarike se hota hai ke chaar commodities aisi hoti hain duniya mein jo ke recession mein boht kam istemal hone lag jati hain, ye workhorses hain. Ek oil, gas, coal aur steel. Aur jab recession hoga to in sab ki prices jo hain neeche aane lag jati hain. Aur Pakistan in chaaro cheezon ka importer hai. Matlab in sab ko agar mila lein to hum shayad 20 billion dollar ek saal mein ye cheezain import karte hain, aur in ki price agar thodi si bhi kam ho to humein us ka boht zyada faida ho jata hai. To global recession wale time pe to oil prices jaise kam ho jayengi to wo to Pakistan ke liye ek boht achi cheez hoti hai. But agar is ko hum abhi ke case ke hisab se dekhein, let's say ye war continue rehti hai, oil prices bhi upar rehti hain aur recession bhi aa jata hai, to phir for the time being government ke liye bada zaroori hoga ke jo price hai na us ko pass on karte rahein.
