**Speaker 1:** Aise kaun si top ten companies hain jo aap is waqt recommend karenge?

**ARN:** Aapne do cheezein dekhni hain sirf ke kis ka raw material jo hai wo oil se linked hai. Doosra factor ke kaun si company hai jo ke na transportation mein jis ka bahut zyada kharcha hota hai, automobile sector se. Us mein ek ye risk hota hai ke jab let's say aapka import bill badh raha hai aur agar Government of Pakistan ye price increase nahi bhi karti aur ye current account deficit bahut zyada badh jata hai, aapke paas dollars kam hote hain, to phir shayad aapko thode mahino ke liye import restrictions lagani padti hain. To jo companies thoda sa oil zyada explore karti hain compared to gas jis mein OGDC aa jati hai, to us ko zyada benefit hoga. Is ke baad refineries bahut zyada jo hai beneficiaries hain. Banks bilkul affect nahi hota. Is tarah se hum fertilizer sector pe aa jate hain. Fertilizer sector mein gas bilkul use hoti hai lekin jo local gas hai, us ki price jo hai fixed hai. Is ke alawa jo pharmaceutical sector hota hai, wo bhi un ke bhi jo saare raw materials hote hain wo oil price se linked hote hain. So saari cheezon ko agar conclude karein, oil and gas exploration companies aur refineries are the beneficiaries. Banks, fertilizer sector aap keh sakte hain ke neutral, unchanged. Import-based companies aur cement companies, ye negatively affect hongi. To is mein aapko opportunity kam price pe us cheez pe focus karna chahiye.
