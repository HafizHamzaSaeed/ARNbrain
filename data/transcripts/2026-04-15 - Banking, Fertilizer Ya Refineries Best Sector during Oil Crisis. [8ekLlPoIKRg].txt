To is wajah se kuch banks hote hain jahan log jaate hain khud jaate hain ke hamara account khul jaye, aur doosre banks hote hain jinke managers koshish kar rahe hote hain ke hum bank ko grow kar rahe hain, to hum logo ko reach out karein, bade size ke deposits jo hain usko attract karein. Both in terms of deposits and in terms of jo log usmein stock market ke through investment karte hain, usko zyada P/E ratio milta hai, ye saari cheezein isi se linked hoti hain.
