To shuru karte hain apne pehle concept se, importance of current accounts. Jaise ke humne pehle kuch banks ki videos mein bhi aap se share kiya hai, kisi bhi bank ki profitability determine karne ke liye kuch important factors hote hain aur un sab mein se sabse central factor hota hai current accounts. Bank ka business asal mein hota hai, jaise aap ko bhi idea hoga, logon se deposits lena aur aage loans de dena, aur beech mein jo paisa bach jaye woh bank ka margin ho jata hai. So agar bank ko deposits par koi profit depositor ko na dena pade, yaani ke current accounts, to naturally bank ke profits badh jayenge. Lekin agar bank ko deposits par return dena ho, yaani savings account, to bank ki income compromise hoti hai. Numbers se bhi is example ko samajh lete hain kyun ke yeh kaafi important hai. Aaj kal interest rate around 11% hai. Average loan rate yaani bank jis rate pe loans aage deta hai, woh 11 se 12 percent ke darmiyan hai. Current account par bank ko 0% return dena hota hai, jab ke savings account par around 8 to 9 percent. Yaani current account par jo spread banta hai bank ka woh hai 11 se 12 percent, jab ke savings par sirf teen se char percent. Matlab roughly teen guna zyada profit on current accounts versus savings account. Aise thodi aap aur main jab bank mein jaate hain account open karwane, to bankers poori koshish karte hain, hamein convince karte hain ke aap current account khulwa lein. Let's suppose, kisi bank ke paas ek hazaar arab rupaye ke deposits hain, aur pehle inke current accounts around 30% the. Aur interest rate 10% agar hum assume karein, aur baaki sab cheezein same rakhein, to 20% extra current account percentage hone se impact on bank's profits aap apni screen par dekh sakte hain. So yeh important factor aap note kar lein when analyzing bank's stocks. UBL ke scenario mein bhi kuch aisa hi hua. 2019 se 2020 mein current accounts jo ke 40% the, ab barh kar 50% ho chuke hain. Aur yeh kuch aise hua ke jab 2024 mein ADR tax laga banks pe, aur tamam banks ne apne deposits shred karne shuru kar diye to manage the tax impact, UBL ne bhi apne high cost savings corporate deposits ko reduce kiya, aur focus shift kar liyah low cost current accounts par.

Ab baat karte hain ke kaise koi bank current accounts badha sakta hai. Is concept ko samajhne ke liye hamein deposit franchise samajhni hogi. Ab yeh deposit franchise aasan lafzon mein kya hota hai? Do tarah ke banks hote hain. Ek jahan log khud chal kar jaate hain accounts khulwane ke liye, aur ek jinke staff logon ko reach out karte hain, baar baar convince karte hain, attract karne ki koshish karte hain, acchi offers dete hain ke aap hamare paas account khulwa lein. Deposit franchise un banks ko kehte hain jinke paas depositors khud chal kar aate hain apna paisa deposit karwane. Inki widespread branches bhi hoti hain, almost har jagah ATMs bhi hoti hain, to customer ke liye ease bhi hota hai. Aur log safe feel karte hain ke itna bada bank hai, meri savings yahan secure hain. Jab customers khud aa rahe hote hain, to deposits grow karna relatively obviously easy ho jata hai. Aur phir bank ke liye unhein convince karna aasan hota hai ke aap current account khulwa lein, specially agar interest rate bahut zyada na hon, taake logon ko jo comparison kar rahe hote hain ke kitna return milega, to agar interest rate kam hote hain to log kehte hain chalo, itna farq nahi padta, current account khulwa lete hain. Isi strategy ko use karte hue ab UBL Pakistan ka second largest bank hai by deposit size. Aur around 13 to 14 percent ka market share rakhta hai in deposits. UBL ne apne pichle do saalon mein apne deposits ko two times badha liya hai, aur December 2025 mein taqreeban 5,200 arab rupaye ke deposits tak pohanch gaya hai, with a healthy almost 50% current accounts. Ab main aap ko batata hoon 2024 mein UBL ki 4 trillion ki bet on interest rates coming down. UBL ki September 2024 ki corporate briefing presentation ke mutabiq, UBL ne taqreeban 4 trillion rupees ke fixed aur floating investments 19 to 21 percent ke rates par first quarter 2024 se lock karna shuru kar diye the. Yeh woh period tha jab policy rate apne peak par tha, aur market yields historically high level par mil rahi thi. Is strategy ke through bank ne taqreeban 20% ke around return ek saal ke liye secure kiya, jab ke doosri taraf yeh amount State Bank of Pakistan se short term basis par borrow ki, jiski initial months mein cost 21% ke around thi, but jaise jaise 2024 mein interest rate neeche aaye, to average borrowing cost roughly 15 se 16 percent ban gayi for 2024. Is tarah roughly 5% ka spread generate kar liya bank ne on rupees 4 trillion, jisko calculate karein to roughly 200 arab rupaye ke kareeb ban jata hai. Is calculative move ne bank ke profits ko mazeed one time boost diya. Agar interest rate is speed se neeche nahi aate aur inflation sharply decline nahi karti 2024 mein, to yeh windfall gains is scale pe realize nahi hote. Isliye yeh ek bold, lekin calculated macro bet thi ke Pakistan ki economy stabilize hogi aur inflation gradually control mein aayegi. Interest rates cut honge aur ab results dekh kar lagta hai ke management ki yeh macro call kaafi had tak sahi sabit hui.

Aksar log yeh samajhte hain, aur kuch saal pehle tak jab maine apni investing ki journey start ki thi, main bhi yahi sochta tha ke jab interest rate high hote hain to banks ka profit bhi high hota hai. Aur jab interest rate low ho jayein to profit girne chahiye. Aur logically share price bhi neeche aani chahiye. Lekin ground reality kaafi different hoti hai. Low interest rates mein banks ke share barh kaise gaye pichle do se teen saalon mein, is confusion ko door karne ke liye teen important points samajhne bahut zaroori hain. Aur jab mujhe bhi yeh samajh aaye, to meri confusion hamesha ke liye door ho gayi. Pehla point hai deposit growth. Low interest rate environment mein economy stimulate hoti hai, business activity badhti hai, aur system mein liquidity increase hoti hai, is wajah se deposits grow karte hain aur banks ki balance sheet expand hoti hai. Doosra point hai high rate par PIBs lock karna, aur current account percentage ka increase hona when interest rates are low. Banks jab high interest rate cycle mein long term PIBs lock kar lete hain, to low rate environment mein bhi unhein kuch saalon tak relatively high return milta rehta hai kyun ke unhone woh fixed rate pe lock kiye hote hain. Aur kyun ke customers low interest rates mein itna bother nahi karte, current account percentage bhi improve ho jati hai. History aur number bhi almost har bank ki yeh cheez show karte hain ke low interest rate environment mein aap ki current account percentage jo hai woh thodi si increase ho jati hai, jisse bank ka faida hota hai. Aur teesra point hai PE ratio. And this is the most powerful. Jaise Warren Buffett kehte hain, "Interest rates are like gravity for stock prices." Low interest rate environment mein companies ko high PE ratios milte hain, isliye even agar profits banks ke normalize ho bhi jayein, share price rally kar sakti hain.

UBL ne recently ek naya strategic angle explore kiya hai, jo traditional banking se thoda aage jata hai, aur woh hai Interest Rate Swap Transaction with Jazz. Yeh deal taqreeban 75 billion rupees ki thi, aur Pakistan ki sabse badi Interest Rate Swap Transaction thi. Simple lafzon mein samjhata hoon. Jazz ne apne loans ko UBL ke through fixed rate mein convert kar liya taake future mein interest rate volatility agar hui to unka business affect na ho, aur woh long term planning kar sakein. Is deal se yeh signal milta hai ke UBL sirf conventional leader nahi hai, balki advanced treasury capabilities bhi offer karne ka plan rakhta hai. Ownership aur governance angle se dekha jaye to UBL ka background kaafi strong hai. UBL ki history 1959 se start hoti hai, jab yeh private sector mein establish hua. 1974 mein nationalization ke baad yeh government control mein chala gaya, aur 2002 mein privatization ke through dobara private sector mein transfer hua. Isi phase mein Bestway Group ne controlling stake acquire kiya, jiske founder and owner Sir Anwar Pervez hain jo UK mein ek large-sized retail aur wholesale chain run karte hain, aur Pakistan mein bhi multiple investments ki hui hain. Bestway ka international footprint aur governance discipline UBL ke liye strong credibility create karta hai aur investors ka trust badhta hai. Iske ilawa UBL ne recently Town Hall initiative introduce kiya hai, jismein regional top bankers ko ikatha karke efficient banking operations par discussions aur feedback liya jata hai. Bank ne industry ke top branch managers ko bhi aggressively hire kiya hai, jinke paas large corporate aur high net worth deposits aur customers ka strong base hota hai. UBL aggressively new branches bhi open kar raha hai, aur recent annual report ke mutabiq, bank ki domestic branches 2,000 cross kar gayi hain. Aur aaj UBL ki sabse badi strength uska deposit franchise hai, aur strong brand positioning ki wajah se deposit inflows ki growth sustainable lagti hai. Bank actively zero cost debit cards aur digital onboarding solutions offer kar raha hai taake zyada se zyada customers ko facilities milein aur bank ko zyada se zyada current account deposits jo long term earnings ke liye beneficial hai.
