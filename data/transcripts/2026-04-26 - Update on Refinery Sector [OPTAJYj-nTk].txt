Refinery sector se related ek achi update ayi hai Government of Pakistan ke liye bhi, Pakistan ke citizens ke liye, aur refinery sector ke liye bhi. Government of Pakistan pehle diesel ki jo price hoti thi pump pe, woh aise decide karti thi ke jo international price chal rhi hai, usko rupees mein convert kar dena aur usme saare freight charges aur taxes jo bhi hain, usko convert karke rupees mein calculate kar leti thi. But ab aaj kal jaise oil agar let's say 120 dollars ka chal, to diesel jo hai woh 200 dollars se bhi upar chal raha tha. Toh yeh because of the global war aur shortages ki wajah se, ab Government of Pakistan ne yeh kiya hai ke jo bhi Dubai crude ki price chal rhi hogi, usme 42 dollars add honge aur yeh refinery margin jo hai diesel ka yahan pe cap ho jayega, isse diesel ki price mein almost 100 rupees ka benefit mil raha hai Pakistani citizens ko aur refineries ke liye bhi ek acha profit jo hai, woh secure ho gaya hai. Isme koi circular debt nahi hogi aur yeh ek achi development hai.
