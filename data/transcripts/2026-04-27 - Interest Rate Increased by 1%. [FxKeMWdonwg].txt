Assalam-o-Alaikum everyone. Pakistan mein aaj interest rates jo hai 1% se increase kar diye gaye aur ab ye 11.5% ho gaye hain. Already secondary markets mein is cheez ki expectation thi aur ek tarah se secondary markets mein jo interest rates the vo already 11% ke aas paas chal rahe the, toh ye koi bahut zyada negative cheez nahi hai stock market ke liye. Haan, ek iske alawa maine bahut ek interesting cheez jo hai MPC ki statement mein read ki aur vo ye hai kyunke government aaj kal petrol aur diesel par utna zyada tax collect nahi kar pa rahi toh uski wajah se unko apna PSDP jo hai vo cut karna padega aur ye ho sakta hai agar ye conflict continue rahe toh FY23 ke budget mein bhi ye cheez continue rahe aur us se phir cement sector pe bahut zyada negative effect jo hai vo ho sakta hai reduced PSDP spending ka.
