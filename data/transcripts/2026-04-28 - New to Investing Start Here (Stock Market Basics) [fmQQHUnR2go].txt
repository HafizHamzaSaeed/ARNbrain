Aksar hamein sunne ko milta hai ke stock market itne hazar points gir gayi ya itne hazar points charh gayi. Matlab kya hai iska? Aur aakhir ye stock market hoti kya hai?

Bhai stock market ki shayad sab se achhi baat ye hai ke ye waqayi ameer aur ghareeb mein koi farq nahi karti. Aap baghair kisi bari sifarish ke is market mein aa sakte hain aur utna hi faida utha sakte hain jitna ke koi bohot ameer shakhs uthayega. Aur ye mein koi mazaq nahi kar raha. Stock market investment karne aur paisa banane ka sab se behtareen platform hai. Ye bilkul safe bhi hai, yahan par aap Shariah compliant, neik, shareef investment bhi kar sakte hain aur zaroorat parne par foran se apne assets bech kar paisa bhi hasil kar sakte hain. Aur hum isko paisa banane ka ek secret rasta bhi keh sakte hain. Kyun ke agar Pakistan ki aabadi 25 crore hai, to sirf 5 lakh log yaani ke 0.2% Pakistanion ne stock market mein account khulwaya hua hai. Clearly, logon ko is ke baare mein nahi pata.

Lekin stock market aur is se related har cheez ko tafseel mein janne se pehle mein apna ta'aruf karwa doon. To bhai mera naam hai Shafe' Butt aur mein hoon aapka naya investing friend. Abdul Rehman bhai ke sath sath ab mein bhi aapke liye kuch bohot dilchasp aur mukhtalif videos lekar aaonga. To chaliye dekhte hain ke stock market aakhir hoti kya hai.

To bhai stock market wo jagah hoti hai jahan par companies aakar apna kuch hissa bechti hain jo ke log khareedte hain. Jab log ye kuch hissa khareedte hain to company ke paas kuch paisa aa jata hai. In paison se company apna business barha kar zyada profit banati hai, jis se company ka bhi faida hota hai aur jin logon ne is company ka kuch hissa khareeda hota hai unka bhi faida hota hai. Ye jo kuch hissa mein baar baar keh raha hoon, ise basically shares ka naam diya jata hai. Ke bhai company ne apne shares beche aur logon ne shares khareede. Thora mushkil ho raha hoga, hum abhi ek example se isko aasan kar lete hain.

Farz karein teen doston ne milkar ek dukan kholi, jis mein wo kapre bechte hain. Kaam achha chalne laga aur dukan mahine ka 10,000 rupiya profit bana kar dene lagi. Har dost ka profit hua taqreeban 3,300 rupay. Ab agar aisi hi do aur dukanien aa jayein, yaani ke total teen dukanien ho jayein, to profit chala jayega seedha seedha 30,000 rupay par. Yaani ke jahan pehle ek dost 3,300 rupay bana raha tha, ab wo seedha 10,000 rupay kama raha hai. Kitna zyada profit hai na? Haan. Lekin masla ye hai ke ek nayi dukan aati hai lakhon ya karoron rupay ki, aur itna paisa in ke paas hai nahi. Wo jo 10,000 ka profit ye log kama rahe hain, agar us profit se nayi dukan kholne ka intezar kiya, to to ho gaya kaam. Kai saal lag jayeinge us mein. To ab ye kya karein?

Bhai in ke paas do se teen raste hain. Pehla rasta to ye hai ke ye apne rishtedaron se ja kar udhaar maang lein. Lekin Allah bachaye aisi majboori se. Ek to jo wo rishtedaron ne mirch masala laga kar ye baat aage phelani hai ke "Rasheeda da munda yaasi pehe manga". Tauba, tauba. Aur agar unhone ye paise wapas de bhi diye, to jo baad mein aapko taane parne hain na, aapne apni dukan bech kar unhein paise wapas karne hain. To bhai ye wala rasta to na hi lein.

Phir doosra rasta ye hai ke aap bank se loan le lein. Is mein aapko karze par interest dena parta hai. Lekin agar aapka profit margin hi thora ho to peeche aapke paas bohot thora profit bachta hai. To ye bhi koi bohot achhi option nahi hai.

Teesra jo sab se ideal rasta hai, wo ye hai ke ye teen dost apna business stock market mein le jayein. Wahan ye logon ko batayeinge ke bhai hum ek dukan se 10,000, ya jitna bhi profit hai, wo bana rahe hain. Aur agar hum teen dukanien kholte hain to profit seedha seedha teen guna barhta hai. Log dekhein ge ke haan bhai in ke business mein potential to hai, agar in ke paas teen aur dukanien ya us se bhi zyada dukanien aa jati hain aur is business mein thora sa hamara share bhi ho to hamara to bohot faida hai. To is tarah log in ki company ke shares khareedein ge aur in ke paas paise aa jayein ge.

To ye ek bohot achhi option hai business owners ke liye capital, yaani paise generate karne ke liye. Aapke paas business ke liye paisa bhi aa jata hai, risk bhi share ho jata hai aur aapka business aapke paas hi rehta hai agar zyada shares aapke apne paas rahein. Yehi wajah hai ke aaj Pakistan stock market mein 500 se bhi zyada companies listed hain. In mein conventional companies bhi hain aur Shariah compliant companies bhi hain. Yaani jo log sirf halal businesses ke sath deal karna chahte hain wo bhi yahan par investment kar sakte hain.

Ab kyun ke ye ek bohot achhi option ban jati hai business owners ke liye capital raise karne ke liye, isiliye hamein aksar khabron mein sunne ko milta hai ke stock market mein ek naya IPO hua hai. Saal 2025 mein total 7 IPOs hue the stock market mein. Lekin ab ye IPO hota kya hai? Bhai ye wo hi kaam hota hai jo un teen doston ne kiya tha. Apne business ko stock market mein list karne ko IPO kehte hain. Is ki full form hai Initial Public Offering. Iska matlab ye hai ke jab koi business pehli dafa apne shares logon ko khareedne ki option deta hai, to usko IPO kehte hain. Bari simple si cheez hai, ek business private se public ho gaya.

Ab hum dekh lete hain ke aakhir stock market se faida kaise uthaya ja sakta hai. Stock market ki achhi baat ye hai ke is se ameer aur ghareeb dono barabar faida utha sakte hain, kyun ke jo bhi faida hona hai wo shares ki base par hona hai. Haan jiske paas zyada shares honge wo obviously zyada faida uthayega.

To stock market mein aapko do teen tariqon se faida ho sakta hai. Sab se pehle aa jata hai capital gain, yaani ke jis qeemat par aapne koi share khareeda hai agar us se zyada par aap use bech dete hain to aapko profit ho jayega. Misaal ke taur par, let's say maine PSO ke 10 shares khareede aur ek share ki qeemat thi 100 rupay. Yaani ke meri total investment ho gayi 1000 rupay. Phir kuch din baad wo jo 100 rupay ka share tha, us ki qeemat barh gayi aur wo 110 rupay ka ho gaya. Yaani ke jo meri 1000 rupay ki investment thi us ki total value ab 1100 rupay ho gayi. 10% ka increase aa gaya price mein. To is time agar mein apne shares bech deta hoon, to mujhe 10% ka profit ho jayega aur mere paas 100 rupay extra aa jayeinge.

To ye ho gaya capital gain. Phir is ke baad doosra tariqa aa jata hai dividends lena. Dividend bhi basically profit hi hota hai, lekin is ke liye aapko apne shares bechne ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Hota kuch yun hai ke jab aapne wo PSO ke kuch shares khareede to aap PSO ke kuch hisse ke malik ban gaye. Ab PSO company jo bhi profit banayegi, us mein aapka bhi kuch hissa hoga. Dividend bas yehi hota hai. Jab tak ye company profit banati rahegi aur aapke paas is ke shares raheinge, aapke paas ye dividend khud-ba-khud aata jayega. Ye bhi ek bohot bari wajah hai ke aapko achhi companies ke shares nahi bechne chahiyein, kyun ke is se aapko dividend yaani profit milta rehta hai, agar wo company dividend deti hai to.

Achha is ke ilawa ek aur cheez hoti hai bonus shares. Bonus shares mein basically ye hota hai ke jis company ke shares aapne khareede hote hain, wo company aapko kuch aur shares de deti hai muft mein. Is mein foran se aapko faida nahi hota, kyun ke jab bonus shares milte hain to share price kam ho jati hai. Lekin long term mein faida zaroor hota hai kyun ke share price aam taur par wapas upar aa jati hai aur ab aapke paas shares bhi zyada hote hain. To future mein jo bhi aapka capital gain hoga ya jo bhi aapko dividend milega wo bhi zyada milega. By the way, jab dividend bhi milta hai to bhi share price kam hoti hai lekin isi tarah aam taur par wapas upar aa jati.

Yaani ke Pakistan ki 100 bari companies mein milakar itne points add ho gaye ya itne points nikal gaye. Aur duniya bhar mein jahan kahin bhi stock markets hoti hain wahan par is tarah mukhtalif indices hote hain.
