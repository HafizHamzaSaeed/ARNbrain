To abhi yeh jaise hi conflict resolve ho jayegi, abhi to obviously news mein oil prices mein number one cheez jo chal rahi hai woh yeh hi hai ke Strait of Hormuz khulta hai ya nahi hota, peace talks kis taraf ja rahi hain. But yeh jab cheezein poori resolve hongi, to UAE ka OPEC ko leave karna aur uski wajah se oil prices ka long-term mein kam hona, yeh Pakistan economy ke liye ek buhat bara positive hai.
