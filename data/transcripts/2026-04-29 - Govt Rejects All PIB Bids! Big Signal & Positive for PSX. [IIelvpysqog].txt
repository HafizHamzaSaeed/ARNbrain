Kal Government of Pakistan ka ek PIB auction tha. Aur ek badi important signal unhone diya hai jo ke aage aane wali mehangai aur jo sharah-e-sood Pakistan ki economy mein Government of Pakistan anticipate kar rahi hai usse related, aur saari bids jo bankon ne karza dene ke liye Government of Pakistan ke against place ki hui thi, yaani ke jaise hi State Bank of Pakistan ne interest rate badhaya tha to bankon ki koshish thi ke long term ke liye ek achi sharah-e-sood pe Government of Pakistan ko karza de diya jaye, lekin Government of Pakistan ne ye saari bids jo hain reject kar dein. Yaani ke wo samajhte hain ke ye situation temporary hai aur even though State Bank ne precautionary basis pe interest rate badhaya hai, long term basis pe hum interest rate ko bohot zyada nahi dekh rahe. Ye stock market ke liye ek bohot positive news hai to maine kaha aap se share karte hain.
