Ek bohat important principle jo ke maine bhi apni life mein aaj se do teen saal pehle adopt kiya tha. Aur agar aap bhi adopt kar lein, to aap ek bohat zabardast business bhi create kar sakte hain. Aur agar aap job karte hain, to ek bohat achi jagah dhoondh sakte hain jahan aapko job karne mein bohat zyada faida hoga. Aur woh principle hai, "Your employees don't work for you, but as the CEO of the company, you work for them." Iska matlab yeh hai ke aapne apne business mein itni growth lekar aani hai ke aapke jo employees hain, unki growth aapki responsibility ban jaye. Aur yeh hota yun hai ke aahista aahista aap apna business jab expand karte hain, to aap apne saare employees ko woh high value tasks jo hain, woh sikha kar, delegate kar ke unke zimme laga dete hain aur jo aapka time bachta hai us se aap apne business ko mazeed jo hai woh growth ki taraf lekar jaate hain.
