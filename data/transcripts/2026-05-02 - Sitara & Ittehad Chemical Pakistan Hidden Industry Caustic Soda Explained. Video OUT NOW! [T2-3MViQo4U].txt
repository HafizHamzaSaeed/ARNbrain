Pakistan mein do bohat achi cheezein hui hain pichle kuch saalon mein. Ek toh humne jis tariqe se solar ko adopt kiya hai aur doosri cheez jo bohat achi hui hai, wo ye hai ke businesses ke liye, industries ke liye, Government of Pakistan ne faisla kiya hai ke bijli ka sasta hona zaroori hai. Aur is realization ko do tariqon se Government of Pakistan implement kar rahi hai: incremental electricity package bhi aa gaya hai, aur industries ke liye char se panch rupay per unit cost ko decrease bhi kiya gaya pichle mahino mein. Toh bohat ziyada log humse puchte hain ke ye itni bari macro economic development ho rahi hai, isse kaunsi companies jo hain wo benefit karengi? Is development ko explain karne ke liye aur iska impact explain karne ke liye aur taake aap future ki achi companies jin mein positive developments ho rahi hain unse pehle hi waqif ho jayein, humne ek detailed video banayi hai on Pakistan's Caustic Soda sector, jisme bijli ka istemal bohat zyada hota hai. Isme humne Engro Polymer, Sitara Chemicals aur Ittehad Chemicals ko detail mein discuss kiya hai. Aur ye video hamare YouTube channel pe live ho gayi hai. Is video ko dekhein aur humein comments mein batayein ke is video ko dekh kar aapne kya seekha aur kaunsi company is sector mein aapko investment ke liye achi lag rahi hai.
