Lekin do cheezein constant rahein aur wo hai is industry ki energy dependency aur import protection. Pakistan mein caustic soda ka import karna bilkul aasan nahi hai. Ye chemical hazard category mein aata hai jis ki port handling protocols bohat tough hote hain. Proper safety, temperature control, corrosion-resistant equipment aur specialized storage required hoti hai. Ports par is chemical ki handling highly regulated hoti hai is liye non-tariff barriers automatically create ho jate hain. Is ke sath sath government ne tariff barriers bhi impose kiye hue hain jaise solid flakes par around 20% custom duty hai aur liquid soda import karne par specific duty of around Rs. 7,000 per ton ye bhi imposed hai. Ye import hurdles market mein local producers ko relative advantage de dete hain. Isi liye Pakistan mein import substitution bohat strong hai caustic soda ko le ke aur domestic suppliers comfortable position mein operate karte hue nazar aa rahe hain. Exports ki taraf dekha jaye to Pakistan sirf caustic flakes export karta hai kyun ke liquid form export karna operationally risky aur bohat costly hai. Kyun ke Pakistan ka textile sector main buyer hai caustic soda ka, policy side par future growth drivers textile sector se closely linked hain. Jaise jaise textile exports pick up hote hain, yarn processing, bleaching aur finishing operations ki demand grow hoti hai to ultimately caustic soda consumption bhi increase hoti hai. Government ki industrial policies jo energy tariffs ko rationalize karne aur consequently export sectors ko support karne ki direction mein ja rahi hain, indirectly chlor-alkali industry yani ke caustic soda ki industry ko bhi uplift karti hui nazar aa rahi hain.
