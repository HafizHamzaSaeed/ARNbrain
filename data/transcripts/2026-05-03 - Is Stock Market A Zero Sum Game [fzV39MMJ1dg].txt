90% log ye sochte hain ke stock market mein to wahi companies hain, roz usi ki trading ho rahi hai, koi buy kar raha hai, koi sell kar raha hai, to phir iski price upar kaise jaati hai? Zahir hai kisi ka nuksaan hoga to kisi ka faida hoga na? Asal mein aisa bilkul nahi hai. Aaj se 10 saal pehle, jo nau mahine the from July to March FY16 ke, usme jo Pakistan Stock Exchange ki top 100 companies thi, unhone profit kamaya taqreeban 300 se sawa 300 arab rupay. Aur abhi jo nau mahine khatam hue hain from July to March, FY24, isme inhone profit kamaya 1243 billion rupees. To time ke sath-sath businesses apne profits barhate hain, to zaroori nahi hota ke stock market mein agar kisi ka nuksaan hoga to doosre ka faida hoga. Jo log is cheez ko samajh jaate hain, compounding pe lag jaate hain, wo long-term mein bohot zyada faida uthate hain.
