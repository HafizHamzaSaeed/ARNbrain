Aaj kal oil prices kafi zyada high chal rahi hain, to ek acchi development aapke saath share karta hoon jisse Pakistan ko bhi bahut zyada faida hoga aur stock market pe bhi do companies jo hain un pe ek positive impact aayega unke business pe. Aaj se thode din pehle aapne dekha hoga KPK ke Nashpa block mein ek bahut badi discovery hui thi Pakistan mein, to aaj OGDCL ne details batayi hain uski ke itni acchi wo discovery hai ke aaj se ek do saal baad jab usme oil ki production increase hogi to ye production 20 se 22 hazar barrels per day tak chali jayegi. Aur just to put it in context, Pakistan mein ek din mein takreeban 80 hazar barrels ki production hoti hai, to ye kafi badi discovery hai jisse Pakistan ko har saal takreeban 500 million dollars ka import bill bhi kam hoga, OGDCL ka revenue aur profits bhi badhenge. Aur ek aur company jisko iska faida hoga wo aapne comments mein batana hai aur hint ye hai ke aap sochein ke ye oil aage phir kahaan jayega production ke baad.
