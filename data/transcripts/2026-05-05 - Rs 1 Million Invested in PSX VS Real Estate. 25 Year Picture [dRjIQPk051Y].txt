2000 mein 10 lakh ki property aur 10 lakh ki stock market investment. Aaye dekhte hain 25 saal baad kya difference banta hai. 2000 ke aas paas Pakistan ke kai shehron mein 8 se 10 lakh mein ek accha residential plot mil jata tha. Koi roughly 250 square yards ya phir jaise kehte hain 10 marla. Aaye ab doosri side dekhte hain. Agar wahi 10 lakh san 2000 mein PSX ki strong companies mein diversified tareeqe se lagaye jaate yani koi index jaisa return aa jata to san 2000 mein index 1500 pe tha aur aaj derh lakh balki ek lakh 60 hazaar ke aas paas ghoom raha hai. To 25 saal baad investment ho gayi taqreeban 10 se 12 crore tak aur doosri taraf property mein sirf 2 crore. Sirf asset alag tha aur result 6 guna zyada. Yeh sirf ek illustration hai. Obviously future mein actual returns thode bohot different ho sakte hain. Seedhi baat, Pakistan mein property ko investment samjha jata hai lekin duniya ke ameer log business aur stocks mein bhi invest karte hain kyunki historically stocks mein compounding ka potential zyada raha hai aur compounding waqt ke saath numbers ko explode kar deti hai. Aap ko kya pasand hai? Stocks ya plot? Comments mein batayein. Aur haan, index jaise returns har saal sirf 5 minute laga kar aap kaise achieve kar sakte hain uske liye yeh video dekh lein.
