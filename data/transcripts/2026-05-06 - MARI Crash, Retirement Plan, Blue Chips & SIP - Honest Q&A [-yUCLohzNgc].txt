Hamesha agar insan ke paas thoda bohat cash available ho to woh safe feel karta hai aur jab acchi opportunity aati hai woh usko invest kar deta hai. To dono cheezon ko combine kar ke, kyunki aapne returns bhi apne maximize karne hain aur saath saath ek aisa system bhi create karna hai ke jiske andar aap usko acche tareeqe se follow kar sakein taake jo numbers screen pe nazar aa rahe hain woh actual mein aapki life mein result bhi show karein.

To in dono aspects ko combine karte hue, main sabko do actionable points jo hain woh recommend karta hoon. First ye hai ke jab bull market framework on ho, aur aapko lag raha ho ke market ne bohat zyada rally kar li hai, 50% barh gayi hai, 60% barh gayi hai in 6 to 9 months... Aur ek correction jo hai woh aa sakti hai agle ek se do mahine mein, to aap 10 to 20% cash jo hai usko hold kar len. Aur phir jab dip aaye, to us opportunity ka fayda utha lein.

Aur doosra actionable step main jo sabko kehta hoon, woh ye hai ke kabhi agar aapko lage ke bull market framework jo hai woh off ho gaya hai aur thode saalon mein lag raha hai ke Pakistan ki economy itni acchi nahi ja rahi, prices bohat zyada high ho gayi hain, bear market ke chances lag rahe hain, to aapki jo percentage of savings ti jo aapne stocks mein lagayi hui ti, let's say aapke paas agar 50 lakh rupaye ki savings ti aur bull market ke time pe aapne 30 lakh ki investment ki hui ti aur 20 lakh jo hai doosri cheezon mein invest kiya hota, to agar bear market aa jaye, to aap us percentage ko kam kar den. Aap 30 lakhs ke bajaye 15 ya 10 lakhs tak bhi aa sakte hain.

To ye jo poora concept hai ke kab invest karna chahiye, kab zyada invest karna chahiye, kab cash rakhna chahiye, usko in do actionable steps se jo hai aap apni investing ko mazeed improve kar sakte hain.

Phir ji Ahsan Babar sahab poochte hain: "What we should check in initial public offering if we are going to invest?" Bohat zabardast sa sawal hai aur IPO bilkul aise hi hota hai jaise ke 300 aur companies stock exchange pe available hain. 300 companies available ti, aapne sab ke baare mein detail mein padha hua tha, aapne sab ki fair value jo hai apne mind mein calculate ki hui ti aur aap dekh rahe the kiski price jo hai woh kya chal rahi hai, kaun si jo hai sabse zyada undervalued hai. To jab ek IPO aata hai, to aapke paas 300 and first company aa jati hai. Usko bhi aapne usi tareeqe se padhna hai, uska business bhi usi tareeqe se understand karna hai aur agar woh... Best opportunity hai yani ke let's say aap portfolio mein apne 10 companies rakhte hain, aur woh top 10 mein aa rahi hai, to aapne usme invest kar dena hai. Koi bhi company agar IPO mein aa rahi hoti hai to woh investor liye koi bohat amazing opportunity ho, iski koi guarantee nahi hoti. Aapne usko usi tareeqe se treat karna hota hai jaise baqi saari listed companies available hoti hain.

IPOs ke baare mein main aapko do aur important baaten batata hoon. Warren Buffett kehte hain ke chances of retail investors finding the best IPO ya phir agar woh IPO mein invest karte hain to unke success ke chances kam hote hain, uske do reasons hain. First ye hai ke aap khud sochein IPO asal mein hota kya hai, ke kisi ne bhi ek apni company create ki hai aur ab woh usko bechne aa raha hai, uska kuch hissa bechne aa raha hai. To koi bhi owner kyun chahega ke woh ek apni company ko jisme usne itni mehnat se banaya hai usko kam price pe beche. To the chances of finding a company really undervalued in IPO is less than ke jo companies already trade kar rahi hoti hain.

Dekhein aur to market mein itni saari cheezon chal rahi hoti hain, koi sector kabhi disadvantage hua hota hai, kisi company mein liquidity kam hoti hai ya kisi company ka result accha nahi aata to uski wajah se uski price neeche aa jati hai, usme ek acchi opportunity ban jati hai. To ek IPO mein bohat hi undervalued company ka aana jo hai, uske chances kam hote hain. Doosra factor ye hai ke jab aap koi normal share buy karte hain stock market mein jo listed hota hai to aapka jo broker hota hai usko 0.15% takreeban commission milta hai us buy-sell pe. Lekin jab IPO mein koi usko subscribe karta hai, to uska jo broker hota hai usko 1% commission milta hai. To dekhein almost seven times zyada commission milta hai, to is se related IPO se related promotion bhi zyada hoti hai.

Aur last ek cheez, bohat zyada log kya karte hain, dekhte hain ke IPO ki price kya thi aur uske 6 mahine baad us share ki price kya chal rahi ti, to dekhte hain ke agar accha gain hai us percentage ko calculate karte hain aur market se compare karte hain. Isme log ek galti kar jate hain. Pakistan mein zyada tar jab bull market chal rahi hoti hai usi time pe IPOs accha perform karte hain aur us time pe oversubscribed hone ke chances bhi zyada hain kyunki market obviously ek bull framework mein chal rahi hoti hai. To aap usko IPO ki price se jo hai compare na karein, kyunki us time pe to mushkil se ek bande ko hazaron ke hi kuch share milte hain kyunki itna zyada oversubscribed hota hai aur agar aapne 2 lakh rupaye ki bid lagayi ti to mushkil se aapko usme se koi 50,000 ke share milte hain.

To jo real price hoti hai jisse compare karna chahiye, woh hoti hai ke jab ek dafa uski trading start ho jaye aur usme itni buying-selling ho jaye ke aap jitna bhi usme invest karna chahte the, 5 lakh, 10 lakh, woh aap easily buy kar sakte. Us price se phir compare karna chahiye kyunki woh ek zyada realistic price hoti hai jis pe aap us company mein invest kar sakte the.

So ye dono chemical ki companies hain, aur ye dono jo chemical banati hain uski international price set hoti hai, usi price pe jo hai ye companies Pakistan mein sell karti hain. Aur electricity cost jo hai na woh is business ka bohat zyada important factor hai. Aur jo EPCL ke profits hain jis tareeqe se kam hue pichle do teen saalon mein, uska ek factor ye tha ke ye gas se bijli banate the aur gas ki keemat captive gas power plants ke liye aapne dekha hoga kitni zyada mehangi ho gayi. To ek dam se woh business ke jo poore profits the woh erode ho gaye.

To ab agar aap Lotchem mein invest karne ja rahe hain to sabse pehle to aapne dekhna hai ke ye kis price pe EPCL ko khareedenge. Aur obviously jan woh payment karenge aur uske liye agar mazeed debt ki requirement hui to woh phir Lotchem ki book pe aa jayegi. To pehle to aapne is cheez ka wait karna hai ke kis price pe ye transaction hogi taake aap jo new business of Lotchem hai jisme EPCL bhi included hai, usko combine kar ke dekh sakein ke iski value kya banti hai.

Iske saath saath Lotchem jo hai apni bijli ki cost ko kam karne ke liye solar, wind, aur shayad coal ka power plant isme invest karne ja rahe hain. To usme bhi aapne dekhna hai ke future mein idhar se jo saving hogi uske hisab se company ke future profits kaise lag rahe hain.

Iske saath saath jo negative developments hain jo future mein affect karenge, woh ye hai ke jaise Pakistan ne IMF ke saath agree kiya hai ke aahista aahista hum import ki duties kam karenge, to in dono products pe jab import ki duties kam hoti hain to phir jis price pe woh product Pakistan mein import ho raha hota hai, companies ko bhi uske hisab se apni price kam karni padti hai. Woh ek additional jo hai burden create kar deta hai profits ke upar. In dono companies pe humne detailed videos banayi hui hain dono ke businesses ko explain karne ke liye, PVC ke business ko bhi explain karne ke liye, aur iske saath saath jo PTA ka business hota hai raw material jo hai woh oil se linked hai ya nahi hai, woh sab hum apne description mein attach kar denge, aap usko bhi dekhein aur...

Framework ye hai ke aapne ab Lotchem plus EPCL ke business ko combine kar ke aur jo balance sheet hogi Lotchem ki us pe jitni debt leni padegi unko ye acquisition complete karne ke liye, uske hisab se dekhein ke is company ke future profits kitne bante hain aur agar ye acchi ek attractive price pe mil raha ho, future saari cheezon ko dekhte hue, to phir aapko isme invest karna chahiye.

Aur ab barhte hain aaj ke last question se related, aur Sanaullah Jatoi sahab poochte hain: "PABC please. PABC ko explain karein keeping in view the Afghanistan situation." Accha ye badi interesting company hai, aap log sab jaise beverage peete honge, Pepsi, Coke, Next Cola... Is sab mein jab bhi aap koi can consume karte hain, uska 100% chance hota hai, agar to woh import nahi hua hoga, to uska can jo hai woh PABC ne banaya hai. Aur aapne na ahli dafa jab koi bhi drink buy karenge kisi super market se, to usko rotate kar ke peeche side pe dekhna bhi hai, aur uspe likha bhi hota hai PABC. To ye ek monopoly business hai Pakistan mein aur Pakistan mein jitne bhi soft drink ke cans jo hain woh bante hain woh PABC banata hai. Aur inki capacity takreeban 1 billion can se bhi zyada ki hai, Faisalabad mein ye factory located hai.

Aur aaj se ek saal pehle tak jo Pakistan mein local consumption hoti ti cans ki, woh koi 300 million se 400 million cans ki hi hoti ti. To baqi saari jo quantity hoti ti, woh Afghanistan zyada tar export hoti ti.

Aur iska reason ye tha ke Afghanistan mein kyunki petroleum to koi plant hai nahi to udhar plastic ki bottles us tareeqe se hoti nahi hain, aur udhar ka jo terrain hai woh bhi bohat zyada upar neeche hai, rugged hai, wahan koi khaas investment bhi nahi ki hui companies ne. To udhar Afghanistan mein bhi bohat zyada cans ki demand hai. Udhar zyada tar jaise Pakistan mein 3 billion PET bottles jo plastic ki bottles hoti hain, woh 3 billion single serving jo hai consume hoti hai, aur cans jo hain sirf 300 million. Lekin Afghanistan mein ulta hai. Afghanistan mein cans ki proportion zyada hai, even though unki overall market jo hai Pakistan se kam hai. To Afghanistan mein almost 600 se 700 million cans ki demand hoti hai.

Aur obviously usme se zyada tar demand jo hai, woh Kabul ke aas paas ke area mein hoti hogi. To agar aap Afghanistan ka map dekhenge to Faisalabad mein jo factory hai, iska distance Kabul se bohat zyada nahi hai. Compare to jo doosre cans ki plants lage hue hain, Uzbekistan mein lage hue hain aur doosri countries mein lage hue hain. To jo zyada tar demand hoti hai, char badi MNCs hain Afghanistan mein jo canned products bechti hain canned soft drinks, to unke liye ye factory jo hai kafi zyada kareeb padti hai. To ye almost 500 million se bhi zyada can Afghanistan bechte hote the.

Ab pichle ek saal mein is company pe do negative developments ho gayi. Ek to Afghanistan ka border band ho gaya. To agar aap Afghanistan export nahi kar sakte aur aap doosre mulkon mein export karenge let's say Sri Lanka kar sakte hain ya jahan bhi aapko order mila, to usme aapke profits per can jo hai woh kam hote hain, kyunki internationally to bohat zyada competition hota hai. Aur iske saath saath jo imported cans pe duty hoti ti woh bhi aahista aahista kam ho gayi aur aage most probably aur zyada kam hogi. To us se local business bhi affect ho gaya.

To crux ye hai ke PABC ka business Afghanistan pe bohat zyada dependent hai. Aur jab tak ye border jo hai woh band rehta hai, to phir is business se bohat zyada profits generate karna mushkil ho jata hai.

To is feature ko jab aap understand kar lenge, aur kal ko agar border khulta hai, to ye company ek bohat badi beneficiary bhi ho jati hai, balki main to kahunga zyada tar log sochte hain ke Afghanistan border khul gaya to coal ki supply jo hai shuru ho jayegi, cement companies export karna shuru ho jayingi to cement sector ko fayda hoga, uske saath saath ye medicines ki companies hain, pharmaceutical sector, woh Afghanistan export karti hain unko fayda hoga, lekin mere hisab se... Number one beneficiary company of Afghanistan border opening is actually PABC.
