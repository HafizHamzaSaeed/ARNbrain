**ARN:** Achha Daniyal, abhi abhi news aayi hai ke OPEC se UAE ne exit le liya hai, to iska kya impact hoga overall, kya kehte hain, oil ki industry mein aur overall economy par?

**Daniyal:** Yeh ek bohat hi important major development hai, aur isko asaan lafzon mein aise samjhein ke koi bhi cartel jo ke apni production jo hai, apni capacity se kam produce kar raha hota hai, jab woh toot ta hai, toh uska jo major player hota hai aur woh zyada produce karne lag jaye toh usse kya hota hai? Us commodity ki price jo hai, woh kam ho jati hai. Toh abhi toh obviously war chal rahi hai, but jaise hi yeh saari cheezein conclude ho jayengi toh overall production jab oil ki badhegi, UAE zyada produce karega, UAE is the third largest capacity jiske paas hai oil produce karne ki OPEC plus mein. Sabse bada Saudi Arabia hai aur uske baad Russia hai, uske baad UAE 5 million barrels ki inki production capacity hai. Toh jab yeh zyada produce karenge toh is se long-term mein oil prices jo hain woh kam hongi aur us se Pakistan ka import bill kam hoga, hamari inflation kam hogi aur PSX ke liye yeh ek achhi khabar hai. Ab yeh samajh lete hain ke UAE yeh kyun kar raha hai. Jo top three members hain OPEC plus ke, Saudi Arabia, Russia aur UAE, us mein se teeno ki economies agar dekhin toh sabse less dependent on oil, kyunki yeh saare apne jab budget bana rahe hote hain toh usme dekh rahe hote hain ke oil ki price jitni zyada hogi utna zyada hume faida hoga, toh UAE least dependent on oil hai. Aur doosra factor yeh hai ke UAE ko apni total production capacity ka least production jo hai woh allow karti thi OPEC plus. Saudi Arabia aur Russia dono taqreeban 9 million barrels produce kar rahe hain aur inke paas spare capacity taqreeban dedh se do million barrels per day ki hai. Lekin UAE jo ke sirf taqreeban saade teen million barrels produce kar raha hai, inke paas dedh million ki spare capacity hai. Yani ke apni production ke hisab se sabse zyada spare capacity jiske paas hai, woh hai UAE. So UAE feel karta hai ke agar hum apni production ko increase kar bhi lenge, yeh jo dono members hain, inke liye oil price ko ek achhe level pe sustain karna zyada zaroori hai aur yeh production jo hai khud-ba-khud cut karenge taake oil prices jo hain woh maintain rahein. Aur hum, agar thodi bohot oil prices decline hongi bhi, jo hum zyada produce kar denge 30 to 40%, usse hamare overall revenues jo hain, woh badh jayenge.
