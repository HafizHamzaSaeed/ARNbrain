Middle class ki future wealth paanch cheezein destroy karti hain. Pehli, sirf salary se wealth build karna mushkil hai. Salary zaroor ek zaroorat hai, lekin investing ki aadat bhi zaroori hai. Doosri cheez, lifestyle inflation. Income badhti hai, kharcha us se tezi se badh jata hai, savings phir bhi zero. Teesri cheez, invest na karna. Saving paisa bachati hai, investing aapka paisa badhati hai. Yeh farq aapki zindagi badal sakta hai. Chauthi cheez, log kya kahenge syndrome. Shaadi, functions, relatives ko impress karna, paisa barbaad ho jata hai. Panchvein aur sabse dangerous cheez, financial education ka na hona. School mein nahi bataya isliye financial education bohot zaroori hai taake informed decisions le sakein. Middle class ka problem paisa kam hona nahi hai. Problem hai paise ko sambhalna kaise hai uski samajh na hona. Aur jis din aapne yeh samajh liya, us din game change ho jayegi. Aapko kya lagta hai? Comments mein batayein.
