Interviewer: Acha Abdul Rehman bhai, interest rate 1% barh gaye hain to iska kya impact hoga overall economy aur stocks pe?

ARN: Kafi zyada log soch rahe hain ke interest rate increase ho gaye hain to ab bull market framework off ho gaya hai, aisa bilkul nahi hai. Past mein bahut zyada examples hain ke jab tak aapke macroeconomic indicators theek hain, aapke results theek hain, to interest rate agar thore se increase ho bhi jayein, lekin woh bahut high level pe na jayein, 14-15% pe na jayein to bull market framework on rehta hai. Aur doosra factor yeh hai ke yeh koi macroeconomic instability ki wajah se ya kisi aur internal issue ki wajah se interest rate increase nahi hua, yeh ek external situation ki wajah se State Bank ne precautionary basis pe interest rate ko temporarily increase kiya hai. Past data bhi show karta hai jab bhi Middle East mein koi war situation hoti hai, oil price increase ho jati hai to thora sa interest rate increase hota hai lekin phir woh baad mein phir se decrease hone lag jata hai. So isme main sab ko yeh kehta hoon ke US-Iran war US ne shuru ki aur ceasefire bhi US ne mangi aur iska matlab hai ke yeh war zyada din nahi chalegi. Aur jaise hi oil prices jo hain niche aane lageingi, agli do se teen monetary policies mein interest rate bhi jo hai wapas niche aane lag jayega.
