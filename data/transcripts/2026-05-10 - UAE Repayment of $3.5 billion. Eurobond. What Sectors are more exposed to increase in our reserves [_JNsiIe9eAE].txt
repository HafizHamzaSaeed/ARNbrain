Assalam o Alaikum everyone, aaiye aaj aap se main share karta hoon ke aaj main kis cheez pe kaam kar raha tha. Ek cheez jo ye poore crisis mein hui hai, oil prices barhti hain, us se current account deficit jo hai us ke barhne ke chances hote hain aur phir reserves neeche aane ke chances hote hain, to ek cheez jo bohat achi ki hai government of Pakistan ne ke apne reserves ko bade ache tareeqay se maintain kiya hai. Jo UAE ko repayment ki wo humein Saudi Arabia se utne hi dollars jo hain wo mil gaye, phir humne Eurobond ka issue bhi kar liya aur ab IMF ki tranche aayegi 1.2 billion dollars ki to hamare jo reserves hain kuch hi dino mein 17 billion dollars tak pohanch jayenge aur ye highest hain is poore cycle mein. To reserves ka sab se zyada impact hota hai auto sector pe aur auto sector pe jo ek thoda sa khatra laga hua tha jab ye crisis shuru hua tha ke reserves neeche chale jayenge aur ho sakta hai inka import ban ho jaye, wo khatra kafi had tak kam ho gaya hai. To aaj maine is pe kaam kiya ke auto sector ki best company kaun si hai, aur aap ne comments mein batana hai ke aap ke hisab se best auto company kaun si lag rahi hai.
