Mahine ke pachas hazar kamate ho, aur sochte ho invest kya karunga? Salary to shuru hone se pehle hi khatam ho jati hai. Main aapka dukh bilkul samajh sakta hoon. Rent, bijli ka bill, ration, petrol, pachas hazar mein ghar chalana aasan nahi hai, bilkul nahi hai. Lekin teen hazar? Sirf teen hazar nikal sakte ho? Chai aur cigarette ka kharcha kam kar do, ya koi aur cheez, jo bhi tumhare liye possible ho, bas itna kafi hai shuru karne ke liye. Ab is teen hazar ko split karo. Do hazar ETF mein dalo, SIP karo har mahine. Har mahine automatic pehli tareekh ko, salary milte hi ETF mein laga do. Bhool jao ke ye do hazar tumhari salary thi bhi, aur jo baki ek hazar hai, use apne upar lagao. YouTube se skill seekho, YouTube bilkul free hai. Graphic design, video editing, freelancing, ye ek hazar tumhari income badha sakta hai. Ab hisab suno. Do hazar per month ETF mein, teen saal mein ye roughly compound ho ke takreeban nabbe hazar ban sakta hai, paanch saal mein dedh lakh ke kareeb. Bohot zyada nahi hai main jaanta hoon, lekin ye zero se dedh lakh ka safar hai. Aage ye khud ba khud compounding pe lag jayega. Aur skill seekhi to income pachas hazar se assi hazar ho sakti hai. Phir invest teen hazar nahi, aath hazar, das hazar per month ho sakta hai. Seedhi baat hai, khud mein invest karo, income badhao, saath saath jitna bhi paisa hai usko invest karo. Kya khayal hai? Accha formula hai? Comments mein batao.
