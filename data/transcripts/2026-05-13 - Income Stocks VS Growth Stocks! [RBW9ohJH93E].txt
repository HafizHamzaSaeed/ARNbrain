Speaker 1: Achha Abdul Rehman ye income stock aur growth stock mein kya farq hota hai? matlab aap kya recommend karenge?

ARN: Income stock basically hota hai jo aapko regular dividends bhi pay kar raha ho. Yani ke agar aap 100 rupay ka stock khareedte hain to aapko at least 6, 7, 8 rupay har saal aapko usme se income bhi generate ho. Aur growth stock hota hai jo ke apne share ki price barha ke aapko return dega. Aur ye basically back-end pe ye hota hai ke jab bhi koi company profit banati hai aur wo phir sochti hai ke isko hum apne partners mein distribute kar dein ya phir isse ek naya project laga dein. To jo growth stocks hote hain, un companies ke paas new investment opportunities hoti hain aur wo us cash ko hold karte hain, reinvest karte hain taake share price jo hai future mein aur zyada value create ho aur uski price aur zyada barhe. Aur jo income stocks hote hain, wo kehte hain ke hamare paas abhi itni zyada investment opportunities available nahi hain, to hum jo profit generate kar rahe hain wo apne shareholders mein divide kar lete hain. Ab aapke liye kaunsa behtar hai? Is mein zyada tar log aapko advise karenge ke agar aapko income ki zaroorat hai, aap retired hain, to aapko income stock lena chahiye, aur agar aapka investment horizon long term hai, aap thode thode paise daal rahe hain apni retirement planning kar rahe hain, to aapko growth stock lena chahiye. Lekin ye ek base level analysis hai. Jo mein karta hoon aur aapko bhi recommend karunga, wo ye hai ke aap dekhein kis stock mein sabse achhi opportunity hai. Ek taraf hai income stock, jismein mein 100 rupay lagata hoon mujhe 10 rupay ki income milti hai lekin wo mujhe capital gain jo hai wo 10 rupay ka hi dega. Doosri taraf hai growth stock, jo ke mein agar usme 100 rupay lagata hoon wo mujhe income bahut thodi si dega lekin wo 100 rupay se 130 rupay ka bhi ho sakta hai. Mein bhi aapko ye recommend karunga, jis stock mein aapko overall zyada return mil raha ho, usme invest karna chahiye.
