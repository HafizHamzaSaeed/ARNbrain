Inflation yani mehangai aur compounding yani wealth creation ek minute mein samajhna chahte ho? Aajaao mere saath.

2010 mein 1 lakh rupay mein kya aata tha? 28 gram sona, 900 gram chandi, 1200 dollars bhi aa jaate the, 900 FFC ke shares bhi aa jaate the, aur 400 Toyota Indus Motor ke shares.

Aur ab kya aata hai 1 lakh mein? 2 gram sona, 150 gram chandi, 360 dollars, FFC ke 180 shares, aur Toyota Indus Motors ke sirf 50 shares.

So, mere doston ye hai inflation aur 2010 ke 1 lakh ki aaj value kya hai?

Gold mein 13 lakh rupay, chandi mein saadhe 6 lakh rupay, dollar 3 lakh 30 hazar rupay, FFC ke shares saadhe 17 lakh rupay, aur Toyota Indus ke shares saadhe 16 lakh rupay.

Yani ke cash kachra hai, gold hedge hai, aur stocks wealth creation hai.
