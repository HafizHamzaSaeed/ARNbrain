Legendary investor Warren Buffett se teen cheezein seekhein. Warren Buffett retire ho rahe hain aur unki zindagi se humein teen cheezein seekh leni chahiye kyunki in teen cheezon ne unhein 400 billion dollar ki wealth bana kar di hai.

Number one, Buffett kehte hain sahi mauqa jab aaye to foran opportunity grab karo aur hesitate bilkul nahi karna. Jo darr gaya wo peeche reh jayega. Aaj kal Iran USA war ki wajah se dobara opportunity aayi hui hai, to kya aap ready hain?

Doosri cheez: compounding, 8th wonder of the world. Warren Buffett ne 9 saal ki umar se investing shuru ki lekin 90% of his wealth came after the age of 60 because of compounding.

Teesri cheez, aur ye mujhe sabse zyada pasand hai. Market gire, public bhaag jaati hai, Buffett khareedte hain kyunki wo long-term growth mein believe karte hain because every crisis is an opportunity for believers.
