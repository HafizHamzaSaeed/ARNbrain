Pakistan mein ek crore rupaye banana kitna mushkil hai? Ek crore bohot bari amount lagti hai, lekin hai kitna mushkil ek crore kamana Pakistan mein? Agar aap 25,000 rupaye har mahine invest karoge aur 17 percent jo ke PSX ka average return hai ek saal ka, wo kamate rahoge, to 12 se 13 saal mein ek crore rupaye tak pahonch jaoge. 25,000 rupaye per mahina, itna to bohot se log gaari ki har mahine ki installment dete hain. Ab agar aap 50,000 rupaye har mahine invest karoge, to phir ek crore aapka ho jayega 8 se 9 saalon mein. Aur agar itna abhi save nahi kar sakte, to no problem. Agar 15,000 rupaye har mahine invest karoge, to ek crore aapka ho jayega 16 se 17 saal mein. Ab batata hoon sab se maze ki baat. Ek dafa ek crore ban gaya, to do crore tak pahonchne mein sirf 4 saal lagenge. Ek crore banne mein bhale 20 saal lage hon, lekin agla ek crore banne mein sirf 4 saal lagenge. Yeh hai magic of compounding. To crux kya hai? Crore-pati banne ke liye Pakistan mein sirf investment karne ki aadat chahiye. Rukna nahi hai, chalte rehna hai aur manzil aa jayegi.
