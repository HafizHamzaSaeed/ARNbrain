Pakistan ki textile industry yani ke sabse bara exporter ek chemical ke bina ek din bhi nahi chal sakti. Aur us chemical ki local production sirf ek company karti hai. Aur ye company kuch saal pehle tak ek South Korean multinational bhi thi, Lotte Chemicals. Ab ownership change ho gayi hai, aur ab naye owners ne Pakistan ki doosri chemical ki monopoly ko bhi khareedne mein interest zahir kiya hai. Yani ek taraf textile ka chemical, doosri taraf plastic aur construction ka chemical. Dono monopolies ek chhat ke neeche hone ja rahi hain. Lekin sawal ye hai kya ye genuinely Pakistan ki next big industrial story hai ya numbers ke neeche kuch aisa hai jo retail investors miss kar rahe hain?
