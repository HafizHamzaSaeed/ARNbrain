Pakistan ki textile industry yani Pakistan ki largest export industry ek chemical par heavily depend karti hai. PTA, Pure Terephthalic Acid. Aur Pakistan mein is chemical ki large-scale local production sirf ek company karti hai, Lotte Chemicals. Interesting baat ye hai ye company kuch saal pehle tak ek South Korean multinational thi. Lekin ownership change hone ke baad ab ye business gradually expansion aur turnaround phase mein enter karta hua nazar aa raha hai.

Aur is waqt story aur bhi interesting ho gayi hai kyunke recently Lotte Chemicals ne Engro Polymer jaise ek aur monopoly chemical business ko acquire karne mein interest show kar diya hai. Yani ek taraf PTA, dusri taraf PVC. Pakistan ke do important industrial chemicals aur dono industries mein dominant players shayad ek hone ja rahe hain. Lekin Lotte Chemicals ki story sirf chemicals ki nahi hai. Ye business oil prices, electricity cost, import duties, aur industrial demand se deeply linked hai. Aur isi wajah se recent macro developments bhi company ke liye important ban gayi hain. Pakistan mein industrial electricity rates pichle kuch quarters mein noticeably neeche aaye hain aur management khud bhi renewables energy aur fixed cost reduction initiatives par aggressively kaam karti hui nazar aa rahi hai. Pakistan mein PTA ki annual demand roughly 7 lakh se le ke 8 lakh ton ke darmiyan hai aur Lotte Chemicals ki production capacity roughly 5 lakh ton hai. Company ka plant Port Qasim Karachi mein located hai aur historically plant almost full capacity ke kareeb operate karta rehta hai. Yani company jitna produce karti hai market uska zyada tar hissa absorb kar leta hai aur remaining demand jo hai wo import se poori hoti hai. Interesting cheez ye hai ke PTA sirf textile industry mein hi use nahi hota balki plastic bottles aur FMCG packaging products mein bhi extensively istemal hota hai. Yani ek taraf Pakistan ki largest export industry yani ke textiles aur doosri taraf FMCG aur beverage companies bhi indirectly isi chemical par depend karti hain. Isliye chances ye hain ke aap ki rozmarra zindagi mein use hone wale multiple products ke peeche kahin na kahin Lotte Chemicals ka role zaroor hai. Ab investors ke liye sawal ye banta hai PTA actually banta kaise hai? Raw material kya hai? Aur isi point par business ko owner ki tarah samajhna important ho jata hai. PTA banane ke liye main raw material hota hai paraxylene. Ye bhi ek chemical hai jo company mostly Middle East aur Asia se import karti hai. Phir paraxylene ko process kar ke PTA banaya jata hai aur ye process kaafi energy intensive yani ke is mein bijli bohat zyada istemal hoti hai. Isliye Lotchem ke business ko samajhne ke liye electricity cost bohat important hai. Ek ton PTA banane ke liye roughly 450 unit of electricity required hain. Pehle company apna captive gas power plant use karti thi electricity generate karne ke liye lekin pichle ek saal se company almost completely national grid par shift ho gayi hai kyunki gas prices aur captive power levies materially increase ho gayi hain Pakistan mein. Kuch saal pehle gas ka rate per MMBTU 1200 rupay hota tha lekin levies ke baad ye cost aur rate barhne ke baad bhi 4000 se bhi oopar chali gayi hai. Aur isi wajah se gas captive electricity generation feasible nahi rahi. Resultantly Pakistan ka grid ka unit 11 cent agar hum assume karein to electricity ki cost roughly $50 per ton ke aas paas ban jati hai ek ton PTA banane ke liye. Iske ilawa company ki kuch fixed cost bhi hain jis mein salaries, maintenance, depreciation aur operational overheads shamil hote hain aur ye fixed cost roughly $20 se $25 per ton ke aas paas rehti hain.

Aaiye ab Lotte Chemicals ke profits ko samajhne ke liye ek bohat important concept samajhte hain. PTA aur paraxylene margins. Kaafi retail investors, aur ye main apne experience se bata raha hoon, yahan confuse ho jate hain. Wo samajhte hain ke agar PTA paraxylene margin $100 se barh ke $150 ho jaye to company ka profit bhi 50% barh jayega, ye simple calculation unke mind mein aati hai. Lekin reality thodi si different hai. Sabse pehle ye samajh lein ke PTA paraxylene margin actually hota kya hai. Ye simply jo PTA chemical ki selling price hai aur jo paraxylene chemical jo ke raw material hai uski selling price hai, unke darmiyan price ka difference hota hai. Profit per ton nahi hota kyunki is margin se electricity cost, salaries, maintenance, depreciation aur baqi jo operational cost hoti hain unho ne bhi abhi deduct hona hota hai. Example se samjhata hoon aap ko. Agar PTA paraxylene margin $100 ho to roughly $50 ki electricity cost aur $25 ki fixed cost deduct hogi, yani company ka actual profit roughly $25 per ton ban jata hai. Lekin agar margin $100 se barh ke $150 ho jaye to interesting cheez ye hoti hai ke electricity ki cost same rehti hai, fixed cost bhi largely same rehti hain aur isi wajah se profit $25 se barh kar roughly $75 tak pahunch jata hai yani sirf 50% margin increase lekin profits almost three times. Aur isi tarah agar PTA paraxylene margin $70 tak gir jaye to company break-even ya loss territory mein bhi ja sakti hai. Isliye Lotte Chemicals ke business mein PTA paraxylene margins bohat closely monitor kiye jate hain. Ab PTA business ka ek aur important angle samjhata hoon main aap ko aur wo hai import duties. Lotte Chemicals ka raw material paraxylene zero duty par import hota hai. Lekin agar finished PTA chemical bahar se import kiya jaye to importer ko custom duties, additional custom duties aur freight cost incur karni parti hai. Aur isi wajah se imported PTA comparatively expensive parta hai Pakistan mein. Aur yahan Lotchem ko ek important advantage mil jata hai kyunki company jo hai imported parity price ke kareeb apni price ko set karti hai. Import parity matlab jitne ka import ban raha hoga with duties, Lotchem bhi usko samajhte hue ke demand to zyada hai aur meri production jo hai poori fulfill kar nahi sakti to main bhi wahi price set karta hoon jitne pe additional imports jo hain wo Pakistan mein pahunchti hain. Aur isi duty protection ki wajah se market prices mein roughly $50 se $100 per ton ka additional support create ho jata hai Lotte Chemicals ke liye jo company ki profitability ko materially improve kar sakta hai. Aur yahan ek aur subtle lekin important point bhi samjhana zaroori hai. Duty protection percentage terms mein hoti hai, jaise ke aap ko pata hoga ke is product pe 10% duty hai, is product pe 20 hai, to ye hamesha percentage mein hoti hai. To agar PTA pe duty roughly 10% ho aur PTA ki global price jo hai wo $700 ho to protection roughly $70 ban jati hai yani ke jo duty ki wajah se aap ko extra profit mil raha hota hai wo $70. Lekin agar global PTA prices kisi wajah se $1000 tak pahunch jayein to wahi 10% ki protection suddenly $100 per ton benefit mein convert ho jati hai. Duty jo hai abhi bhi 10% hai lekin uska jo impact hai wo $70 se $100 pe chala gaya price increase hone ki wajah se. Yani sirf duty rate hi important nahi hota balki global PTA prices bhi equally important variable hai. To ab tak humne samjha Lotchem ka business model, PTA paraxylene margins ko kaise dekhna hai aur duty protection ka import.

Lotchem ke naye owners ne recently Engro Polymer yani EPCL mein strategic investment karne ka interest show kiya hai. Aur interesting cheez ye hai ke EPCL Pakistan ka effectively only large-scale PVC producer hai. Yani ek taraf PTA aur doosri taraf PVC. Pakistan ke do important industrial chemicals aur dono industries mein dominant local players ek hone ja rahe hain. Management ke mutabiq acquisition process ki due diligence advanced stage mein hai aur share purchase agreement process bhi initiate ho chuka hai. Lekin yahan sabse interesting angle sirf acquisition nahi balki energy yani bijli ki economics hai kyunki dono businesses heavily electricity intensive hain. Aur isi point par Lotchem ko potentially significant advantage mil sakta hai EPCL acquire karne ka. EPCL currently apni jo 54 megawatt ki electricity requirement hai wo mostly khud generate karta hai aur uski electricity cost roughly 50 rupay per unit hai jis ki wajah se annual electricity expense jo hai wo 22 arab rupay tak pahunch jata hai EPCL ka. Aur iska major reason hai ke EPCL ke paas direct grid ka connection nahi hai aur ye abhi tak gas se bijli banate hain. Doosri taraf Lotchem ki electricity cost comparatively kaafi lower hai kyunki company already national grid par shift ho chuki hai jahan effective electricity rates jo hain wo 30 se 35 rupay per unit ke aas paas aa gaye hain. Aur interesting baat ye hai ke BOTH companies Port Qasim Karachi mein hi located hain aur Lotchem ka electricity connection EPCL ki factory ke kareeb se hota hua guzarta hai. Isliye Lotchem ki management ka estimate hai ke agar EPCL ko acquisition ke baad optimized grid infrastructure se connect kar diya jaye to annually roughly PKR 10 billion ki saving create kar sakte hain. Jis mein takreeban 7 arab jo hain wo grid connection ki wajah se aur 3 arab rupay jo hai wo renewable energy initiatives ki wajah se. Aur yahan is acquisition ko samajhne se related ek aur bohat important strategic point bhi samajhna zaroori hai. PTA aur PVC margins ka aapas mein jo correlation hai historically wo sirf 0.3 hai yani dono businesses ek hi cycle par move nahi karte. Agar PTA margins pressure mein hon to zaroori nahi PVC margins bhi simultaneously kam chal rahe hain. Aur isi wajah se ye acquisition Lotchem ke liye ek natural hedge bhi create kar sakta hai jo single product dependency risk ek business ko hota hai usko kam kar sakta hai. Iske sath sath Lotchem khud bhi aggressively energy optimization projects par kaam kar raha hai. Lotte Chemicals ka 6.5 megawatt solar phase 1 project recently complete hua hai aur bohat jald operational ho jayega. Aur phir uske baad 8 to 9 megawatt solar phase 2 October 2026 tak expected hai. Aur phir 8 megawatt wind project jo ke 2028 ki timeline par planned hai. Renewables ka interesting aspect ye hota hai ke inka payback period investment ka generally sirf do se teen saal ke darmiyan hota hai jaise ke hum sab aaj kal apne ghar mein solar bhi laga rahe hain. To renewables ke initiatives ke sath sath management simultaneously engineering, procurement aur operational contracts ko bhi renegotiate kar rahi hai jahan multiple agreements mein roughly 15 se 30% savings ko target kiya ja raha hai. Aur overall management ka target hai ke in cheezon ke sath aur fixed cost ko reduce karne ke sath sath har saal jo hai 500 million rupay ki saving ki jayein. Aur honestly Lotte Chemicals ke turnaround thesis ka sabse important aspect bhi shayad yahi hai kyunki ek aisi company mein jahan margins historically oopar neeche hote rehte hain wahan cost optimization ofttimes sabse reliable technique hoti hai profits ko stable rakhne ke liye. Iske sath sath global PTA demand, new plants yani ke PTA supply additions aur international chemical cycle mein kya chal raha hai. Isliye PTA business ko samajhne ke liye ye dekhna bohat important hota hai ke globally naye plants kitne lag rahe hain yani ke supply kitni aa rahi hai, existing plants jo hain unki utilization kis level par chal rahi hai, aur China se production ka behavior kya hai kyunki China global PTA market mein dominant role play karta hai. Interesting cheez ye hai ke 2026 ke liye market mein ek relatively positive signal nazar aa raha hai. Kaafi time baad aisa lag raha hai ke globally major PTA capacity additions jo hain wo is saal mein koi itni zyada nahi aayengi jabke China khud bhi kuch existing plants ko band kar raha hai ya phir jo doosre plants hain unki utilization levels ko kam kar raha hai. So naturally agar supply growth slow ho jaye to PTA paraxylene margins ko support mil sakta hai. Aaj kal global PTA plants kis utilization level par chal rahi hain aap apni screen par dekh sakte hain. Aur phir doosri taraf ek aur bohat important factor hai jo Lotte Chemicals ke long-term economics ko materially impact kar sakta hai aur wo hai IMF ka tariff rationalization plan for Pakistan. Jaise aap ne dekha hoga pichle budget se le ke aage bhi ye continue rahega, aahista aahista Pakistan aur IMF ne agree kiya tha ke hum import duties jo hain wo kam karenge. Historically Lotte Chemicals imported PTA pe duty protection enjoy karta raha hai kyunki company ka raw material paraxylene zero duty par import hota hai lekin finished PTA import karne par additional duties aur taxes lagte hain aur isi wajah se imported PTA comparatively expensive hota hai. Lekin budget 2025 mein Government of Pakistan ne IMF ke sath trade liberalization framework ke under PTA duty structure mein significant reduction already kar di. Pehle custom duty roughly 16% थी aur additional custom duty roughly 4% thi lekin pichle budget ke baad custom duty ko 5% pe lekar aa gaye aur additional custom duty ko effectively zero kar diya. Aur isi wajah se imported PTA, specially Chinese PTA, ab comparatively lower landed cost par Pakistan mein compete kar sakta hai. Yani duty protection jo historically Lotte Chemicals ke margins ko support karti thi wo gradually weaker hoti hui nazar aa rahi hai.
