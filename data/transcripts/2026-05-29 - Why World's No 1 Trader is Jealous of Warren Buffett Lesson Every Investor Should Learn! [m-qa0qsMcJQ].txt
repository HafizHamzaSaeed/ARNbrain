Dunya ka sab se bara trader Warren Buffett se jealous kyun ho raha hai? Aaye aap ko batate hain. Aap ne trader Paul Tudor Jones ka naam to suna ho ga. Wo banda jo 50 saal se dunya ki markets ko hila raha hai. Us ne haal hi mein ek aisi baat ki jo har Pakistani investor ko sunni chahiye. Paul Tudor Jones ne kaha, "Main ek bewaqoof tha jo saalon tak Warren Buffett ka mazaq urata raha." Paul Tudor Jones ko ab samajh aya ke asli daulat trading se nahi balkay compounding se banti hai. Wo cheez jo Warren Buffett 9 saal ki umar se kar rahe thay. 2026 ki Berkshire AGM mein Warren Buffett ne phir wahi sabaq sab ko sikhaya. Business ko owner ki nazar se dekho, sirf share price ki nazar se nahi. Cash buffer hamesha rakho taake jab dunya dar rahi ho, aap khareed sako. Aur phir compounding dunya ka aathwan ajooba hai. Isay kaam pe lagao. Main ne to ye teen lessons seekh liye hain. Aap bhi seekhein aur financial freedom achieve karein.
