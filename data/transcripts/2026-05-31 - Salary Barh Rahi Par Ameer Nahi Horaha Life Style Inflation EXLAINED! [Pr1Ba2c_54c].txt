Salary barh rahi hai, lekin main ameer nahi ho raha. Paanch saal pehle 60,000 meri salary hoti thi aur ab dedh lakh ho gayi hai. Income dhai guna barh gayi hai lekin savings aaj bhi zero hain. Aisa kyun? Kyunki mere dosto, income barhne ke sath sath lifestyle bhi barh raha hai. 60,000 ki jab salary hoti thi to Cultus chalate the. Ab dedh lakh ki salary hai, Corolla chalate. Ghar ka rent double, bara ghar le liya. Bahar khane ka kharcha triple aur phone har saal naya. Income barhi kharche us se tezi se barh gaye. Is ko kehte hain lifestyle inflation. Dunya ka har ameer insan aik rule follow karta hai aur aap ne bhi yahi follow karna hai. Income barhe, lifestyle rok do. Jo farq hai us ko invest kar do. Dedh lakh kamate ho, 20,000 har mahine invest karoge to paanch saal mein compounding ke sath 20 lakh ka portfolio ban sakta hai aur wo 20 lakh khud ba khud aage compound hote rahenge.
