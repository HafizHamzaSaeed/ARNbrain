Service Long March Tyres ka jo IPO ho raha hai, itni acchi development hai aur shaayad is company ke aane se Pakistan mein ye jo smuggled tyres aate the gaariyon ke, truckon ke, shaayad is se bhi humari jaan jo hai wo choot jaaye.

Aap kabhi agar search karenge, to Long March duniya ki top 10 tyre companies mein aata hai. Aur ab kyunki America, China pe duties laga raha tha, to unhone Pakistan ke Service Group ke sath mil ke yahan pe ek factory set up ki, taake wo America export kar sakein tyre, through Pakistan. 

So ye company local tyres bhi bohot acche tareeqe se bech sakti hai, America bhi export kar sakti hai, bohot hi latest technology based plant hai, profitability bohot acchi hai, margins bohot acche hain.

Aur agar is cheez ko dekhein ke iska impact humare tyre sector pe kya hoga, to jo existing companies hain wo zyaadatar jo hai bikes aur tractors ke tyres banati hain, to un pe koi impact nahi aayega. Sirf ek company hai jiska naam hai Ghandhara Tyre, jo ke gaariyon ke tyres bhi banati hai aur is company ka plan hai IPO ke baad passenger cars ke bhi tyres banane ka, to jab ye announce karenge ke hum kaun se size ka gaariyon ka tyre banane ja rahe hain, to ho sakta hai ke ye Ghandhara Tyre ko competition dein aur un ke liye ye ek negative development ho.
