Retirement ke liye kitna paisa chahiye? Aaj main aapko sach bataunga. Aap sochte hoge retirement ke liye ek crore rupay kafi hain. Bilkul ghalat. Aaj agar har mahine ek lakh ka kharcha hai to 20 saal baad mehengai ke baad pata hai ye har mahine ka kharcha kitna hoga? Paanch se cheh lakh minimum. Matlab retirement ke baad aapko har saal 60 se 70 lakh rupay ke to sirf kharche karne hain. Sirf jeene ke liye. To aapko retirement ke liye chahiye 10 se 12 crore. Ye 10 se 12 crore aapko ek drauna khwaab lag raha hoga. Lekin darne ki bilkul zaroorat nahi hai. Agar aaj se har mahine 40,000 invest karoge 20 saal tak, average 17% return jo ke stock exchange ka average return hai, to roughly 10 crore ban sakte hain. 40,000 per month aaj mushkil lagta hai lekin budhape mein bina paison ke jeena wo zyada mushkil hai. Seedhi baat, retirement plan nahi hai to retirement nahi hogi, sirf budhapa hoga. Aur budhapa bina paison ke Pakistan mein sabse barhi aziyat hai. To aaj se saving karna shuru karo aur invest karo.
