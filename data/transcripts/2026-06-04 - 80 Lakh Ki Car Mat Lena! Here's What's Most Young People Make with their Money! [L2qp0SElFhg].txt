Aap ke paas 80 lakh rupaye hain to new car le kar apni zindagi ka sab se badi financial ghalti mat karna kyun ke ek simple technique mein batane ja raha hoon jo aap ki gaadi ka petrol aur maintenance bilkul muft kar sakti hai. 80 lakh ek hi gaadi par lagane ke bajaye aap 50 lakh ki usi tarah ki ek used gaadi le lo aur baqi bache hue 30 lakh rupaye Pakistan Stock Exchange mein dividend paying companies mein 10% dividend par invest kar do. Ab suno genuine math aur magic. PSX mein lagaye gaye 30 lakh par 10% return ke mutabiq aap ko har saal ka 3 lakh bank mein dividend aa raha hoga yani har mahine takreeban 25,000 rupaye jis se aap ka petrol aur maintenance ka kharcha nikal gaya. Aur maze ki baat, char saalon mein most probably ye 30 lakh dividend kharch karne ke baad bhi 40 se 50 lakh tak pohanch jayein ge capital gain ke sath. Ye karein, is se aap ka capital bhi badhta jaye ga aur aap ki jo gaadi ki requirement hai wo bhi achhe tareeqe se fulfill ho jaye gi.
