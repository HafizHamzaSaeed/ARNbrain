[Published: 2026-06-05]

Budget 2025, auto sector ke liye ek bara twist aa gaya hai. Aaj se kuch din pehle aisa lag raha tha ke budget mein duties aur sales tax, PHEVs, EVs aur jo hamari hybrid gariyan hain un sab pe kam hone ja rahi hain, lekin ab rumors aise aa rahe hain ke hybrids jin pe abhi 8.5% sales tax hai, ye shayad barh ke 25% tak bhi chala jaye aur jo PHEVs hain un pe jo 8.5% sales tax hai, us ki baatein ho rahi thin ke wo 1% tak aa jayega, ab ho sakta hai ke ye 8.5% pe hi maintain rahe. So ye un companies ke liye negative hai jin ke paas sirf hybrid gariyan hain aur un ke paas PHEVs aur EVs jo hain apne portfolio mein nahi hain, jis ke andar Indus Motors aa jati hai kyunke abhi tak unhone koi PHEV launch nahi ki aur unki jo hybrid gariyan hain wo mehengi ho jayengi. Sazgar ki baat karein to Sazgar ke paas normal gariyan bhi hain, hybrids bhi hain aur PHEVs bhi hain, to wo jo hai is se unaffected rahenge. Aur pichle mahino ka star, Jaecoo, jo ke NCPL aur NPL hai, us pe bhi negative effect aa sakta hai kyunke unki best-seller J5 abhi tak sirf hybrid version mein aayi hai aur wo kafi zyada mehengi ho sakti hai jiski wajah se unki sale kam ho sakti hai.
