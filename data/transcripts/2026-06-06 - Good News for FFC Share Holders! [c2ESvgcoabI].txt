[Published: 2026-06-06]

FFC ke investors ke liye bohat bari khabar. Shayad bohat saalon baad Pakistan mein ek naya fertilizer plant jo hai wo lagne ja raha hai. CPEC 2.0 ke under FFC ne FEED study ka contract jo hai wo sign kar liya hai ek Chinese company ke sath jismein FFC Thar Coal ko pehle gas mein convert karegi aur phir usse urea banayegi. Ye plant almost 1 billion dollar ki investment hogi aur 0.7 million ton urea produce karegi. Thar Coal ki jo per ton price hai uske hisab se is project mein jo urea banega uske margins ache honge aur ho sakta hai kuch saalon ke liye FFC ke is project ko tax exemption bhi mil jaye special economic zone mein. Aur is project se jo profit ayega usse FFC ka EPS jo hai wo almost 10 rupay per share se increase ho jayega. Lekin ye project lagne mein taqreeban 5 saal lagenge aur company plan kar rahi hai ke ye 2031 tak jo hai wo online ayega.
