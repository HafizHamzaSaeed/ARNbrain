Har mahine 20,000 rupay invest karne wala haar gaya aur sirf 10,000 rupay se shuru karne wala jeet gaya. Yeh jadoo kaise hua? Chaliye dekhte hain. Ali har mahine fix 20,000 rupay invest karta hai agle 25 saalo ke liye. Dusri taraf hai Bilal, sirf 10,000 rupay se shuru karta hai lekin har saal apni investment ko 15% barha deta hai. Jaise jaise uski income barhti hai, har mahine ki saving jo invest karta hai usko barha deta hai. Isko kehte hain step-up SIP. 25 saal baad agar dono ko average har saal 16% return mila to Ali ka fix 20,000 har mahine ka ban gaya taqreeban 5 crore rupay. Lekin Bilal ka 10,000 ka monthly contribution jo har saal 15% barh raha tha, wo ban jayega taqreeban 8.5 crore. To samajhne ki baat kya hai?
