Dosto, agar main aapko do option doon, aaj hi ek crore cash lelo ya fir sirf ek rupya dunga aaj lekin har din wo double hota rahega ek mahine ke liye, to kya choose karoge? Ek crore, obviously. Ek brand new gadi lunga, aish hi aish honge. Ab doosra option dekhte hain. Pehle din ek rupya, doosre din do rupaye, teesre din char rupaye, phir aath rupaye aur daswen din tak ye paanch sau rupaye ho jayenge, aur beeswen din tak ye ho jayenge paanch lakh rupaye. Lekin phir bhi, ek crore? Ab asli jadoo shuru hoga. Beeswen din jo paanch lakh hai wo jab har din double hoga to teeswen din ye ban jayenge paanch crore chhatees lakh rupaye. Aapne kya choose kiya tha? Ek rupya ya ek crore? Apne doston ko bhejo aur dekho wo kya choose karte hain. Ise kehte hain power of compounding. Zindagi ho ya paisa, consistency se hi asal game badalti hai.
