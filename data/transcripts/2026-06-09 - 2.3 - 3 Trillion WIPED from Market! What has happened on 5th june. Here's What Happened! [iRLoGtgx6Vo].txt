5 June 2026, investing aur global markets ki dunya mein hamesha yaad rakha jayega kyunki is din almost dhai se teen trillion dollar tak ka investors ko nuksaan hua. Ab zyada tar log pichle dinon mein soch rahe hain ke iski wajah ho sakti hai ke jang phir se shuru hone wali hai, is wajah se logon ne already jo hai Friday ko selling kar di hai. Lekin iska asal reason hai, jo log follow kar rahe honge, America mein teen bade IPOs jiske andar Claude, jisko Anthropic kehte hain, SpaceX, aur ChatGPT OpenAI ke aane wale hain, jiski valuation jo hai 4 billion dollar tak expect ki ja rahi hai. To pehle aise lag raha tha ke jo log ETF mein invest karte hain unko already iska exposure mil jayega, yeh index mein include ho jayenge. Lekin ab yeh cheez jo hai mid 2027 tak delay ho gayi hai, to logon ne in IPOs mein invest karne ke liye apni liquidity arrange ki hai. Aur jab logon ne in IPOs mein invest karne ke liye stock market se bhi paisa nikala, gold se bhi paisa nikala, even crypto se bhi paisa nikala, to har jagah jo hai prices down ho gayin aur is wajah se yeh ek mini crash global markets ne experience kiya.
