Pakistan mein Islamic banking ka baadshah kaun hai? Sab kahenge Meezan Bank. Aur woh ghalat bhi nahi keh rahe. Lekin ek aur bank hai, jo sirf kuch saal pehle tak conventional bank hota tha. Aur aaj fully Islamic Bank ban chuka hai, Faysal Bank. Aur conversion ke baad kuch aisa hua jo kisi ne expect bhi nahi kiya tha. Deposits barhne lage. Current accounts ki percentage jo hai upar jaane lagi. Aur ab iske saath ek aur twist hai. Maple Leaf, ek cement ki company, is bank mein up to 30% stake lene ki koshish kar rahi hai. Is tarah ki investments Maple Leaf Cement ne pehle bhi ki hain. Pioneer Cement ke saath, AgriTech Fertilizer ke saath. Aur jab market ko samajh aaya to Pioneer Cement ek premium par trade hona shuru ho gaya. To sawal ye hai, kya Faysal Bank ke saath history phir se rhyme karne wali hai?
