Aur banking business mein higher current accounts ka matlab hota hai lower funding cost aur potentially better profitability. To shuru karte hain apne pehle aur sabse important concept se, Current Accounts. Banking industry mein kisi bhi bank ki profitability ko determine karne ke liye multiple factors hote hain lekin un sab mein se sabse important cheez hoti hai funding cost, yaani ke depositor aap ke paas jo paisa rakhwaya hua hai, aap ne us ko us par kitna return dena hai. Banking business asal mein kaafi zyada simple hota hai. Bank customers se deposits leta hai aur unhi paison ko aage financing, loan ke taur de doosre customers ko de deta hai. Aur deposits ki cost ya deposits pe kitna profit dena hai aur lending yield yaani ke loan kis rate pe diya hua hai, in dono ke beech ka jo difference banta hai wo actually bank ki profitability ko drive karta hai. Isi liye har deposit equally valuable nahi hota bank ke liye kyunki different-different profit earn kar rahe hote hain. Savings account mein bank ko customer ko return dena padta hai jabke current accounts mein effectively koi return pay nahi kiya jata. Aur isi wajah se current accounts banking industry mein bahut important hote hain. Aaiye aap ko ek maze ka qissa sunata hoon. Aaj se kuch mahine pehle main ek bank mein account khulwane gaya. To jaise jaise account opening ke forms wagera fill ho rahe the, saath saath mein bank manager se baatein bhi karta ja raha tha, un se samajhne ki koshish kar raha tha apni investing ko improve karne ke liye ke saare banks jo hain un ki branches hoti hain. Even agar aap saare bade banks bhi dekh lein un ki har jagah branches hoti hain lekin current account ki percentage banks mein different ho sakti hain. To main un se samajhna chah raha tha ke ye difference jo hai ye kaise create hota hai. So ye conversation jo hai wo chalti gayi lekin phir mujhe bank manager ne ek badi important baat batayi. Aur unho ne mujhe bataya ke jab mere paas koi bhi customer aata hai, main us ko zyada se zyada facilities deta hoon, hamari ATM machines ke baare mein batata hoon, hum log jo cards pe facilities de rahe hote hain us ke baare mein batata hoon, aur meri koshish hoti hai ke us ke badle wo hamare saath ek current account open kar le. Aur phir unho ne mujhe bataya ke Islamic banking ka yehi advantage hai ke jab customer aap ke paas aa raha hota hai aap ki branch mein aur wo dekh ke aa raha hota hai, Islamic banking dekh ke attract ho kar aa raha hota hai to us ka mind pehle se hi thoda bahut bana hota hai ke mere liye current account hi behtar hai. To isi wajah se aap dekhenge ke jo Islamic banking network hai, us ke current accounts conventional industry se behtar hain. Numbers se bhi is concept ko aur clearly samajh lete hain. Aaj kal interest rate roughly 11 ke aas paas chal rahe hain, aur average loan ke rate bhi takreeban 11 se 12% ki range mein chal rahe hain. So current accounts pe bank poora 11 se 12% ka spread jo hai wo earn kar leta hai. Lekin savings account par bank ko apne customer ko roughly saal ka 8-9% return dena padta hai. Yaani savings account par bank ka spread sirf 3 se 4% ke aas paas rehta hai. Aur doosri taraf current account pe almost 11 to 12%. Yaani ke bank ke liye current account 3 to 4 times more earn karke de raha hota hai. Faysal Bank ke paas roughly 1,500 arab rupaye ke deposits hain. Aur is mein se takreeban 40% current accounts hain. Isi deposit size ke jo conventional banks hain, us mein agar aap current account ki percentage dekhenge to wo 25 se 30% hai. Lekin Faysal Bank ki 40% hai jo ke conventional industry se kaafi behtar hai. Aur isi trend ka impact Faysal Bank ke numbers mein bhi clearly nazar aata hai. 2019 tak Faysal Bank ka current account ratio roughly 30% tha. Us waqt ye bank conventional hota tha. Lekin Islamic conversion ke baad ye gradually badhkar around 40% tak pahunch gaya.

Ab chalte hain apne doosre important concept pe aur wo hai Deposit Franchise. Deposit Franchise kya hai? Deposit Franchise essentially un banks ki strength hoti hai jahan depositors naturally khud aakar paise rakhne mein comfortable feel karte hain. In banks ki widespread branches bhi hoti hain, ATMs jo hain almost har ilaake mein available hote hain, aur customers generally institution par trust bhi feel karte hain kyunki itna bada bank hota hai. Aur jab customers naturally deposits la rahe hon, khud chal kar, to deposit growth achieve karna bhi aasan ho jata hai. Aur jaise maine aap ko bataya jab customer khud chal kar aa raha ho aur strong bank ka network aur us ki facilities bhi enjoy karega future mein, to current account open karwane ke chances bhi badh jaate hain. Maple Leaf ki ek recent corporate briefing aaj se do teen mahine pehle, us mein management ne explain kiya tha ke Pakistan jaise high tax economies jahan pe tax almost 45% tak chala jata hai, yahan pe long-term returns ko materially improve karne ke liye best tareeqa hota hai ke aap zyada se zyada jo hai bank se loan lein, apne paise kam se kam lagayein taake jo bank ki financing cost hai wo aap ke profit ko reduce kare aur aap ko kam se kam jo hai wo tax pay karna pade. Aur is strategy ko business terms mein leveraged buyouts ya leveraged-based expansion ya phir bank se loan le lena aur phir different businesses aur assets acquire kar lena ye soch ke ke financing cost aaj kal kam hai aur jo main business buy kar raha hoon us se mujhe zyada return aayega aur us se tax ki payment bhi kam ho jayegi. Aur isi wajah se group pichle kuch saalon mein apni expansion strategy aggressively pursue karta hua nazar aa raha hai. Aur historically agar aap Pakistan mein dekhenge to banking aur fertilizer jaise sectors stable businesses mein shumaar hote hain jabke cement business naturally economic cycle ke saath fluctuate karta hai. Interesting cheez Faysal Bank ki share price ke liye ye hai ke 2022 mein jab Maple Leaf Cement ne Pioneer Cement mein aggressively buying karna shuru ki to market ko idea hona shuru ho gaya ke future mein full buyout pipeline mein ho sakta hai.
