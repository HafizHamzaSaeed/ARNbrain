First, salaried class ko kitna relief degi government? Jab tak salaried class pe tax rate kam nahi hoga, Pakistan economy flourish nahi kar sakti. 

Second, agar Pakistan ko FDI, Foreign Direct Investment, attract karni hai, to income tax rate aur super tax rate ko kam karna hoga. 

Aur teesri aur chauthi cheez is related to the environment aur jo climate change ka challenge bana hua hai, first, kya Government of Pakistan refineries ko upgrade karne ki policy jo hai finally approve karti hai, jis se jo hai hamari pollution kam hogi aur Euro 5 compliant fuel Pakistan mein banega. 

Aur aakhri, auto sector ke andar auto policy aati hai jo ke new energy vehicles, both bikes, trucks aur gaariyon ko promote karti hai ke nahi.
