Salary barh rahi hai, lekin savings nahi ho pa rahi. Kya karoon? Pehli job 40,000 rupay pe lagi thi. Tab lagta tha bas 80,000 ho jaye, sab set ho jayega. Aur aaj 80,000 kama leta hoon, aur ab lagta hai bas derh lakh ho jaye, tab sukoon mil jayega. Hamesha sochta rehta hoon ke yeh sukoon kaise aayega, kyunki har baar salary barhti hai aur meri zaroorat us se pehle barh jati hai. Jab 40,000 salary thi, tab bike thi. Jab derh lakh salary hui, to gaari pe aa gaya. Income upar gayi, lekin bachat aaj bhi zero. Yeh ek trap hai. Jitna kamaoge, utna kharch karoge aur retire hote waqt, tab bhi haath khaali honge. Is ka sirf ek hal hai. Jab bhi salary barhe, us increment ka kuch hissa PSX mein daal do. Tumhara paisa tumhare liye kaam karna shuru kar dega. 40,000 ka increment mile agli dafa, to 20,000 invest kar dena. Baqi enjoy karo. 10 saal aisa karo, average 15% return, roughly 55 se 60 lakh ka portfolio ban sakta hai, sirf un increment se jo tum waise bhi kharch kar dete thay. Seedhi baat, maslon ka hal dhoondo aur hal hai investing aur compounding. To agla increment lifestyle pe lagaoge ya apne financial freedom pe? Comments mein batao.
