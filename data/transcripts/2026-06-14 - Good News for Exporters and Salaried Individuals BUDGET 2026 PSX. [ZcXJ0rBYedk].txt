PSX investors ke liye budget kaisa tha? Bohat zabardast. Na koi naya dividend tax, na koi naya capital gain tax, aur uske saath saath bohat zada companies pe super tax 10% se kam ho ke 8% ho gaya hai. Aur exporters pe toh super tax khatam karne ki baatein ho rahi hain. 

Iske saath saath middle to high income salary earners of Pakistan, unke liye toh shayad 2020 se le ke aaj tak ka ye sabse acha budget tha. Kaafi zada government ne salary pe tax relief di hai, jiska poora result mere hisab se PSX pe ye hoga ke market FY27 mein kaafi bullish rahegi. FY26 mein PSX ne taqreeban 50,000 points ki rally show ki aur mujhe lagta hai isi tareeqe se FY27 mein bhi ye rally 50,000 points ki repeat ho sakti hai.
