Cement sector ke liye budget 2024-25 kaisa raha? And the answer is very positive. Sab se pehle toh Government of Pakistan ne real estate transactions pe buying aur selling pe jo advance tax pay karna hota tha usko dono ko half kar diya hai. Cost side pe aa jayein toh bohot zyada chances hain ke coal pe jo 5% ki custom duty thi, usko 0% kar diya gaya hai. Is ke sath sath, sab se important news ke cement sector pe super tax jo hai usko 10% se 8% pe Government of Pakistan le aayi hai. Aur jo cement export hota hai, uspe super tax jo hai woh bilkul khatam hone ki baatein bhi chal rahi hain. Is sab ke beneficiaries kaun honge? Almost saara cement sector: Kohat Cement, Lucky Cement, Maple Leaf Cement, Fauji Cement, DG Khan Cement.
