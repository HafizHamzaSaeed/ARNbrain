Textile sector ke liye budget 2026 kaisa tha? Answer is positive. Wazir-e-Azam ki hidayat pe exporters pe super tax 10% se 0% karne ki khabrain hain. Is ke sath sath exports pe jo 2% ka minimum tax hota tha, us ko bhi reduce kar ke 1.25% kar diya gaya hai. Is ke sath sath exports mein jo raw materials use hote hain, un pe bhi custom duties jo hain wo kam ki gayi hain. Aur exporters ko bank se karza to already 4.5% interest rate pe mil hi raha hai. In sab developments ke beneficiaries kaun honge hamari stock market pe? Interloop Limited, Nishat Mills, Gul Ahmed Textiles.
