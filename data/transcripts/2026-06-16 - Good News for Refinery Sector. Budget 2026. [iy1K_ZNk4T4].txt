Refineries ke liye budget 2026 kaisa tha? And the answer is game-changer. Itne saalon se jo refinery policy delay hoti ja rahi thi, finally us ka final roadblock jo hai woh budget 2026 mein khatam kar diya gaya. Aur ab refineries Pakistan mein upgrade hongi. Pakistan mein petrol aur diesel jo hai usme self-sufficient bhi ho jayenge aur Euro 2 ke muqable mein ab hum Euro 5 fuel jo hai woh bhi locally banayenge. Iske sath sath super tax jaise refineries pe 10% se 8% pe aa gaya hai, toh usse jo aaj kal refineries ke bumper profits chal rahe hain because of the USA Iran war aur global oil shortage, us mein bhi companies ki profitability mein fayda hoga. Charon listed refineries hi upgradation ki taraf jayenge. Ab aap ne mujhe comments mein batana hai ke charon mein se aap ko kya lagta hai ke kaun si refinery ko sab se zyada  fayda hoga.
