Sab se pehli cheez budget mein dekhni hoti hai ke jo Government of Pakistan budget le kar aayi hai us se macro-economic stability aur jo current account deficit hai, fiscal deficit hai yaani ke logon ka jo economy pe ek tarah se trust bana hota hai jis se jo hai hum bust cycle mein nahi jaate wo continue rahe ga ke nahi? To bohot zyada log soch rahe the ke government ke paas to bilkul fiscal space hi nahi hai to phir ye jo itne zyada taxes kam kar diye hain aur salaried class ko bhi itni zyada relief di hai ye sab aya kahan se? Pehle mein aap ko ye explain kar deta hoon. Federal government aur provincial governments ke beech mein ek agreement hua hai ke abhi ye jo saal khatam ho raha hai is mein hum FBR ke taqreeban 13.5 trillion ke jo hain taxes jo hai wo collect karein ge aur aap ko to pata hai 57.5% share jo hai soobon ko chala jata hai. To agle saal ke liye ye agreement hua hai ke agle saal ka jo FBR ka target hai 15.25 trillion ka us mein 13.3 trillion se upar ki jo collection ho gi jo ke almost 2 trillion rupay ban jaate hain us mein federal government jo hai provinces ke sath share nahi kare gi. To agar hum apna tax target jo hai wo achieve karte hain to ek tarah se federal government ko additional 1200 arab rupay jo ke soobon ko chale jaate wo ab nahi jayein ge. Agar hum ye tax target achieve nahi bhi karte phir bhi 700 se 800 arab rupay jo hai ek tarah se federal government ko fayda ho raha hai aur usi ko use karte waqt federal government ne apna fiscal discipline yaani ke fiscal deficit, primary surplus ka target ye sab jo hai IMF ke sath jo agree kiya hua hai us ke hisab se hi rakha hai aur sath sath tax rate kam karne mein bhi in ko ek gunjaish mil gayi hai. To overall budget macro-economic stability point of view se bilkul theek hai aur is ko mein ek growth budget bhi kahoon ga.

PSX ke upar jaise mein ne aap ko intro mein already bata diya capital gain tax koi increase nahi hua, dividend tax koi increase nahi hua, oil and gas exploration sector, fertilizer sector aur banking sector ko chorh ke baqi sab sectors pe jo hai super tax Government of Pakistan ne 10% se 8% kar diya hai aur exporters ke liye zero karne ka wada kiya hai. To ek tarah se tax point of view se ye budget sab companies ke liye jo hai positive hai other than banks, fertilizer companies aur oil and gas exploration companies. Oil and gas sector ki jo puri chain hai us ko discuss kar lete hain ke budget mein us se related kya hai. Sab se important cheez refinery policy mein jo ek final hurdle jo hai wo aya hua tha wo mein ne aap ko apni preview video mein bhi bataya tha ke refineries jo hain government ke sath bohot zyada cooperate kar rahi hain. Duniya bhar mein diesel jo hai bohot zyada mehanga ho gaya tha 200 dollar, 250 dollar tak chala gaya tha lekin refineries ne apne profit ko pichle do teen mahino mein thoda sa sacrifice kiya jis ka reward jo hai ab refineries ko Government of Pakistan ne payback kiya hai aur ab jo brownfield expansion jo ke charon refineries Pakistan mein ho gi jo listed hain us pe sales tax jo hai un ko pay nahi karna pare ga. Listed space mein sari refineries jo hain wo upgrade karne ka plan rakhti hain aur let's say example le lete hain National Refinery ki, Attock Refinery ki, ye donon refineries jo hain taqreeban 500 million dollar ki expansion plan kar rahe hain. To jab in ko 18% sales tax machinery import pe pay nahi karna ho ga to ek tarah se taqreeban in ke 100 million dollar jo hai wo save ho jayein ge aur refinery upgradation ki feasibility improve ho gaye gi. Is ke elawa petrol aur diesel pe jo Government of Pakistan Petroleum Development Levy collect karti hai, Finance Minister ne bataya hai ke wo taqreeban 80 rupay per liter pe hi rahe gi aur ye jo increase hua hai PDL ki collection agle saal ke liye wo zyada petrol bechne ki wajah se aur kyun ke pichle saal ke jo shuru ke mahine the us mein ye 80 rupay nahi hota hota tha to is wajah se ye increase budget ke numbers mein nazar aa raha hai. Lekin government ne ye bhi wada kiya hai ke ye taqreeban 80 rupees per liter hi rahe gi is se zyada nahi ho gi on average basis. Is ke elawa ek choti si cheez udhar bhi behtari hui hai ke kuch chemicals aise hote the jis ko log petrol aur diesel mein mix kar ke aur phir fuel pumps pe supply karte hote the aur us pe koi tax nahi hota tha, us se un ki bohot zyada cost saving ho jati thi. Is se gaadiyan bhi kharab hoti thin to is pe bhi Government of Pakistan ne jitna petrol aur diesel pe PDL hai utni hi FED laga di hai. To ek tarah se ye jo ek loop-hole bana hua tha ye khatam ho jaye ga aur is se thodi si diesel aur petrol ki demand jo hai wo increase ho jaye gi.

So refinery policy ke liye jo ek bottleneck bana hua tha wo ab jab hat gaya hai to ab ye sector jo hai ye thoda sa limelight mein aa jaye ga. Ye ek disadvantaged sector bana hua tha kyun ke refinery policy continuously, continuously delay hoti ja rahi thi aur phir pichle saal furnace oil pe jo Government of Pakistan ne kaafi zyada tax laga diya tha jis ki wajah se jo local furnace oil bikta tha us ko export karna parta hai aur wahan jo hai refineries ko loss bardasht karna parta hai. Lekin ab jo hai ye sector ek tarah se negative development wale sector se ek positive development wale sector mein aa gaya hai aur even though refineries upgrade hone mein bohot zyada time lagta hai lekin ek sentiment jo hai wo bohot zyada improve ho jaye ga aur ye ek viable business Pakistan mein phir se ban jayein ge aur phir super tax jo hai wo bhi reduce ho gaya hai refineries pe to aaj kal jaise acche margins chal rahe hain diesel pe us se refineries ke profits bhi acche aayein ge, us pe tax rate bhi reduce ho gaya hai to us se bhi refineries ko fayda ho ga.

Ab aa jaate hain construction supply chain mein jis mein cement sector aa jata hai, steel sector aa jata hai aur glass sector aa jata hai. Real estate ki transactions pe buying aur selling pe jo advance tax hota tha donon ko Government of Pakistan ne jo hai half kar diya hai aur ab ye 1.25% aur 2.75% ho gaye hain. Late filer ka concept jo hai wo bhi khatam ho gaya hai. Is ke elawa agar aap ke paas ek se zyada property ho to us pe jo aap ko 7E ka 1% tax har saal dena parta tha FBR value ke hisab se wo bhi is budget mein khatam kar diya hai aur is ke sath sath abhi lately jo Government of Pakistan ne apna ghar scheme announce ki thi jis ke andar 10 saal ke liye aap ko 5% interest rate pe ghar banane ke liye ek crore tak ka qarza mil raha tha us ke liye bhi budget mein 71 billion rupay jo hain wo rakh diye hain. So is sab se construction sector jo hai behtari ki taraf ja sakta hai, jis se cement, steel aur glass teenon ki demand pichle saal ke muqable mein zyada rehne ke chances hain. Is ke elawa cement sector ki baat karein to ek to again in pe super tax jo hai wo kam ho gaya, us se in ke profits jo hai wo barh jayein ge, is ke elawa cement sector ka main raw material coal, us pe bhi custom duty jo hai shayad 5% se 0% kar di gayi hai. Shayad mein is liye keh raha hoon kyun ke jo duties ki details hain wo abhi tak Government of Pakistan ne release nahi kin. Is ke elawa steel sector ka jo sales tax hota tha kyun ke is mein bohot zyada undocumented companies bhi kaam karti hain to sale pe sales tax ke bajaye aap jo bijli istemaal kar rahe hain us mein hi per unit sales tax lag ke aa jaye ga jis se undocumented sector mein operate karna aur sales tax evade karna mushkil ho jaye ga. To ye ek positive hai steel sector ke liye aur is ke sath sath FATA PATA jaise pichle budget mein agree hua tha ke us ka jo sales tax hai wo bhi har saal 2% barhta jaye ga to wo bhi steel sector ke liye ek positive cheez hai aur jo international development ho rahi hai Iran aur US se related, us se jab oil prices neeche aati hain to phir aahista aahista coal prices bhi reduce hone ke chances hote hain to wo bhi is sector ke liye ek positive hai.

So cement, steel aur glass sector mein agar aap investment plan kar rahe hain to meri taraf se ye mashwara ho ga ke cement sector aur glass sector ko zyada prefer kiya jaye kyun ke jo Pakistan ka IMF ke sath agreement hua hai jis mein aap ne import duties jo hai us ko aahista aahista kam karna hai us mein steel pe duties bhi kam hone ke chances hain. To even though agar steel sector ke profits thode se increase ho bhi rahe hon ge, ek tarah se ek talwar jo hai un pe latki hui hai jis ka impact do teen saal baad aa sakta hai aur is tarah ki koi talwar ho kisi sector pe to us ki value realization jo hai wo thodi si affect ho jati hai aur cement sector mein bhi jo south-based companies hain kyun ke wo cement export karti hain to exports pe agar super tax jo hai wo khatam ho gaya to wo bhi ek beneficiary ho sakte hain aur Karachi mein jaise residential plots ko commercial mein convert karne ki jo restriction hai wo khatam hui hai to us se south ki local cement demand bhi improve ho sakta hai. Lekin phir bhi long-term ke liye investment north-based cement plants mein jo hai wo zyada acche returns dene ke chances hote hain kyun ke ye local market mein zyada cement bechte hain aur ye import se bhi protected rehte hain. Ab baat karte hain auto sector ki aur auto sector ka asal budget jo hai ministers keh rahe hain ke agle kuch hafton mein aaye ga kyun ke auto policy agle paanch saalon ke liye jis ke andar sara decide ho ga ke PHEVs pe kya duties hon gi, hybrids pe kya duties hon gi, hybrids pe sales tax kya ho ga, PHEVs pe sales tax kya ho ga wo thode dino mein expected hai. Abhi ke liye is budget mein jo cheez announce ki hai wo ek to jo EVs pe 1% sales tax tha 50 kilowatt battery se neeche wo ek saal ke liye aur extend kar diya hai aur is ke sath sath jo mehangi imported gaadiyan hain us pe FEDs increase kar di hain aur is ke sath sath mehangi gaadiyon mein EVs jo ke do crore se upar hain un pe bhi FED apply kar di hai. EV bikes, EV rickshaws, EV buses aur EV trucks pe jo hai sales tax ko 1% kar diya gaya hai aur is ke sath sath jo kuch relaxations expire ho rahi thin 30 June 2026 us mein sales tax on PHEVs jo ke taqreeban saade aath percent tha aur hybrids jo ke saade aath aur saade barah percent tha wo ab expire ho gaya hai. To agar to nayi auto policy thodi si delay ho jati hai to phir in donon type of gaadiyon pe sales tax jo hai wo 18% ya 25% tak chala jaye ga. Ye ek negative to zaroor hai lekin is ke sath sath ye jo new auto policy aa rahi hai us pe kaafi zyada kaam already ho chuka hai aur kuch hi hafton mein us ke approval ke chances hain to ek tarah se market forward looking hoti hai sab expect kar rahe hain aage PHEVs aur EVs pe government jo hai duties bhi kam rakhe gi, sales tax bhi kam rakhe gi. So jitni bhi companies hain jo specially PHEVs produce karti hain un ke liye auto policy jo hai wo ek positive ho jaye gi. PHEVs mein NCPL, NPL aa jati hai jo ke JAC brand ko Pakistan mein lekar aaye hain, phir Sazgar aa jati hai, inhone apni sari gaadiyan jo hain PHEV version mein launch ki hui hain aur jaise mein ne apni pichli video mein bataya tha government agar acchi ek paanch saal ke liye PHEVs ko encourage karne ke liye policy lekar aati hai to ho sakta hai Indus Motors bhi Corolla ka PHEV version jo hai Pakistan mein launch kar de.

Ab aa jaate hain IT sector pe aur IT sector ke liye bhi budget kaafi accha raha. Do bohot important cheezein, ek ke IT exports pe jo 0.25% of export value final tax tha income tax, wo agle teen saal ke liye continue kar diya gaya hai. So ab ye jo IT ka income tax agle teen saal ke liye fix ho gaya hai to jis speed se IT exports barh rahi hain us ke continue rehne ke chances hain aur aaj hamari freelance plus jo companies ki IT exports hain wo taqreeban saade paanch se che billion dollar per year hain, ye easily jo hain 10 billion dollar tak ja sakti hain teen saal mein. Is ke sath sath IT sector ke liye ek aur khushkhabri hai kyun ke IT sector mein hum bohot saare tools jo hai online use kar rahe hote hain international website se jis mein credit card se payment karni hoti hai. To abhi Pakistan mein aap credit card se koi bhi payment karte hain to us pe kaafi zyada taxes aur charges bank ke charges lag jaate hain. To ek advance tax jo ke 5% lagta tha us ko government ne reduce kar ke 0.5% kar diya hai. To yahan ek dollar agar aap spend karein ge to aap ki koi taqreeban 10 se 11 rupay per transaction, per dollar jo hai wo saving ho jaye gi. Is ke elawa YouTube aur different social media platforms se jo aap earning karte hain, even though aisi koi khaas companies hain nahi listed space mein other than Hum Network, un pe jo hai 5% tax Government of Pakistan ne apply kar diya hai. So IT sector mein Systems Limited jo hai is development ka ek beneficiary ho sakta hai lekin is ke sath sath kyun ke Systems Limited Middle East mein bohot zyada export karta hai to wahan ki economy kis tareeqay se is shock se recover karti hai wo bhi ek important cheez ho gi dekhne ke liye before making any investment decisions. Aur ab aa jaate hain textile sector pe aur textile sector mein jo super tax Government of Pakistan ne wada kiya hai abolish karne ka exporters ke liye us ka sab se bada beneficiary ho ga Interloop, kyun ke Interloop ek high margin business hai aur us pe jab aap ka tax rate 39% se 29% pe aaye ga to us se after tax profits jo hain kaafi zyada increase ho jayein ge aur ye ek bada accha step hai Government of Pakistan ka agar aap exports pe taxes jo hai kam karein ge us se exports barhne ke chances hote hain, exports mein further capacities lagne ke chances hote hain aur us se phir aap sustainable growth ke model pe chal parte hain. Is ke sath sath abhi jab aap koi bhi cheez export karte the, goods export karte the to aap ko 1% advance tax bhi dena hota tha aur 1% minimum turnover tax bhi dena hota tha. Is minimum turnover tax ko 1% se barha ke 1.25% kar diya gaya hai aur advance tax jo hai us ko khatam kar diya gaya hai. Is development pe industry ke mixed opinions hain kyun ke jo advance tax hota tha wo aap ke doosre refunds jo hain us ke against adjust ho jata tha lekin jo minimum turnover tax hota hai wo to aap ne pay karna hi karna hota hai. To jo bohot hi low margin wale businesses hain jo textile companies hain jo export karti hain un ke liye ye ek thodi si negative development ho sakti hai. Aur is ke sath sath ek aur acchi development ye bhi hai ke aaj se do teen mahine jo Wazir-e-Azam Pakistan ne announce kiya tha ke ab se exporters ko working capital ke jo loans milte hain bank se wo 4.5% pe milein ge us ke liye bhi government ne taqreeban 80 se 90 arab rupay jo hai budget mein rakhe hui hain. So is se exporters ki jo finance cost hoti hai wo kaafi zyada kam ho jaye gi aur ye sab companies ko almost benefit kare ga.

Ab thodi si baat kar lete hain salaried individuals ki. Pichle saal Government of Pakistan ne jo first two slabs the jis mein tax rate ko kam kiya gaya tha, is bar Government of Pakistan ne zyada focus kiya hai ke do lakh se upar agar aap ki salary hai per month to wahan pe tax rates jo hain us ko kaafi zyada kam kiya jaye aur agar aap ek company ki C-suite mein hain aur aap ki salary jo hai taqreeban aath, nau lakh rupay per month tak chali jati hai to aap pe ye jo 9% ka surcharge laga hua tha us ko bhi Government of Pakistan ne abolish kar diya hai. Is ka affect ye hota hai ke salaried individuals ki jo after tax salary hai jab us mein thodi improvement aaye gi to phir is se almost har sector ko hi thoda thoda fayda hota hai. Un ki disposable income jo hai wo increase ho jati hai to wo FMCG ke products jo hain wo bhi buy kar lete hain zyada thode se, thoda sa ye bhi sochte hain ke chalon ab apna ghar banane ka sochte hain, jis ne already construction start ki hoti hai wo us ko complete karne ka sochta hai, isi tareeqay se har sector ko thoda thoda demand increase mil jati hai.

Ab baat kar lete hain pharma sector ki aur pharma sector ke liye Government of Pakistan ne baqi companies ki tarah super tax jo hai us ko 2% se kam kar diya hai aur jo companies export bhi karti hain us pe phir exports earning pe super tax completely khatam hone ke chances bhi hain. Is ke elawa abhi jo duties ki details hain jaise mein ne aap ko bataya abhi aani baqi hain, to wo jaise aayein gi to us mein bhi expected hai ke pharma sector ke kuch raw materials pe duties jo hai wo mazeed kam hon gi jis se cost of raw materials jo hai wo thodi si aur kam ho jaye gi. So in donon developments ki wajah se pharma sector ke liye bhi budget jo hai ye positive raha aur ab agar super tax exports pe khatam ho ga to companies ko ek bohot bada signal mile ga ke hum Pakistan se bohot acche tareeqay se pharma sector ke products jo hain wo export kar sakte hain aur aap ko pata hai ke textile aur jo doosre established export sectors hain us ke elawa jo growing export sectors hain us mein se ek jis pe Government of Pakistan ka bohot zyada focus hai, jaise ke IT sector, us mein pharma sector bhi shamil hai. To ek tarah se agar Pakistan se exports zyada hon gi to again hum sustainable growth achieve karne ke chances barh jaate hain. So pharma sector mein almost sari companies ko hi benefit ho ga kyun ke super tax jo hai us ka rate kam ho raha hai.
