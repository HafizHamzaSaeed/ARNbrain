Auto sector ke liye budget 2026 kaisa tha? And the answer is auto sector ka asal budget abhi aana baqi hai. Jaise ke aapko pata hoga government of Pakistan auto policy 2026 pe kaafi zyada kaam kar chuki hai aur ministers keh rahe hain agle kuch hafton mein ho sakta hai final budget ke sath ye bhi aa jaye, jismein expected hai ke PHEVs aur EVs pe duties aur sales tax jo hai wo kaafi zyada kam rakhe jayenge taake Pakistan mein fuel ka istemaal kam se kam ho aur hamara oil import bill jo hai wo kam ho. Aur abhi jo budget aaya hai uspe mehengi gadiyon pe jo hai FED increase kar di gayi hai, 2000 cc se upar ki gadiyan imported wo bhi aati hain aur agar koi EV imported hai jiski price do crore se upar hai, wo bhi aa jati hai. Is category pe theek thaak taxes laga diye gaye hain. Aur hybrid aur PHEVs pe jo reduced sales tax tha jo 30 June 2026 ko expire ho raha tha ho sakta hai usse pehle hi ya uske kuch der baad hi ek new auto policy aa jaye to uska effect jo hai wo nullify ho jayega. Ab aap humein comments mein batayein ke auto sector mein aapki favorite company kaunsi hai aur aap kis company mein investment karne ka soch rahe hain?
