Pharma sector ke liye budget 2026 kaisa tha? And the answer is positive. Sabse pehle to super tax 10% se reduce ho ke 8% pe aa gaya hai, jis se almost har company ki jo after tax profits hain wo 3 se 4 percent se barh jayenge. Dawaiyan to hum sab khate hain lekin sath sath is cheez ki bhi samajh honi chahiye jis company se hum dawai khareed rahe hote hain us ke profit jo hain upar ja rahe hain ya neeche ja rahe hain. Budget 2026 mein most probably jo raw materials hote hain jis se pharma products bante hain un pe bhi duties jo hain thori thori kam ho jayengi. Is ke ilawa export pe kafi zyada chances hain, Government of Pakistan ne wada kiya hai ke exports pe super tax jo hai us ko hum bilkul zero kar denge. To is se bhi companies ko mazeed benefit hoga. Ab aap comments mein batayein ke aap ke hisab se kaun si pharma sector ki company hai jis ka portfolio sabse zyada strong hai aur jo aap ko investment ke liye sabse achhi lagti hai?
