Neutral is liye ke baqi sab companies pe super tax jo hai woh 10 percent se kam ho ke 8 percent ho gaya, lekin Government of Pakistan ne dekhte hue, in teenon sectors pe super tax ko 10 percent pe hi maintain kiya hai.
