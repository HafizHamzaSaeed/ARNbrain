30 saal ki umar mein zero savings? Ghabrao mat, yeh plan suno. Roz aisa lagta hai ke der ho gayi. Der nahi hui, lekin ab har saal qeemti hai. Maan lo aaj se shuru karte ho, 15,000 har mahine PSX mein, average 15% return. Har saal jab salary barhe toh monthly investment ko bhi barha dena, ziada nahi 10% se. Saath saath life bhi toh enjoy karni hai, paisa qabar mein le kar thodi jaana hai sirf. 10 saal baad aapka portfolio pohanch sakta hai 55 lakh tak. Abhi ruko, abhi toh party shuru hui hai. 20 saal baad ho jayenge pauney chaar crore rupay aur 30 saal baad jab retire hoge almost 20 crore rupay. 30 saal ki mehengai ke baad bhi theek thaak amount hai. Toh seekha ye ke 30 saal ki umar mein jo zero savings hain wo koi sharam ki baat nahi hai lekin start na karna bohot dangerous hai.
