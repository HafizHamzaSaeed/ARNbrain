Speaker 1: PE ratio.

ARN: Price to earning ratio aur isko sabse asan samajhne ka tareeqa hai ke ek jaise dukaan hoti hai rent, jiska ek lakh rupay rent aa raha hai aur uski value chal rahi hai das lakh rupay, to PE ratio ho gaya ten times.

Speaker 1: EPS.

ARN: Earnings per share, iska matlab hai ke ek company mein agar ek arab share hain aur woh total che arab rupay kama rahi hai, to EPS ho gaya six rupees per share.

Speaker 1: EBITDA.

ARN: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. Aur Pakistan mein, waise to duniya bhar mein bhi yeh metric jo hai bohat hi fazool mana jata hai, Pakistan mein aur bhi zyada fazool hai kyunke yahan pe interest bhi bohat zyada hota hai, taxes bhi bohat zyada hote hain, to yeh to company ki real earnings hoti hi nahi hain.

Speaker 1: ROIC.

ARN: Yeh mera favorite indicator hai, Return on Invested Capital. Isko aap aise sochein ke ek poora business chalane ke liye agar us mein sau rupay ki investment hoti hai ek company mein, to woh aap ko ek saal mein kitna kama ke deta hai? Bees rupay kama ke deta hai, tees rupay kama ke deta hai.

Speaker 1: Working capital cycle.

ARN: Working capital cycle hota hai ke agar aap ne sau rupay ke apne raw material khareede, us se product banaya aur apne customer ko bech diya, aur kitne din baad aap ko woh sau rupay wapas milenge? To us mein total jitne din lagte hain, woh hota hai working capital cycle.
