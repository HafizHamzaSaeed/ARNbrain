5 second aur 8 arab rupay. Ji haan, 5 second. 5 ghantay nahi, 5 minute nahi, sirf 5 seconds lagay Pakistan ke largest ever IPO ko subscribe honay mein. 70 arab rupay se ziyada ki investor demand sirf 7.8 billion ke IPO ke liye. Aur subscribe hua sirf 5 second mein. To sawal ye hai ke is company mein aisa kya tha ke itne saare log ek sath ek hi waqt mein is mein partner banna chahte thay? Ye company hai Service Long March Tyres, Pakistan ka pehla radial truck aur bus tyre manufacturer. Sirf teen saal pehle commercial production start hui thi is company ki. Aur aaj Pakistan ka largest IPO. Ab aap soch rahe honge tyres, normal si commodity hai, phir itni excitement kis baat ki? Kya aapko pata hai ke aap aur mein apni gaari ya bike ke tyres takreeban do saal baad change karwatay hain, lekin trucks aur buses wo ek saal mein do aur kabhi kabhi to teen baar apne tyres change karwa letay hain. Itni running hoti hai in commercial vehicles ki. Pakistan mein almost paanch lakh trucks aur buses hain. Yaani har saal 4 se 5 million tyres ki demand. Aur is poori demand ke liye saalon se ek hi jawab tha: imported tyres ya smuggled tyres. Phir aaya CPEC 2.0. Chinese manufacturing technology, Pakistan ki low-cost labor, renewable energy aur raw materials par almost zero duty. Aur is ne smugglers ki kamar tor di. High quality tyres kam cost mein Pakistan mein banne lagay. Lekin ek aur angle bhi hai jo mujhe personally sab se interesting lagta hai. Mein is company ko kehta hoon CPEC 2.0 ki flagship misaal. CPEC 1.0 mein China ne Pakistan mein infrastructure banaya, mostly IPPs. CPEC 2.0 ka idea ye tha ke China ki export industry Pakistan aaye, yahan se manufacture kare aur duniya ko export kare. SLM, Service Long March Tyres, exactly wahi kar raha hai. Aur dosto, 2026 mein ye company 6 lakh 20 thousand tyres export karne ki taraf ja rahi hai, jis ki value roughly 100 million dollar ke aas paas hogi. To ab aap pen aur paper saath rakh lein, aaj hum full detail mein Pakistan ke largest IPO ko discuss karne wale hain. Main hoon aap ka investing friend, Abdul Rehman Najam. Aaiye start karte hain.

Service Long March Tyre ki economic feasibility aapko pata hai kisne banayi? President Donald Trump ne. Ji haan, Service Long March Tyres ki story asal mein 2017 se shuru hoti hai. Aapko yaad hoga Donald Trump ke pehle tenure mein USA ne Chinese exports par aggressive anti-dumping duties lagani shuru kar di thin. Is wajah se Chinese manufacturers ke liye China se direct exports karna mehenga hota gaya. Aur isi period mein duniya bhar ki bahut si Chinese companies ne ek strategy adopt ki jis ka naam tha China Plus One, yaani China ke ilawa kisi aur mulk mein manufacturing base establish karna. Isi backdrop mein China ki one of the leading truck aur bus radial tyre manufacturer, Long March ne Service Industries ke saath joint venture sign kar liya aur Pakistan mein Service Long March Tyres ka safar shuru hua. March 2022 mein commercial production start hui aur company ki initial capacity 7 lakh 40 thousand tyres thi. Strategy shuru se hi export-led thi. FY23 mein 1 lakh 35 thousand tyres export kiye. FY24 mein ye number barh ke 3 lakh 27 thousand tak pahunch gaya. FY25 mein 4 lakh 34 thousand aur FY26 ke sirf first half mein around 3 lakh tyres export kiye gaye. Demand ko dekhte hue company ne continuously expansions kin. Capacity shuru mein 7 lakh 40 thousand tyres ki thi ek saal mein aur barhte barhte ye 16 lakh tyres tak pahunch gayi. Jis ke liye takreeban company ne total Rs. 30 billion ki investment ki. Scale ke saath production cost per tyre kam hoti gayi, aur is point par aakar management ne local market ko aggressively target karna shuru kiya. Kyunki local market mein smuggled aur under-invoiced tyres se mukabla karna tha. Ab yahan ek aur important concept samajhna zaroori hai. Truck tyres do major categories mein aate hain: Bias tyres aur Radial tyres. Radial tyres premium category mein count kiye jaate hain. In ki durability behtar hoti hai. In ko use karne se trucks ki fuel efficiency bhi improve ho jaati hai, load carrying capacity bhi ziyada hoti hai aur tyre life bhi lambi hoti hai. Isiliye duniya bhar mein transport industry gradually ab bias tyres se radial tyres ki taraf shift kar rahi hai. Ye ek behtar technology hai. Ab aate hain smuggling wale sawal par, jo yakeenan aapke dimaag mein bhi aa raha hoga ke smugglers aur under-invoiced imports se ye company kaise compete kar leti hai? Aur honestly, jab maine pehli dafa ye business dekha tha to mera pehla sawal bhi yahi tha. Smuggled tyres jo hain wo sales tax bhi nahi dete, duty bhi avoid kar letay hain, to phir SLM un ke saath kaise compete kar sakta hai? Compete bhi kar leta hai aur us ke saath itna achha profit bhi kaise kama leta hai? Exports ka business to samajh aa raha tha ke wahan pe smugglers se aap compete nahi kar rahe hote, lekin local market ke economics mere liye bhi ek shock tha. Reality ye hai ke smuggling ki bhi apni ek cost hoti hai. Extra freight kyunki aapko ghoom-ghoom ke product different location se Pakistan mein lana parta hai, border movement aur har stage par additional cost. Doosri taraf SLM ke paas hai superior Chinese manufacturing technology jo ke low cost hai. Saath mein Pakistan ki relatively low cost labor, renewable energy aur raw materials par comparatively almost zero duty. Misaal ke taur par natural rubber aur synthetic rubber par import duty almost zero hai, aur yahi do main raw materials hain is industry ke. Market survey ki baat karein to Pakistan ka most used radial tyre size R20, imported brands mein around 1.5 lakh se 1 lakh 60 thousand par pair mil jata hai, jabki SLM ka pair tyre approximately 1 lakh 40 thousand se 1 lakh 45 thousand ki price range mein hai. Sab se important baat ye hai ke imported smuggled tyres mein se bahut se bias tyres hote hain jabki SLM all-steel radial tyres manufacture karti hai aur saath warranty bhi provide karti hai. Yaani transporter jo ke khud ek businessman hota hai, usko almost same price range mein behtar technology, warranty aur reliable availability mil rahi hai. Isi wajah se company ne sirf teen saal mein domestic truck bus radial market ka around 43% share capture karne mein kamyab ho gayi hai.

Ab baat karte hain business ke sab se important metric yaani ROIC ki. December 2025 tak company ne approximately 30 billion apni property, plant aur jo 1.6 million tyres ki annual capacity wala plant hai, us mein invest kiye hain. Kyunki kuch investment us waqt bhi ki gayi thi jab dollar jo hai 160 rupay ka hota tha, to ye dollars mein total investment roughly 150 million dollar ban jaati hai. Is invested capital ke against company roughly 1.5 million tyres bech kar aur har tyre par takreeban 7,000 ka profit kama kar ek saal mein annual profit roughly 9 se 10 arab rupay kama leti hai, jis se ROIC around 20 se 22% dollarized ki range mein ban jata hai, jo kisi bhi heavy industrial manufacturing business ke liye kaafi strong hai. December 2026 tak company capacity ko 1.6 million tyres se 2 million tyres tak le ja rahi hai, jis ke liye additional investment jo hai wo takreeban 10 arab rupay ki ki ja rahi hai. Ek reason is achhe ROIC ka ye bhi hai ke company Nooriabad Special Economic Zone mein located hai, jahan 2032 tak income tax exemptions available hain. Ab baat karte hain IPO price ki, attractive hai ya ziyada hai, aur saath hi 2027 SLM ke profits ke liye kaisa lag raha hai. Is se pehle main aapko ek important baat batana chahta hoon. Ab baat karte hain IPO price ki. Ek modern 2 million ton manufacturing tyre plant ki replacement cost roughly 200 million dollar se 250 million dollar ki range mein hai. SLM ki IPO valuation takreeban 550 million dollar, yaani investors ne plant ki value se roughly do se dhai guna ziyada pe subscribe kiya hai. To kya ye saare investors ghalat thay? Main aap se apne real thoughts jo hai wo share karta hoon. ROIC 22 se 25% ki range mein hai is business ka. Seedhi baat ye hai ke ROIC to bahut attractive hai lekin ROIC to cost of investment par calculate kiya jata hai, aur yahan cost aur price ka jo ratio hai, wo almost 2 se 2.5 times hai. Aisi situation mein aap asal mein premium isliye pay kar rahe hote hain ke ye business ka ROIC jo hai wo kaafi ziyada attractive hai, to agar aaj main premium pay kar raha hoon, to jo future ka ROIC itna attractive hai us ka mujhe jo hai cost price ke hisab se faida hoga. Aur jaise jaise company expansions karti rahegi us se jo high ROIC generate hoga, us ka main pehle se hi partner bana hunga. Ye logic hoti hai ek high ROIC business mein premium pay kar ke long-term partner banne ki. Tyre manufacturing ek energy-intensive business hai. Bijli ek major cost hai, aur SLM ne aggressively renewable energy mein invest kiya hai, jis mein 7.5 megawatt wind turbine bhi shamil hai. Result, bijli ki cost sirf 7 cents per unit tak near future mein aa jayegi. Ab 2027 ki outlook ko numbers mein dekhte hain. Agar company ki capacity 2 million truck and bus radial tyres tak pahunch jaati hai 2026 ke aakhir tak, aur roughly 55% local aur 45% export sales mix assume kiya jaye, to 2027 mein total revenue approximately 85 se 87 billion rupees tak ban sakta hai. Is revenue base par gross profit roughly 21 se 22 arab rupay expected hai. Distribution aur admin expenses sales ke comparison mein controlled nazar aati hain. Aur despite ye ek manufacturing business hai jo ke capital intensive nature ka hota hai, finance cost bhi manageable level par reh sakti hain. Jis ka ek reason ye bhi hai ke company ne LTFF aur exports ke liye jo 4.5% pe interest financing mil rahi hai, wo avail kar rahe hain. Resultantly, 2027 mein company ka projected profit after tax roughly 16 to 18 billion tak pahunch sakta hai. Aur EPS aap apni screen par dekh sakte hain. Ye tamam numbers assumption based analytical scenarios hain aur actual results market demand, pricing aur policy developments par materially different bhi ho sakte hain. Ab sawal ye hai ke agar truck aur bus radial tyre business itni tezi se grow kar gaya hai, management ka agla growth engine kya hai? IPO kis wajah se kiya ja raha hai? Jawab hai Passenger Car Radial Tyres. IPO se company ne approximately 7.8 billion raise kiye hain, aur management ka plan hai ke ye paisa passenger car radial tyre plant mein invest kiya jaye. Yaani future mein sirf trucks aur buses ke tyres hi nahi, balki passenger cars jis ko aam zubaan mein gaariyan kehte hain, un ke tyres bhi locally manufacture kiye jayenge. Pakistan mein har saal takreeban dhai lakh new cars sell hoti hain, yaani 1 million tyres ki demand to yahan se aa gayi. Is ke ilawa replacement market alag se exist karti hai, jo size mein new cars ke mukable mein kaafi ziyada badi hoti hai. Company ka plan hai around 3 million tyres annual capacity ka PCR yaani ke Passenger Car Radial plant lagane ka jis ki estimated cost takreeban 22.5 billion ke kareeb hai. Aur production 2028 tak start hone ki expectation hai. Ab baat karte hain kuch important risks ki. Tyre manufacturing business ka sab se bada risk mere hisab se currency hota hai. Natural rubber, synthetic rubber aur carbon black jaise raw material largely imported hote hain. Isliye jab Pakistani Rupee depreciate hota hai to production cost bhi barh jaati hai, lekin SLM ke case mein ek important cushion maujood hai. Company ki meaningful production exports mein jaati hai. Isiliye Rupee depreciation ka impact company ke liye utna severe nahi hoga jitna purely domestic businesses ke liye hota hai. Aur yahan ek aur interesting cheez samajhne wali hai. Agar kisi waqt local market demand temporarily kisi bhi wajah se slow bhi ho gayi, to company ke paas export markets ka option maujood hai. Export orders par margins shayad local market se thode kam hote hain lekin export market ka size bahut bada hota hai. Aap jitna chahein jo hain thode se time mein export orders generate kar sakte hain, specially kyunki is company mein Long March Tyres ki association maujood hai, jo ke duniya mein bahut badi exporter hai.
