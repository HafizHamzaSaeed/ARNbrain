Speaker 1: Debt to equity.

ARN: Debt to equity aise hai ke agar maine ek poori company ya ek plant apne paas lagaya hua hai usme se kitna portion mera apna paisa hai aur kitna portion jo hai woh bank ka ya kisi aur ka qarza hai.

Speaker 1: Price to book ratio.

ARN: Price to book ratio ka matlab hai ke company ke agar saare assets jo uski value hai usko plus karlein aur jitne bhi company ke upar qarze aur liabilities hain usko minus kardain to woh hojati hai book value.

Speaker 1: Dividend yield.

ARN: Dividend yield ka matlab hai ke agar main is company ke 100 rupay ke shares khareedunga to agle saal company mujhe kitna dividend de degi aur yeh agar 12 se 15 percent ho yaani ke 100 rupay lagaye, 12 se 15 rupay ek saal mein dividend aaraha hai to usko bohot accha maana jata hai.

Speaker 1: Operating margin.

ARN: Operating margin hota hai ke ek agar 100 rupay ki sale ki to us mein company ko kitna profit hota hai? To is mein product banane ka kharcha bhi minus hojata hai aur jo distribution ka kharcha hota hai woh bhi minus hojata hai, marketing ka jo kharcha hota hai woh bhi minus hojata hai.

Speaker 1: Market capitalization.

ARN: Market capitalization ka matlab hai ke yeh jo company hai iski total stock market pe kitni value chal rahi hai yaani ke jo uski share price chal rahi hai usko multiply karein jo total number of shares hain to woh market cap aapke paas aajata hai is company ki value.
