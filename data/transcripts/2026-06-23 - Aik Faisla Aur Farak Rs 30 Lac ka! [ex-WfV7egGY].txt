Saat saal ka loan hai, total doge roughly 21 lakh rupe. Iske sath sath 10 lakh ki down payment bhi ki thi shuru mein. Aur saat saal baad gaari ki value hogi taqreeban 30 se 35 lakh rupe. Matlab 21 lakh rupe diye aur saat saal baad bhi almost wahi value. Ab suno doosra raasta, compounding wala raasta. Aao yahan dekhte hain, yahan kya milta hai. Wahi 10 lakh rupe aur pachees hazaar har mahine PSX mein lagao. Strong companies, banks, fertilizer, energy, index investing. Saat saal, average return 15 se 16 percent. 10 lakh down payment barh kar ho jayegi taqreeban 30 lakh.
