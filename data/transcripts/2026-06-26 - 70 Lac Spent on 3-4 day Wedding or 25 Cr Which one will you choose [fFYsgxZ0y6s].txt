Kya aap apne bacho ki teen char din ki shaadi par 70 80 lakh kharch karne ja rahe hain ya aap chahte hain ke apne bacho ko, apne grandchildren ko 25 crore rupey mil jayein? Kya choose karenge aap? Aaiye iski asal math samajhte hain.

Let's look at the expenses.

Shaadi ka hall aur decor, sirf cheh ghante mein khatam. Catering, khana, log phir bhi koi na koi nuqs nikalenge. Mehnge kapde, zindagi mein sirf do bar pehne ke liye. Total kharcha, 70 80 lakh rupey. Aur badle mein kya mila? Sirf un logo ki validation jine aap shayad pasand bhi nahi karte.

Ab soch ko thoda sa badalte hain. Aap dhoom dham se shaadi bilkul karein, khushi ka mauka hai. Lekin, budget cap karein 25 30 lakh par. Elegant aur simple. Jo baki ke 50 lakh bachein, unhe apne grandchildren ke liye Pakistan Stock Exchange ke index mein invest kar dein. 16% ki average compounding ke sath, 15 saal baad woh 50 lakh ban jayenge pooray 5 crore. Aur 20 saal baad, yeh ho jayenge 11 crore rupey. Aur 25 saal baad, pooray 25 crore rupey.

Sochein, kitne log Pakistan mein shaadi pe kharch kar ke apna financial freedom khud gawa baithte hain. Zara sochiye. Aur agar aap janna chahte hain ke yeh 16% compounding aap kaise kar sakte hain, bilkul asan tareeqe se, to comment karein "25 crore" aur main aapko DM karunga.
