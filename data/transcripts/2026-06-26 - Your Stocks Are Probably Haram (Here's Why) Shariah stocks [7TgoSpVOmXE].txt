To chalein aaj ki video shuru karte hain. To bhai sabse pehle dekh lete hain ke ye behas zaroori kyun hai ke koi cheez Shariah compliant hai ya nahi. Iski wajah hai ke hum ek Islamic mulk mein rehte hain jo ke international economic aur banking systems ko follow karta hai. Yaani ke Islam mein to clearly sood ko mana kiya gaya hai lekin hamare haan banks jo hain wo sood mein openly dealing karte hain. Isi tarah kuch businesses hote hain jo kaam to Pakistan mein kar rahe hote hain lekin unka business model Islamic principles ke totally khilaf hota hai. To jo log stock market mein invest karte hain unka ye sawal hota hai ke jo hamari kamai ho rahi hai, jo paisa hum bana rahe hain wo halal hai ya nahi. In other words, wo business Shariah compliant hai ya nahi. To is tarah hamare paas do categories aa jati hain, ek Shariah compliant stocks ki aur ek non-Shariah ya conventional stocks ki. Ab zaroori nahi hai ke ye jo non-Shariah ya conventional companies hain inke businesses totally haram hon, aisa bilkul nahi hai. Hum abhi thodi der tak wo rules discuss karenge jinki base par kisi bhi company ko Shariah compliant ya non-Shariah declare kiya jata hai. Lekin usse pehle hum kuch numbers discuss kar lete hain. To ye data to 2024 ke bilkul end ka hai lekin isse hamein andaza hota hai ke Pakistan Stock Market mein Shariah compliant assets ka rujhan kitna zyada hai logon mein. Ye main aapko PSX ka official document dikha raha hun, jismein bataya gaya hai ke 530 mein se 271 listed companies jo hain wo Shariah compliant thin aur ye poori market ka taqreeban 51% banta hai. Yaani ke aadhi se zyada companies jo stock market mein trade ho rahi hain wo Shariah compliant hain aur inka total market cap, yaani ke jo in companies ki total value hai wo poori market ka 55% se bhi zyada banti hai. Aur latest data bhi kuch isi tarah ka hai. Ek podcast mein sun raha tha jismein PSX ke Chief Market Operations Officer bhi kuch isi tarah ke numbers bata rahe the. Khair, iske ilawa har roz Pakistan Stock Market mein trade hone wale shares ke total volume ka taqreeban 60 se 70% volume Shariah compliant shares ka hai. Yaani ke ye ek minority part nahi balki majority part ban chuka hai market ka. Bohot zyada log, bohot zyada interested hain Shariah compliant assets mein. To bhai Shariah compliant stocks kya hote hain aur inki importance kya hai hamare muashre mein wo to humne dekh liya. Ab baat karte hain ke ye Shariah compliance ke rules aakhir banata kaun hai? Kis ki marzi se ye faisla hota hai ke ye company Shariah compliant hai aur ye company Shariah compliant nahi hai? To bhai investors ke interest ko dekhte hue is baat ki zaroorat mehsoos ki gayi ke conventional ke saath saath logon ke paas Shariah compliant investment ki bhi opportunities honi chahiye thin. Isi liye san 2008 mein Pakistan Stock Exchange aur Meezan Bank ne milkar KMI 30 Index launch kiya. Aur isi tarah san 2015 mein Pakistan Stock Exchange aur Meezan Bank ne milkar KMI All Share Index ka launch kiya. Ek Index jaisa ke aapko idea hoga ke dher saari companies ka ek group hota hai. Ek group mein ek khaas tarah ki companies hoti hain. Misaal ke taur par jis tarah KSE 100 Index hai, usmein Pakistan ki top 100 listed companies aa jati hain. Isi tarah jo KMI 30 Index hai, usmein Pakistan ki top 30 Islamic companies aur KMI All Share Index mein Pakistan ki tamaam listed Shariah compliant companies aa jati hain. Khair, to 2014 mein ek committee banayi gayi jiska kaam tha ke Pakistan mein jitni bhi Shariah compliant companies hain unka ek index banaya jaye jisse jo conventional companies hain aur jo Islamic companies hain inmein farq karna aasan ho jayega. Is maqsad ke liye SECP, State Bank of Pakistan, Karachi Stock Exchange, Mutual Funds Association of Pakistan aur Meezan Bank ne milkar kaam kiya. Meezan Bank is liye kyunki unki corporate finance and Islamic finance mein expertise jo hain wo already developed thin, jisko poori duniya mein kaafi zyada credibility bhi haasil thi. Yahi wajah hai ke 2014 mein jo committee banayi gayi uske chairman Dr. Muhammad Imran Usmani sahab the jo ke Mufti Taqi Usmani sahab ke bete hain aur ye doctor sahab jo hain wo aaj bhi Meezan Bank ke Shariah Board ke member hain. To isse aapko ye jo committee thi aur ye poora process tha iski credibility ka andaza ho gaya hoga. Aaj bhi jo Meezan Bank ka Shariah Board hai wo aap khud bhi check kar sakte hain, aaj khud Mufti Taqi Usmani sahab jo hain wo is Shariah Board ke chairman hain. Khair, is committee ne apna kaam kiya aur 2015 mein KMI All Share Index bana diya gaya jismein Pakistan ki tamaam Shariah compliant listed companies aa jati hain. To ab hamein ye to pata chal gaya ke ye Shariah compliance wala process kisne banaya tha aur unki credibility kya thi. Ab hum dekh lete hain ke aakhir wo rules kya hain jinki base par kisi bhi company ko Shariah compliance ya non-Shariah compliance ka status jo hai wo diya jata hai. To bhai is committee ne Shariah screening ke liye che filters, che rules banaye the aur aaj bhi kisi bhi company ki Shariah compliance inhi rules ki base par check ki jati hai. Ab ye rules kya hain, aein inhe hum screen par dekh lete hain. To ye hamare paas eligibility criteria aa gaya, ismein hum dekh lete hain ek ek karke ye che points. Sabse pehle hai Business of the Investee Company, ismein ye keh rahe hain ke jo business model itself hai wo hi jo hai haram nahi hona chahiye. Yaani ke misaal ke taur pe agar koi company sharab bana rahi hai ya uske ilawa haram meat ka kaam kar rahi hai, is kism ke jo haram cheezein hain wo agar kaam hi company ka wo hai to wo to bhai bahar hi ho gayi is se. Iske ilawa unhone kaha hua hai ke further companies carrying out tobacco and explosive business will be excluded on ethical grounds. To ye wale bhi jo hain wo Shariah compliant companies ki list mein nahi aenge. Phir aa jata hai hamare paas Interest Bearing Debt to Total Assets should be less than 37%. Iska matlab ye hua ke company ne jo qarza liya hua hai us pe jo interest payments karni pad rahi hain, jo sood deni pad rahi hai wo total assets ka less than 37% hona chahiye. To ye doosri aa gayi condition, phir teesri aa rahi hai ke Non-Compliant Investments to Total Assets should be less than 33%. Iska matlab ye hai ke jo company ka total assets hain unmein jo aise investments ki hui hain company ne jo non-compliant jagahon pe hain, misaal ke taur par bank mein deposit kiya hua hai to wahan se bhi jis tarah savings account mein aapko interest milta hai wo bhi sood hoti hai. To is tarah jitne bhi non-compliant jagahon par investment hai wo less than 33% honi chahiye total assets ka. Aage dekh lete hain jo chautha point hai wo hai Non-Compliant Income to Total Revenue. Iska matlab ye hai ke jo bhi company ke paas total revenue aa raha hai, paisa aa raha hai total, usmein jo non-compliant sources se kamai ho rahi hai, let's say bank se koi paisa aa raha hai ya kuch aise sectors se aa raha hai, beshak wo stock market mein investment ho jo Shariah compliant nahi hain, to jitni bhi income hai wo total revenue ka 5% se kam hona chahiye. Phir agla point hamare paas aa jata hai Illiquid Assets to Total Assets should be greater than 25%. Illiquid assets wo assets hote hain jo ke aap foran se sell nahi kar sakte, usmein zara time lag jata hai. To aise assets jo hain wo total assets ka 25% se zyada hona chahiye company ka. Phir agla point hamare paas aa raha hai ke Market Price per Share should be at least equal to or greater than Net Liquid Assets per Share. Again thodi technical cheez hai, iska neeche formula bhi diya hua hai. To behrahal ye is tarah che points aapke paas ban jate hain jinki base par ye company ko Shariah compliant ya non-Shariah compliant declare kiya jata hai. Isi tarah technical screening ka filters bhi hain, ye bhi again ek aam bande ke liye important nahi hai, zara technical cheezein hain. Misaal ke taur par unhone bataya hua hai ke let's say the company which is on defaulters' counter or its trading is suspended. Zaahiri baat hai jo trading nahi ho rahi hogi wo nahi aaegi, to again wo list mein bhi nahi aaegi. Ye zara technical cheezein hain to itni koi important nahi hain. Mutual funds both open-ended and close-ended are ineligible for inclusion in All Share Index. Again mutual funds ko keh rahe hain ke nahi aa sakte. To ye jo hai wo iske filters hain. Iske ilawa ye selection criteria mein ek important line hai: "During the selection process, each company's financial reports are thoroughly reviewed by research analysts of Al Meezan to ensure company meets benchmarks or thresholds for Shariah compliance." Yaani ke jo hai wo research analysts jo hain Al Meezan ke wo continuously dekhte rehte hain ke agar koi company jo hai wo in rules ko follow nahi kar rahi to unko Shariah compliant list se nikal diya jata hai. To ye hain wo rules jinki base par koi bhi company Shariah compliant ki list mein add ya remove ki jati hai. Ab ye check kaise karte hain ke koi company jo hai wo Shariah compliant hai ya nahi? Accha, ye jo recomposition list hoti hai ye aapko PSX ki website se mil jati hai aur ismein saara bataya hota hai ke kaun si companies jo hain wo add ho rahi hain, ye income incoming companies ki form mein, aur isi tarah outgoing companies yaani jin companies ko nikala gaya hai is list se wo bhi aapke samne hai. To ye saari jo list hai ye aapko mil jati hai stock market ki website se aur ismein full detail se har ek cheez har ek company ka bataya hua hai. To is tarah se aap check kar sakte hain ke aapne jis company mein investment ki hui thi, wo kahin Shariah compliant se non-Shariah compliant ki category mein to nahi aa gayi. Aur agar aa gayi hai to kya karna hai? Iska bhi tareeqa bataya gaya hai. Aur wo tareeqa ye hai ke jo revised list aati hai us date pe aapne apne shares ki total value dekh leni hai. Let's say aapne do mahine pehle 10,000 rupay ki investment ki thi, ab aaj ke din mein agar aapke shares ki value 10,000 hai ya usse zyada hai, 11-12,000, to aap apne shares bech dein aur wo paise jo extra hain wo aap le lein, wo aap rakh sakte hain, capital gain aapka. Lekin agar let's say aapke jo shares ki value hai wo kam hai, 10,000 se kam hai, to aap wait karein, itna ke shares ki value dobara se 10,000 pe aa jaye taake aap apni jo initial investment thi wo aap nikal sakein, kyunki most probably wo to aapne halal paison se hi ki hogi, to wo apne paise aap wapas nikal sakte hain.
