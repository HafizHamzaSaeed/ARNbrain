Duniya mein do tarah ke retailers hote hain. Pehle jo price pe apna business model banate hain jaise ke Target, Walmart, Asda, inka poora business model ek cheez pe khada hota hai: sabse sasta product. Dusri taraf, dusri taraf hote hain retailers jo brand pe jeete hain, Nike, Adidas, Hugo Boss, inka poora business model ek alag cheez pe khada hota hai: aspiration, identity, quality, status. Aaiye ab aap se ek bohot mazay ki baat share karta hoon. Aur yeh hai stock market ki power. Kabhi-kabhi jo asal business owners hote hain aur jo stock market investors hote hain, unki soch mein farq aa jata hai. Misaal ke taur par ek business owner ko pata hai ke woh ek aisi project mein invest karne laga hai jo agle 5 saal tak koi khaas return nahi dega lekin uske baad jo 20 saal ya 30 saal hain, usmein usko 25 percent ROIC dega, to business owner ke liye yeh investment karna bilkul ek samajhdaari hai. Lekin stock market ke investor ke paas ek extra advantage hota hai. Stock market investor ko zaroori nahi ke woh aaj hi us business mein paisa lagaye, woh wait kar sakta hai. Jab project apni earning power aur apna moat establish kar le aur earning power ke qareeb aa jaye aur market ko uski value nazar aani shuru ho jaye, to tab bhi aap invest kar sakte hain. Yaani business owner ko shuru ke 5 se 10 saal intezar karna padta hai jiske minority shareholder ya stock market investor ke paas flexibility hoti hai ke woh value realization ke qareeb enter ho jaye. Isliye stock market investors ke paas business owners ke muqable mein zyada flexibility hoti hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ke aap do ya teen mahine ke liye investing shuru kar dein, lekin agar aapka horizon teen se chaar saal ka hai, to aap un companies mein invest kar sakte hain jahan agle kuch saalon mein value realization ke chances zyada hon. Yeh sab karne ke liye businesses ko owners ki tarah samajhna bohot zaroori hai. Lekin invest us waqt karna chahiye jab earning power aur value realization dono nazar aa rahi hon. Agar is cheez ko Interloop pe apply karein, to Interloop ke apparel project pe yeh concept bilkul theek baithta hai. Aur sawal yeh banta hai: Kya yeh project 5 se 10 saal baad profits dikhaye-ga ya phir ek se do saal mein hi earnings aur moat develop ho jayega aur iske acche results jo hain woh visible hone lag jayenge? Jo ke bohot important hain value realization ke liye. Agar profits aur moat jaldi nazar aane lag gaya, to shayad yeh investment ka sahi waqt ho. Agar nahi, to minority shareholders ke paas wait karne ki luxury bhi maujood hai.
