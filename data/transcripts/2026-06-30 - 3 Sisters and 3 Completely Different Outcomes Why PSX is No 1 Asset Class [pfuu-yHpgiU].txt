Teen behnein Zainab, Aisha, aur Sana. Teeno ki shaadi hui June 2001 mein. Aur sabko mila ek ek lakh rupya. Zahir hai purana zamana tha sasta zamana tha. Aisha ne kaha main ne to bilkul risk nahi lena, main saare paise daal rahi hun savings account mein. Zainab ne thora dimaag laraya aur usne kaha main le leti hun sona aur usko locker mein band kar diya. Sana ke mind mein aaya ek aur idea, ke main is ek lakh ko Pakistan Stock Exchange ke index mein invest kar deti hun. Aaiye dekhte hain 25 saal baad kaun kahan khara hai. Aisha ka ek lakh bank mein barh kar 8 lakh 60 hazaar rupya ban gaya hai. Kya baat hai, risk free return baithe baithe. Zainab ka gold 6,500 par tola se jump laga kar ab pahunch gaya hai 4 lakh 40 hazaar par tola. Aur Zainab ke ek lakh ho gaye hain 67 lakh rupees. Ab Sana ka dimaag dekhein. KSE 100 Index jo ke us waqt 1400 points pe tha, ab aa gaya hai 1 lakh 75 hazaar points pe. Sana ko dividends bhi mile jo usne compounding dekhte hue reinvest kar diye aur aaj Sana ke paas hain 1 crore 20 lakh rupaye. To dosto ye reel save kar lo aur agli dafa lambe arse ke liye savings account mein paise rakhne se pehle ye phir se dekh lena.
