Yeh woh kaam hai jo Pakistan mein taqreeban 35 se 40 feesad log kar rahe hain. Iske baare mein jaante taqreeban sabhi hain, aur ameer ho ya ghareeb, yeh shauq har tarah ke bande ko hai. Lekin yeh kaam shayad aapke paise ke liye bilkul accha nahi, kyunki shayad faide se zyada is se aapka nuksaan ho raha hai. Main baat kar raha hoon committee daalne ki.

Assalam-o-Alaikum dosto, mera naam hai Shafi Butt aur aaj ki video mein hum baat karenge ke kyun itne zyada Pakistani committees ke through savings karte hain? Kyun yeh kaam accha nahi hai, aur woh kya kaam hai jo aap karke committee ki tarah paise bhi bacha sakte hain aur us se aapka nuksaan bhi nahi hoga.

Toh bhai, sabse pehle committee itself ki baat kar lete hain. Isey ballot committee ya aunty committee bhi kaha jata hai. Uski wajah yeh hai ke isme sabse zyada participation aam taur par khawateen ki hoti hai. Isme hota yeh hai ke kuch log, aam taur par ek mohalle ke ya koi bhi janne wale, aapas mein yeh ek amount decide karte hain ke hum itne ki committee daalenge. Misal ke taur par, mahana 10,000 rupaye ki committee daalenge.

Ab agar isme 10 log hain, toh yeh 10 log apni taraf se har mahine jo bhi is committee ka manager hai usey 10,000 10,000 rupaye de denge, aur woh manager har mahine kisi ek bande ko, jiski bhi baari hogi, usey yeh total paise jo ke ek lakh rupaye banenge, woh de dega. Toh is tarah yeh committee 10 mahine chalegi aur baari-baari karke har mahine ek bande ko ek lakh rupaye milte jayenge.

Ab isme hua kya ke aapne 10 mahino mein thoda-thoda karke ek lakh rupaye diye aur kisi ek mahine mein aapko ek dam se woh saare paise ek lakh rupaye mil gaye. Na aapka koi profit hua, aur ba-zahir aapka koi loss bhi nahi hua isme. Agar aapko yeh total ek lakh ki amount jaldi mil jayegi, toh bhi aapne jo committee ke remaining months hain, jitna time rehta hai, usme apni payment karte rehna hai, taake jinki baari rehti hai unhe bhi apni payment poori mil jaye.

Ab waise toh yeh sunne mein bahut accha lagta hai ke yaar log khud mil kar savings kar rahe hain, apni zaroorat ke liye paisa jama kar rahe hain, lekin isme bahut saare masle hain. Sabse pehle jo main masla hai us par baat kar lete hain, woh hai inflation ya afrat-e-zar, aasan zaban mein mehangai.

Ab inflation yeh hoti hai ke jo paise aapke paas hain woh paise toh utne hi rehte hain, lekin un paison mein jo cheezein aati thi woh time ke sath kam ho jati hain. Misal ke taur par, let's say, aaj petrol 200 rupaye litre ka hai aur aapke paas 1000 rupaye hain, toh 1000 rupaye mein aap 5 litre petrol dalwa sakte hain. Lekin ek saal baad petrol jo hai woh adhai sau rupaye litre ka ho jata hai, toh ab aap 1000 rupaye mein sirf 4 litre petrol dalwa sakenge. Aisa inflation ki wajah se hua hai. Pehle jahan 1000 rupaye mein 5 litre petrol aa raha tha, saal baad unhi 1000 rupaye mein sirf 4 litre petrol aa raha hai.

Ab is inflation ko measure karne ke mukhtalif tareeqe hote hain, lekin hum us detail mein nahi jaate. Hamare liye important yeh hai samajhna ke inflation hoti hai, aur Pakistan mein on average taqreeban 9 se 10 percent tak hoti hai. Kabhi jo hai woh thodi hoti hai, lekin kabhi-kabhi bahut zyada ho jati hai. Toh iska matlab hua ke agar aapke paas saal ke shuru mein ek lakh rupaye pade hain, toh saal ke aakhir tak ho sakta hai ke un ek lakh rupayon ki value sirf 90,000 rupaye ke qareeb reh jaye. Dekhne mein woh ek lakh rupaye hi honge, lekin unki buying power, jo petrol wali misal humne dekhi, woh value kam ho chuki hogi.

Isi liye, chahe aapne bank mein paise rakhe hon ya committee daali ho, dono suraton mein inflation aapke paison ko kha rahi hoti hai, aur jitne zyada aapke paise honge, utna zyada aapka nuksaan hoga. Ab committee mein yeh hota hai ke kisi ki baari pehle aa jati hai aur kisi ki end par. Toh jiski baari pehle aa rahi hai, woh toh accha reh jata hai, kyunki poore saal mein jo inflation ka effect hona tha uske paison par, woh us se bach jata hai, usey poore paise shuru mein hi mil jaate hain. Lekin jiski baari end par aati hai, woh jo hai woh nuksaan mein rehta hai, kyunki apni taraf se toh woh 10-10,000 karke poore ek lakh rupaye de dega, lekin end par jab uski baari aayegi aur usey ek lakh rupaye milenge, toh unki value jo hai woh kam ho chuki hogi.

Saal ke shuru mein agar yeh banda, let's say, ek motorcycle le sakta tha, second-hand hi sahi ek lakh rupaye mein, toh ab saal baad jo hai woh motorcycle ek lakh das hazaar ya ek lakh bees hazaar ki hogi, aur yeh banda woh nahi le sakega. Toh isi liye committee jo hai woh ek unfair system hai. Isme kisi ka faida hota hai aur kisi ka nuksaan. Aur yeh nuksaan hamein nazar toh nahi aata, lekin ab hamein samajh lag chuki hai ke asal mein nuksaan ho toh raha hota hai.

Chalein, inflation ko toh humne detail mein samajh liya, lekin iske alawa aur bhi kuch masle hain is committee system mein, un par baat kar lete hain. Toh bhai, ek aur masla jo hai woh yeh hai ke yeh poore ka poora system sirf aur sirf trust ki base par kaam kar raha hota hai. Aur jahan trust ho, wahan trust ka faida bhi uthaya ja sakta hai, yaani ke koi aapke sath fraud bhi kar sakta hai, koi aapke paise lekar bhaag bhi sakta hai.

Aisa hi waqia Karachi mein kuch saal pehle hua jahan ek khatoon, jo ke social media ke through khana bechne ka kaam karti thi, aur sath apne customers se committees dalwati thi, unhone allegedly taqreeban 42 crore rupaye ka scam kiya. In khatoon ko log 8 se 10 saal se jaante the aur multiple committees daali hui thi inke through. Yaani ke ek deep trust tha logon ka in par, lekin fraud ho gaya logon ke sath, lakhon rupaye gawa baithe log apne.

Ab masla yahi hai na isme ke yahan koi regulations wagera toh hain nahi, jis tarah hum baat karte hain ke stock market ko Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan regulate karta hai, poora ek check and balance rakha hota hai, toh woh cheez yahan par nahi hai. Isi wajah se is tarah ke fraud ho jate hain logon ke sath.

Khair, iske alawa ek aur masla yeh bhi hai ke, let's say, koi banda fraud nahi bhi karta, lekin genuinely uske paas paise nahi hain, toh woh apni payment par default kar jayega. Yaani ek-do mahine diye usne apne committee ke paise lekin phir kisi mahine ja ke uske paas paise nahi the toh woh nahi de saka. Ab jiski baari hai committee milne ki, paise milne ki, usey ya toh apne poore paise milenge nahi, ya jo banda committee ko manage kar raha hai woh apni taraf se paise daal ke dega, yaani ek ajeeb tamasha sa ban jayega, toh poora nizaam jo hai committee ka woh kharab ho jayega, toh yeh masle bhi hote hain isme.

Toh ab baat kar lete hain ke agar committee nahi daalni toh phir kya kiya jaye? Toh bhai, iska bhi solution bilkul maujood hai, aisa solution jo ke safe bhi hai aur aapko inflation ke nuksaan se bhi bacha lega, aur woh hai low-risk mutual fund. Mutual fund investment ka ek tareeqa hai, jis mein aap ke paas bahut saari options aa jati hain, is mein aap ke paas high-risk option bhi aa jati hai, medium-risk, aur low-risk ki option bhi aa jati hai. Aap agar committee ka alternate dhoond rahe hain, toh aap ne low-risk mutual fund ki taraf aa jana hai, kyunki is mein aap ke paise ki value kam nahi hogi. Haan, profit bhi thoda hoga, lekin at least aapko jo inflation ka nuksaan hai, us se aap bach jayenge.

Aur mutual funds mein woh fraud wale masle bhi nahi, kyunki yeh bhi fully regulated hai Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan ki taraf se.
