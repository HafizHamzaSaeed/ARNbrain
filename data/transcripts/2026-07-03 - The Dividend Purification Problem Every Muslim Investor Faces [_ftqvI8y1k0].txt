To sab se pehle baat kar lete hain kisi company ki non-Shariah income aur uski purification ki. To bhai pehla sawal to ye aata hai ke kya ye possible bhi hai ke ek company jo already Shariah compliant hai, wo KMI All Share Index mein bhi maujood hai, is ke bawajood us company ki income jo hai wo non-Shariah sources se aa rahi ho? To is ka jawab hai haan, ye bilkul possible hai. Jo hamari last Shariah compliance wali video thi, us mein humne wo che rules discuss kiye the jin ki base par kisi bhi company ko Shariah compliant ya non-Shariah compliant declare kiya jata hai. Un che mein se ek rule ye bhi tha ke kisi bhi Shariah compliant company ke liye zaroori hai ke uski total income mein se jo non-Shariah income ka part hai, wo five percent se kam hona chahiye. Yaani ke ek Shariah compliant company ki jo total income hai, us mein se ninety-five percent ya us se bhi ziyada percentage of income jo hai wo halal sources se aa rahi hogi. Bas ek thodi si gunjaish de di gayi hai, nahi to bohat saari companies jo hain wo non-Shariah declare ho jayengi.

To ab agla sawal aata hai ke jis company mein maine invest kiya hua hai, agar us company ki income ka kuch hissa jo hai wo non-Shariah sources se aa raha hai, yaani halal nahi hai, to mere liye kya hukam hai? To bhai jaisa ke aapko pata hai ke hum stock market mein mainly do tareeqon se paise kamate hain. Pehla hai capital gain, yaani ke so rupay ki cheez khareedi, ek so bees rupay ki bech di, to jo bees rupay ka profit hai, wo aapka capital gain ho gaya. Aur isi tarah doosra tareeqa jo hai wo dividends ke through hai. Yaani ke jis company mein aapne invest kiya hua hai, jab wo company kuch munafa kamati hai, to apne us profit ka kuch hissa apne shareholders yaani aapko bhi wo profit mil jata hai. To ye do tareeqe ho gaye. Aaj ki video mein hum in dono scenarios ke baare mein baat kar lete hain.

Sab se pehle agar capital gain ki baat kar lein, to us ke baare mein humne last video mein bhi briefly discuss kiya tha ke jo SECP aur PSX ka official document hai, us mein yehi mentioned hai ke ek Shariah compliant company mein capital gain ke upar aapko kisi qism ki purification karane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Yaani ke aapne check kiya usi process se jo humne last time discuss kiya tha ke KMI All Share Index jo hai wo saal mein do dafa re-compose hota hai, do dafa update hota hai saal mein. To jis din wo update hua aapne jaa ke dekha ke aapki Shariah compliant company jo hai wo non-Shariah ho gayi hai, to ab aapke samne do scenarios hain. Pehla to ye ke let's say aapki jo invested amount thi, us se zyada aapke shares ki value hai us din. Misal ke taur par aapne das hazar invest kiya tha ab wo barah hazar rupay hai. To aap apne shares bech dein aur side par ho jayein, aapko kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi. Lekin agar aapke shares ki total value jo hai, wo aapki invested amount se kam hai, yaani das hazar invest kiya tha us din nau hazar hai, to aap wait kar lein. Itna wait kar lein ke wapas se aapke shares ki total value aapki invested amount das hazar rupay wapas aa jaye. To jab ye ho jayega aap apne shares bech dein aur phir aapko kuch extra karne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Ab capital gain par profit purification karne ki zaroorat kyun nahi hai, is ki wajah ulema ye batate hain ke kisi company ki jo share price hai, wo kyunke zaroori nahi hai ke sirf us company ki income ya us ke profit badhne ki wajah se ho, share price to bohat saari reasons ki wajah se badh sakti hai. Misal ke taur par kisi news ki wajah se bhi share price badh sakti hai, ya company ke kuch future plans hain agar, us wajah se badh sakti hai, jo share hai us ki demand and supply ki wajah se ho sakta hai, matlab bohat saari reasons hain kisi share price ki value badhne ke peeche. Aur jo capital gain wala profit hai, wo kyunke share price ke sath linked hai, is liye aapko uski purification karane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Main is hawale se kisi video ka link bhi laga doonga description mein taake aap khud bhi dekh lein ise aur aapki zara mazeed tasalli ho jaye. Haan ye baat bhi main clear kar doon ke kuch ulema jo hain wo ye kehte hain ke capital gain ki soorat mein bhi aapko purification kara leni chahiye. To ye phir jo bhi aapka sect hai ya aapka school of thought hai, aap us hawale se bhi iski mazeed details check kar sakte hain.

Accha ek aur sawal yahan shayad kuch logon ke zehan mein aaye, wo ye ke agar aapne ek Shariah compliant company mein investment ki hui thi aur wo company non-Shariah nahi hui, wo abhi bhi Shariah compliant hai, to aapne kya karna hai? To is case mein aapko kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi kyunke jo capital gain wala profit hota hai wo aapko sirf tabhi hota hai jab aap apne shares bechte hain. Agar aapki company ka status change nahi hua, wo abhi bhi Shariah compliant hi hai, to aap use hold kar ke rakhein aur aapko kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Khair ye to capital gain ki baat ho gayi, ab hum hamare main sawal ki taraf aa jate hain dividend ke baare mein. Ke let's say humne ek Shariah compliant company mein investment ki hui hai aur uski kuch income jo hai wo non-Shariah source se aa rahi hai, lekin itni nahi hai ke wo poori company hi non-Shariah compliant ho jaye. Yaani ke uski jo non-Shariah income hai wo 5% se kam hai, aur wo company humein dividend bhi de rahi hai, to is soorat mein humein kya karna hai?

Yahan par aapko dividend purification karne ki zaroorat padegi. Ye kya hota hai aur kaise karte hain us par to baat karte hain, lekin agar kisi ka ye sawal hai ke ye karna hi kyun hai, zaroorat kya hai iski? To is baare mein pehle baat kar lete hain. Bhai dekhein, ye koi qanooni taur par to farz hai nahi, yaani ke State ka aisa koi law nahi hai jo aapko kahe ke aapne dividend purification lazmi karani hai, nahi to aapko koi jurmana ya koi izaafi tax dena hoga. Aisa kuch nahi hai. Ye bas hum apni income ko mukammal taur par halal rakhne ke liye karte hain. Ke yaar agar main so rupay kama raha hoon aur us mein se do rupay jo hain wo halal nahi hain, to un do rupayon ko main apni income se nikal deta hoon. Is tarah jo humein ulema ne Islamic principles bataye hain wo bhi follow ho jate hain, aur humein jo hai wo zehni sukoon bhi ho jata hai ke yaar main theek kaam kar raha hoon. To is liye hum dividend purification karte hain.

Ab baat kar lete hain ke ye kaam karna kaise hai. To jaisa ke humne pehle discuss kiya ke kisi bhi Shariah compliant company ki jo maximum non-Shariah compliant income hai, wo 5% tak ho sakti hai. To jo bhi aapko dividend milega us ka maximum 5% aap charity mein dein ge, baghair sawab ki niyat ke. Ab ye jo 5% wala number hai ye maximum hai, yaani ke zaroori nahi hai ke aapke case mein 5% aapne charity mein dena ho. Ho sakta hai aapke case mein ye 2% ho ya 1% ho, ya bilkul bhi aapko kuch charity karne ki zaroorat na ho. To ye number aap check kaise karenge? Aayein dekh lete hain.

Ye aapke paas PSX ki website se ek official document hai jis mein bataya gaya hai ke dividend purification jo hai wo Shariah compliant shares pe kaise ki ja sakti hai. Ye aap yahan se parh bhi sakte hain. Is mein ek inhone formula bhi bataya hua hai jo ke bazahir to simple hai, lekin main aapko jo iska aasan tareeqa bata deta hoon. Pehle aap aa jayein Google pe, yahan pe aapne URL bar mein likhna hai: psx.com.pk. Is pe aapne enter kar dena hai to ye aapke paas PSX ki website aa gayi. Is mein aap aa jayein yahan par ye Products and Services. Is pe aap click karenge ye aa raha hai Indices. Is mein aap neeche aa jayein, aur yahan pe aapke paas ye different indices aa rahe hain. To aap koi bhi kholein hum let's say is pe bhi click kar dete hain KMI 30. Ye saare aapko ek hi page pe le aayenge.

To ye page aa gaya aapke paas, theek hai? Is mein aapke paas ye KMI 30 bhi hai, PSX KMI All Share hai, Meezan Pakistan... hum filhal jo KMI All Share hai is ke neeche aap dekhein: Recent Review of Shariah Compliant Companies. Is pe click kar dein. To yahan par aapke paas ek naya page khul jayega, aur yahan pe saari companies aa jayengi. Ye aapke paas load ho gaya. Ab dekhein ye: Recomposition of PSX KMI All Share aur ye date batayi hui hai May 29, 2026 yaani ye abhi May ke mahine mein update hua hai. Aur kis date tak update hua hai ye bhi is mein bataya hua hai December 31st, 2025. To aapko pata lagega ke aap kab tak ka data dekh rahe hain. Is mein aapko bataya hua hai ke incoming companies jo abhi is index mein nayi companies add hui hain wo ye saari hain, outgoing companies jo is se nikli hain wo ye saari hain. Is mein agar aap neeche aa jayenge, to aapke paas aur details aa jayengi.

Ye aapke paas saari wo details aa gayi. Ye jo red hain, ye apne ratios ko follow nahi kar rahe, to is liye in companies ko nikala gaya hai. To yahan se aap dekh sakte hain ye: Income Ratio. Ye aapko bataya hai Non-Compliant Income divided by Total Revenue less than 5%. Dividend purification ke liye aapne ye check kar lena hai. Main isko agar thoda sa zoom kar doon, to haan. Ab ye dekhein ye kisi company ki 44% pe gayi hui hai, to usko nikal diya list se, kisi ki 6% hai, yahan par aapne apni company jo hai wo search kar leni hai. Let's say hum Fatima Fertilizer dekh rahe hain, to main Ctrl+F dabaoonga aur ye maine Fatima likha idhar. To ye mere samne aa gaya hai, 111 pe Fatima, aur is mein jo hum dekh rahe hain number, kaun sa hai? Ye, Income Ratio. To 111 ke aage jo hai wo aa raha hai 0.93, right? To ye sirf apne dividend ka 0.93% jo hai wo maine charity mein karna hai, baqi mujhe 5% karne ki bhi zaroorat nahi hai. Ye less than 1% hai jo mujhe nikalna hai.

Isi tarah aapki jo bhi company hai wo aap yahan se check kar sakte hain. Ek aur company agar hum search kar lein, let's say main karta hoon Millat, to ye aa gayi Millat Tractors. 233 pe dikhaya hai inhone, aur is ki jo hai wo 0.07% pe aa rahi hai, Net Income Ratio, yes. To jise dividend mila hai Millat Tractors mein, wo apni dividend ka 0.07% jo hai wo charity kar de. Ye itna simple sa tareeqa hai.

To is tarah se aap apni companies check kar ke un par sirf utna hi dividend purify kar sakte hain jitne ki zaroorat hai. Aapko koi 5% jo hai wo purify karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Balki even ho sakta hai aapko ek rupaya bhi dene ki zaroorat na pade charity mein agar aapki company ki jo non-compliant income hai wo 0.00 hai, to aapko koi charity karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. To is tarah aap KMI All Share Index ke through apni company check kar sakte hain. Aur ye list jo hai wo saal mein do dafa update ho jati hai. Ek dafa May ke mahine mein, ek dafa November mein jaa ke. Aur jo dividend hai wo bhi aapko at least teen mahine to lagte hain, teen mahine baad jaa ke milta hai. To is tarah aap updated data jo hai wo dekh sakte hain aur apne dividend purification kar sakte hain.
