Toh sabse pehle, ek zaroori cheez samajhte hain, cement ek commodity business hai. Matlab, Maple Leaf ka cement aur kisi bhi competitor ka cement almost ek jaisa hota hai. Bahut kam log aise hote hain jo kehte hain ke sirf Maple Leaf ka cement chahiye. Price mein zyada farq hua toh wo dusri company se bhi cement le lete hain. Toh jab selling price almost sabki ek jaisi ho, toh farq sirf ek jagah se aa sakta hai, aur wo hoti hai production cost. Jo company cement sasta banaegi, wo zyada kamaegi. Ek average cement company Rs 3,000 per ton profit before tax jo hai, wo kama leti hai. Lekin agar kisi company ke pass, let's say, sasti electricity hai, sasta coal hai, aur favorable location bhi hai, toh wahi profit jo hai 3,000 ke bajaye 4,500 per ton ya us se bhi upar ja sakta hai. Yaani, sirf cost advantages ki wajah se profitability aur ROCE mein 50% tak ka difference aa sakta hai. Aur ab main aapko batata hoon ke kin teen factors ki wajah se ye difference aa sakta hai, aur Maple Leaf jo hai in factors mein kahan stand karta hai, kitna accha perform karta hai. Sabse pehle baat karte hain bijli ki. Ek ton cement banane ke liye, roughly 80 se 90 units bijli ke chahiye hote hain. Aaj kal jo company grid se bijli khareed rahi ho, toh uski effective unit ki cost taqreeban Rs 30 se Rs 35 per unit ban jaati hai. Grid ke muqable mein captive coal power plant wali companies, jaise ke Maple Leaf Cement, yahi bijli Rs 20 per unit mein produce kar sakti hain. Yaani, har unit par taqreeban Rs 12 ki bachat ho gayi. Jo per ton cement mein taqreeban 1,000 ka extra profit contribute kar sakti hai. Aur Maple Leaf Cement ne aaj se taqreeban 10 saal pehle 40 megawatt ka coal power plant laga liya tha, aur pichle kuch saalon mein solar mein bhi investment ki hai. Ab aate hain dusre factor ki taraf, aur wo hai coal. Cement industry mein coal sabse important raw materials mein se ek hai. Aaj kal local coal roughly 30 se 35,000 per ton mil raha hai, jabke imported coal roughly 38,000 se 42,000 per ton ke kareeb available hai. Ek ton coal se taqreeban 7 se 8 ton cement ban jata hai. So, roughly speaking, ye difference per ton, jo coal ki cost hai, cement profitability per ton mein 700 se 800 tak ka impact daal sakti hai. Aur yahan bhi interesting cheez ye hai ke last few years mein industry evolve kar chuki hai. Aaj sirf local aur imported coal ki baat nahi hoti. Kuch companies, jaise ke Maple Leaf Cement, DG Khan Cement, biomass bhi use kar rahi hain coal ke bajaye. Aur kai cases mein ye biomass jo hai, ye local coal se bhi zyada sasta parhta hai. Lekin har company is advantage ko utilize nahi kar sakti kyunki plants ki technology aur design different hota hai. Kuch plants sirf 20 se 30% local coal ya biomass use kar sakte hain, jabke kuch plants jo hain, wo 100% local coal pe bhi chal sakte hain, aur usme bhi 35 se 40% biomass istemal kar sakte hain. Aur Maple Leaf un companies mein shamil hai jo almost 100% local coal bhi istemal kar sakti hai, aur roughly 35 se 40% biomass bhi use kar rahi hai. Aur ye ek meaningful cost advantage hai. Aur ab teesra factor, aur ye hai location. Aur yahan main aapko seedha bata deta hoon ke is factor mein Maple Leaf industry best position mein nahi hai. Aaiye aapko samajhata hoon aisa kyun. Limestone, jo cement ka buniyaadi raw material hai, us pe royalty har soobe mein alag alag hai. Sindh mein taqreeban Rs 250 per ton, KPK mein taqreeban Rs 350 per ton, aur Punjab mein roughly Rs 1,000 per ton. Punjab mein, Pioneer Cement bhi Punjab mein hai jise Maple Leaf ne abhi acquire kiya hai, aaj kal ki soorathal mein ye ek real disadvantage hai. Ek factor jo is disadvantage ko thoda sa kam kar deta hai, wo hai ke Maple Leaf ke paas railway transportation contracts hain jin ke zariye coal aur imported fuel factory tak laya ja sakta hai. Rail transport jo hai, wo trucks ke muqable mein aapko sasta parhta hai. Aur aaj kal, jaise diesel itna mehenga hua hua hai, toh ye ek kaafi faide-mand cheez hai. Aur is advantage ko Maple Leaf Cement jo hai, Pioneer Cement ki acquisition mein bhi use kar sakta hai.
