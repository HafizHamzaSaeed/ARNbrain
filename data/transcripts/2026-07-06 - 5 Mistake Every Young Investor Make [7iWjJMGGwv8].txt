Announcement wali companies mein invest kar lena, choti company announcement karna ke mein bohot bara kaam jo hai karne ja raha hoon. Doosri sab se bari mistake, tools use kar lena aur low PE ratio aur low price to book value wali companies nikal ke sochna is mein invest karenge to zyada faida hoga. Teesri ghalti, kisi achi company mein invest kar liya aur market ne rally kiya, to bech diya yeh soch ke, ke thora sa neeche aaye ga phir khareed lenge. Fourth mistake is leverage, kyunki leverage mein koi bhi volatility aaye market mein, to aapke poore paise jo hain wo khatam ho jate hain. Aur fifth mistake is, kisi se bhi sun lena ke company mein yeh development ho rahi hai aur aankh band kar ke company le lena.
