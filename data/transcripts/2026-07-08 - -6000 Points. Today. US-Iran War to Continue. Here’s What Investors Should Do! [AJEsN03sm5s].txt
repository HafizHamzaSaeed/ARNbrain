To aaiye sabse pehle important cheez se start kar lete hain ke aaj kya wajah bani ke PSX jo hai wo almost che hazar point tak bhi ek time pe decline kar gayi thi. Aur wajah ye thi ke oil prices jo hain wo aaj increase ho gayi aur uski wajah ye thi ke Donald Trump ne ek statement de di ke meri hisab se Iran ke sath jo humne MoU sign kiya hai uski koi importance reh nahi gayi, lekin ye headline thi, jab aap isko detail mein padhenge to wo keh rahe the ke basically humari jo team hai wo aapas mein negotiate karti jayegi lekin mujhe lag raha hai aisa ke MoU jo hai usme jis tareeqe se Iran ko respond karna chahiye tha, jo unko relaxations deni chahiye thi, Strait of Hormuz jis tarah se open kar dena chahiye tha bilkul, wo nahi ho raha. To jo bilkul naye log honge wo isko on the face of it is statement ko dekhenge, lekin jo thode intelligent log honge, jinhone is cheez ko thoda sa follow kiya hoga aur humari purani videos bhi dekhi hongi ke jang ke andar saari cheezein kya hoti hain, macros kis direction mein jaate hain, jo leaders hote hain wo apni local audience ko dikhane ke liye ke hum log hi jang jeet rahe hain is tarah ki statements jo hain wo dete rehte hain. Lekin Iran chahta hai ke jisne bhi guzarna hai humse approval le ke guzre, hum approval de denge, har kisi ko de denge lekin approval le ke aapne guzarna hai. To aasi hi ek ship maybe inhone try kiya hoga ke Qatar ki LNG ki ship jo hai na try karte hain, wo already inke friendly terms pe hain, to maybe inko ye without approval jo hai allow kar dein. Lekin aisa nahi hua, us ship pe attack ho gaya even though abhi tak kisi ne jo hai uski zimmedari qabool nahi ki, but Iran ne show kiya ke abhi tak agar aapko Strait of Hormuz se guzarna hai to aapko humari approval leni hogi kyunki ye unke liye ek bohot important cheez hai. Iski retaliation mein doosri side obviously wo bhi chup nahi reh sakte, to unhone kya kiya ke phir USA ne Iran pe jo oil sanctions ek tarah se lift kiye hue the unko wapis jo hai wo apply kar diya aur iske sath sath Iran mein kuch strikes bhi ki. Ab ye ek aisi cheez hai ke again local audience ko show karne ke liye aur MoU mein dono ke jo important points hain jispe wo chahte hain ke agla banda concede kare, usko karne liye aisi cheezein chalti rehti hain. Kuch din pehle MoU sign hone se pehle bhi I think ek British ship thi wo Strait of Hormuz se again usi tareeqe se guzarne ki koshish kar rahi thi without approval, Iran ne uspe attack kiya aur phir retaliation mein America ne thodi si strikes ki aur phir end mein wahi hota hai jo abhi Iran ki taraf se statements bhi aa rahi hain ke USA ki jo kuch bases hain Kuwait mein, Bahrain mein, udhar wo attack karte hain aur ye jo hai jo ek try hota hai ek tarah se doosri side se ke maybe hum log koshish karte hain Strait of Hormuz se guzarne ki without approval, wo dekhte hain uska ye poora ek cycle ban jata hai aur end mein cheezein jo hain wapis thodi si normal pe aa jati hain aur phir sab jo hai wo approval le ke jo hai guzarne ki taraf aa jate hain. To mere hisab se is poore event ko aapko is tanazur mein dekhna chahiye ke obviously dono taraf ki jo sides hain wo apni local audience ko dikhana chahte hain ke hum log in control hain aur wo thoda thoda koshish karte rehte hain, pressure daalte rehte hain statement ke through ya movement ke through ke MoU mein jo humari baatein hain na unko zyada weight diya jaye.
