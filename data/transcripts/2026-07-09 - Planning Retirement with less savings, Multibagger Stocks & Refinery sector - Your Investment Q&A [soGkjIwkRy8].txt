Aur is ke saath saath aap ne ye bhi dekha hoga ke ye group, jo hai, Attock Cement ko bhi own karta hai, aur unhone exit kiya, us se minority share holders ko, jo hai, bohat achhi price mil jaati hai apne shares ko sell karne ke liye, to ho sakta hai future mein, aur ye humne dekha bhi hai, jab bhi Saudi delegation aati hai, to baat hoti hai ke Saudi investment, jo hai, wo Attock Refinery mein aa sakti hai. Obviously, rumors hote hain. But kyun ke kuch history hai ke ye group Pakistan se exit karne ki koshish kar raha hai, to ho sakta hai future mein kahin jaa ke Attock Refinery ki bhi aisi koi deal ho jaye. But obviously, is mein time bhi lag sakta hai. Achhi baat ye hai ke company ki balance sheet bohat strong hai, bohat valuable land bhi hai inke paas, aur refinery sector ko bhi dekhein, to local advantages, jo hain, wo is company ke paas maujood hain, aur I think ye best placed hai is refinery policy se faida uthane ke liye. Mein hamesha aap ko three step process batata hoon: first understand, then verify, and then invest. Ye step-by-step process agar aap follow karenge, to phir aap ke results kaafi achhe ho sakte hain. Aur is investing, compounding ki journey pe agar aap hume join karna chahte hain, to link description mein maujood hai.

Ab hum apne next question pe chalte hain aur bohat hi important question hai multibaggers se related question hai. To aap ko bhi yakeenan is mein kaafi zyada interest hoga. Qurratulain poochti hain, balkeh mein do questions combine kar leta hoon Qurratulain aur Mohammad Abdul Rehman ka: "How to know that a particular stock has reached its fair value? Just make us an expert in calculating fair value of a stock." Aur phir doosra part hai: "How to pick multibagger stock? Please make detailed video on this topic again. Which factors to consider to invest in a multibagger." To jin logon ko multibagger ka ikna idea nahi hai, multibagger basically wo stock hai jo ke agar ek average stock market ki company 18% har saal increase ho rahi hoti hai, to wo 25 se 30%, jo hai, aap ko return de raha ho. Aur ab aap dekhein lambe arse pe ek particular stock mein, theek hai, kabhi koi interest aa jata hai, koi news aa jati hai uski price badh jaati hai, lekin agar das saal, bees saal ke liye ek cheez ne 25%, 28% pe compound hona hai, to actual uska business, jo hai, wo profit kama raha hoga to wo increase hoga na baar baar, warna, otherwise, ek cheez jo apni value khud increase nahi kar rahi, usko aap kitna pump kar lein. To jo business ka poora ek model hai, wo kaafi achha hona chahiye, aur uska jo ROIC hai jo hum aap ko har video mein bhi batate rehte hain, wo ROIC itself, jo hai, wo 30% ya us se zyada hona chahiye. Kyun ke ROIC hota hai ke aap ne us business ko start karne ke liye jitni investment ki, us se har saal kitna profit aa raha hai. To yaani ke agar koi business, jo hai, ek lakh rupay mein shuru ho raha hai aur us pe 30,000 rupay ka har saal profit aa raha hai, to 30% ROIC ban jata hai. To kuch jo cheezein jo is ROIC ko high ROIC mein convert karti hain, wo ye hoti hain ke aapki jo sales hain, wo cash pe hon. Aap ko apne customer ko credit na dena pade, warna to agar aap ko credit dena hoga to aap ko us business ko chalane ke liye us mein mazeed paisa, jo hai, wo jitni growth hogi utna zyada paisa inject karna padega, aur capital required to run the business increase ho jayega, ROIC reduce ho jayega. Doosra phir jitna zyada mehenga plant hoga, initially, utne zyada chances hote hain ke ROIC neeche aa sakta hai, again kyun ke investment required to run the business bohat zyada hai. To ye do combinations maine dekhi hain jis se ek high ROIC business banta hai: ke ya to uska jo plant hai, wo ek decent investment ho, bohat badi investment na ho, aur us ke saath saath phir wo ek tarah se apne customer ko credit de sakta hai. Lekin agar plant mein investment bohat zyada hai, to phir customer ko credit dene ke saath saath high ROIC business banna, jo hai, wo bohat mushkil ho jata hai. Phir is ke saath saath kyun ke Pakistan mein thodi si informal economy bhi hai, informal businesses bhi operate kar rahe hote hain, to aise sector mein multibagger milne ke chances, jo hain, bohat kam hote hain jahan informal players, jo hai, wo operate kar rahe hon, kyun ke wo sales tax bhi evade kar rahe hote hain aur wo income tax bhi nahi de rahe hote, to wo apni price us hisaab se rakhte hain, aur phir jo documented player hota hai, wo us price pe sales tax bhi deta hai, income tax bhi deta hai, to uska return, jo hai, wo bohat zyada kam ho jata hai. To ye bhi ek cheez aap note kar lein to find multibaggers. So, multibaggers dhoondne ka sab se important part hota hai, aur ye 70, 75% of the work hota hai, aur wo ye hota hai ke is business ka actual ROIC kya hai. Theek hai, phir us ke baad aap ne us mein invest karte waqt do teen cheezein aur dekhni hoti hain. Ek cheez hoti hai ke aap ne agar kisi multibagger mein invest karna hai, to aap ne bohat zyada high price pe nahi khareed lena. Warna phir hota ye hai ke business to achha hota hai, business grow ho raha hota hai, lekin stock price itni upar gayi hoti hai ke wo do teen saal ke liye sideways ho jaati hai, aur aap sochte hain ke ab do saal ho gaye hain, teen saal ho gaye hain, maine socha tha maine multibagger stock liya hai, ye meri investment ko double kar dega teen saal mein, har saal 25% badh ke, lekin ye to badhta hi nahi hai, to frustration mein aake log, jo hai na, bech dete hain. Halaankeh ghalti ye hoti hai ke aap ne usko ek high price pe khareed liya tha, aur ab wo jo overvaluation hai, wo correct ho rahi hai. To ek aap ne make sure karna hai ke aap jis multibagger mein invest kar rahe hain, wo ek decent P/E ratio pe trade kar raha hai taake aap ke saath ye jo poora frustration wala episode hai, wo na ho. Phir aap ne ek badi mashhoor book hai: "What got you here won't get you there." Ab kyun ke businesses, jo hain, over time evolve karte rehte hain, government ki policies bhi change hoti rehti hain, aur ek choti company se jab aap medium size ki company ban rahe hote hain, phir bade size ki company ban jaate hain, bohat dafa us growth ko continue karna, jo hai, wo iitna simple nahi hota. To aap ne ye dekhna hai ke pichle 10-15 saalon mein, agar koi stock, jo hai, wo multibagger bana hai, to us ke reason kya the, aur agar wo reasons aaj bhi intact hain, to wo agle saalon mein bhi multibagger rehne ke chances hain. Mein aap ko do teen examples se explain karta hoon. For example, Lucky Cement pichle 20-25 saalon mein jo multibagger bana hai, uski wajah ye hai ke ye cement plants ke andar Chinese technology ko adopt karne wale sab se pehle the. Aur inhone gas power plants ke andar, jo hai, investment karke, pehle Pakistan mein gas sasti hoti thi, baaqi competitors ke muqable mein sasti bijli ka faida utha kar, jo hai, baaqiyon se zyada profits kamaye aur rapid expansions kiye. To ab agar hum sochein ke ye cement industry mein number one player ban gaye hain, to kya ye next few saalon mein bhi multibagger banega, to is ke liye hume is cheez ka answer dhoondna hoga ke pehle jo saari growth thi, wo cement business se aa rahi thi. Lekin ab jo growth hai, wo IPPs se bhi aa rahi hai, wo automobile industry se bhi aa rahi hai, aur wo thodi bohat, jo hai, cement industry se bhi aa rahi hai. Aur phir saath saath Lucky Cement ka mining business ke andar, jo hai, enter hone ka bhi plan hai. To ab ye jo saari new growth ke avenues hain, agar inka ROIC future mein achha hoga, to phir hi ye company, jo hai, wo future mein multibagger rahegi. Isi tareeqe se agar hum Interloop ki baat kar lein, to textile industry mein LTFF ki facility, long-term pe aap ko saste qarze bhi milte hote the, income tax rate, jo hai, wo bhi kam hota tha. Interloop ne khud bhi rapid expansions keen aur zabardast tareeqe se growth ki, achhe customers ko on-board kiya, aur socks ke andar, jo hai na, duniya mein ek bohat bada naam ban gaye. Is wajah se Interloop, jo hai, ek multibagger company ban gayi. Lekin ab socks mein growth to chal rahi hai, lekin us ke saath saath jab company ne apparel aur denim mein expansion ki hai, to phir in sectors ko bhi, jo hai, achhe profits kamane honge, taake jo multibagger story hai, wo continue rahe. To ab jo changes ho gayi hain, wo ye hain ke LTFF ab us tareeqe se available nahi hai, income tax bhi lag gaya hai, pehle sirf 1% of value ka tax hota tha, ab, even though super tax khatam ho gaya hai exporters pe, lekin phir bhi 29% income tax, jo hai, wo exist karta hai. To ye saari cheezein aap ne sochni hoti hain, aur dekhna hota hai ke koi company agar past mein multibagger thi, jin cheezeon ko pehle identify karein ke in cheezeon ne isko multibagger banaya, aur agar wo future mein continue raheingi, to phir ye stock bhi future ka bhi multibagger hai.

What is the procedure? Can you guide me, and can I invest while I am in Saudi Arabia?" The answer is yes. Bilkul yes. Aaj se kuch saal pehle, Pakistan ne ek bohat zabardast cheez launch ki thi, Roshan Digital Account. Jis ke andar overseas Pakistanis, jo hain, Pakistan mein jab invest karenge, aur investment pe profit kamayenge, to kal ko kuch saalon baad ya kuch mahinon baad jab bhi unka dil chahe, wo paise, jo hai, waapis overseas phir se le ke jaa sakte hain. Kyun ke aap ko to pata hai ke zyada tar log jo overseas Pakistanis hain, unka sab se bada darr yehi hota hai ke Pakistan mein jo investment chali gayi, wo kabhi waapis foreign currency mein nahi aayegi. Lekin is cheez ko ab almost five years ho gaye hain, aur Government of Pakistan ne har payment ko, jo hai, honor kiya hai. Jis jis investor ne apne paise waapis maange hain, wo time pe within one to two days, aap ko foreign currency mein aap ke account mein waapis mil jaate hain. Aur iska tareeqa ye hai ke aap ne jo bhi Pakistan ke teen chaar bade banks hain, us mein aap ne jaa ke batana hai ya unki online website ya customer support se kehna hai ke mujhe Roshan Digital Account, jo hai, wo open karana hai. RDA account mein jitni investment hoti hai, uska custodian, jo hai, wo CDC hota hai, to custody point of view se bhi aap ke saare shares, jo hain, wo safe hain, aap ko Pakistan aane ki zaroorat nahi hai koi sign karne ke liye, koi photocopy submit karane ke liye, sab kuch, jo hai, baahar baithe ho jayega.
