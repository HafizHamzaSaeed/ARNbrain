Pakistan mein har saal aise 30 crore can se zyada beverage cans jo hain, istemal hote hain. Aur interesting baat ye hai ke unme se almost har can ke peeche ek hi naam likha hota hai. PABC yani Pakistan Aluminum Beverage Cans. Pakistan ki pehli aur abhi tak ki only aluminum beverage can manufacturing company. Major customers mein Pepsi, Coca-Cola ke saath saath local brands jaise Cola Next aur Gourmet Cola bhi shamil hain. Yani company ki demand sirf multinational se nahi balki Pakistan ki nai ubharti hui brand se bhi linked hai. Aur agar aap cold drink industry ko ghaur se dekhein to quietly ek major shift chal raha hai. Beverage companies aahista aahista glass aur plastic bottles se cans ki taraf move kar rahi hain aur ye ek global trend aur requirement ban chuki hai FMCGs ke liye, kyunki cans environmentally behtar hain aur plastic waste reduce karne mein help karte hain. Isi trend ka impact PABC ke business mein bhi clear nazar aata hai. 2017 mein company ki production capacity roughly 700 million cans ki thi, aur kuch hi saalon mein ye capacity barh kar 1.2 billion cans per year tak pahunch gayi. Aur ab summer season bhi start ho chuka hai, jo historically beverage industry ke liye strongest periods mein se ek hota hai. Aur yahan ek aur interesting angle ye bhi hai ke PABC ka business sirf Pakistan ki demand par depend nahi karta, balki company ka significant export business Afghanistan se linked hai. Aur Pakistan se zyada cans to company Afghanistan mein sale karti hai. Ab sawal ye hai ke ek aluminum can company itna profitable business kaise ban sakti hai? Kyunki surface level par ye sirf ek packaging business lagta hai, lekin numbers ke neeche kahani kaafi interesting hai. Toh chaliye shuru karte hain.

Sabse pehle ye samajhte hain ke is business ki economics actually kaam kaise karti hai. PABC ne apna initial plant 2017 mein roughly 70 to 80 million dollars ki investment karke set up kiya tha. Aur uske baad further expansions par bhi around 20 se 30 million dollars ki investment ki. Yani roughly 100 million dollars ki total investment ke baad company ek aisa plant build kar chuki hai jo saal ke around 1.1 se 1.2 billion cans jo hai wo produce kar sakta hai. Ab interesting part yahan se start hota hai. Agar PABC har can par average 5 rupay let's say profit kar raha hai, aur plant roughly 85% capacity par operate kar raha hai, to annually almost 1 billion cans jo hain wo sell ho jayeinge. Aur isi simple math ki wajah se business ka annual profit roughly 5 arab rupay ki range mein pahunch sakta hai, yani roughly 100 million dollars ke plant se aapne 18 se 20 million dollars ka annual profit generate kar liya. Aur isi ko investors generally ROIC yani Return on Invested Capital kehte hain. Lekin yahan ek important nuance bhi hai. Ye ROIC jo hai ye fixed nahi rehta. Is business mein do aisi cheezein hain jo profits ko materially upar ya neeche leke ja sakti hain aur wo samajhna bohat zaroori hai. First important point ye hai ke Pakistan mein imported aluminum cans par total roughly 36% duties apply ki hui hain. Is mein 20% custom duty, 6% additional custom duty aur 10% regulatory duty bhi shamil hai. Aur kuch countries aur companies par to anti-dumping duty bhi apply hoti hai jo certain cases mein additional 20 se 26% ki range mein hai. Yani imported cans mangwana Pepsi, Coke, Next Cola ke liye zyada expensive ban jata hai. Aur is cheez ko jaante hue PABC ne local price, export price se zyada rakhi hui hai. Aur yahan aapne ek important nuance jo hai wo samajh liya. Har can ki profitability same nahi hoti. Local market mein sold cans ka profit per can, exports ke muqable mein materially higher ho sakta hai. Isee liye experienced investors sirf ye nahi dekhte ke total sale kitni ho rahi hai, wo ye bhi dekhte hain ke sale kis market mein ho rahi hai, local market mein ho rahi hai ya export mein ho rahi hai. Aur ab doosra factor aur ye aur bhi zyada interesting hai. PABC ka plant Faisalabad ke Special Economic Zone mein located hai jahan company ko 2027-2028 tak income tax exemptions hasil hain. Aur iska direct impact company ki profitability par parhta hai. Simple calculation hai, agar ye tax exemptions exist nahi kartiin to company ko profits ka significant portion income tax mein pay karna parhta. Aur average profit per can jo ke around 5 rupay hai, wo gir kar 3 rupay ke aas paas aa jata, aur ROIC pe bhi iska effect aata. Yani business ki economics sirf can demand se nahi chal rahi, import duties aur tax incentives bhi yahan major role play karte hain. Aur ab baat karte hain seasonality ki. Seasonality bhi is business ka important part hai. Company ki local demand ka major portion north Pakistan specially Punjab aur KPK se aata hai, kyunki major population hi is area mein hai Pakistan ki. Aur jo exports hain unka bhi jo large portion hai wo Afghanistan se linked hai. In dono regions mein winters kaafi zyada thandey hote hain, jis ki wajah se beverage consumption aur can ki demand winter months mein kaafi zyada kam ho jati hai. Yani quarterly numbers ko samajhte waqt seasonality ko ignore karna investors ke liye dangerous ho sakta hai. To ab tak humne PABC ke business ke teen important pillars jo hain wo samajh liye. Business ki core economics, import protection aur pricing advantage, is ke saath saath tax structure aur seasonality ka impact. Ab sawal ye hai in tamam factors ko dekhte hue agle saalon mein company ki outlook kaisi lag rahi hai? Aur kis tarah US-Iran war aur Afghanistan border closure company ko affect kar sakta hai? Chaliye ab wapis PABC ki outlook ki taraf aate hain, kyunki Afghanistan border close hone ka impact samajhna yahan bohat important hai. Agar hum pichle char quarters ko dekhein to picture kaafi clear ho jati hai. Second quarter 2025 yani ke April se June 2025 mein company ka revenue roughly 8.8 billion tha. Phir agle teen mahine third quarter 2025 mein bhi revenue around 7.4 billion raha. Pichla quarter jo hai wo summer ka tha, jaise maine aapko explain kiya isliye usme sale thodi si zyada thi. Lekin phir October 2025 mein Pakistan-Afghanistan border close kar diya gaya. Aur uske baad fourth quarter 2025 mein revenue gir kar sirf 3 arab rupay reh gaya. Jee han, sirf 3 arab rupay. Aur yahan sirf sales affect nahi huiin, July se September ke quarter mein company ne roughly 1.7 billion ka profit before tax generate kiya tha, lekin uske agle teen mahino mein business ne roughly 300 million ka loss generate kiya. Yani ek hi event ne quarterly profitability ko completely reverse kar diya. Aur ab first quarter 2026 mein slight recovery zaroor nazar aa rahi hai aur revenue around 3.7 billion tak improve hua hai, jaise jaise company Afghanistan ke bajaye doosri exports markets jo hain wo dhoond rahi hai. Ab naturally agla sawal ye banta hai ke agle saalon mein PABC ka business kaisa perform kar sakta hai? Iske liye humne do scenarios consider kiye hain. Pehla scenario hai Afghan border khul jana aur business recover kar jana. Aur doosra scenario hai jahan Afghan border band rehta hai aur company gradually alternative export markets jaise Bangladesh ya Central Asia, isko ek market mein develop karti hai lekin comparatively lower profitability ke saath. Idhar main aapko na ek important cheez samjhana chahunga. Kaafi zyada log jaise dekhte honge jo companies export karti hain export par margin thode kam hote hain specially agar Pakistan mein us cheez pe duties lagi hoon, lekin is company ke case mein jab ye company Afghanistan export karti thi to profit per can kam to hota tha lekin itna bhi kam nahi hota tha. Aur uski wajah ye thi ke aap agar dekhein Afghanistan mein khud to koi investment hai nahi, udhar koi na koi plastic ka plant hai na udhar koi aluminum ka plant hai, to wo log different different countries se cheezein import hi karte hain. To unke paas do option hote hain. Ek wo Uzbekistan, Tajikistan is tarah ki economies se import kar lein ya phir wo Pakistan se import kar lein. Ab jahan PABC ka plant hai Faisalabad se, wahan se Kabul ka distance jo hai wo bohat zyada nahi hai. And obviously Afghanistan mein jo cans ki demand hai us ka 50% 60% to Kabul se hi aayega, wahi ek major shehar hai. So agar Kabul se dekhein distance wise sabse kareeb kaun si factory parhti hai to wo PABC hai. Isi wajah se unke liye bhi ye best option hota hai aur is pe export karne pe company ko ek medium level ke margins jo hain wo generate ho jate hain. Ye jaan kar aapko hairangi hogi ke Afghanistan mein deyrh arab cans ki demand hai ek saal mein, aur Pakistan mein sirf 350 million. Aur uski wajah ye hai ke Pakistan mein plastic zyada istemal hota hai. Agar aap plastic ko bhi include kar lein, jisko technical language mein kehte hain single serve plastic bottles, to Pakistan ki total demand hai 3 arab se bhi zyada, lekin usme se cans abhi sirf 10 se 12% market capture kar paaye hain. Lekin doosri taraf Afghanistan, wahan pe koi plastic bottles ke plants nahi hain to wo zyada tar beverages sab cans mein hi consume karte hain. Ab main aapko explain karta hoon ke Pakistan mein aisa ek aur plant lagne ke yani ke PABC ka competitor khada hone ke chances bohat zyada kam kyun hain? Kyunki is plant ki jo minimum capacity hoti hai jis par costing achhi feasible ban jati hai, wo hai around 700 se 800 million cans. Aur ek company agar already 1 billion cans ki capacity le kar baithi hogi aur demand jo hai abhi 350 million cans hai, to phir ek new investment almost 100 million dollars ki koi kyun karega? To Pakistan ki demand jab shayad koi arab, deyrh arab cans tak pahunch jayegi, to phir jo hai ek additional player is market mein aane ke chances hain. Aur ab jaise aap dekh rahe hain ke Pakistan-Afghanistan ka border jo hai wo close ho gaya hai, to company jo hai wo koshish kar rahi hai ke alternative markets jaise ke GCC ya phir Bangladesh ya phir Uzbekistan, wahan pe apne cans ko sell kare. Iske saath saath aapne dekha hoga aaj se thode din pehle Iran ke saath bhi jo kuch corridors hain trade ke wo open hue hain, to company wahan pe bhi approvals lene ki koshish kar rahi hai, aur shayad wahan par bhi can supply kare. Lekin ye jitni bhi saari markets hain, is mein already plants available hain. To wahan pe penetrate karna aur bohat zyada profits earn karna thoda mushkil ho jata hai. Aur aapke mind mein ye bhi aa raha hoga ke Iran ka border agar open ho jaye, to company Iran ke through wapis apne Afghanistan customers ko jo hai wo can supply kar sakti hai, lekin agar aap distance calculate karein from Faisalabad to Kabul, to wo koi 1000 kilometer ke aas paas banta hai. Lekin agar aap Iran ke through wapis Afghanistan mein jayeinge aur Kabul jaane ki koshish kareinge, to wo distance 4000 kilometer ke kareeb chala jata hai. Aur aaj kal jo diesel ki price hai to phir ye possible nahi hai. Ab aate hain kuch forward looking developments par, jo is company ki story ko aur interesting bana dete hain. PABC ne 2025 mein Afghanistan mein apna aluminum can plant announce kiya tha. Is project ki announced capacity roughly 1.3 billion cans hai, aur estimated capex 110 million dollars bataya gaya tha. Aur interestingly border related disruptions ke baawajood company abhi bhi is project ko continue kar rahi hai. Management ka long term thesis relatively simple lagta hai. Afghanistan mein beverage demand already exist karti hai, lekin wahan par local manufacturing capacity limited hai. Yani agar local production successfully establish ho jaye to supply gap address ho sakta hai. Transportation cost ki savings ho jayeingi, aur border band hone ka risk jo hai wo bhi materially kam ho jayega. Doosri important cheez, abhi Pakistan mein imported aluminum cans par roughly 36% duty apply hoti hai. Isi protection ki wajah se company ke local cans par achhe profits hain. Is achhe profits ko affect kar sakta hai Pakistan IMF tariff rationalization program. Yani import duties gradually reduce karne par company ke business pe bhi affect aa sakta hai. Aur agar future mein duties materially reduce huiin upcoming budget mein, to naturally foreign competition increase ho sakta hai, jiska direct pressure pricing power, profit per can, dono par aa sakta hai.
