Kya aap jante hain ke Pakistan mein har saal 30 crore se zyada cold drink ke cans bikte hain aur un mein se almost har can ek hi factory mein banta hai. Poori Pepsi, poori Coca Cola, poori Cola Next, sab ek jagah se aata hai. Aur asli interesting cheez yeh hai ke is company ke jitne cans Pakistan mein bikte hain us se almost double ek neighboring country mein bikte hain, Afghanistan. Kyun? Kyunke Afghanistan mein na koi plastic ka plant hai, na koi local manufacturing, bas cans ki demand hai aur supply sirf yahan se aati hai. Aur ab yeh company PABC wahan bhi apna plant lagane ja rahi hai. To sawal yeh hai, kya ek packaging company do countries ki monopoly ban sakti hai?
