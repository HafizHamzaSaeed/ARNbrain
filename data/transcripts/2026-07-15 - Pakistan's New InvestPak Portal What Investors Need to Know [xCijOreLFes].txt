Kya aap bhi aisi investment dhoond rahe hain jo safe ho? Jahan aapko paisa doobne ka dar na ho? Jahan aap acche profits bhi bana sakein aur is sab ki guarantee khud hukoomat le le? Agar haan, to aaj ki video aapke liye hai kyunke State Bank of Pakistan ek aisi cheez lekar aaya hai jisse aap ghar baithe ye saare faide haasil kar sakte hain. Assalam-o-Alaikum dosto, mera naam hai Shafee Butt aur aaj ki video mein hum baat karenge Invest Pak ki. Invest Pak ek naya digital platform hai jiske zariye aap government securities mein ghar baithe invest karke profit bana sakte hain, wo bhi sirf paanch hazar rupayon se. Ab ye government securities hoti kya hain? Faida kya hai iska? Isko aam awaam ke liye kyun launch kiya gaya hai? Aur aap iske zariye investment karke kaise faida utha sakte hain? In sab sawalon ke jawab hum aaj ki video mein discuss karenge. Acha, sab se pehle hum dekh lete hain ke government securities aakhir hoti kya hain, matlab kya hai iska? To bhai, government securities mein mainly aa jate hain treasury bills aur treasury bonds. Ye basically ek aisa instrument hota hai, ek aisa tareeqa hota hai, jiske zariye investors hukoomaton ko qarza dete hain, paise dete hain. Waise ye jo bonds hain, ye badi companies bhi nikaal sakti hain paisa raise karne ke liye, lekin aam taur par hukoomatein hi karti hain. Jo governments hoti hain, hukoomatein, unhein apne kharchon ke liye paise chahiye hote hain. Kuch paise wo taxes wagera se jama kar lete hain jo hum hi dete hain, aur uske badle humein kuch milta nahi, at least directly nahi milta. Khair, ye taxes jo hain, inse aam taur par hukoomaton ke kharche poore nahi hote, unhein aur paise chahiye hote hain. To ye treasury bills aur bonds ke zariye hukoomatein apne hi mulk ke andar se qarza leti hain. Lekin isme qarza dene wale yaani investors ka kya faida hota hai? Bhai, unhein profit milta hai. Hukoomat ye T-bills aur bonds jaari karti hai aur kehti hai ke aap isme invest karein, humein paise dein iske badle, aur hum kuch time baad aapko aapke paise bhi waapis kar denge aur uske upar profit bhi denge. Ab ye jo time hai ke kitne time baad aapko aapke paise waapis milenge, ye vary kar sakta hai, mukhtalif ho sakta hai. Aam taur par jo treasury bills hote hain, unka time teen mahine, che mahine, nau mahine, ya barah mahine, yaani ek saal tak ka hota hai. Yaani agar kisi ne che mahine wala T-bill liya hai to usko uske paise che mahine baad milenge, aur saath hi uska profit bhi mil jayega. Jo bonds hote hain, wo zara zyada lambe arse ki investment hoti hai. Teen saal, paanch saal, das saal, pandrah saal, ya even isse bhi zyada lambi investment ho sakti hai. Isiliye aam taur par jab bonds mein koi invest karta hai, to usse uska profit time poora hone par, yaani maturity date par nahi balki beech mein bhi milta rehta hai. Aam taur par teen mahine ya che mahine baad profit milta hai. Aur ye profit aam taur par jo State Bank ka interest rate chal raha hota hai, uske aas paas hota hai. Haan jitne zyada time ki aapne investment ki hogi, utna zyada profit milega aapko. Aur ye jo profit ya return hota hai, iske bhi mukhtalif tareeqe hote hain. Jo T-bills hote hain, unka aam taur par tareeqa-kar ye hota hai ke unki jo face value hoti hai, aapko wo usse saste par mil jaate hain. Yaani ke let's say aapne teen mahine ka ek 5,000 wala T-bill lena hai, to ye aapko 5,000 ki bajaye 4,600 ya 4,700 ka mil jayega, aur teen mahine baad jab iska time poora hoga, to aapko iske badle 5,000 rupees milenge. To ye jo price ka difference hai, yehi aapka profit hai, isme aapko alag se koi profit nahi diya jayega. Isiliye isko zero coupon security bolenge. Haan jo zyada lambe time ki investments hoti hain, jo bonds hote hain, un mein aapko beech mein bhi profit milta hai, isiliye unhein coupon bonds kehte hain. Aur isme bhi aapko fixed rate coupon bonds aur floating rate coupon bonds mil jaate hain. Fixed rate mein aapka jo profit hai wo fixed hota hai, aur floating mein jo hai aapka profit, wo market decide karti hai. And by the way, ye jo bonds hote hain, ye bhi ek dafa jab government bech deti hai, to baad mein log isko buy aur sell kar sakte hain apni zaroorat ke mutabiq. Kisi ne agar paanch saal ka liya hua hai aur wo paanch saal nahi hold karna chahta, to isko jaldi bhi bech sakta hai aur log jo hain khareedar wo market mein available hote hain. Jis tarah hum stock market mein dekhte hain ke ek dafa stock jo hai wo market mein aa gaya, to log isko buy aur sell karte hain apni marzi se. Ab Pakistan mein aapko mukhtalif qisam ke bonds mil jaate hain, jaise ke Pakistan Investment Bonds, Defense Savings Certificates, Government of Pakistan Ijarah Sukuk, jo ke islamic version hain. Lekin hum already jo hai wo government securities ki kaafi detail mein ja chuke hain, isliye filhaal hum in bonds ki detail mein nahi jaate. Abhi hum ye discuss kar lete hain ke aakhir ye Invest Pak ko launch kyun kiya gaya. To bhai baat ye hai ke hamari jo pyaari hukoomat-e-Pakistan hai, uske ye jo T-bills aur bonds ke sab se bade investors hain, wo hain hamare commercial banks. Invest Pak ki launching ceremony par bataya gaya ke government securities mein taqreeban 78% investment banks ki hai. Yaani ke almost saari government securities banks hi le rahe hote hain. Ab banks isliye khushi khushi hukoomat ko qarza de dete hain, kyunke government securities ko ek safe investment tasawwur kiya jata hai. Kyunke hukoomat aapke paise waapis karegi iske chances bohat zyada hain, as compared to ke bank kisi Mustaqeem bhai ko qarza de de unke business ke liye. To banks ke liye ye safe bhi hai aur asaan bhi, kyunke unhein koi background check nahi karna padta qarza dene se pehle. Lekin isse nuksaan ye hota hai ke jab private sector banks ke paas jata hai qarza lene ke liye, apna business chalane ke liye, to banks kyunke already apna majority paisa jo hai wo hukoomat ko de chuke hote hain, to ab in private sector businesses ko dene ke liye unke paas paisa hota nahi. To is tarah private sector ki jo growth hai, wo mutasir hoti hai. Doosra ye nuksaan bhi hai ke hukoomat jo hai wo mainly sirf ek source par rely kar rahi hoti hai apna qarza lene ke liye, in banks par. To hukoomat bhi ab kuch diversification karna chahti hai, aur banks par apni reliance kam karna chahti hai. Isiliye ye Invest Pak ko launch kiya gaya hai, ke iske zariye aam log jo hain, retail investors, wo directly hukoomat ko paisa dein, hukoomat ki banks par reliance kam ho, aur banks bhi private sector ko kuch qarza dena shuru karein. To isme sabhi ka faida hai. Awaam ko bhi apni savings karne ke liye ek accha aur safe platform mil gaya hai. Warna hum log to kametiyon mein paise daal kar hi apna nuksaan karwate rehte hain. Khair, isi silsile mein recently State Bank ne MDR, yaani minimum deposit rate ke rules mein kuch changes kiye hain, aur ab jin logon ne apne savings account mein ek crore se zyada paise rakhe hue hain, unke saath banks kisi fixed rate ke mutabiq profit sharing karne ke paband nahi. Yaani ke banks ya to apni marzi se ya phir un logon ke saath kuch negotiate karke profit rate ki sharing jo hai wo tay kar sakte hain. Yaani ye bade savings account wale logon ke liye ek acchi news nahi hai. To ho sakta hai ke ab yahan se bhi kuch log Invest Pak ke zariye directly hukoomat ke saath investing karein, kyunke paisa to unhon ne fixed income mein hi lagana tha. To wo savings account mein na sahi yahan hukoomat ke saath sahi. Aur isi tarah Ministry of Finance, State Bank of Pakistan, aur JazzCash ne mil kar June 2024 ke mid mein JazzCash users ke liye T-bill mein investment karne ki option bhi mutaarif karayi. Waise main check kar raha tha JazzCash mein to mujhe abhi chaar options hi nazar aayin, aur saari ki saari three months ki T-bills ki options thin. To abhi limited options hain lekin at least kuch na kuch ho raha hai. Khair, ab Invest Pak ke zariye aapko bohat saari options mil rahi hain, aur ye investment aap sirf 5,000 rupaye se shuru kar sakte hain. Aur aap ko sirf ek Pakistani currency wala local bank chahiye, baqi aapko kahin jaane ki zaroorat nahi, ghar baith kar ye kaam shuru kar sakte hain. To ye investments aap kaise kar sakte hain, aain dekh lete hain. Acha ji, Google par ja ke aapne likh lena hai 'Invest Pak', theek hai? Isko enter karenge, ye aapke paas pehla link aa raha hai, investpak.sbp.org.pk, theek hai? Is par aap aa gaye to ye aapke paas website aa gayi Invest Pak ki. Ab isme aapko bohat saari options mil rahi hain. Ye upar Pakistan Bonds, iski saari details di hui hain. Bond market ka structure ke wo kaise kaam karti hai. Primary market ka data hai, secondary market ka data hai. Iske baare mein hum abhi aage ja kar baat karte hain. Neeche bhi aapke paas aur kaafi saari information aa rahi hai. Isme aap jo hai wo ye jab auctions ho rahi hongi inki, yaani ye kaam start hoga, uski bhi saari details aap dekh sakte hain. Market treasury bills, agar hum isko check kar lein ek minute ke liye to isme saara data aa raha hoga, ye dekhein aapke paas ye information aa gayi. Ye unki dates aa rahi hain, ye uska total size aa raha hai, provincial governments se related hai ye, target bataya hai, ye saari information aapke paas mil rahi hai yahan par. Theek hai? Isi tarah aapko aur bhi cheezon ki mil jayegi, isme inhon ne baqi cheezon ka samjhaya bhi hua hai, ke ye hoti kya hain, kaam kaise karti hain to aapko bohat saari information mil jayegi. Ye hai aapka jo information portal, theek hai? Is par aapko ye saari cheezein dekhne ke liye koi account banane ki zaroorat nahi, aap directly aa kar ye saari cheezein dekh sakte hain. Investment karne ke liye aapko account banana padega jo ke aap yahan transaction portal se bhi kar sakte hain, aur ye neeche 'Register as Investor' yahan se bhi kar sakte hain, hum filhaal iske through kar lete hain. Dono aapko same page par le aayenge. Yahan aapke paas ye options aa rahi hain saari jo aap fill karenge. Individual aap kar lein, corporate agar koi company wagera wo bhi kar sakti hai, hum filhaal individual pe ja ke single account hai, yaani aapka joint account agar hai to aap joint kar lein, waise aam taur par single hote hain logon ke. Phir yahan aapne apni details add kar deni hain. Let's say main filhaal 'ABC' kar dete hain, mother's name jo hai wo kuch rakh lete hain idhar. Date of birth jo bhi aapki hai wo aap add kar lein. Filhaal main randomly jo hai wo kar raha hoon. CNIC bhi jo aapka theek wala ho wo aap de dijiyega, main filhaal aise hi enter kar raha hoon, theek hai? Phir aapke samne ye RDA ki option wo pooch raha hai, agar aapka RDA account hai to usko kar dein, hamara nahi hai filhaal. Phir aapne currently resident of Pakistan 'Yes' is pe kar dein. Ab aapne apne jo aapka bank account hai, uski details aapne daal deni hain. To yahan aap IBAN number daalenge apna. Aage ye islamic bank hai ya nahi wo aapne select kar lena hai. Islamic hai, baqi wo usne khud hi detail nikaali ke kaun sa bank hai, aur usko foran se verify bhi kar liya. Acha, phir aapne mobile number daal dena hai, wo keh rahe hain ke jo aapke port mobile hote hain na, wo keh rahe hain wo na daalein. To jo aapka theek number hai wo add kar edin, filhaal hum kuch bhi kar dete hain. Aur email address apna de dein. Acha, phir aapne 'Get OTP Code' pe click kar dena hai, aapko email pe bhi ek OTP aa jayega, mobile number pe bhi. Wo aapne add kar dena hai, uske baad IPS account information. Ye aapka investment portfolio of securities account hai, jo ke already aapka nahi bana hoga, to isko aap 'No' kar dein. Aur ye bhi phir aapko add karne ke zaroorat nahi hai. Iske baad ek captcha aayega, aapne wo enter karke submit pe click kar dena hai. Acha jab aapki details approve ho jayengi, to aapko aapke credentials mil jayenge email ke zariye. Wo phir aap jo hai wo is transaction portal pe ja ke enter karenge, login karenge, apni details add karenge, captcha add karenge, aur phir aap se jo hai wo password reset karne ke liye ek naya password set karne ke liye kaha jayega. Aapne apna password add kar dena hai, pin jo hai wo add kar deni hai, ye pin bohat kaam ki hai, aapki saari transactions ke liye yehi pin use hogi. Phir aapne jo hai wo OTP source bata dena hai, email ya SMS, dono bhi aap kar sakte hain, aur phir aapne submit kar dena hai. Phir jab aapka password reset ho jayega, aapne apni nayi details add karni hain, aur phir OTP jo aayega wo add karna hai aur login kar lena hai. Phir aap investor portal pe aa jayenge. Yahan pe aap directly primary market se jo hai wo government ki securities mein investment kar sakte hain. Primary market hota hai ke jab government apna ye security launch karti hai, to wo primary market mein aata hai, uske baad jab log usi security ko aapas mein buy ya sell karte hain, to wo secondary market mein aa raha hota hai. To wo saari options bhi yahan pe aapko mil jaati hain orders lagane ki. Phir aap apna portfolio jo hai, usko bhi check kar sakte hain. Aur uske baad aapki jo history hai saari, wo bhi yahan par aapko show ho jayegi. Yahan pe aapko saare apne buy orders, sell orders, wo saare mil jayenge. Phir agar hum primary market ko thoda sa dekh lein, to yahan se aap isko khol sakte hain, aur yahan pe aapko jo bhi us waqt auctions chal rahe honge, wo nazar aa jayenge. Ye aap dekh sakte hain yahan pe aapke paas three year wali fixed income mein bhi option aa rahi hai, aur five year ki option bhi aa rahi hai, Pakistan Investment Bond ki. Aur yahan pe unki saari details bhi di gayi hain, ke kitne target amount hai, coupon payment frequency jo hai wo kya hai, auction status open hai, aur is tarah unki dates aa rahi hain. To yahan pe aapko unki saari details jo hain wo show ho jayengi, aur iske zariye aap yahan pe investment kar sakte hain. Aapne ye 'Place Bid' pe chale jana hai, aur jo bhi aap investment karna chah rahe hain, wo aapne apna pehle bank select kar lena hai, phir uske baad aapne apni pin add kar leni hai, phir aapne jo amount leni hai, wo aapne add kar deni hai, aur phir aapne submit par click kar dena hai. To is tarah se aap investment kar sakte hain. To is tarah se aap apna account khulwa kar directly government securities mein invest kar sakte hain, aur safe tareeqe se profits bana sakte hain. Aur again isme conventional options bhi maujood hain aur islamic options bhi maujood hain. Aur iski mazeed detail jo hai, wo aap Invest Pak ki website se bhi dekh sakte hain, wahan par video tutorials mein bhi account kholne ka tareeqa, aur ye investment karne ka tareeqa jo hai, wo bataya hua hai. Main again jo investment wali cheezein thin uski zyada detail mein nahi gaya, kyunke wahan par kaafi zyada options hain, to wo thoda confusing bhi ho sakta hai, to maine abhi ek jo simple process hai, usko explain kiya. To bhai ye tha Invest Pak, aur is se mutaliq saari information. Humne isko kaafi detail mein discuss kar liya hai, ke government securities hoti kya hain, inka istemaal kya hai, faida kya hai, aur hum kaise isme hissa le sakte hain.
