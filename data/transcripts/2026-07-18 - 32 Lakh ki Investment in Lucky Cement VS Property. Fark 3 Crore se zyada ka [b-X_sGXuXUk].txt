Main har waqat sochta rehta hoon ke property mein invest kar loon ya woh company jo ke cement banati hai jis se construction hoti hai uske share mein invest kar loon? Aur main believe karta hoon ke data doesn't lie, to aaiye dekhte hain.

January 2016 mein LDA Avenue Lahore mein 5 marla plot taqreeban 32 lakh ka tha, wahi 32 lakh agar 2016 mein jo ke stock market ka top tha, us mein agar Lucky Cement ke shares khareed lete to aaiye dekhte hain agle 10 saal ki kahani teen hisson mein divide kar ke kaise lagti hai. 

Pehle teen saal, 2016 se 2019, plot 32 lakh se 52 lakh par pahunch gaya, 20 lakh ka faida, aur stock market neeche aane ki wajah se sirf 40 lakh tak pahuncha. Yani ke start property ka zyada accha tha. 

Agle teen saal, 2019 se 2022 tak, plot 52 lakh se 62 lakh ka ho gaya, 10 lakh ka mazeed faida, aur stock pehle to 40 lakh se barha, lekin phir 2022 mein jab Pakistan ki economy thodi si crisis ki taraf gayi aur PSX undervalued hui, to flat raha 40 lakh pe hi. Aur yeh woh waqt hai jab zyada tar log ghabra kar stock bech dete hain. 

Aur phir aakhri chaar saal ki kahani, 2022 se 2026 tak, plot LDA Avenue ka 32 lakh se barh ke ab 86 lakh ka ho gaya hai taqreeban, 24 lakh ka mazeed faida ho gaya, aur stock 40 lakh se seedha barh ke ho gaya 3 crore plus. Aur is time mein thode bohot dividends bhi aaye. 

To doston, same 32 lakh ki investment, same 10 saal, property ne aap ko total 54 lakh ka faida diya, stock ne 3 crore se zyada. Chalein Lucky Cement ko chorein, agar aap index investing hi kar lete, jis ke liye parhne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai, to aaj bhi aap ki investment jo hai woh deyrh crore ke aas paas hoti.
