Acha ji, agli news ki taraf chalte hain, wo aa rahi hai hamare paas ek kaafi serious news. Pakistan ko aane wale time mein fuel ki serious shortage ka samna karna parh sakta hai. Jo hamari oil industry hai usne government se request ki hai ke jo price differential claims rehte hain unko jaldi se release kiya jaye. Ye wo paise hain jo government ne in OMCs ke dene hain, jo ke lagbhag 66.7 billion rupees bante hain. Ab ye paise jo hain wo kyunke inke phanse hue hain jis wajah se in OMCs ko jo agli inventory hai wo laane mein kaafi mushkilat ka samna hai, jis wajah se aane wale time mein jo hamein fuel shortages hain Pakistan mein wo dekhne ko mil sakti hain. 

Khair ye sirf ek wajah nahi hai, iske ilawa aur bhi wajah hain jaise ke ek to waise hi fuel ki prices badhi hui hain to us wajah se bhi financial jo mushkilat hain companies ko wo face karni parh rahi hain. Uske ilawa, is waqt Pakistan ke paas jo inventory padi hui hai Motor Spirit, fuel ki, wo hai takreeban 370,000 tons, jo ke takreeban 15 din ka cover banta hai. Yani 15 din ke Pakistan ke paas fuel hai uske aage nahi hai. Aur ye jo 370,000 tons ka fuel hai hamare paas isme se bhi ek acha khasa portion jo hai uske abhi tak customs se clearance hi nahi hui. To actual available jo fuel hai wo is se bhi kam hai. Yani ke 15 din ka fuel bhi is waqt Pakistan ke paas nahi hai. Haan, abhi Motor Spirit ke teen cargos jo hain wo aane wale hain, lekin agar inme bhi koi customs ka masla ho jata hai ya koi aur masla ho jata hai to Pakistan mein serious fuel shortage ho jayegi. 

Aur isi wajah se jo oil industry hai usne government se kaha hai ke ye jo 66.7 billion rupees ke claims hain inko foran se clear kiya jaye. Uske ilawa government jo hai wo customs clearance mein help kare, ye jo petrol ke cargos hain ye phanse hue hain customs mein, aur uske ilawa government jo hai wo uninterrupted import and distribution of petroleum products iski jo hai wo yakeen dehani karaye. Government apni intervention kare taake fuel ki shortage na ho. Ye khaas tor pe jo upper area hai Pakistan ka, northern area, wahan pe ye situation zyada serious ho sakti hai. Aur jab hamein ye khabrein sunne ko milti hain ke fuel shortage hone lagi hai to waise hi log jo hain wo hoarding kar lete hain, cheezon ko jama karna shuru kar dete hain, jis wajah se is mein mazeed izafa ho jata hai aur prices mazeed badh jaati hain. To is waqt ye teen char jo hain wo serious issues aaye hue hain. Ek to waise hi price hike hui hai fuel ki, uske ilawa OMCs ke jo hain wo kuch paise phanse hue hain government ki taraf se. 

Iske ilawa ek aur update aayi hai ke ab se OGRA har roz petrol ki prices update karega. Pehle ye har hafte ya do hafte baad update ki jaati thin lekin ab se har roz petrol ki prices update hongi aur ye OGRA ki website pe bhi show hongi. 

Acha ab agli news ki taraf chalte hain to wo bhi hamare paas oil se related hi aa rahi hai. Pakistan jo hai wo Saudi Arab se 6.7 billion dollar ki concessional oil financing facility lena chahta hai 15 saal ke period ke liye. Isme sources ne bataya ke Pakistan jo hai wo 1% interest rate ke upar 15 saalon ke liye ye 6.7 billion dollar ki oil se related facility, concessional facility lena chahta hai aur usme 5 year ka, paanch saal ka grace period bhi maanga gaya hai. To 2019 se Saudi Arab ne jo hai wo Pakistan ko ek saal ki deferred payments pe oil jo hai wo provide kiya hai yani Pakistan pehle oil le leta hai aur ek saal baad uski payment karta hai. To ye jo hai wo 2019 se Pakistan kar raha hai aur jo last isme Pakistan ne February 2025 mein ye facility li thi jo ke Pakistan ko 6% interest rate ke upar mili thi. To ab Pakistan chah raha hai ke 1% interest rate ke upar 15 saalon ke liye ye facility mile. To agar to ye mil jaati hai to ye bohot acha hoga kyunke Pakistan ko is ke upar jo interest rate hai wo takreeban na hone ke barabar hi dena padega, 6% aur 1% mein jo hai wo bohot zyada farq hai. To dekhte hain ye Pakistan ko mil paati hai facility ya nahi.
