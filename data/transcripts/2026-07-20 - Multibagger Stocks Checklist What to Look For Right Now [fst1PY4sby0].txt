Step one se start karte hain, main aap ko batata hoon ke different-different sectors mein kaunsi aisi business mein special cheez hoti hai jo ke ek company mein agar ho to uske andar potential hota hai ek multibagger banne ka. Usse pehle main thoda sa multibagger ko explain kar deta hoon. Bhai dekhein, stock market ka long-term record hai ke ek average company jo hai wo 16%, 17%, 18% pe compound karti hai aur ye main kaafi lambe arse ki baat kar raha hoon, 20-25 saalon ka record ye show karta hai to ye jo 20-25 saal hote hain isme kabhi jo hai let's say P/E ratio seven bhi hota hai, kabhi eight bhi hota hai, lekin jab aap itne lambe arse pe kisi cheez ko compare karte hain to phir P/E ratio ka jo effect hota hai yani ke base effect wo khatam ho jata hai. Yani ke itne lambe arse mein jo value create hui hoti hai kisi bhi asset mein, kisi bhi company mein, wo actually wo business grow hua hota hai ya uske profits increase hue hote hain. To agar aap ko koi multibagger identify karna hai to aap ko sirf ye nahi dekhna hoga ke ye aaj kitna undervalued chal raha hai, aap ko ye bhi dekhna hoga is business ke andar khud bhi ability hai ke iske andar agar let's say Rs. 100 ki investment hoti hai to ye har saal uske upar 25%, 30% jo hai profit generate kar sakta hai. So gist ye hai ke agar aap ne lambe arse pe heavy compounding karni hai to us business ke andar ye khud ye ability honi chahiye ke khud wo apni value jo hai har saal 25 se 30% badha raha ho, warna otherwise jab bull market chal rahi hogi to P/E ratio increase hone ki wajah se, valuation increase hone ki wajah se aap kuch companies mein faida to utha sakte hain lekin lambe arse pe ye sustainable nahi hota. To aaiye ab ek overview karte hain aur main aap ko batata hoon har sector mein aisi kaunsi ek special cheez hoti hai jiski wajah se us company ke multibagger banne ke yani ke baqi businesses ke muqable mein zyada profit kamane ke jo chances hain wo badh jate hain.
