Pakistan ke sab se valuable bank, jis ki value 1,000 arab se bhi zyada hai, UBL bank ka result announce ho gaya hai. Aur ek decent result aane ke bawajood market ne is ko koi khas value nahi di. Aur is ki kya wajah thi, yeh main aap ko bataunga. Sab se pehle banking industry ka raw material hota hai deposits aur UBL ne apne deposits pichle teen mahino mein 5.4 trillion se barha ke ab kar liye hain 6.1 trillion. Lekin jo net interest income thi, woh taqreeban 100 arab se jo hai 90 billion pe aa gayi hai. Toh sab se pehle jab is ke detailed accounts aayenge, aap ne check karna hai ke yeh jo naye 600, 700 arab rupay ke deposits aaye hain, is mein se current accounts kitne hain?

Aur itni achi deposit growth aur itne ache profits ke bawajood market ne is ko value is liye nahi di kyun ke inhone apna dividend jo hai woh increase nahi kiya. Market mein expectations thi ke 8 rupay quarter wala dividend jo hai ab woh 9 ya 10 ho sakta hai kyun ke bank ke profits ache the. Lekin saath hi bank ne kuch subsidiary mein investments announce ki jo ke taqreeban 30 billion rupees ki banti hain, jis se yeh expectation ke agle ek do quarters mein maziid dividend increase ho sakta hai, woh bhi mere hisab se die down ho jayengi. Toh is wajah se mere hisab se market ne UBL ke is result ko itna zyada celebrate nahi kiya.
