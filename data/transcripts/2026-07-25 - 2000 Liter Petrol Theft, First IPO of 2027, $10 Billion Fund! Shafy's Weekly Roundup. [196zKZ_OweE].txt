Iske liye ek dedicated helpline bhi launch kar di gayi hai taake aap khud call karke is pe mazeed information bhi le saken.

Basically masla saara geopolitics ka hai. Pehle hum jo hai woh Monday ki closing jo hai usko dekh lete hain. Monday ko market ne 0.07% positive closing di, mamooli si thi lekin uske baad agle chaar din jo hain, Tuesday se Friday tak, woh market ne negative closing di aur again geopolitics reason hai iski, iski reason Pakistan ke jo macroeconomic halaat hain woh nahi hain kyunki macroeconomic situation toh Pakistan ki improve ho rahi hai, Pakistan ki rating B- se B hui hai, positive jump aaya us mein aur yeh Pakistan ke kisi idaare ne nahi kiya, yeh bahar ke credit agency ne kiya hai toh zahiri baat hai ke koi wajah hai toh aisa hua hai na. Jo stock market hai usme kyunki kaafi hadd tak woh sentiment par bhi chalti hai hamare investors ke toh is wajah se kyunki oil prices jo hain woh upar ja rahi hain, jo geopolitics mein escalation ho rahi hai Iran, America ki, usme jo hai woh tezi aa rahi hai, abhi pehle Strait of Hormuz band thi ab jo doosri side hai Red Sea wali Bab-el-Mandeb usme bhi jo hai woh usko band kiya gya hai aur jo Saudi Arab ke do oil tankers the unke upar attack kiya gaya toh mazeed humein negative news dekhne ko mili hai is wajah se mazeed negativity aayi market mein. Tuesday wale din jo hai woh taqreeban again zyada change nahi aaya negative 0.12% ka drop tha, phir agle din Wednesday ko taqreeban 1% ka drop tha 0.97, phir jo Thursday hai usme humein taqreeban negative 1.54% ka drop dekhne ko mila aur again Friday ko bhi jo hai woh negative 0.42% ki closing hui, toh bahut zyada bade drops nahi aaye lekin again closing negative hoti rahi hai. Ab jaise ke maine pehle bataya Pakistan reason nahi hai, geopolitics reason hai toh aapne jo hai woh ghabrana nahi hai, aapki agar investments hain toh aap usko hold karke rakh sakte hain, aapko koi zaroorat nahi ke aap loss mein bechen, meri personal investments bhi hain aur maine bhi koi stock nahi becha abhi tak.
