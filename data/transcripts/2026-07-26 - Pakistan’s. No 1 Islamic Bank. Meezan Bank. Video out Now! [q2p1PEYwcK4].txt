Pakistan mein 20 se zyada listed banks hain, lekin market ne trillion rupee club mein sirf do banks ko dala, jis mein se ek aisa bank hai jo mujhse bhi zyada young hai aur woh bank hai Meezan Bank. Aur sawal yeh hai ke market ne is bank mein aisa kya dekha jo baki 20 banks mein nahi tha? Aur jawab ek chote se number mein chupa hai jo aksar log balance sheet mein dekhte hi nahi hain. Industry ke baki banks ke paas yeh number 35 se 40 percent hai, lekin Meezan Bank ke paas 50 percent. Sirf 10 se 15 percent ka farq, lekin isne is bank ki earning power ko baki industry se 50 percent se zyada barha diya. Ab sawal yeh hai ke kya is bank ki kahani abhi bhi baki hai ya jo investors aaj enter kar rahe hain, woh top par khareed rahe hain?
