Aksar log samajhte hain ke agar interest rates kam ho jayein to banks ki share prices bhi kam honi chahiye, kyunki bank ke margins kam ho rahe hain. Lekin reality itni simple nahi hoti. High interest rates mein banks ki earning generally improve hoti hain lekin uske sath sath jo depositors ko return dena hota hai wo bhi increase ho jata hai aur loan defaults ka risk bhi increase ho jata hai. Isiliye market high interest rate environment mein banks ko aam tor par lower P/E multiples deti hai. Doosri taraf jab interest rates gradually neeche aate hain to financing demand improve hoti hai, asset quality behtar hoti hai aur market banks ko higher valuation multiples dena shuru kar deti hai. Jaise Warren Buffett kehte hain: 'Interest rates are like gravity. The higher the interest rates, the more they pull down share prices.' Low interest rate environment mein companies ko high P/E ratios milte hain. To is wajah se share price upwards jaane ke chances zyada hote hain, even though interest rate neeche aa rahe hote hain. So is lens se agar aap dekhenge to ye jo confusion hoti hai ke agar interest rate neeche ja raha hai to bank ka share bhi neeche jaana chahiye, ye shayad clear ho jaye.
