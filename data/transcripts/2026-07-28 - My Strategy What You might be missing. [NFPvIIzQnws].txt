Sabse simple formula hai ke aap dekhein ke stock market ka jo PE ratio hai yaani ke ek business agar 100 rupaye kama raha hai ek saal mein, jab bhi uski price jo hai 500 rupaye se neeche aa jaye, yaani ke PE ratio 5 se neeche aa jaye, toh phir aapne jo hai zyada invest karna hai stock market mein. Aur khi yeh PE ratio jo hai 10 cross kar jaye toh aapne phir sochna hai ke ab market jo hai thodi over-valuation ki taraf jaa rahi hai aur main aahista aahista jo hai apne stock market portfolio ko percentage of savings jo stock market mein invested hai usko kam kar deta hoon. Toh aap isme ek rough rule bana sakte hain ke main hamesha jo hai 30 aur 65 percent ki range mein jo hai woh maneuver karta rahunga. Jab let's say stock market ka PE ratio 5 pe aa jayega toh main 50 percent pe chala jaunga, agar woh 4 pe aa gaya market aur zyada under-valued ho gayi, toh main shayad saat-aath percent aur badha doon. Aur phir jab yeh market ka PE ratio 11 pe chala jayega toh main apni percentage savings ko 45 percent pe le aaunga, phir agar yeh 12 pe gaya toh main 35 pe aa jaunga. Aur phir agar yeh 13 PE ratio pe gaya toh main apni percentage of savings jo stock market mein hain woh 30 percent pe le aaunga. Is tareeqe se jitni zyada under-valuation, utni zyada investment in the stock market aur jitni zyada over-valuation utni kam investment in the stock market, is simple rule ke through hi aap jo hai boom bust cycle ko ek hadd tak time kar sakte hain. Jab devaluation hoti hai usse market mein uncertainty phailti hai aur log jo hai doosre asset classes jaise ke gold aur even dollar mein jo log invest karte hain uski taraf chale jaate hain.
