Investing to start kar di hai, ab yeh samajh nahi aa raha ke market order lagana hai ya phir limit order lagana hai? Kaun sa behtar hai? Answer is limit order. Market order us waqt ki order book mein jo bhi sellers demand kar rahe hote hain, yani ke unki munh boli qeemat, woh aap pay kar ke buying kar lete hain, yani ke expensive buying. Limit order mein aap market price ke aas paas apni bid set karte hain, aur 90% chance hote hain ke aapka order aglay 15 se 30 minute mein, jaise hi market mein chhoti moti fluctuation chalti rehti hai, woh complete ho jayega.
