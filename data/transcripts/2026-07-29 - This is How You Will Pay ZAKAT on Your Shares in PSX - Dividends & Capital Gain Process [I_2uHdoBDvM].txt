Toh abhi apne aaj ke topic ke upar aa jate hain aur sabse pehle zakat itself ki baat kar lete hain. Ab main iski zyada detail mein toh nahi jaunga kyunki obviously meri area of expertise mein yeh cheez nahi aati, lekin iski jo moti-moti information hai, main information, woh aap ko de deta hoon, jise obviously aap apne school of thought ke scholars se confirm bhi kar sakte hain. Toh bhai zakat ka jo general tareeqa hai woh yeh hai ke zakat aap ne deni hoi hai apne cash ke upar, woh chahe kisi bhi currency mein ho. Jo bhi aap ke paas paisa hai, naqd mein, bank mein, uske upar aap ne zakat deni hai. Uske ilawa aap ne sone ke upar, chandi ke upar, aur tradable assets yani koi bhi aisi cheez jo aap ne bech kar profit banane ke liye khareedi hoti hai, uske upar aap ne zakat deni hoti hai.

Ab in cheezon ki value kitni honi chahiye ke aap sahib-e-nisab banenge? Yani aap ke upar zakat farz hogi? Toh agar toh aap ke paas sirf aur sirf sona hai, gold, toh woh agar sadhe-saat tola hoga, toh aap sahib-e-nisab kahlaenge aur aap ko zakat deni hogi. Lekin aaj ke daur mein obviously sirf sona toh kisi ke paas bhi nahi hota, sath aur cheezein bhi hoti hain. At least kuch na kuch cash toh hota hi hai. Toh agar toh koi bhi aur asset hai aap ke paas jin ki humne pehle baat ki, sona, chandi, tradable assets, toh phir aap un sab ki total value nikalenge mila kar aur agar toh woh value chandi ke sadhe-bawan tola ke barabar ban rahi hui, toh aap sahib-e-nisab kahlaenge aur aap ko apne maal ke upar adhai feesad, 2.5%, zakat deni hogi.

Misal ke taur par, aaj chandi ki qeemat taqreeban 6,800 rupay chal rahi hai ek tola ki. Toh agar hum ise sadhe-bawan se multiply kar lein toh hamare paas aata hai taqreeban 3,60,000 rupay. Yani ke agar aap ke total assets ki value, jin mein sona, chandi, cash ya koi bhi aisi cheez jise aap ne profit banane ke liye khareeda hai, inki total value 3,60,000 ya isse zyada hai, toh aap ne is total value ke upar apne maal ke upar 2.5% zakat deni hai. Haan yeh hai ke jis din aap ke paas itni value ke assets jama honge pehli dafa, uske ek saal baad aap ne zakat deni hoti hai. Aur yeh jo ek saal wala time hai, yeh aap ne qamri calendar ke mutabiq dekhna hai. Yeh angrezi calendar jo January February wala hota hai iske mutabiq nahi dekhna.

Aur yeh toh ho gaye aap ke assets, lekin zakat dene se pehle aap ne apni liabilities nikalni hoti hain. Yani ke jo paise aap ne dene hain, misal ke taur par aap ne ghar ka bill dena hai, kiraya dena hai ya kisi se qarza liya hua hai toh woh sari aap ki liabilities hain, unko apne assets ki value se nikal kar phir aap ne hisab lagana hai aur phir jo value aayegi us par zakat deni hai. Aur yeh zakat obviously baaligh mard aur aurat par farz hai, bachon par nahi hai. Toh yeh humne zakat ki kuch main cheezein discuss kar lein. Ab ismein obviously aur bhi kafi sari details hain toh iska hisab lagane se pehle, apni zakat dene se pehle aap poori tarah research kar lein aur ulama se raye le lein, phir apni zakat dein. Humne abhi jo main cheezein discuss ki hain woh isliye taake humein ek general sa idea ho jaye. Ab hum baat kar lete hain ke stock market mein zakat jo hai woh kis tarah se di jati hai.

Toh bhai stock market mein jo hum shares lete hain, woh bhi kyunki hamare assets hi hote hain isliye unpar bhi zakat lagti hai. Kuch zakat jo hai woh aap ko khud deni hoti hai aur kuch zakat aap se khud le li jati hai yani automatically deduct ho jati hai. Yani ek toh woh tax kat hi raha hota hai dividends wala, uske ilawa zakat alag se katti hai. Jab hum apna account khula rahe hote hain broker ke through toh wahan par hamare samne do options aati hain. Ek Muslim Zakat Deductible aur ek Muslim Zakat Non-Deductible. Aam taur par hum deductible wali option choose kar lete hain kyunki jo doosri option hoti hai, uske liye humein ek form fill karke dena hota hai. Yeh form isliye hota hai ke agar aap chahte hain ke aap se automatically zakat na li jaye toh yeh form fill kiya jata hai. Aur jo zakat hamare se li jati hai woh dividends ke upar li jati hai.

Toh agar toh aap chahte hain ke aap ke dividends ke upar automatic zakat deduction na ho, toh ek form aata hai CZ-50 form, usko fill karke aap jo hai woh apne broker ko dete hain. Uska sara process bhi main batata hoon. Pehle ek sawal aata hai ke zakat aakhir katti kitni hai hamare dividends ke upar? Toh bhai iski jo maine research ki hai, iske mutabiq mujhe koi sahi iska fix jawab nahi mila. Personally maine khud bhi yeh form nahi jama karwaya hua aur meri toh zakat nahi katti. Lekin main apne kuch aur doston se pooch raha tha toh unki jo hai woh mukhtalif numbers mein zakat katti hui hai, kisi ki 20% bhi kat rahi hai, kisi ki 28% even up to 50% bhi aap ki zakat kat sakti hai yani agar aap ko dividend mila 100 rupay ka toh uspar 50 rupay ki zakat bhi kat sakti hai. Toh iska koi fix number nahi hai, isliye behtar yeh hai ke aap yeh form jo hai woh apne broker ko jama kara dein aur jo automatic deduction hai usse aap nikal jayein aur khud apni zakat jo banti hai uska hisab laga ke aap de dein.

Toh yeh hai woh form jise CZ-50 form kehte hain apni zakat deduction ko rokne ke liye, aur ismein basic details aap ne deni hoi hain, apna naam batana hota hai, age, address aur apna fiqah batana hota hai. Uske ilawa aap ko iske liye do witnesses chahiye hote hain, unke aap ne sign karwane hote hain aur yeh form jo hai yeh aap ko Google se mil jata hai badi aasani se. Main neeche description mein bhi iska ek link laga deta hoon jahan se aap yeh form le sakte hain. Khair, yeh form jo hai woh aap ne ek stamp paper ke upar print karana hota hai. Stamp paper jo hai woh alag-alag regions ke unke alag-alag rates hain toh woh aap jo hai woh apne broker se bhi confirm kar sakte hain aur even stamp paper wale jo hain, woh bhi aap ko iska bata denge. Toh yeh form jo hai woh aap ne download kar lena hai stamp paper ke upar jo bhi relevant hai, ismein details fill kar leni hain apni aur jo witnesses ki hain unki. Isko attest kara lena hai aur isko phir aap ne apne broker ko de dena hai toh aap ki jo automatic zakat deduction hai woh phir nahi hogi.

Khair, yeh toh ho gayi automatic zakat deduction ki baat. Let's say aap ne yeh form jama kara diya aur ab aap ki automatic zakat nahi kategi. Ab aap ne khud apni zakat deni hai. Toh bhai jaisa ke humein pata hai ke stock market mein humein do tareeqon se faida hota hai. Ek hota hai capital gain aur ek hota hai dividends. Toh humein in donon par zakat kaise deni hai, iske baare mein baat kar lete hain. Jo SECP ki guidelines hain PSX ke hawale se, jin ko hum pichli videos mein bhi discuss kar chuke hain, us document mein bhi yehi mentioned hai aur jo maine ulama ko sun kar research ki hai, unke mutabiq bhi same tareeqa hai jo hum abhi discuss karne lage hain.

Toh pehle hum dekh lete hain capital gain ko yani agar aap ne investment hi isliye ki hai ke jab aap ke shares ki value badhegi toh aap unhein bech kar profit banayenge, toh is par toh aap ne seedha-seedha jo bhi aap ke shares ki value banti hai uske upar us hisab se zakat de deni hai. Yani let's say aap sahib-e-nisab hain aur aap ka ek saal poora ho gaya hai yani zakat dene ka time aa gaya hai. Toh aap ne apne jo sare assets hain unki value nikalni hai jaise cash ho gaya, chandi, sona woh sari jitne bhi baqi tradable assets hain unki value nikalni hai aur sath hi sath apne stock market ke shares ki bhi value nikal leni hai aur inko total assets mein add kar lena hai. Ab jo bhi aap ke assets ki value ban rahi hai us par aap ne 2.5% ke hisab se zakat de deni hai. Yani yeh bilkul simple process hai jo bhi aap ke shares ki value ban rahi hai uske mutabiq aap ne zakat de deni hai chahe aap ka portfolio aap ke shares jo hain woh profit mein hon ya loss mein. Even agar loss mein bhi hain toh bhi unki koi value unki koi worth toh hai na, woh bhi aap ki wealth mein hi consider honge. Zakat jo hai woh income ke upar nahi woh wealth ke upar di jati hai.

Ab let's say aap ke jo total assets hain, woh 8 lakh rupay ke hain jin ke upar aap ne zakat deni hai aur jis din aap ne zakat deni hai us din aap ke jo shares hain unki worth hai 2 lakh rupay toh aap ke total assets ho gaye 10 lakh rupay ke. Ab iske upar aap ne 25,000 yani 2.5% zakat de deni hai.

Haan agar aap ne investment sirf aur sirf dividends ke liye ki hai, aap ka shares bechne ka koi irada nahi, bas yeh niyat hai ke main sari zindagi is se dividends leta rahoonga, yeh meri ek income source hogi. Toh is case mein aap kyunki shares bechne ke liye nahi le rahe, yeh aap ke liye tradable assets nahi hain, aap sirf partnership kar rahe hain business ke sath. Toh is case mein hamare paas do tareeqe aa jate hain. Pehla toh yehi hai simple wala ke jo bhi aap ke shares ki value hai aur jo bhi aap ko dividends mile hain, aap us sab ko apni wealth mein consider karein, hisab lagayein apne poore maal ka aur uske mutabiq 2.5% zakat de dein, simple process.

Lekin jo doosra tareeqa hai, woh thoda sa complicated ho sakta hai balki complicated bhi nahi bas thoda sa aap ko kaam karna padega usmein. Ab hota yeh hai ke kisi bhi company ke jo assets hote hain, unmein do tarah ke assets aa jate hain. Ek hote hain current assets aur ek hote hain non-current assets. Jo current assets hote hain, woh short-term assets hote hain. Inmein company ke paas maujood cash aa jata hai, investments ho sakti hain mutual funds mein, banks mein ya kahin bhi, kisi aur company ke shares liye ho sakte hain, toh aise assets jo liquid hote hain jinhein jaldi se cash mein convert karaya ja sakta hai, inhein current assets kehte hain. Doosre hote hain non-current assets jo ke long-term assets hote hain. Inmein company ke plants ho sakte hain, real estate properties ho sakti hain, aisi cheezein jo liquid nahi hotin jinhein jaldi se cash mein convert nahi karaya ja sakta, inhein non-current assets kehte hain. Toh jo zakat hai, ulama ke mutabiq woh current assets par deni hoti hai, non-current assets par dene ki zaroorat nahi kyunki non-current assets toh company ke ishtemal mein hote hain, woh tradable assets yani bechne ke liye nahi hote.

Toh aap ne company ke jo current assets to total assets ki ratio hai, woh dekh leni hai aur uske mutabiq jo share price banti hai us par zakat de deni hai. Main aap ko dikha deta hoon ke yeh ratio aap kaise dekh sakte hain aur phir hum isko mazeed samajh lete hain. Equity and liabilities bataya hua hai, uske neeche aap aa jayein toh yeh aap ke samne assets aa gaye, theek hai? Ismein yeh non-current assets hain aur current assets. Yahan pe iske sare non-current assets ko total karke yeh bataya hua hai number, aur isi tarah jo current assets hain unko total karke yeh number bataya hua hai.

Acha hum isko easy karne ke liye, aasani se samajhne ke liye jo hai woh chote numbers le lete hain. Let's say non-current assets jo hain woh Rs. 40 banti hai uski value aur jo current assets hain, unki value Rs. 60 banti hai aur inko mila ke Rs. 100 jo ke 100% assets hain company ke, woh ban rahe hain, theek hai? Ab share price hum le lete hain company ki Rs. 100. Hum kyunki current assets ke upar zakat dete hain jo ke yahan par share price ka 60% ban raha hai, toh hum share price ki 60% ke upar zakat denge bajaye iske ke 100% par dein. Toh yahan par Rs. 100 ka 60% hua Rs. 60. Ab iske upar hum zakat denge jitne bhi hamare shares ban rahe hain. Misal ke taur par hamare paas 1000 shares hain, toh ek share mein hum le rahe hain 60% jo ke ban raha hai Rs. 60. 60 ko hum 1000 se multiply karenge, 60,000 aa jayega hamare paas. Toh in Rs. 60,000 ko hum apne poore jo hamari value hai total assets ki, jo baqi hamare paas sona, chandi, cash wagera hai usmein milayenge aur uske upar hum 2.5% ke hisab se zakat de denge. Toh is tarah se aap zakat calculate kar sakte hain agar aap ne shares sirf aur sirf dividends ke liye khareede the.
