Agli news ki taraf chalte hain to ye bohat important news hai, Pakistan ne finally oil refinery policy update kar di hai aur isse Pakistan mein directly 5 se 6 billion dollar ki investment aa sakti hai. Cabinet Committee on Energy, jise khud Prime Minister chair kar rahe the, usne finally ye Brownfield Refining Policy 2023 mein amendments karke isko upgrade kar diya hai, aur isse jo hai wo expected hai ke 5 se 6 billion dollars ki jo investment hai wo Pakistan mein hogi, in refineries ki taraf se. Ab isme jo hai wo taqreeban 6 nayi provisions add ki gayi hain, jisme jo hai wo stability clauses add ki gayi hain ke jo foreign investors hain, unko jo hai wo assurances milein, wo jo hai tassali se apni investment kar sakein, aur unke paas ek security ho apne projects ko apni refineries ko upgrade karne se pehle. Ab ye jo policy mein update kiya gaya hai isse basically hoga ye, ke Pakistan ki jo barhti hui energy demand hai isme jo hai wo security milegi isse ek. Uske baad jo hum petroleum products bahar se import karte hain, refined products, unpe hamari bohat zyada reliance hai is waqt, to usme kami aayegi kyunke ab hum khud se jo hai wo refine karna shuru kar dein ge in petroleum products ko. Uske ilawa, isse jo hai wo Euro 5 compliant fuel paida hoga jo ke bohat zyada environment friendly hai, abhi jo hai wo lower grade fuel hum bana rahe hain, hamari jo refineries hain, to usse jo hai wo environment ko zara damage hota hai. To Euro 5 petrol jab banega usse, wo environment ke liye bhi bohat achha hoga. Aur uske ilawa jo hai wo, abhi jo hai furnace oil hamari refineries wo bohat zyada banati hain. Furnace oil se jo hai wo refineries ko loss hota hai kyunke uski demand itni zyada nahi hai, to ab ye refineries high-speed diesel aur jo motor gasoline yaani petrol hota hai ye zyada banayeingi aur isse inke jo profits hain wo improve ho sakein ge. To finally saalon ki consultation jo hai uske baad ye Brownfield Policy ko update kar diya gaya hai refineries ke liye, aur isme jo hai wo 7 saal ka ye fiscal incentives diye jayein ge in refineries ko, jisse jo hai wo ye investment kareingi aur isse Pakistan ka bhi fayda hoga aur obviously in refineries ka bhi fayda hoga. Aur again ye jo foreign investment aayegi Pakistan mein, to isko jo hai wo aise clauses isme lagaye gaye hain policy mein, ke jo investors hain unko protection mile, yaani agar koi Pakistan ke andar taxation ke hawale se changes aati hain, koi fiscal policies change hoti hain, environmental regulations mein tabdeeli aati hai, to usse jo hain wo ye investors ki jo investments hain ye protected raheingi. Achha ye jo policy hai ye originally August 2023 mein notify ki gayi thi, baad mein isme amendment bhi ki gayi lekin again ye jo ek mix bana rahi thi refineries furnace oil ka, petrol ka aur diesel ka, isme furnace oil ki jo ratio hai wo kaafi zyada thi. To again us wajah se refineries ko loss hota tha aur humein import jo karne parte the petroleum product, refined petroleum products, uski wajah se Pakistan ko har saal jo hai wo taqreeban dhedh se do billion dollars ka nuksaan ho raha تھا. To again ye policy jo hai wo bohat achhi baat hai ke isko upgrade kar diya gaya hai aur isse ye filhaal abhi refineries jo hain wo isme time lagega upgrade karne mein, 5 se 6 saal lag sakte hain uske baad ja ke ye upgrade hongi. Lekin finally ek policy aa gayi hai to ab hopefully jo hai wo ek raah mil gayi refineries ko ke wo apni upgradation karein, Pakistan mein investment layein, aur apna petrol aur diesel ka jo mix hai usko behtar karke apne profits hain unko bhi improve karein.

Achha ye refineries jab upgrade ho jayeingi to jo production mein increase aayega uske baare mein baat kar lete hain.
