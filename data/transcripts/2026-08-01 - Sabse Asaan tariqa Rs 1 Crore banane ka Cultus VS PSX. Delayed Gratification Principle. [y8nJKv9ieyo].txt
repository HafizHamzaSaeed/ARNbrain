Sabse aasan tariqa bataun Pakistan mein apna first karor kamane ka? Is combo ko agar aap PSX ke long term 17 se 18 percent ke return pe compound karenge, to 7 saal mein aapka first karor ban jayega. Aur jab ek karor ka portfolio ban jayega, to yeh portfolio aapko har saal 10 lakh ki passive income bhi de sakta hai. Aap chahein is 10 lakh ko vacation pe kharch karein, 3 saal baad gaari upgrade kar lein ya apne doosre goals jo life goals hote hain, unko poora kar lein yeh passive income ko use kar ke.
