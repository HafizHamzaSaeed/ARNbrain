Toh sab se pehle samajhte hain cement business mein paisa kaise banta hai. Ek cement company ki profitability ek cheez se shuru hoti hai aur woh hai plant lagane ki cost. Aaj ke rates pe 3 million ton ka plant, jiski annual capacity 3 million ton hai, woh takreeban 180 se 200 million dollar cost karta hai, yaani ke roughly 50 arab rupaye. Aur cement ka ek aur faida yeh hai ke cash pe bik jata hai. Customer ko advance nahi dena padta, toh working capital ki jo zaroorat hoti hai is business ko chalane ke liye woh kaafi kam hoti hai. Ab agar profits ki taraf aate hain, ek average efficient cement plant har ton par takreeban sade chaar hazar rupaye profit kama leta hai. Toh jo 3 million ton ka plant hai, 90% capacity pe agar operate kare toh is invested capital ke against annual profit takreeban 8 billion rupees ke kareeb ban sakta hai aur isko ROIC mein agar convert karein toh yeh takreeban jo industry ki average hai, 16 se 18% uski range mein aa jata hai. Lekin Lucky Cement ne pichle do teen decades mein industry se consistently behtar kamaya hai. Toh yeh samajhna bahut zaroori hai ki yeh farq aata kahan se hai taaki hum future mein bhi isko understand kar sakein. Early 2000s mein Pakistan ki cement industry expand ho rahi thi aur zyada tar companies European technology ko prefer kar rahi thin jo ke reliable thi, proven thi aur premium consider ki jaati thin. Aur jo European plants hote the unki cost hoti thi per ton takreeban 90 se 110 dollar. Lucky Cement ne is time pe ek alag faisla kiya. Inhone Chinese technology ko istemal kiya jo ke relatively nayi thi aur kam proven thi lekin inki cost jo thi woh 60 se 70 dollar per ton thi. Lucky Cement Chinese cement plants ka ek early adopter tha aur aahista aahista phir dusri companies ne bhi isko adopt kiya. Lucky Cement ka doosra advantage: bijli. Aap ko pata hai cement banane mein bijli ka theek thaak istemal hota hai. Toh jab gas sasti thi, Lucky Cement ne 70 se 80 megawatt captive gas-based power plants lagaye. 2022 tak aap ko pata hoga ke Pakistan mein captive gas se bijli grid se almost aadhe rate pe mil jaati thi yaani bijli ki cost mein theek thaak bachat. Phir gas 2022, 2023 mein mehangi hone lag gayi aur Lucky Cement ne phir se adopt kar liya. Is baar 74 megawatt solar, almost 29 megawatt wind energy aur ab Lucky Cement cement industry mein BESS yaani ke battery energy storage system adopt karne wali pehli company ban chuki hai. Aur BESS kya karta hai, solar aur wind energy ko batteries mein save kar leta hai, toh raat ko bhi solar se aap energy use kar sakte hain aur is se renewable energy behtar effective tareeqay se iski efficiency increase ho jati hai. Aaj company apni 50 se 55% power requirement in-house generate kar rahi hai renewables se aur 40 se 45% grid pe reliance hai kyun ki aaj kal jo grid hai woh gas se bhi chaar paanch rupaye per unit sasta pad raha hai. Aur dosto teesra factor jo Lucky ko baqiyon se behtar banata hai woh hai coal. Lucky apne plants mein zyada tar imported coal use karta hai, Tanzanian aur South African, inki quality consistent aur supply reliable hai. Jab global coal prices normal hon toh imported coal ki cost local alternatives ke muqable mein competitive range mein hi rehti hai. Aur chautha factor hai location. Aur yeh woh factor hai jiski importance pichle kuch saalon mein ubhar ke aayi hai aur yahan pe genuinely aaj kal Lucky Cement ko kaafi faida hai. Jaise main aap ko batata rehta hoon, limestone cement ka buniyaadi raw material hai, is par royalty har soobe mein alag alag hai. Punjab mein ek hazar rupaye per ton, Sindh mein dahi sau rupaye per ton aur KPK mein takreeban sade teen sau rupaye per ton. Lucky Cement ki 10 million ton cement ki capacity Pezu yaani ke KPK mein hai aur 5 million ton ki capacity Karachi ke kareeb hai, yaani ke dono low limestone royalty zones. Toh yeh sab factors: Chinese plant technology, in-house cheap power generation, coal sourcing aur location advantage mil ke Lucky ko Pakistan cement business mein ek efficient low cost producer bana dete hain, jiski local cement capacity ko hum 65 dollar per ton ki value assign kar sakte hain jo ke plant lagane ki actual cost se kaafi zyada kareeb hai. Plant ki asset value, net debt aur profitability ko adjust karne ke baad, Lucky Cement ke Pakistan cement business ki value mere hisab se takreeban 320 se 350 billion ki range mein ho sakti hai, agar hum isko stock market jis tareeqay se cement plants ko value karti hai us angle se dekhein. Kyun ki Pakistan mein capacity utilization, yaani ke agar aap ke paas 1 million ton ka plant hai toh woh koi takreeban 60 se 70% ek saal mein production karta hai. Iraq aur Congo mein yeh almost 100% hai, yaani ke full production har waqt aur wahan tax rates bhi lower hain Pakistan ke muqable mein. Isiliye in plants ko Pakistan ke 65 dollar per ton ke muqable mein aap 100 dollar per ton ki valuation bhi assign kar sakte hain. Earning power zyada hai toh valuation bhi zyada hogi. Iraq mein Lucky ki 3.3 million tons ki capacity hai aur Congo mein expansion ke baad takreeban 4 million tons ki, toh combined capacity roughly saat se sade saat million tons banti hai. Dono projects 50-50 joint ventures hain. Ab second important business pe aate hain aur iski baat karte hain: Lucky Electric Power Company ki. Lucky Cement ek 660 megawatt coal fired power plant ki 100% owner hai. Aur agar aapne hamari power sector ki videos dekhi hon toh aap ko pata hoga ke IPPs ko value karne ka sab se seedha tareeqa hota hai ke unki installation cost ko dekha jaye, jo ke government of Pakistan ki policy hai, jitna aap IPP mein invest karenge uske hisab se aap ko ek fixed return milega. Is plant ko lagane ki cost thi takreeban 800 se 900 million dollar jis mein se Lucky ki 25% investment thi aur baaki debt thi, yaani ke Lucky Cement ne is mein takreeban 200 se 250 million dollar invest kiye. Aur ab is plant ko operational hue hue teen char saal ho chuke hain. In saalon ki earnings ko bhi agar account kar lein toh uske baad is business ki fair value roughly 400 million dollar ban jati hai. Aur ab woh warning jiska maine aap ko thodi der pehle hi zikr kiya tha, jaisay hi aap Lucky Cement ka consolidated EPS yaani ke earnings per share dekhte hain aur yaada tar log isko bas P/E ratio se multiply kar dete hain fair valuation nikalne ke liye, consolidated EPS mein aap ko ek bahut important cheez jo hai woh samajhni hogi kyun ki iske andar jo IPP ke profits hain, woh bhi aa rahe hote hain. IPPs ki accounting policies aisi hoti hain ke first 12 years mein jab plant apna jo debt hota hai usko repay kar raha hota hai, toh debt repayment bhi earnings ka part ban jati hain. Is plant par jo government of Pakistan ke saath contract hua hai, har saal pehle 12 saalon mein takreeban yearly loan repayment jo hai woh 50 se 60 million dollars hai, jo ke rupees mein roughly 15 billion rupees aur agar usko per share mein convert karein toh 10 se 12 rupaye ban jati hai. Toh jab bhi aap Lucky Cement ka EPS consolidated ko aap dekhein, toh yeh sawaal zaroori poochein, is mein IPP ka debt repayment separately account hua hai ya nahi. Agar nahi hua hua toh phir aapne jo hai, woh jo debt repayment wali amount hai, woh us mein se subtract karni hai aur phir aap real earnings tak pahunchenge.
