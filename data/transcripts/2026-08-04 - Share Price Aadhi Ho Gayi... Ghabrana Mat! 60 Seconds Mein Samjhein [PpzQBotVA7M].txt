Naya naya portfolio bana liya hai 5 lakh ka, aur har roz ab subah mein 9:40 pe uth ke sabse pehle prices check karta hoon. 22 June, Monday ke din jab main utha, to Millat Tractors ki share price aadhi ho gayi, 600 se seedhi 300. Mere toh haath paon phool gaye. Office mein sab mujhse pooch rahe the ke theek to ho? Tabiyat theek hai? Phir mujhe dopahar mein jaa kar ek colleague mila. Usne bhi mere saath Millat Tractors mein invest kiya hua tha. Ab woh bilkul theek tha, toh maine usse apna dukh jo hai wo share kiya. Pehle to usne mera bohot mazaak udaya, phir bataya usne ke company ne split announce kiya hai. Tumhare 1000 shares jo hain wo ab 2000 shares mein convert kar diye gaye hain free mein. Is liye company ki jo share price hai wo 600 se 300 ho gayi hai, tumhari investment ko kuch nahi hua. Toh dosto, PSX mein companies splits announce karti rehti hain. So, ye important investing lesson aap apne doston se share karein jo split ko dekh ke price girne se ghabra jaate hain, aur agar poori investing seekhni hai, seekhna hai ke kaise karte hain poori investing, to comment karein, main seekhaon ga.
