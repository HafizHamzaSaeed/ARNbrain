Abhi check kar lete hain. Pakistan Stock Exchange mein har roz hone wali trades ka data hamare paas rozana ki buniyaad par aata hai FIPI aur LIPI reports ki form mein. FIPI se banta hai Foreign Investors Portfolio Investment aur LIPI se banta hai Local Investors Portfolio Investment. Ye data aap bohot aasani se NCCPL ki website se dekh sakte hain. Aur is data se hamein pata chalta hai ke rozana ki buniyaad par Pakistan Stock Market mein foreign investors, overseas Pakistanis, local companies, aur local individuals kitni khareed-o-farokht kar rahe hain. Yani ke ye data hamein clearly bata deta hai ke rozana ki buniyaad par kaun stock market ko control karta hai? To aaein check kar lete hain ke Pakistan Stock Market kitni manipulated hai aur kitni nahi.

To Google par aake aapne NCCPL likh lena hai aur enter press kar dena hai. Yahan aapke paas NCCPL ki website aa jayegi, isko aapne khol lena hai. Yahan pe aapke paas ye market information aa raha hai, ispe click kar dein. To ye aapke paas market information ka page aa gaya, is mein agar aap neeche aa jayein to ye aapke paas saari FIPI normal, FIPI sector wise, aur ye saari details aa rahi hain. Aur yahan pe aap date jo hai wo bhi apni marzi se select kar sakte hain. To hum filhaal FIPI pe aa jaate hain aur yahan pe aapko show ho raha hai ke foreign corporates ne kitni buying ya selling ki, foreign individuals ne kitni buying selling ki aur overseas Pakistanis ne kitni ki. Yahan aap dekh sakte hain ye equity mein saari details hain, aur gross buying, gross selling, net buying, net selling, ye saari information aa rahi hai. Hum agar is gross buy mein in teeno ko combine kar lein, to ye hamare paas ban raha hai 3.8 billion rupees, ye PKR mein aapko dikha rahi hai, theek hai? To 3.8 billion rupees ki buying hui hai, isi tarah jo gross selling hui hai, wo 3.6 billion ki hui hai. To ye Pakistan se bahar se ho raha hai. Ab agar hum LIPI mein aake dekh lete hain ke Pakistan ke andar se kitni buying selling ho rahi hai, to ye hamare paas saari wo aa rahe hain, banks, brokerage, proprietary, companies, individuals, ye saare. Ab is mein hum dekhein agar ke in sab ko mila ke ye jo total ban raha hai, ye hamare paas 40 billion ban raha hai. Jo foreign se ho rahi thi, wo thi 3.8 billion, aur ye 40 billion hai, yani ye usse 10 times se bhi zyada hai. To Pakistan ke andar se jo participation ho rahi hai, wo bahar ke muqable mein 10 times se bhi zyada hai. Isi tarah agar hum gross selling mein dekh lein, to ye hamare paas 40 billion ki again gross selling ho rahi hai Pakistan ke andar se, aur jo foreign se ho rahi thi gross selling wo thi 3.6 billion ki almost. To again ye usse 10 times se bhi zyada hai, to ise hamein ye pata chala ke Pakistan ke andar se jo hai wo zyada participation hai balki bohot zyada participation hai as compared to jo foreign participation hai. Accha ab agar hum inki individual details dekh lein, to banks ne jo hai wo taqreeban 262 crore ki buying ki hai, isi tarah jo brokerage se hai wo taqreeban 5 billion ki ho rahi hai, aur companies ne again 1 billion ki ki hui hai. Agar hum individuals ko dekhein, to yahan pe 31 billion ki jo hai gross buying hui hai, ek din ke andar. Aur isi tarah baqi ki bhi thodi thodi hai, aur isko milate hain agar hum to ye phir hamare paas 40 billion ka figure banta hai. To is 40 billion mein se sirf individuals ne jo hai yani aapke aur meri tarah ke jo log hain unhone 31 billion ki buying ki hai. Isi tarah agar hum selling mein dekh lein to jo gross selling hai 40 billion ki, us mein sirf individuals ne jo selling ki hai wo 31 billion ki hai. To ye aapke aur meri tarah ke log hain jo itni bade figures mein participation kar rahe hain aur baqi saaron ko mila bhi lein to wo iske muqable mein kuch bhi nahi banta. To is data se hamein clearly pata chal gaya ke na to Pakistan Stock Market ko bahar se koi control karta hai aur na Pakistan ke andar se bade bade idare ya log isko manipulate karte hain. Balki Pakistan Stock Market mein jo aam log, aapke aur meri tarah ke log hain, inki participation sabse zyada hai, aur har mahine hazaron naye log stock market mein aa rahe hain, yani ye participation aur bhi zyada badh rahi hai.

To ye to humne clear kar liya ke Pakistan Stock Market ko koi ek ya chand log control nahi karte, na hi koi ek ya chand companies control karti hain. Ye natural tareeqe se chalti hai. Lekin koi banda ye sawal bhi kar sakta hai ke chalo poori market na sahi lekin kisi ek individual company ko to koi bada magarmach control kar sakta hai na? Koi ek banda agar dher saare shares lekar ek sath bech de market mein, to share price to furan se gir jayegi. Bhai, aisi baatein karne wala banda bhi sirf apni kam-ilmi ki wajah se karta hai ya phir usi snowball effect ka hissa banne ki wajah se karta hai. Kyunki Pakistan Stock Market mein listed jo acchi companies hain, unke market cap hi itni bade hain ke unhe control kar pana almost impossible hai. Aur agar koi banda ye koshish karta bhi hai to hamare haan stock market mein aise rules bane hue hain jo ye kaam karna aur bhi mushkil bana dete hain. Market cap hota hai ek company ka size, ek company ki value, ke rupees mein iski value kitni chal rahi hai. To ye teen zero agar hum is mein add karein to ye banti hai 776 billion rupees, again ek accha bohot bada number hai iska. Aur ye uske total shares hain, free float shares ye hain. Free float shares wo shares hote hain jo ke stock market mein trading ke liye available hote hain. Baqi saare shares jo hain wo company ke apne paas hote hain. Aur free float percentage ka matlab ye hua ke 20% shares yahan pe jo hain wo company ke trading ke liye available hain. Accha agar hum market cap ki baat kar lein ke ye nikalta kis tarah hai to uske liye jo total shares hote hain unko hum multiply karte hain share price ke sath.
