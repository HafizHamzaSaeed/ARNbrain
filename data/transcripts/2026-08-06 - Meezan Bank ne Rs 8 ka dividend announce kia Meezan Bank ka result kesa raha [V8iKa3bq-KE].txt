Aap sab ke favorite aur Pakistan ke number one Islamic bank, Meezan Bank ne aaj apna result announce kiya aur apna dividend jo hai, apne share holders ke liye, usko increase kar diya. Aaye quickly 1-2-3 is result ko review kar lete hain. Deposits jo ke kisi bhi bank ki ek tarah se backbone hoti hain, usme bhi increase hai. Bank apne operating expenses bhi ache tareeqe se jo hai, control kar raha hai, profits bhi increase ho rahe hain, aur dividend bhi increase ho gaya hai. Aur ab har share holder Meezan ke, usko per share 8 rupaye ka dividend milega. Lekin in sab achi developments ke baad bhi, share price jo hai usme koi khas increase nahi aaya, aur uski do wajah mere hisaab se. Ek ye ke is bank mein thori si expectation thi ke Meezan Bank split announce karega. Lekin jab woh split announce nahi hua, to utna zyada interest jo hai bank mein nahi bana. Aur doosra mere hisaab se ye bank ab ek already premium valuation pe trade kar raha hai. Agar hum aage jaake assume karein bhi har quarter ye 10 rupaye ka dividend bhi dene lag jaate hain, tab bhi jo current price hai, woh uski dividend yield jo hai, 7 percent se bhi kam ho gayi hai. To is wajah se, jab koi share already premium valuation pe pahunch jata hai, to jab uska acha result bhi aaye tab bhi uski share price us tarah se react nahi karti.
