Dosto, refinery sector jiske andar waise hi itni dhoom machi hui thi aur itna zyada interest tha har investor ka, kyunki budget mein refinery upgradation pe sales tax bhi khatam ho gaya tha aur recently CCOE ne refinery policy bhi approve ki hai. Aaj is sector ki pehli company ne apna April se June tak ka result jo hai, announce kar diya hai. Aur sab expect kar rahe the ke losses aayenge, kyunki pichle teen mahino ke andar oil price jo hai, woh thodi si niche aayi thi, MoU sign hua tha, toh refineries ne kyunki crude oil hold kiya hota hai aur jab price niche aa jati hai toh unko loss pe bechna padta hai. Lekin iske bawajood, duniya bhar mein crude oil aur diesel aur petrol ke beech ka jo difference hota hai, jisko gross refining margins kehte hain, woh itne strong chal rahe hain ke is inventory loss ke bawajood company ne boht achhe profits post kar diye. Aur achhe results ki wajah se, refinery policy ki wajah se, aur ek jo poori duniya mein high GRMs chal rahe hain because of the war, uski wajah se refinery sector jo hai, boht achha perform kar raha hai.
