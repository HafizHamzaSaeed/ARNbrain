Export ki ijazat milegi ke nahi, isko samajhne ke liye sabse pehle aapko samajhna hoga ke ek normalized inventory kya hoti hai urea ki. Ke kitna urea jo hai, woh ikthha ho jayega to phir Government of Pakistan kahegi ke ab jo hai humein ise export kar dena chahiye, is opportunity ka fayda uthana chahiye. Aur yahan main aapko batata hoon ke sab analysts jo hain, jo ghalti kar rahe hain woh asal mein dekh rahe hain ke do saal pehle jo inventory hoti thi ya teen saal pehle jo urea ki inventory hoti thi, aaj jo hai woh usse chaar guna ho gayi hai, paanch guna ho gayi hai. Is wajah se Government of Pakistan ko export ki ijazat de deni chahiye. To is poori cheez ko samajhne ke liye hum zara chale jate hain do teen saal pehle. Us waqt jo hai, aapko pata hai urea mein black marketing chal rahi ti. Bag banta nahi tha, usse pehle jo hai, uske buyers jo hain factory ke bahar khade hote the. To jo urea ki inventory thi, woh koi lakh do lakh ton pe aa gayi thi. Aur agar aap dekhein Pakistan mein jo normal demand hai ek mahine ki, woh paanch lakh se bhi zyada hai. Aur aap mein se jo log thoda sa industry mein kaam karte honge, aapne dekha hoga ke do se teen mahine ki inventory rakhna kisi bhi industry mein, woh bahut hi normal hota hai. To mere hisab se to ek million ton ki bhi agar aap ke paas inventory ho jaye urea ki, to woh sirf do mahine ki inventory hai. Aur kaun si government chahegi ke pehle woh usko export kare, phir agar koi crisis aa gaya to usko phir se import kare mehenge daamon pe aur ek poora jo hai political crisis create kare. Aur chalein agar Government of Pakistan ne export ki ijazat de bhi di, to aapko nahi pata yeh baat ke yeh jo poori industry hai, isko kam price pe gas di jati hai taake farmers ko kam price pe urea diya jaye. Aur Government of Pakistan jab yeh ijazat de rahi hogi export ki, to woh companies ko kyun allow karegi ke woh bag jo aap 5000, saarhe chaar hazar bech ke accha profit kama lete hain, woh muft mein aap 10,000, 12,000 ka bechein. Dosto, ab hum aa jate hain GIDC pe aur Pressure Enhancement Facility jisme Engro Fertilizer jo hai, 100 million dollar ki investment karne ja rahe hain. GIDC pe meri analysis yeh hai ke Engro Fertilizer ne jo GIDC mein amount payable ho sakti hai, woh koi 18-20 arab rupaye ke kareeb hai. Aur yahi amount jo FFC hai, uske liye koi 80-85 billion rupees hai. Aur aapne dekha hai kuch mahino pehle jo hai ispe discussion ho rahi hai ke ho sakta hai court ki taraf se decision aa jaye ke aapne yeh payment karni hai. Ho sakta hai do saal, teen saal, chaar saal ka payment plan mil jaye, lekin is cheez ke bhi chances hain ke yeh jo cess apply hua tha, yeh fertilizer companies ko pay karna pade. Aur is cheez ko agar hum combine kar lein pricing power se, jaise aapko pata hai ke yeh jo Pakistan mein urea ki price hoti hai, international ke muqable mein bahut zyada kam hoti hai. Aur agar koi aisa faisla aata hai ke aapne GIDC ki amount jo hai woh chaar saal mein pay karni hai, ho sakta hai yeh companies koi taqreeban 100 rupaye apni bag price increase kar dein. Aur agar aisa scenario hua, to phir kyunki Engro Fertilizer ki jo amount pay karni hai GIDC ki, woh compared to FFC kam hai, aur jo benefit hoga 100 rupaye price increase karne ka, woh Engro Fertilizer ka proportionately basis pe zyada hoga. To Engro Fertilizer GIDC ki ek beneficiary bhi ban sakti hai. Lekin yahan pe humne ek bahut important assumption ki hai ke GIDC collect karne ke liye kuch marginal sa increase urea bag price mein ho sakta hai. Aur agar woh nahi hua, to phir Engro Fertilizer ko bhi iska net nuksan hoga. Jo ke taqreeban after tax basis pe agar dekhein, to 10 rupaye per share ban jata hai.

Ab baat kar lete hain Pressure Enhancement Facility ki aur yeh ek project hai ke company ko aur poori industry ko jo hai, sahi tareeqe se sahi supply mein gas supply jo hai milti rahe from Habib Rahi reservoir jo ke Mari Energy ke andar aata hai. Kyunki yeh reservoir bahut purana ho gaya hai. Aur yeh jo Pressure Enhancement Facility hai, yeh first quarter of 2027 mein jo hai complete ho jayegi. Aur Pressure Enhancement Facility ki long-term implications, maine yeh trend dekha hai jab bhi yeh fertilizer companies koi badi investment karti hain to Government of Pakistan se jaake kehti hain ke ab hum yeh investment karne ja rahe hain to iske against aap humein 50 rupaye, 100 rupaye par bag urea ki price jo hai woh increase karne dein, taake hum apni investment jo hai, uspe ek decent return jo hai kama sakein.
