2022 Pakistan ki economy default ke kareeb. Do investors, Talha aur Hamza. Dono ke paas hain 50 lakh rupay PSX mein. Talha ghabra gaya. Roz news dekh raha tha, roz portfolio red. Usne kaha bas, aur nuksaan ab bardasht nahi hota. Talha ne sab bech diya aur paisa transfer kar diya savings account mein. Sakoon-dar zindagi, no tension. Hamza ne kuch different kiya. Usne kya kiya ke apni trading app sirf band kar di aur usne bharosa kiya Pakistan ke top businessmen pe aur khud apne kaam pe focus karne lag gaya agle kuch saalo ke liye. Aur aaj, 2026, Talha ki sakoon-dar zindagi mein wealth barh kar 87 lakh ho gayi hai jo ke uske savings account ne usko return diye. Aur aaj Hamza ke paas hain 2 crore 25 lakh se bhi zyada. Ab batayen kis ki zindagi zyada sakoon-dar hai? Dono mein farq sirf sabar ka tha. Market girti hai aur hamesha market waapis bhi aati hai. Market kabhi nahi haarti, bechne wala insan hi hota hai jo haar maan leta hai. Ye reel save kar lein, agli dafa jab market gire to dobara dekhiyega. Aur agar aap chahte hain ke hamari team aapko reach out kare to hamari website pe jayein, financial freedom pe click karein aur apni details share karein aur hum aapko reach out karenge.
