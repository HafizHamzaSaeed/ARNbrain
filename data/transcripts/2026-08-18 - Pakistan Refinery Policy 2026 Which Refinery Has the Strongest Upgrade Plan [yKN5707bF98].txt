Asal mein refinery sector ek import substitution sector hai. Abhi Pakistan mein bohat zyada petrol jo hai wo import hota hai directly. Hum ye bhi kar sakte hain ke agar humari refineries jo hain wo top quality ki hon, jo latest technology hai us ki hon to phir humein sirf crude oil mangwana paray, to jo petrol, diesel, aur crude oil ki price ke beech mein jo difference hai, us amount se humara jo import bill hai wo kam ho sakta hai. Aur aap ko pata hoga aaj kal jaise ye crude oil, diesel, aur petrol ki bohat zyada shortage chal rahi hai to ye jo gap hota hai between crude oil price aur diesel, ye to ek time pe $60 tak bhi chala gaya tha. To ek estimate ke hisab se ye jo poori refinery policy approve hui hai, jab ye new refineries upgrade ho jayengi to Pakistan ki economy ke liye taqreeban $1.5 billion ki import substitution hogi on normalized difference between crude oil aur petrol aur diesel. Is ke sath sath ye bhi hoga ke abhi jo humari refineries hain, kuch Euro 2 compliant fuel banati hain, kuch Euro 3 to ye sab Euro 5 compliant fuel jo hai wo banane lag jayengi. Us se Pakistan mein pollution ka jo problem hai wo bhi solve hoga aur aap ne dekha hoga Lahore, Karachi mein jitni zyada smog ho jati hai, wo bhi bohat hadd tak us mein bhi help hogi. Aur is sab ko karne ke liye jo hai, $5 billion ki investment hogi from all the refineries aur is cheez ke kaafi zyada chances lag rahe hain ke ye mostly jo investment hogi, ye bahar se FDI hogi. To ek tarah se aap ke jo reserves hain us pe bhi bohat kam pressure aayega. Teen se paanch saal lagenge refineries upgrade hone mein, aur mein teen se paanch saal isliye keh raha hoon kyunki ye jo policy approve hui hai, uske andar incentive diya gaya hai ke agar aap teen saal mein apni refinery ko upgrade kar lenge, teiz teiz upgradation kar lenge to aap ka jo incentive hai wo increase ho jayega. Aur agle kuch hafton mein hi signing expected hai. Unofficially sab refineries jo hain wo yahi kehti hain ke hum log bohat jald is refinery policy pe upgrade karne ke liye razamand ho jayenge aur official signing bhi bohat jald expected hai. Pichli policy ke muqable mein jis mein thoda sa problem aa gaya tha, first major change ye hai jo budget mein aaya tha ke ye jo poori $5 billion ki ek tarah se investment hogi, is ki jab machinery bahar se import hogi to us pe sales tax jo hai wo budget mein maaf kar diya gaya tha. To is wajah se project ki jo economics hain wo improve ho jati hain. Phir is ke sath sath kuch clauses jo hain is policy mein add kiye gaye hain jo ke tightness of the policy ko improve karte hain, both from the point of view of government and the refineries. Refineries ko bhi zyada certainty di gayi hai ke future mein ja kar aap upgradation karenge do teen saal baad kuch hum change nahi kar denge aur same for the refineries as well. Aur jo chaar major point hain jin pe aap ko focus karna chahiye, jis se is ki poori economics aap derive karenge ke kaun si refinery acchi hai aur kahan se in refinery ke future ke jo profits hain after upgradation wo improve ho sakte hain, wo hain: First ke abhi jo furnace oil banta hai us ki percentage kam ho jayegi aur diesel aur petrol ki percentage jo hai wo increase ho jayegi. Jaise aap ko pata hoga furnace oil agar crude oil taqreeban $80 ka chal raha hota hai to furnace oil ki price jo hai $55 to $60 hoti hai. Aur jo diesel hota hai, wo koi 100, 110 ke aas paas chal raha hota hai. To ye jab shift aayega furnace oil ki production kam hogi, diesel, petrol ki zyada hogi to profits increase honge. Phir jaise mein ne bataya, Euro 5 compliant fuels jab produce honge to abhi jo refineries penalty deti hain jo Euro 5 compliant fuels nahi bana rahin, wo bhi nahi deni padegi. Aur phir aaj ke hisab se apni production ko optimize karne ke liye refineries bohat zyada chemicals use karti hain. Lekin jab technology hi acchi ho jayegi, jis mein zyada petrol aur diesel produce ho raha hoga to in chemicals ki consumption bhi kam ho jayegi, wahan se bhi saving hogi. Aur lastly and very important, abhi ek average refinery jo hai Pakistan mein taqreeban 50-60% utilization pe chal rahi hai. Jab in ki back-end technology acchi ho jayegi aur furnace oil ki production kam ho jayegi to bohat zyada chances hain ke ye 90, 95 even 100% pe operate karengi.

Ab aa jate hain ke is poori news ke hisab se is ko investing mein kaise translate karna hai. To dosto, ye to hai momentum investing. Kyunki jo actual refinery hai, wo to chaar se paanch saal baad aani hai. Aur aap ye nahi kar sakte ke chaar se paanch saal baad ki earnings jo hain wo calculate karen aur us ke hisab se us ki backward working karen, aaj ki fair value nikalen. Is poore arsay mein jo hai, bohat saari cheezain change bhi ho sakti hain. To mein aap ko ek behtar tareeqa bataunga momentum investing ka, ke kaise aap is cheez ko jo hai, numbers mein convert kar sakte hain. Aur is momentum investing karne ke liye, step number one hai: Calculate the ROIC of the investment. Yaani ke ye jo upgradation hone ja rahi hai, us ka aap estimate lagayen ke is upgradation se overall profits kitne increase honge aur us investment ka ROIC kya hai? Aur phir aap is ko aise samajhye ke aap ka ek dost hai, wo aap ko aa ke ek bohat zyada accha business idea batata hai jis se bohat acche profits expected hain. To jitni acchi future profitability hoti hai to phir us business mein participate karne ki aaj ki price jo hai, wo ek tarah se us ki option value hoti hai. For example, agar ek refinery let's say $500 million ki investment karne ja rahi hai, aur us ka ROIC jo hai wo 30% ke aas paas aa raha hai. To aap kahenge itna accha business hai, thode saalon mein online bhi aa jayega to agar is ki investment $500 million hai, to aap sochenge ke ye itna accha business hai, aur future mein investment karne ja raha hai, aur by the way, is investment mein 27% jo hai government ka hissa bhi hoga. Ye bhi refinery policy ka ek incentive hai. Yaani ke agar ek refinery ne $1 billion ki investment karni hai, to us mein se $270 million before tax jo hai government of Pakistan, through deemed duty on diesel aur petrol, jo hai wo collect kar ke degi. To aap kya karenge, ke agar koi ek aisa business hai jis ke andar $500 million ki investment hone ja rahi hai, aur future accha lag raha hai, to phir aap us business mein participate karne ke liye aaj agar us ki price $150 million, $200 million bhi chal rahi hogi, to aap us mein participate kar lenge. Kyunki jo future investment hogi, aap us ke hissedar ban jayenge. Aur ye already aap ne dekha hoga, PSX pe already. Jaise koi choti company, koi bohat bada project announce karti hai to phir wo jo ek future value of the project hoti hai, wo thodi thodi price mein inbuilt hone lag jati hai. Lekin yahan aap ne careful bhi rehna hai. Ye nahi karna ke ek refinery jo hai $500 million ka project karne ja rahi hai aur aap us ko jo hai $500 million ki existing valuation pe buy kar rahe hain. Kyunki ye jo $500 million is ne invest karne hain, ye is ko kahin na kahin se debt, ya rights issue, ya doosre tareeqay se arrange bhi karne hain. To jitna zyada accha is investment ka ROIC hoga, utna zyada log jo hai, us ko thodi si upar wali price pe bhi buy karne ka sochenge. Aur yaad rakhen, chaar sources of savings hain: First ye ke furnace oil ki production kam hogi, petrol aur diesel ki increase hogi, chemicals kam consume honge, jo penalties hoti hain Euro 5 non-compliance ki, wo jo hai eliminate ho jayenge, aur fourth benefit ke aap ki production bhi increase ho jayegi. To in saari cheezon ko dekhte hue, aap apni screen pe ab dekh sakte hain ke pre and post production, jo saari refineries ne apne plans share kiye hain ke aaj hum itna diesel banate hain, aaj hum itna petrol banate hain, itna furnace oil banate hain, aur hum jo new technology lekar aa rahe hain, uske baad humari jo production hai wo kis tareeqay se change ho jayegi, wo aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain. Dosto, ab aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain, wo four sources of savings aur is ko hum ne National Refinery pe jo hai apply kiya hai. Ek-ek kar ke mein aap ko saari explain kar deta hoon. First: Conversion from furnace oil to petrol diesel. So National Refinery ke case mein, zyada tar jo change aa raha hai, wo diesel mein thoda sa hai aur mostly petrol mein aa raha hai. Taqreeban 15% percentage jo hai wo furnace oil ki neeche ja rahi hai aur 12-13% petrol ki upar ja rahi hai. To is wajah se jo difference aayega us ko aap aise calculate kar sakte hain, ke jo petrol hai, taqreeban us pe jo margin hota hai above crude oil, wo koi $12 to $15 hota hai. Aur jo furnace oil hota hai, wo to -$25, -$30 pe bikta hai. To jo price ka difference hai dono ke beech mein, wo hai $40 per barrel. To aap ne kya kiya? $40 per barrel ko 15% se multiply kiya, phir jo existing production thi, 30,000 barrels per day, us se multiply kiya, aur saal mein jo 330 days hote hain, us se multiply kiya. To aap ke pass taqreeban $60 million ka number aa gaya. Yaani ke upgradation se jo furnace oil kam ho raha hai, aur jo petrol aur thoda sa diesel zyada ban raha hai, us se jo change in revenue ya change in profit hai, wo $60 million ka ho gaya. Phir aa gayi Euro 5 penalty. To is ko aap check kar sakte hain ke aap company ki annual report ya jo nau mahine ki report aa gayi hai us ko khol ke dekhen, aur revenue wale section mein ja kar aap dekh sakte hain ke in nau mahino ke andar is company ne Euro 5 ki aur RON 92 ki jo penalty hai, wo kitni pay ki hai. Us ko aap poore year pe adjust kar den, aur ye saving aap ke pass aa jayegi ke is category se kitni saving hogi upgradation ki wajah se. Aur phir dosto aa jate hain chemicals pe. Same cheez aap ne karni hai. Annual report ya quarterly report mein aap ne cost of sales mein ja ke dekhna hai, ke chemicals mein kitna kharcha ho raha hai. Aur jo refineries guide karti hain ke upgradation ke baad is ki cost jo hai, yaani ke jo chemicals ka use hai wo taqreeban aadha required hoga. Phir sabse important cheez: More production. National Refinery jo hai almost 55,000-60,000 barrels per day ki refinery hai. Lekin kyunki furnace oil zyada banta hai us ki wajah se loss hota hai, is wajah se is ko kam levels pe chalaya jata hai. To jab ye problem solve ho jayegi, to phir production bhi jo hai 50,000 barrels per day tak easily ja sakti hai. To pehle aap 30,000 ke hisab se calculation kar rahe the, ab aap 50,000 ke hisab se calculation karenge to saari jo savings hain, wo 60% se increase ho jayengi. Lekin yahan pe conservative rehte hue, mein ne 50% increase jo hai, wo assume kiya hai. Yaani ke hum 30,000 ke bajaye 45,000 barrels per day pe operate karenge. To in saari savings ko agar hum accumulate karen, to ye ban jati hain taqreeban $115 to $125 million. Aur jo investment required hai in this upgradation, wo waise to $600 million hai. Lekin us mein se 20 to 22% jo hai government of Pakistan ne bhi put in karna hai incentive ke taur pe. To jo company ki taraf se investment hogi, wo hogi taqreeban $480 to $500 million. Aur dollar terms mein ek attractive 25% ke aas paas ka ROIC ban jata hai.

To ye ek aisi policy hai, jo company ke liye bhi acchi hai, Pakistan ki economy ke liye bhi acchi hai, humare climate ke liye bhi acchi hai. Isi wajah se is jung ke time pe, jab ye refinery margins bohat zyada increase hue, aur ek shortage build hui, aur petrol, diesel khareedna jo hai, government of Pakistan ke liye, logo ko provide karne ke liye, bohat zyada mushkil ho gaya, to refineries ki jo upgradation policy hai, wo finally approve ho gayi. Ab mein aap ko ek bohat hi interesting baat batata hoon, jo aap ne second-order analysis jis ko kahenge. Sab ye dekh rahe hain ke is refinery ka itna furnace oil hai, baad mein itna ho jayega, to jo sabse zyada reduction aa raha hai furnace oil mein, us ko sabse zyada fayda hoga. Lekin mein aap ko bataun, ke agar chaar mein se do refineries bhi apna furnace oil khatam kar den, to baqi jo do refineries hongi jo thoda bohat furnace oil produce kar rahi hongi, un ke liye bhi ye ek beneficial development hai. Thoda sa mujhe lag raha hai, aap confuse ho gaye hain, mein aap ko aur aasan lafzon mein explain karta hoon. Let's say, aaj jo hai hum log 20,000 barrels per day furnace oil produce karte hain saari refineries mein. Aur jo local demand hai, wo hai koi 7,000-8,000. To yaqeenan sab ko jo hai export karna padta hai. Aur export pe losses zyada hote hain, transport ka kharcha bhi hota hai, price bhi kam milti hai. Lekin agar chaar mein se do refineries ne apna furnace oil jo hai wo khatam kar diya, ya bohat kam kar diya, aur supply jo hai wo 20,000 se gir ke 11,000 pe aa gayi, aur demand jo hai 8,000-9,000 ki hai, to supply reduction ki wajah se, jo refineries hain jo thoda bohat furnace oil bana bhi rahi hain, un ke liye bhi bechna aasan ho jayega. Kyunki local market mein furnace oil ko behtar price mil jati hai. To -$30, -$25 per barrel loss ke bajaye, shayad aap ka sirf $10 per barrel loss ho. Aur is development ke hone mein, ek rukaawat ye bhi hai, jo Pakistan ne jab climate facility sign ki thi, to furnace oil pe ek levy laga di thi. Aur climate facility jo hai ek, dedh saal mein jo hai expire hone wali hai. Aur agar aap recent petroleum minister ke interviews sunen, to wo aap ko batayenge ke wo IMF ko bohat zyada explain kar rahe hain ke jo furnace oil hai, wo ek traveling fuel nahi hai. Yaani ke wo gaadiyon mein aur bikes mein istemal nahi hota, wo balkay industry mein istemal hota hai. Ye ek industrial fuel hai. To is pe aap jo levy laga rahe hain, wo petrol, diesel ke bajaye, jo industrial levy hoti hai, wo lagayen. To agar agle kuch saalon mein ye levy jo hai furnace oil pe kam ho jati hai, aur chaar mein se do refineries jo hain, wo deep conversion ya jis mein furnace oil bohat hi zyada kam ho jata hai, us pe nahi bhi jatin, tab bhi jo hai, un ka furnace oil jo hai, utni zyada loss pe nahi bikega. To ye tha wo important point, dosto. Kyunki mein refinery policy ko teen, chaar saal se follow karta hua aa raha hoon, to mein ne kaha aap se share karta hoon.
