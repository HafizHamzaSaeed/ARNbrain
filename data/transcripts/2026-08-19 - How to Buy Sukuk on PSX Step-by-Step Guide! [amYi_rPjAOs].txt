Isi judgment ko seconding karte hue Constitution of Pakistan mein bhi amendment kar di gayi. Khair yeh Government of Pakistan Ijarah Sukuks mein investment ke liye aapko multiple options mil jati hain. Ek to aap jo hai woh different time periods ke hisab se ismein investment kar sakte hain, jis mein pehle 1 saal, 3 saal aur 5 saal ki options available thin, lekin ab recently ismein short-term yaani 3 mahine aur 6 mahine ki options ko bhi add kar diya gaya hai aur is par government ko boht accha response dekhne ko mila investors ki taraf se.

Lekin in different time periods ke ilawa, ek aur cheez jo aapko dekhni hoti hai Sukuk khareedne se pehle, woh yeh hai ke ismein aapka profit kis tarah se hoga. Ismein bhi aapke paas multiple options aa jati hain, jaise ke ek hai discounted Sukuk, jis mein aap jo instrument ya jo Sukuk khareedte hain, woh aapko ek discounted price par milta hai aur time pura hone ke baad jab aap ise bechte hain to yeh apni actual value par bik jata hai, to yeh jo price ka difference hota hai, yeh aapka profit hota hai.

Misaal ke tor par, 100 rupaye ka Sukuk aapko 97 rupaye mein mil gaya aur 3 mahine baad ya 6 mahine baad jab bhi uska maturity time pura hua to aapne woh 97 rupaye mein khareeda hua Sukuk 100 rupaye mein bech diya apni actual value par, to is tarah se yeh jo price ka difference tha, 3 rupaye, woh aapka profit ho gaya.

Phir aata hai aapke paas fixed rental rate Sukuk. Ismein aapko ek fixed rate ke mutabiq profit milta hai saal mein do dafa, har 6 mahine baad. Misaal ke tor par, aapne 3 saal ki duration wala 100 rupaye ka Sukuk khareeda 12% ke fixed rental rate par, to aapko 3 saal tak har 6 mahine baad 6% ka profit milta rahega. Saal mein do dafa 6% ka profit yaani saal ka 12% profit aur 3 saal baad aapko aapki principal amount, yaani aapke jo 100 rupaye aapne 3 saal pehle invest kiye the, woh wapas mil jayenge.

Aur bilkul isi tarah ek variable rental rate wala instrument bhi hota hai, jis mein aapko har 6 mahine baad milne wala profit jo hai woh tabdeel hota rehta hai. Yeh adjustment jo hai woh latest T-bills ki weighted average ke mutabiq hoti hai. Ek khas tareeqa hota hai ise nikalne ka, lekin again ismein koi boht zyada difference nahi aata is rate mein, jo interest rate chal raha hota hai, uske kareeb-kareeb hi hota hai yeh rate bhi. To is tarah se aapke paas yeh multiple options aa jati hain investment ke liye. Aap apne hisab se, jo aapko suit karta hai, usmein invest kar sakte hain.
