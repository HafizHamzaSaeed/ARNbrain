To dosto, jaise main aapko batate rehta hoon, cement industry ko agar owners ki tarah samajhna ho to teen cheezein bohat important hain. Coal, bijli, aur location. Kyunki dosto, cement ek commodity business hai. Aap selling price ko hamesha control nahi kar sakte lekin cost of production ko aap bilkul control kar sakte hain. Aur Fauji Cement in teeno factors mein: coal, bijli, aur location mein kaafi strong position rakhta hai. Aaye, ek ek karke main aapko explain karta hoon. Pehla factor hai coal. Cement banane mein coal sabse badi production cost hoti hai. Fauji Cement apni zaroorat ka taqreeban 50 se 60% local coal se poora karta hai. Aur local coal doosre coals ke options, jaise ke imported coal ya phir Afghan coal, unse relatively sasta hota hai. Dosto, ek ton coal se taqreeban 7 ton of cement ban jata hai. Aur agar hum dekhein, ek average company ka profit before tax per ton of cement taqreeban agar 4000 rupay chal raha hai, to agar local coal, imported coal se 3-4 hazar rupay bhi sasta ho, to ye profit before tax per ton 4000 se badh ke saadhay 4 hazar tak ho sakta hai.

Phir dosto, doosra factor important hota hai bijli, aur yahan Fauji Cement ke paas cement sector ka genuinely impressive portfolio hai. 64 Megawatt waste heat recovery aur 54 Megawatt already installed solar. To yahan to aap dekh rahe hain ke already bijli ki cost kaafi kam hai Fauji Cement ki. Ab chalte hain teesre factor ki taraf, aur mere hisab se ye sabse important hai: Location. Fauji Cement ke zyada tar plants north region mein hain. Jhang mein 3.5 million tons ki capacity, Nizampur aur Wah Cantt mein mila ke saadhay 4 million ton ki capacity, aur abhi recently kuch saal pehle hi DG Khan mein 2 million tons ki capacity. Nizampur ke plants KPK mein aate hain. Aur aapko pata hai ke KPK mein limestone royalty Punjab ke muqable mein considerably kam hai. Aur kyunki limestone cement ka buniyaadi raw material hai, to royalty ka seedha asar production cost par padta hai. Aur dosto, location ke sath ek aur cheez bhi important hai: plant ki age aur technology. Jitna naya plant hota hai, jitni acchi technology hoti hai, utni kam cost of production hoti hai. To Jhang wala plant, Fauji Cement ka full European technology par based hai. Jabke jo DG Khan ki brand new line hai jo ke 2022 aur 2023 mein hi online aayi thi, ye bhi kaafi zyada efficient hai. Aur jaise maine aapko bataya ke jitni acchi technology, jitna naya plant, utni lower cost of production, aur utne zyada profits.

In tamam advantages ko agar hum combine karein, to phir samajh aata hai ke kyun Fauji Cement ke operating margins aam taur par approximately 30% ki range mein rehte hain, jo ke industry ki behtar companies mein shumar hota hai. Aur isi operational efficiency ko dekhte hue, hum Fauji Cement ki approximately 10 million tons ki jo cement ki capacity hai, usko $65 per ton ki valuation assign kar sakte hain. Yaani roughly 650 million dollar ki replacement cost based on efficiency of the plant and earning power. Iske sath sath, company ke paas Attock Cement ke recently acquired approximately 63 million shares bhi maujood hain. Jo ke ab recent result mein company ne bataya hai ke wo in dono companies ka merger karne ka bhi soch rahe hain. Aur in shares ki current market value roughly 14 se 15 billion banti hai. To Fauji Cement ke cement operations ki value, plus Attock Cement ki investment, borrowings aur current assets ko agar hum adjust kar lein, to phir fair valuation based on replacement cost aap apni screen pe dekh sakte hain.

Ab dosto, baat karte hain Fauji Cement ki FY27 ki expected earnings ki. Cement demand aur company ki available jo capacity hai usko agar dekhein to FY27 mein hum Fauji Cement ki sales approximately 6.5 se le ke 6.7 million tons assume kar sakte hain. In volumes ki buniyaad par total revenue roughly 110, 115 billion ke kareeb ban sakta hai. Operating margins jismein kaafi zyada kharche jo hain wo nikal jaate hain, wo hum 27 se 28% ki range mein assume kar rahe hain. Ye pichle levels se thoda sa conservative estimate hai, aur jaise ke aapko pata hai ke value investors hamesha thoda sa conservative hi rehna chahiye. In assumptions par Fauji Cement ka operating profit approximately 29 se 30 billion ban sakta hai. Phir Rs 5 to 6 billion ki finance cost, taxation aur baqi expenses nikalne ke baad FY27 ka net profit estimate hamare hisab se 14 se 15 billion rupees hai, yaani ke agar isko per share mein convert karein, to ye ban jata hai 5 to 6 rupees. Aur Fauji Cement ke paas Attock Cement ka 46% stake bhi hai, to is 46% stake ka jo profit Fauji Cement ki earnings mein reflect hoga, ye bhi roughly 800 million se 1 billion rupees ban sakta hai. Yaani dono ko agar combine kar lein, to FY27 mein Fauji Cement ki total earnings approximately 6 to 7 rupees per share reh sakti hain.

Ab earnings aa gayi hain, sawal ye hai ke market in earnings ko kitna multiple de sakti hai? Is mein main aap se apne thoughts thode se share karna chahta hoon. Dosto, Pakistan ka cement sector, is waqt doosre jo cyclical manufacturing sectors hain unke muqable mein relatively attractive nazar aa raha hai. Aur uski wajah hai ke IMF ka duty rationalization program ke baad, kai manufacturing sectors jo imported products se zyada competition ka samna kar rahe hain aur aapko pata hai ke is plan ke andar duties jo hain wo kam ho rahi hain, to un pe ek negative outlook aaj se do teen saal baad jo hai wo ubhar rahi hai. Cement sector mein situation thodi si different hai. Cement ek heavy aur low value per ton product hai, aur transportation cost kaafi high hoti hai. Is element ko main kehta hoon: "Cement cannot travel by road." Isi wajah se agar is IMF program ke andar cement pe let's say duty khatam bhi ho jati hai, to bhi specially jo north wale players hain, imported cement jab tak port se Punjab aur KPK tak pahuchega, to usko trucks ko itna zyada kiraya dena pad jayega ke wo local cement se compete hi nahi kar payega. Yaani ke jo north ke cement ke plants hain wo naturally import se protected hain. Isi context mein jab investors ne economic growth dekh kar cyclical sectors mein investment karni hoti hai, to ek to waise hi cement sector jo hai uski liquidity, uska size kaafi accha hai, logon ka is pe bharosa bhi kaafi accha hai, iske long-term returns bhi achhe hain, aur phir is factor ko bhi mila lein, to mere hisab se hum Fauji Cement ya phir cement sector ki doosri top companies ko 9 to 10 ka P/E multiple assign kar sakte hain. Aur 6 to 7 rupees EPS pe agar hum ye P/E ratio apply karein, to mere hisab se Fauji Cement ki fair value jo hai wo koi taqreeban 60 se 70 rupees ke beech mein ban sakti hai.

Ab dosto, aate hain us balance sheet line par, jiska maine aapko bataya tha ke bohat important hai. Aur maine kaha tha zyada tar investors isko miss kar rahe hain. Fauji Cement ki fair valuation mein ek important factor hai Fauji Foundation se liya gaya Rs 7.3 billion ka long-term loan. Ye wo loan hai jo Askari Cement ne apne Nizampur plant ki expansion ke liye liya tha, aur merger ke baad 2023 mein Fauji Cement ki balance sheet ka hissa ban gaya. Agar aap thode saal pehle jayein ge, 2022, 23 ya 21 mein, to Fauji Cement pe debt kaafi zyada hoti thi. Aur ye expansion bhi kar rahe the. To is wajah se sponsor se ye loan liya gaya tha. Ab interesting cheez ye hai ke sponsors ke paas ise kisi bhi waqt equity mein convert karne ka option maujood hai, at least annual statement ki wording ye indicate karti hai. Yaani is amount ke badle apne naam par naye shares issue karwa sakte hain. Waise to ab Fauji Cement ki debt position jo hai kaafi acchi ho gayi hai, lekin company ne conversion ki detailed terms and conditions jo hain wo disclose nahi ki hui. Isliye ab hum certainty se nahi keh sakte ke ye conversion hogi bhi ke nahi, aur agar hogi to kis price par hogi? Lekin ek value investor ke taur par isko monitor karna zaroori hai. Kyunki dosto, agar future mein conversion prevailing market price jo chal rahi hogi us se significant discount par ho gayi, to existing shareholders ke liye value dilute ho sakti hai. Isi wajah se dosto, mere liye ye Rs 7.3 billion sirf balance sheet par padi hui ek line nahi hai. Ye wo development hai jisko Fauji Cement shareholders ko agle results aur annual reports mein closely monitor karna chahiye. Balki main apni taraf ko koshish bhi karunga ke ab jab company ki next AGM hogi to us pe iske baare mein maseed details hasil karoon.

Jis tareeqe ki conflict Iran aur US ke beech mein chal rahi hai, aur har din hum sun rahe hote hain ke Middle East ka ye LNG plant jo hai ye sahi tareeqe se operate nahi kar raha, phir Strait of Hormuz se jo hai ships guzarne mein difficulty ho rahi hai. Aur aapko pata hai ke duniya mein jo zyada energy ki demand hoti hai, wo Pakistan mein to garmiyon mein hoti hai lekin baqi poori duniya mein kyunki sardi zyada hoti hai wo sardiyon mein hoti hai, heating ke liye. Aur LNG prices jo hain already mere hisab se $25 se upar gayi hui hain spot market mein. To agar ye poora crisis jo hai agle ek-do mahino mein solve nahi hota, aur kisi wajah se ek global energy shortage create ho jati hai, to phir ho sakta hai bohat zyada mulk jo hain wo mehenga coal prefer karein compared to LNG. Aur obviously energy to provide karni hoti hai local population ko, to us poori cycle ke andar coal prices kahin out of control na ho jayein. Aur last time jab ye super cycle 2021 ki sardiyon mein aaya tha, to us waqt Pakistan ki jo life line thi cement sector thi wo tha Afghan coal.
