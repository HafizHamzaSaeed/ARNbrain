Chaar aisi cheezein jo aapka stock broker aapko kabhi bhi nahi bataye ga jab wo aapko stock tip de raha hoga, aur jo chauthi hai, wo game changer hai.

First, business cycle. Aap jis company mein invest karne ja rahe hain, aapne sabse pehle dekhna hai ke agle kuch saalon mein uski sales aur profits increase ho rahi hain ya niche ja rahi hain. Aur is cheez ko samajhne ke liye aap kya karenge? Aap pichle teen saalon ki financial statements padhenge. Aap dekhenge profit increase ho raha tha ya decrease ho raha tha, sales upar ja rahi thin ya sales niche ja rahi thin, yani ke company growth trajectory pe hai ya decline pe hai. Is particular step pe main khud ye karta hoon ke main dekhta hoon ke company ka product kya hai, aur wo jo main raw material use karta hai, un dono cheezon ki jo prices hoti hain, uska trend zaroor study karta hoon. Jaise ke cement sector mein cement bag ki price aur coal ki price. Kyunki un dono ka jo difference hota hai, wahi profit hota hai end mein.

Phir, third step, Evaluate the quality of the management. Isme aap long term track record dekhein, 10 saal ka, 15 saal ka, ke unhone aapko kya kama ke diya hai.

Aur fourth, sabse important, portfolio allocation. Jo new investors hain, aapne to ek company mein apne portfolio ka 10% se zyada kabhi daalna hi nahi hai. Aap kitni bhi acchi company dhoond lein, lekin agar aap usmein ghalat percentage invest kar denge to agar usme koi volatility hui to acchi company ko bhi aap ghalat price pe bech denge.

Aur agar aap chahte hain ke humari team aapko reach out kare, to humari website pe jayein, financial freedom pe click karein, aur apni details share karein, aur hum aapko reach out karenge.
