Aaj kal ki one of the top dividend paying companies Hubco ne apna result announce kar diya. Aur bohat zyada jo hai uncertainty bhi chal rahi thi is company se related, kuch reports bhi aayi thi, log bhi ghabraye hue thay. Lekin is result ki sabse acchi baat ye hai ke Hubco ne apna 5 rupay ka jo dividend hai quarterly usko maintain kiya hai. Ab agar Hubco ke businesses ki baat kar lein, toh wo mostly jaise IPPs aur BYD toh abhi aahista aahista scale ho raha hai, toh IPPs mein capacity payment hoti hai toh wo toh more or less fixed hi hoti hai. But ye achi cheez hai ke pichle ek do saalon se jaise power sector ki circular debt jo hai wo controlled hui hai, toh jo cash profits hote hain, wo Hubco ke kaafi zyada behtar ho gaye hain. Isi wajah se company pichle ek deidh saal se 5 rupay ka quarter ka dividend jo hai wo announce kar rahi hai. Aur yahi sabse important cheez hoti hai is company ke liye. Aage jaate hue is company mein aapne phir dekhna hai ke BYD ki gaadiyan aapko sadkon pe kitni zyada nazar aati hain, uske hisaab se ye share phir apne profits ko increase kar sakta hai.
