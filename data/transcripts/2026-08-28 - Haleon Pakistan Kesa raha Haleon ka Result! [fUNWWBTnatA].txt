Panadol Pakistan ki number one selling medicine banane wali company Haleon ne apne pichle teen mahine ka profit announce kar diya. Aur pichle teen mahino mein Haleon ne Panadol aur CAC bhi inki ek badi mashoor medicine hai, inko bech ke dedh arab se zyada ka bhi profit jo hai wo bana liya. Aur apne shareholders ke liye das rupay per share ka half-yearly dividend bhi announce kiya hai. To aap dekhein ke ek aisi cheez jo hum sab apni zindagi mein shayad roz ya har hafte apne ghar mein istemal karte hain, uski company kitne acche profits bana rahi hai. Expert insights ki baat kar lein to is quarter mein company ka jo distribution expense hai wo increase hua hai. Aur most probably ye jo naye versions aaye hain Panadol ki, unki promotion ke liye, unki market create karne ke liye company ne ye additional expense kiya hai. Ab aapne jo cheez dekhni hai agle result mein wo ye ke Haleon ke andar Panadol banane mein jo chemical istemal hota hai, paracetamol, wo oil prices se bahut zyada linked hota hai. To agar oil prices increase rehti hain to ho sakta hai agle quarter mein inki cost of sale jo hai thodi si increase ho jaye aur profit thode se reduce ho jayein. To ye cheez aapne zaroor agle result mein gaur se dekhni hai taake aap soch samajh ke is company mein invest kar sakein.
