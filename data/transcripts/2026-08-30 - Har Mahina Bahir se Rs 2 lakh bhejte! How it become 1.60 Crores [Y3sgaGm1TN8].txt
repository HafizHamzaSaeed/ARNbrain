Main har mahine baahar mulk se do lakh rupay ghar bhejta hoon, remittance ke taur pe. Ek lakh kharche mein nikal jata hai aur baaki ek lakh jo hai na us se main bohot tang hoon. Asal mein meri family ki na financial literacy itni achi nahi hai. Kabhi kisi scheme mein, kabhi plot ki file mein, kabhi aisi jagah jiska aakhir mein kuch nahi banta, kahin na kahin zaya ho jate hain. Aur ab maine ek naya rule jo hai wo follow karne ka faisla kar liya hai. Ek lakh ghar ke kharcho ke liye bhejunga aur ek lakh jo hai Roshan Digital Account ke zariye seedha PSX mein invest karunga. RDA account ghar baithe khul bhi jata hai, Pakistan aane ki zaroorat bhi nahi hai aur mera plan hai ke saat saal tak har mahine ek lakh invest karta rahunga. 17% par jab compounding hogi, to portfolio saat saalo mein ek crore 60 lakh tak pahunch jayega aur us portfolio se mahine ka ek lakh ka dividend mujhe aana shuru ho jayega aur portfolio apni speed se grow hota rahega. Yaani saat saal baad ghar ka kharcha meri jeb se nahi chalega, balki mere portfolio ki passive income se chalega. Aur RDA mein jitni investment hai wo fully repatriable bhi hai, jab chahoon apni currency mein waapis mangwa loon. To dosto, wahi paisa jo abhi bhi ja raha hai lekin direction badalni hai, aur saat saalo mein financially free hona hai, Inshallah.
