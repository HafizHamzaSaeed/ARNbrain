Toh sabse pehle OGDC ke business ko numbers mein samajh lete hain. OGDC Pakistan ki sabse badi oil and gas exploration and production company hai. 50% crude oil, 30% natural gas akele OGDC produce karti hai. OGDC ki daily production roughly 37,000 barrels crude oil aur 650 MMCFD gas hai. Major fields ki baat karein toh Nashpa, Uch, Qadirpur, Tal block, ye sab shamil hain. Lekin dosto, ye numbers sirf scale batate hain is company ka. Asal sawal ye hai ke is business ka actual ROIC kitna attractive hai. Aaiye is business ka ROIC calculate karte hain. Oil and gas ka business exploration se start hota hai. Pehle company reserves dhoondne par investment karti hai. Discovery ho jaye toh uske baad drilling, salary aur well ko operate karne ki cost aati hai. Dosto, OGDC ne pichle do saalon mein 16 exploratory wells ki drilling par approximately 30 arab rupay ki investment ki, jisme se 10 discoveries huin, yaani ke success rate of 63%. Lekin ROIC calculate karne ke liye, kyunki hum value investors hain, hamesha conservative rehte hain, Pakistan ka long-term average exploration success rate 37% hai aur hum wahi use karenge. Toh dosto, jaise aapne dekha pichle do-teen saalon mein average ek well drill karne mein takreeban do arab rupay ki investment hoti thi aur har teesre well mein ek successful discovery ke jab chances hote hain toh ek successful well drill karne ki cost ho gayi che arab rupay. Ab dekhte hain ke ye che arab rupay ka successful well OGDC ke liye kitna business generate karta hai. OGDC ke approximately 900 wells hain lekin company ki almost 95% production jo hai wo top 100 active wells se hi aati hai. Toh in numbers ko agar hum simplify karein, toh ek active well average basis par 370 barrels oil din ka aur 6.5 MMCFD gas produce karta hai. Crude oil ki average price agar hum $80 le lein aur gas ki price $5 assume kar lein toh ek active well ka total revenue approximately ek saal ka char se saade char arab rupay ban jata hai aur OGDC ka net margin roughly 40% hai. Toh ek successful active well roughly dedh se paune do arab rupay annual profit generate kar sakta hai. Aur investment thi che arab rupay ki, toh ROIC ban gaya roughly 29 to 30%. Dosto, oil and gas exploration ka business globally by nature highly risky aur bahut zyada capital intensive hota hai. Lekin Pakistan mein ise high ROIC businesses mein main categorize kyun karta hoon, wo is wajah se ke Pakistan mein salaries aur operating costs jo hain wo abhi bhi dollar terms mein comparatively kaafi zyada kam hain. Aur iske sath-sath Pakistan ek oil and gas ka importer bhi hai toh isi wajah se jo oil and gas ki prices set hui hain petroleum policies mein wo dollar-linked hain aur international prices se linked bhi hain. Toh is wajah se mere hisab se ye ek high ROIC ban jata hai. Ab aap soch rahe honge ke agar OGDC ke successful wells ki economics itni attractive hai toh main toh sath-sath ye bhi sunta rehta hoon ke pichle das saalon mein OGDC ka share dollar terms mein grow nahi kar paya. Toh ye kaise hum explain karenge? Iski wajah sabse badi dosto hai circular debt. Pichle almost das saalon mein OGDC Sui companies ko gas produce karke sell toh karti thi, lekin uski cash payments company ko time pe milti hi nahi thi. 2013 mein OGDC ke trade debts, yaani ke jo aapne apne customer se paise lene hote hain, wo approximately 55 arab rupay the aur 2024 tak yehi trade debts badhkar kahan tak pahunch gaye, 635 arab rupay. Yaani 11 saalon mein OGDC ne total around ek hazaar arab rupay ka net profit kamaya, lekin us profit lekin us profit mein se 600 arab rupay almost company ko time par mile hi nahi. Ab sochiye koi company profit toh kama rahi hai books pe, lekin us profit ka bahut bada hissa cash ki form mein receive nahi ho raha, toh wo naye projects aur naye wells mein reinvest kaise karegi? Isi wajah se dosto, OGDC ki reinvestment capacity affect hui aur production bhi declining trend par aa gayi. 2012-13 ke time par Pakistan mein exploratory aur development wells ko agar mila lein, toh milakar takreeban ek saal mein 100 wells drill hote the lekin 2023-24 tak ye number gir gir ke circular debt ki wajah se 35 se 40 wells reh gaya aur market ne bhi is trend ko dekhte hue OGDC ko is problem ki wajah se disregard kar diya. Circular debt problem se pehle 2006-2007 mein agar hum jayein toh OGDC ka P/E ratio around 10 to 12 times hua karta tha, market se bhi premium pe trade karta tha kyunki ye ek intrinsically high ROIC business hai. Lekin ek waqt aisa bhi aaya jab circular debt jo hai wo peak pe thi aur ye company do-dhaai P/E ratio pe chal rahi thi. Lekin 2023-24 mein ek signature se ye problem reverse ho gayi. Gas sector mein IMF conditions ki wajah se circular debt ko control karne ke liye steps liye gaye aur pichle do se teen saalon mein OGDC ke cash profits improve hokar 100% ho gaye hain aur company ko apni kuch purani outstanding payments bhi receive hona shuru go gayi hain.
