Kis financial advisor ko fees deni hai aur kis ko bilkul nahi deni? Aaiye iska simple formula aapse share karta hoon. Aur jo third type of advisor hai, woh sabse best hai. Pehle samajh lete hain ke Pakistan Stock Exchange jo hai, uska long term ek saal ka profit takreeban 17 to 18% hai. To pehle hamare advisor sahab jo hain, woh humein record show kar rahe hain ke pichle paanch das saalon mein ye less than 15% profit de rahe hain. To inko to aapne bilkul fees nahi deni. Ye agar aapko free mein bhi advice dein, to aapne kehna hai, no thank you. Doosre hamare stock advisor hain, jinka pichle paanch das saal bees saal ka track record hai 17 to 18% ka. Yaani ke ye jo market mein return aa raha hai, uske aas paas hai. Inko phir bhi hum convenience ki 1% fees jo hai, de sakte hain kyunke ye aapko aapki journey mein help kar rahe hain aur aapka thoda sa time bhi bachta hai. To 1% fees ETFs ki ho gayi, ye more or less market ko track karte hain aur aapka kharcha bhi kam hota hai. Ab jo third type of advisor hai, ye sabse accha hai. Aur ye hai jo track record show karta hai of above 20%. Yaani ke ye teen chaar percent extra aapko kama ke de raha hai. To inko aap jitna extra return woh laa ke de rahe hain, uske hisab se fees de sakte hain. Yaani ke agar inka track record 25% ka hai, to shayad 3% bhi inko fees dena bura na ho kyunke ye aapko saat aath percent extra profit kama ke de rahe hain. Ab is category mein mutual fund bhi aa sakte hain lekin agar unka track record show karta ho, pandrah se bees saal tak unhon ne market ko outperform kiya hai. Aur ye aap MUFAP ki website pe check kar sakte hain. Ab agar koi aisa advisor hai jiska track record itna lamba nahi hai, woh market mein abhi aaya hi naya hai, to phir aap usko 50-50 rule se judge karenge. 50% aap uska ek se do saal ka track record check karenge, 50% weightage usko denge ke ye outperform kar raha hai ke nahi. Aur 50% aap weightage denge uske method ko, woh kitne acche tareeqe se aapko explain karta hai ke uski strategy kya hai. Agar us se bhi aapko conviction milti hai, to acche chances hain ke ek do saal bhi agar usne outperform kiya hai, to woh aage bhi kar sakta hai.
