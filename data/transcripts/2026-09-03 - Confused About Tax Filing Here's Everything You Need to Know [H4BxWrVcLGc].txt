Shafee Butt: السلام علیکم everyone. Mera naam hai Shafee Butt aur aaj ki video mein hum dekhenge ke kya aapko filer banna chahiye ya nahi. Agar banna chahiye toh kyun banna chahiye? Kya hain wo faide jo ek filer ko hasil hote hain aur ek non-filer ko nahi hote? Toh abhi sabse pehla sawal toh shayad aapka ye ho ke main toh already tax de raha hoon government ko. Jab dukan se bread khareedta hoon, toh uspe sales tax hota hai. Jab bijli ka bill ghar par aata hai, toh usme kai taxes hote hain. Jab bahar restaurant par khana khane jata hoon, toh tax deta hoon. Gaari mein petrol dhalwata hoon, toh tax deta hoon. Toh haan, aap theek keh rahe hain lekin ye saara hota hai indirect tax. Indirect tax kyun? Kyunki ye saara tax jo aap de rahe hote hain, ye government aap se directly nahi le rahi hoti balki aap jis se apni cheez khareed rahe hote hain, ye tax us se liya jata hai. Maslan, aapne bread khareedi toh tax toh usme shamil hai lekin ye tax hukumat bread bechne wale yani us company se legi jisne bread aapko bechi hai. Ye alag baat hai ke tax ye bhi aap hi denge kyunki company ne toh jo tax hai wo bread ki qeemat mein shamil kar liya hua hai. Lekin wo tax jo aap khud hukumat ko dete hain, wo hota hai direct tax aur ye tax aap apni income ke upar dete hain yani ye aam taur par source se hi kat jata hai. Ab aap chahe business kar rahe hon, kahin doctor hon ya engineer hon, ya kahin bhi job kar ke aap salary le rahe hon, jab aap apni income ke upar tax denge toh hum kahenge ke haan bhai isne tax diya hai aur ab aap filer kehlayenge. Waise ye filer ya non-filer wali jo term hai, ye hamare hi mulk mein hai. Baqi mulkon mein aapko ye option nahi milti ke aap chahein toh tax file karein ya chahein toh na karein. Bahar ke mulkon mein ye zaroori hota hai aur agar aap tax apna file na karein toh aapko saza ho sakti hai. Toh ye tax ki alag alag qismein toh humne discuss kar lein aur ye bhi dekh liya ke jo banda income tax file karta hai usko filer kehte hain aur jo nahi karta usko non-filer kehte hain. Lekin yahan par shayad aapke zehn mein ek sawal aa raha ho ke bhai main toh job karta hoon aur jo mujhe salary milti hai us par mera income tax already cut ho jata hai toh yani main toh phir filer hua. Nahi, aisa nahi hai. Tax toh ye wahi tha jo aapne jama karana tha, aapki taraf se aapke employer ne kara diya lekin aapne khud se FBR ko yani hukumat ko ye batana hota hai ke bhai ye dekho ye meri income thi, ye us par tax banta tha aur ye dekho mera tax certificate, jo mera tax hai wo pehle se aapke paas aa chuka hai. Jab aap ye kaam karenge, baqaida tax file kar ke hukumat ko apni saari details denge ke bhai ye mere assets hain, ye meri job ki income hai, ye mere doosre sources se income hai, ye saari details jab aap dete hain FBR ko, tab ja kar aap filer kehlate hain. Toh agar toh aapki income par already tax kat raha hai aur aap apna tax file nahi kar rahe toh aapka double nuqsan ho raha hai. Double nuqsan kaise? Bhai dekhein, ek toh aapka already tax kat raha hai, upar se jo faide ek filer ko hasil hote hain aapko wo bhi nahi ho rahe toh ye aapka double nuqsan hai kyunki hukumat ke record ke mutabiq toh aap non-filer hi hain. Toh agar toh aapne apna tax file kiya hua hai toh aapko pata hoga lekin phir bhi agar for instance aapko confusion ho rahi hai ke bhai pata nahi main filer hoon ya nahi, mera tax kat raha hai, employer jo hai wo kaat leta hai, maine pata nahi kuch kiya hai ya nahi, koi bhi confusion hai aapko, aap ye bahut aasani se Iris ke portal par ja kar check kar sakte hain ke aapka status jo hai wo Active Taxpayer List mein aa raha hai yani aap filer hain ya phir nahi hain, main aapko dikhata hoon. Achha ji, Google pe aake aapne likh dena hai "Iris" aur ye aapke samne Iris 2.0 aa jayega jo ke FBR ka web page hai, isko aapne khol lena hai toh ye portal aa jayega jahan se ja kar aap apna tax filing karte hain. Lekin aapne kyunki sirf ye check karna hai ke aapka naam Active Taxpayer List mein aa raha hai ya nahi toh aap neeche aake yahan par parameter mein CNIC select kar lein, registration number mein apna ID card number add kar dein aur date already selected hai yahan pe, ye jo number aa raha hai ye aapne captcha mein as it is add kar dena hai aur phir aapne verify pe click kar dena hai. Toh ye aapke samne saari details aa jangayen aur ye filing status mein aapko active show ho raha hai, jis ka matlab hai ke aapka naam jo hai wo Active Taxpayer List mein aa raha hai yani aap filer hain. Agar aap filer nahi hain toh yahan pe ye "No Record Exist" aa jayega, jis ka matlab hai ke aapka naam abhi Active Taxpayer List mein maujood nahi hai. Toh is tarah se aap bahut aasani se apna status jo hai wo check kar sakte hain. Ab hum ye dekh lete hain ke banda agar filer banta hai toh usko kya faide hasil hote hain jo ek non-filer ko nahi hote. Toh ye mere samne kaafi saari details aa gayi hain aur ye bahut saare taxes hain alag alag tarah ke, toh bahut zyada detail hai, hum jo main cheezein hain filhal unko dekh lete hain. Achha ji, sabse pehle let's say aapne jo hai wo bank mein savings account mein jo hai wo apna paisa rakhwaya hai, cash rakhwaya hai aur us pe aapko profit milta hai. Toh wo agar aap filer hain yani aapka naam Active Taxpayer List mein aa raha hai toh us profit ke upar aapka jo tax hai wo 20% katega lekin agar aap filer nahi hain toh ye tax double ho jayega, 40% tak chala jayega. Isi tarah aap agar jo hai wo debt securities mein yani t-bills ya bonds mein investment karte hain, us par bhi profit pe aapka jo hai wo 20% tax katega filers ke liye aur 40% katega non-filers ke liye, toh yahan bhi ye double ho gaya. Achha, isi tarah aap agar jo hai wo bank se paise nikalwate hain, cash withdraw karate hain toh 50,000 tak toh koi tax nahi hai lekin uske upar agar aap filer hain toh phir aap jitne marzi nikalwa lein aapke upar koi tax nahi katega lekin agar aap non-filer hain toh 50,000 se upar ki jo cash withdrawal hai uske upar 0.6% tax katega aapka. Phir agar aap gaari khareedne jate hain toh uski jab registration karayenge toh usme bhi bahut jo hai wo tax ka farq aa jata hai, ye alag alag slabs hain. Hum dekhte hain jaise 850cc tak ki jo gaari hai, uspe filer ke liye hai 0.5% tax jo bhi gaari ki value hai aur non-filer ke liye 3x ho jata hai, 1.5% of the value of car. Toh ye again ek massive difference ban sakta hai. Isi tarah 850 se leke 1000cc tak ki jo gaari hai usme 1% hai filer ke liye tax aur non-filer ke liye again 3x yani 3% ho jata hai. 1000 se leke 1300cc tak ki jo gaari hai usme filer ke liye 1.5% tax hai aur non-filer ke liye 4.5% of the value of car ye tax hai. 1300 se 1600cc tak ki jo gaari hai usme filer ke liye 2%, non-filer ke liye 6% jo bhi gaari ki value hai aur isi tarah ye aage aur kaafi zyada slabs hain toh ye dekhein aap ke kitna zyada jo hai wo tax mein difference aa jata hai. Isi tarah jab aapne gaari bechni hogi toh jo transfer ke rates hain usme bhi tax mein jo hain farq aa jata hai. 850 se leke 1000cc tak ki jo gaari hai usme filer ke liye hai 5000 rupees ka tax aur non-filer ke liye again 3x ho jata hai yani 15,000 ka tax aur again 1000 se 1300cc tak ki gaari, filer ke liye 7500 rupees ka tax hai, non-filer ke liye 22,500. 1300 se 1600 wali category mein filer ke liye 12,500 hai, non-filer ke liye 37,500. Toh ye again ek bahut bada jo hai wo tax mein difference aa jata hai. Phir let's say aapne koi property khareedni hai toh wahan bhi tax mein bahut zyada difference hai filer aur non-filer mein. Jo filer hain unke liye toh flat 1.25% ka tax hai jo bhi value hogi property ki. Jo non-filers hain unke liye kuch slabs banayi hui hain jaise ke 50 million tak ki jo property hai us pe tax hoga 10.5% aur 50 million se 100 million tak ki jo property hai us pe ye tax 14.5% ho jayega aur again 100 million se jo upar ki property hai us pe ye tax 18.5% tak chala jayega. Toh kahan filer ke liye 1.25% ka tax aur kahan non-filer ke liye 18.5% ka tax, ye bahut bada difference hai. Phir agar property bechni ho toh yahan bhi difference hai. Jo filer hai uske liye 2.75% ka tax hai aur non-filer ke liye 11.5% ka tax hai jo property ki value hogi. Achha, mobile aur internet jo hote hain iske jab aap load karate hain toh iske upar bhi 15% tax hota hai dono ke liye lekin again jo filer hai wo adjust kara sakta hota hai, ispe hum aage ja kar baat karte hain abhi. Agar aapka bijli ka bill 25,000 se upar aa jaye toh wahan bhi 7.5% ka tax lag jata hai, ye bhi again adjustable hota hai. Achha, jo international transaction aap karte hain let's say aap Netflix waghera khareedte hain ya koi aur bahar ki company mein aapne payment karni hai international transaction toh filer ke liye 0.5% ka tax hai aur again jo non-filer hai uske liye ye 1% ka tax ho jata hai. Aur jo freelancers hain, wo freelancers jo ke registered hain Pakistan Software Export Board ke saath, unka jo income tax hai wo hai 0.25% aur jo freelancer registered nahi hain unka ye tax hai 1% ka. Toh dekha aapne ke jo dono categories hain unmein tax ka difference bahut bada ho jata hai. Ab ye toh humne general baat kar li. Agar hum stock market ki baat kar lein toh wahan par bhi tax ke jo hain difference aa jate hain. Jo dividend hai usmein toh jo filer hain unke liye 15% ka tax hai aur jo non-filer hain unke liye ye double ho jata hai, 30%. Agar hum capital gains ki baat kar lein toh yahan bahut zyada detail hai aur ye NCCPL ki website par mentioned hai. Hum isko thodi si detail mein dekh lete hain. Agar aapne stocks jo hain wo July 2013 se pehle ke khareede hue hain toh phir aapke liye chahe aap filer hon ya non-filer, is case mein 0% tax hai. Lekin let's say aapne jo hai wo July 2013 ke baad khareede hain aur 30 June 2022 se pehle khareede hue hain toh phir aapka jo, agar aap filer hain toh aap pe 12.5% ka tax lagega aur non-filer ke liye ye double ho jayega, 25%. Achha, July 2022 aur June 2024 ke beech mein agar aapne jo hai wo stocks khareede hain toh inpe ye jitna zyada aap time hold karte hain us hisab se tax kam hota jata hai. Misal ke taur par agar aap apne stocks jo hain wo ek saal se zyada hold karte hain lekin do saal se kam toh filer ke liye ye again 12.5% ka tax hai aur non-filer ke liye 25%. Isi tarah do se teen saal ke beech mein aapka filer ka 10% ka tax hai aur non-filer ka 20. Aur again teen se chaar saal ke beech mein 7.5% aur 15%, bilkul double ho jata hai ye bhi. Toh is tarah ye mazeed details hain is mein jo ke aap dekh sakte hain, ye aap NCCPL ki website se saari tafseel dekh sakte hain ke kitne time tak hold karenge toh kitna tax hai, main iska link jo hai wo description mein laga dunga. Isi tarah se kuch mutual funds mein bhi hisab hai. Ye bhi tax ki saari details aapko Mutual Funds Association of Pakistan ke under ye aapko mil jati hain ke kitne time tak aap hold karte hain toh filer ke liye, ATL ka matlab hai ke ye Active Taxpayer List mein hain aap yani filer hain, aur jo not appearing in ATL hain wo non-filer hain, toh yahan bhi ye saari details jo hain wo mentioned hain. Aur jo bonus shares milte hain humein stock market mein wahan bhi difference hai, filers ke liye 10%, non-filers ke liye double, 20%. Toh is tarah filer aur non-filer ka jo tax hai wo different hai, filer ke liye koi rate hai, non-filer ke liye koi hai aur ye jo difference hai ye kuch cases mein bahut zyada ho jata hai. Khair, ye kuch taxes hote hain jo final tax hote hain aur kuch taxes hote hain jo adjustable hote hain. Adjustable tax wo hote hain jo jo filers hain wo apne income tax mein se nikalwa sakte hote hain yani ke aap ye tax toh jo hai wo pehle se de chuke hain lekin jab aap FBR ke paas ja kar apna income tax dene lagte hain toh aap batate hain ke bhai ye mera tax katta hua hai pehle se, ye aap meri income tax se nikal lein toh aapka income tax bhi jo hai wo kam ho jata hai aur ye adjustable tax jo hai ye sirf filers adjust karwa sakte hote hain, non-filers ke paas ye option nahi hoti.
