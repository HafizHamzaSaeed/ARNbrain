Acha dividend lene ke chakkar mein apni principal investment ko deemak na lagwa liyega. Aur high quality dividend stocks kaise main dhoondta hoon, uski checklist kya hai main aapse share karta hoon. Aur jo point number four hai, wo sabse important hai.

To sabse pehle, ye sab aap karte honge, dividend yield check karte hain, ke main agar 100 rupay lagaunga to mujhe kitna dividend jo hai wo mausool hoga. Theek hai, aage badhte hain. Phir payout ratio, dividend history. Kahin aisa to nahi hai ke ek do dafa hi bas dividend diya tha, koi koi cash mil gaya tha hukumat se ya kisi aur jagah se, kisi customer se aur wo bas ek dafa payout kar diya aur aage wo nahi karega. To aapne pichle paanch saal ki minimum dividend history check karni hai. Agar consistently dividend diye ja rahe hain, aur boht hi acha ho agar ke increase bhi ho rahe hon har saal, to phir ye ek achi dividend company ban jati hai. Phir aapne ye bhi check karna hai ke company mein koi recent negative development ki wajah se jo hai wo cash levels neeche to nahi chale gaye. Debt to nahi increase ho gayi jo ke future dividend paying capacity jo hai usko hurt kar sakta hai. Aur aakhri sabse important, wo ye hai ke aapne iska comparison karna hai doosri companies ke saath. Doosri companies ke saath aapne dividend yield bhi check karni hai, aapne uski history bhi check karni hai, aapne uske cash levels bhi check karne hain, aur dekhna hai ke profitability increase ho rahi hai ke nahi, taake aap jitna bhi pool hai dividend companies ka, us mein se best select kar sakein.
