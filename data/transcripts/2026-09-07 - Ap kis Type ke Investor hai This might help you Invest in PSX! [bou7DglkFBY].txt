Doctor kabhi bhi ek bachay ko aur ek baray insan ko same dose nahi deta ek dawai ki. Isi wajah se jo log investing mein struggle karte hain, bohat dafa wajah ye hoti hai ke unko pata nahi hota ke unki investor type kya hai. To mere hisab se teen investor types hoti hain: first, conservative, balanced, aur high conviction. Aur aapko apni category ke hisab se best strategy kya follow karni hai, ye bhi main aapko bataunga. Aur agar aap apni investing ko improve karna chahte hain, to isko zaroor understand karein. Conservative investor woh hota hai jisko stocks, even though long-term mein sabse accha return provide karte hain, uski hamesha jo hai, usse ghabrahat hoti hai. Aur ye apni savings ka less than 15% stocks mein invest karte hain. Jo balanced investor hota hai woh somewhere between 20 to 30% apna stocks mein invest kar raha hota hai. Aur jo high conviction investors hote hain, ye companies ko follow bhi kar rahe hote hain, more than 40% apni savings stock market mein lagate hain, kyun ke unko pata hota hai ke long-term mein acchi compounding hogi. Ab conservative investors ko kya karna chahiye? Ke apni knowledge thodi si broaden karein. Thodi si conviction level agar aap apne upar kaam karenge, aap jo hai woh increase kar sakte hain aur conservative investors ne kabhi khud investing nahi karni, khud stock picking nahi karni. Aapne mutual funds aur ETFs ke through koshish karni hai apna confidence barha ke is 15% ko 30% tak leke jaana hai. Phir jo balanced investors hain, ye already usually ETFs aur mutual funds ke through invest karte hain, aur inko bhi koshish karni chahiye ke apna kuch hissa of portfolio jo hai, us mein khud stocks pick karein aur apni jo knowledge hai aur jo confidence hai, usko apply karein. Aur baqi jab aapka ye experience khud hoga, aur aapki knowledge improve hogi to aap is percentage ko bhi increase kar payenge aahista aahista. High conviction investors ko bohat zyada focus karna chahiye, aap already stock market mein accha amount laga rahe hain, to aapne allocation control zaroor karni hai. Allocation control ka matlab hai ke ek stock mein maine bohat zyada percentage of portfolio nahi lagani, kyun ke abhi aap expert investor nahi hain. To aapne mere hisab se max 10% ek stock apne portfolio mein lena hai.
