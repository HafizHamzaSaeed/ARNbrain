To mujhe yaad hai jab maine Joharabad Sugar mein bhi invest kiya kya tha, to koi 15 rupees ke aas-paas uska share hota tha, to maine bas apne paas reminder laga liya tha ke jab yeh 50 cross karega phir hi main isko dekhunga, phir dekhunga us waqt baqi sugar mills ki value jo hai, woh teen-char arab hi chal rahi hai, ya kam ho gayi hai, zyada ho gayi hai, to jab yeh koi do billion ke kareeb iska market cap aa jayega, to phir main sochunga isko sell karne ki. Aur phir aise hi hua ke woh within I think two years jo hai, 60-70 ki range mein pahunch gaya aur us waqt maine phir usko sell kar diya. To instead of roz-roz aap dekhein jisme upper lock lag raha hai, usme enter ho jayein, agle din woh neeche bhi aa sakta hai, phir kabhi kabhi lower lock lagta hai to aap sell bhi nahi kar sakte, aap aisi company dhoondein jo ke apni fair value se koi five times kam pe chal rahi ho, aur ek small percentage of portfolio jo hai na usme laga dein, aur bas phir intezar karein, kabhi jo hai cheh mahine mein hi valuation jo hai woh theek ho jati hai, kabhi ek saal lagta hai, kabhi do saal lagta hai, lekin upside itni zyada hoti hai ke agar aapko do saal bhi intezar karna pade, to profits are really good.

Acha, ab next question pe aa jaate hain, aur yeh question hai Ghani Chemical Industry se related. Danial Mir Sahab puchte hain: "Impact of Mari LNG projects and GCWL plant coming online on the earnings of GCIL and future growth."

Acha, isme pehle do-teen important baatein main aapko batata hoon. Ek aap iska theme samajh lein ke yeh jo contracts ho rahe hain GCIL ke, pehle Mari ke sath hua, phir OGDC ke sath hua, yeh ek theme hai, yeh out of the blue nahi hua. Oil and gas industry mein carbon footprint kam karne ke liye jo hai, is tarah ke projects jo hain woh kiye ja rahe hain, jisse jo waste gases hoti hain, unko reduce karne ke liye ya woh collect karke usse koi aur gas banane ke liye, yeh saare plants jo hain woh lag rahe hain. To aisa bhi ho sakta hai ke in fields ke baad dusri fields pe bhi is tarah ke projects ki announcements jo hain woh aati rahein, aur abhi tak sirf Ghani Chemicals hi thoda initiative show kar rahi hai oil and gas industry ke ki jo companies hain, unke sath kaam karne ke liye. To yeh jo Mari gas ke sath inka JV hai, woh takreeban dhed saal mein online aayega, aur agar woh 100% uski production hoti hai jo planned hai, to usse phir jo GCIL ki taraf profit banega, woh takreeban 2 rupees per share hoga. Lekin pehle saal mein jab yeh online aa jayega FY29 mein, to ho sakta hai ke pehle saal mein 100% production na ho, aahista-aahista time lagta hai ramp up hone mein, to 60-70% assume karna chahiye. Yani ke dhed rupya per share GCIL ki earnings mein add ho sakta hai FY29 mein. Aur jo OGDC ke sath CNG wala project hai, woh paanch saal ka contract hai, takreeban ek saal mein online aayega aur usse jo earnings per share increase hongi, woh takreeban half rupees per share hai. Ek aur factor bhi buhat important hai, yeh to sirf in projects ka impact hai na, ek aur indirect impact yeh bhi hota hai, jaise jab koi ek medium-sized ki company ek badi company ke sath JV karne lag jaye, to us company ki jo profile hoti hai na, woh upar chali jati hai. Investors ka confidence us company mein upar chala jata hai. Aur yeh humne dekha tha Tariq Glass ke case mein jab unho ne us waqt ICI, aur ab Lucky Core Industries ke sath ek glass plant mein JV sign kiya. To ek medium-sized ki company ki jo value realization hoti hai, yani ke log usko agar 6 P/E ratio de rahe hote hain to unka trust badh jata hai aur woh phir 8 P/E ratio dene lag jaate hain, woh factor bhi I think is company mein in play ho sakta hai. Iske sath-sath is company ka ek naya plant aaya tha, aur woh tax-free zone mein located hai, aur uska sabse bada faida yeh hota hai ke ab jaise dekhein LSM growth hogi, ship breaking industry agar phir se shuru hui, to GCIL jo oxygen banata hai, woh ship breaking mein istemaal ho sakti hai, ya oxygen ki price increase ho gayi, to uski wajah se jo additional profit aata hai, jab aap tax-free zone mein hote hain, to agar aapka let's say profit 500 million se badhta hai, because of growth, because of price increase, to woh saara jo hai bottom line mein aa jata hai, uspe 40% jo tax rate hai, woh apply nahi hota. To yeh bhi ek buhat important factor hai, in saari cheezon ko combine karein, to is company ki outlook kafi achi hai, earnings growth ki bhi expectation hain, projects bhi naye kar rahe hain, aur woh jo maine aapko factor bataya ke investor confidence increase ho jata hai kyun ke aap badi companies ke sath partnership kar rahe hote hain, woh logon ko zyada trust aa jata hai management pe, woh wala factor bhi in play hai. Aur isse pehle hum next question pe badhein, main yeh clear kar doon: "This is not financial advice. Please consult your financial advisor before making any investment decision."

Ab humare paas ek GAL se related question aaya hua hai, Ghandhara Automobile. "GAL is fundamentally strong but cash flow from operations is negative. What could be the short term impact on stock price? Either hold it or sell it on current price?"

To main aapko exact recommendation to nahi de sakta, but company zaroor explain kar sakta hoon. Asal mein is company ne jab JAC T9 launch ki 2025 ke shuru mein, to almost unki 3,000 se 4,000 ki bookings hui thin. Aur aahista-aahista jab uski delivery shuru hui March-April mein, aur phir woh aahista-aahista 3-4 hazar gaadiyan deliver huin, to pura time jo hai is company ki achi earnings aati gayin. Lekin aaj ki aap situation dekhenge, even though 4x2 version bhi aa gaya hai same gaadi mein, lekin aaj is company ki utni achi sales expected nahi hain. Pehle ispe on bhi chal raha tha aur delivery mein time bhi tha, ab jo hai yeh aapko readily available ho sakti hai balki kuch jo hai dealership kehti hain hum shayad aapko jo official price hai usse discount bhi de dein. To us tarah ki jo expectation thi ke yeh ek saal mein 4,000-5,000 gaadiyan bikengi, ab shayad 1,500-2,000 gaadiyan biken ek saal mein. Phir ek aur factor kyun ke log ispe dekh rahe hote hain na ke iske pichle saal ki earnings kitni thin aur uske hisab se P/E ratio kya banta hai, to ek to yeh bookings jo hui thi T9 ki initially, uska impact tha, phir ek aur impact yeh hai ke inki jo subsidiary hai, woh imported trucks bhi bechti hai. To agar aap imported trucks ka data dekhein, to FY26 ka jo first half tha, uske andar woh abnormally zyada tha, aur woh phir baad mein jo hai, normalize ho gaya hai. Aur even agar aap ek standard figures jo Pakistan ki hoti hain imported trucks ki usse bhi compare karein, to jo first six months yani ke July to December 2025 tha, woh usse kafi zyada high tha. Lekin jo future earnings hain, woh ho sakta hai past earnings ke muqable mein thodi si different hon. Aur ab jo pickup market hai, usme competition bhi kafi zyada aa raha hai, lekin auto sector mein dekha hai aapne, yeh jo JAC ke sath partnership hai GAL ki, inka global portfolio kafi acha hai. To ho sakta future mein yeh koi nayi gaadi launch karein, usse jo hai ek new business start ho jaye. To abhi tak ki jo soorat-e-haal hai uske hisab se, jo log keh rahe hain ke iska P/E ratio buhat zyada kam hai, main usse agree nahi karta, lekin again, jo maine bataya ke company jo hai koi nayi gaadi bhi lekar aa sakti hai, woh ek element hai jo ke aapko dekhte rehna chahiye. Aur ek aur question jo abhi humne lately index ki apni analysis maine share ki thi 4,50,000 se related aur maine bataya tha ke reserves buhat important hain. Usse related question aaya hua hai, Ehsan Salahuddin Sahab ka, keh rahe hain: "Is there a rule of thumb regarding a threshold of foreign exchange reserves? At what level should we start to consider that a bust cycle might be coming?"

To main reserves wale question ko pehle address kar deta hoon. Dekhein, isme three months of import ek aisa level hota hai, jiske baad aapko apne reserves mazeed increase karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti, aur meri analysis show karti hai ke agar humare reserves 20 billion dollar tak pahunch jaate hain, aur yeh most probably ek saal ke andar pahunch jayenge, to 20 to 25% rule jo hai, aap use kar sakte hain. For example, 20 billion dollar pe hum pahunch gaye, to jab tak hum 16 billion dollar se upar hain, aap yeh assume kar sakte hain ke abhi jo hai bust cycle nahi aaya. 15 to 20% rule jo hai na, woh aap use kar sakte hain. To agar humara let's say reserves 20 billion dollar highest pe pahunchte hain aur hum 17 pe aa jaate hain, to us waqt aapko thoda sa review karna chahiye ke kya yeh one-off repayment thi aur yeh reserve wapis 19, 18 pe chale jayenge, aur agar yeh jo hai downward continue rahenge, to phir yeh bust cycle ka start ho sakta hai. To 15 to 20% rule apne mind mein rakhein, aur agar humare reserves jo hain 25 billion dollar tak chale jaate hain do-teen saalon mein, to phir aap 20% rule use kariyega. Phir jo hai bust cycle ko jab hi consider kariyega jab reserves jo hain 25 se gir ke 20 pe aa jayein. Aur SIP question se related, ke agar koi company apni fair value pe pahunch jati hai, ya koi aasi company jo neeche aa rahi hai, usme SIP continue karni chahiye? To dekhein, SIP aur lumpsum investment ko agar dekhein na, lumpsum investment hamesha us company mein hoti hai, jo aapko buhat zyada undervalued lag rahi hai. Aapko lagta hai ke ek se dhed saal ke andar hi na iski price buhat zyada increase honi hai, to mere paas abhi jo amount hai mujhe isme invest karni chahiye. Aur SIP aap us company mein karte hain, jo ke fundamentally buhat zyada strong ho, uska long term track record bhi ho, aur kyun ke aapne to agle teen char paanch saal ke liye SIP karte rehna hai. Agar aapko lagta hai ke usne ek-do saal mein hi jo hai apni undervaluation khatam kar deni hai, to phir to aap usme buhat zyada invest kar hi nahi payenge. To yeh aap apne paas rule bana lein ke jo "mispriced securities" wali strategy hoti hai, woh lumpsum investment pe zyada apply karni chahiye, aur jo "good companies at decent prices for the long term", woh SIP strategy se zyada feasible hoti hain. Aur isi cheez ko jab aap follow karenge, to jab ek baat aati hai ke ab koi cheez agar apni fair value pe pahunch gayi hai, to usme SIP continue karna chahiye ya usko sell kar dena chahiye, to uska jawab bhi isi cheez mein hai. Agar company "good quality" ki hai, aur uska purana track record bhi buhat acha hai, aur aage koi risk bhi nahi aa rahe, to aap apni SIP usme continue karein. Lekin agar company jo hai "medium quality" ki hai, ya thodi "low quality" ki thi lekin aapne buhat kam price pe usko khareed liya tha, aur ab woh apni fair value pe aa gayi hai, to phir aap usko sell kar dein to behtar hai, aur dusri koi opportunity dhoond lein.
