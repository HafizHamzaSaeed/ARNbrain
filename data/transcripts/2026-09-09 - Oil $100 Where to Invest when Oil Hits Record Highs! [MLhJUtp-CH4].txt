Jab oil 100 dollar ka ho jaye to investing kaise karni chahiye?

Step number one hota hai jab bhi oil prices increase hoti hain aapne pehle to check karna hota hai ke is se bull market framework jo hai wo intact rehta hai? Agar wo intact rehta hai to phir jin companies ko oil price increase hone se faida ho raha hota hai unke shares achay perform karne ke chances hote hain. To kyunke Pakistan ke dollar reserves bohot tight hain aur Government of Pakistan subsidy nahi de rahi oil prices pe to pehla point to tick ho jata hai.

Ab aa jaate hain kaun se sectors pe effect nahi hoga oil prices increase hone ka ya phir wo us ke beneficiary bhi ho sakte hain. To dosto banking sector aur fertilizer sector jo hai wo oil prices se unaffected hota hai. Iski top companies mein aap bilkul investment kar sakte hain.

Aur do mere favorite sectors jinko main khud bohot ghaur se dekh raha hoon, ek hai oil and gas exploration sector aur is mein OGDC mere hisab se sabse achi company hai current prices pe, aur doosra hai refineries. Refineries oil price increase hone ki wajah se aur jo nayi refinery policy aa rahi hai us wajah se ye sector jo hai bohot zyada ubhar ke saamne aa raha hai. Pichle saalon ki policies ki wajah se ye bohot undervalued chal raha tha aur ab iski value realization ho rahi hai. Aur refinery sector ki sabse strong company hai Attock Refinery.
