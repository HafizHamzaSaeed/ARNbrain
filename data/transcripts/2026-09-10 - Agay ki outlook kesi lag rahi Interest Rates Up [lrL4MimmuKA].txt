Toh aaj hum jab khade hain toh yeh intensity jo hai thodi peak pe gayi hui hai aur agle 10-15 din mein most probably jo hai yeh neeche aayegi.

Aur jaise aapko pata hai, aaj kal ke scenario ke hisab se US-Iran tensions aur uski wajah se jo oil price mein movement aati hai, wohi sabse important cheez hai.
