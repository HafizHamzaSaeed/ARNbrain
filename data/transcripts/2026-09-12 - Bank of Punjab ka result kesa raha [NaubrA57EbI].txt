Bank of Punjab ka second quarter 2023 ka result kaisa raha? And the answer is bahut accha. Profits bhi increase hue aur company ne jo new dividend policy apni lekar aayi hai, uske hisab se ye ek bahut hi zabardast dividend stock bhi ban gaya hai. Aaiye aapko batata hoon. Latest result mein company ne 6 month mein Rs 1.5 dividend jo hai wo announce kiya hai, jo ke pehle company ki policy thi Rs 1 every 6 months. To is dividend ko agar hum ab annualize karein aur teen, sawa teen rupay ka saal ka dividend assume kar lein, to company ki current share price jab isme se dividend minus ho jayega to takriban ye 10% dividend yield offer kar raha hai. Performance bhi bank ki kafi achi rahi, deposits bhi increase ho rahe hain, current account ki percentage bhi increase ho rahi hai, aur one-off impacts ke bawajood company ne 6 mahino mein takriban 3 rupay ka profit per share show kiya hai. Aur ek aur uncertainty jo bank se related chal rahi thi, kyunki is bank ka jo pura turnaround hua hai, usme Zafar Masud saab ka bahut zyada role hai, to ye thodi si chahmaguiyan chal rahi thi ke shayad National Bank ke president jo hain wo Zafar Masud saab tayyanat kar diye jayenge, lekin ab National Bank ka bhi notification aa gaya hai aur isse clarity aa gayi hai ke Bank of Punjab ke helm pe Zafar Masud saab jo hai wo continue karenge aur is bank ko jaise pichle saalon mein turnaround kar rahe hain, mazed improve karte rahenge.
