Sabse pehle dosto samajh lete hain ke fertilizer sector ek defensive sector kyun hai? Aap is cheez ko understand karein ke Pakistan mein fertilizer sector mein zyada tar jo hai, wo urea produce hota hai. Aur Pakistan mein ek poora system aisa bana hua hai ke urea banta hai mainly gas se, aur Government of Pakistan ki poori koshish hoti hai aur ye poori policy mein ek tarah se banaya hua hai ke hum aapko ek fixed rate pe jo hai gas dein ge aur uske hisab se aapne jo urea ki price hai, wo farmers ke liye more or less stable rakhni hai taake poora Pakistan ka jo agriculture ka system hai, wo theek tareeqe se chalta rahe. Aur is poore system mein, kyunke achhi price pe gas provide ki jaati hai, toh Pakistan mein jo urea bag ki price hai, wo imported price se hamesha almost jo hai, wo kam rehti hai. Aur aaj kal, jaise duniya mein oil crisis chal raha hai uski wajah se urea bhi mehanga ho jata hai, toh aaj kal toh jo international price chal rahi hai, us se aadhi keemat se bhi kam pe jo hai Pakistan mein local price chal rahi hai. Is wajah se, in companies ke jo profits hain, wo stable rehte hain. Agar let's say kabhi gas price increase ho bhi jaye, toh ye apni thodi si bag ki price increase karke apne profits jo hain unko maintain kar lete hain. Aur kyunke Pakistan mein jitna urea banta hai, utna bikta bhi hai, uski utni demand bhi hoti hai, toh is wajah se quantity point of view se bhi companies ko utna bada challenge face nahi karna padta ke is saal jo hai meri sale hogi ki nahi hogi. Uski wajah se prices bhi stable rehti hain aur aapke profits jo hain, wo bhi more or less stable rehte hain. Aur kyunke companies ne agar expansion karni hogi, ek naya plant lagana hoga, toh pehle unko ek gas allocation ki zaroorat hogi. Toh business point of view se bhi ye poori economics hain duniya ki aur Pakistan ki, uske hisab se bhi ye ek defensive sector ban jata hai, aur kyunke dividend bhi achha aa raha hota hai, toh jo stock market ki valuation hoti hai, uske point of view se bhi log isko kaafi zyada pasand karte hain.
