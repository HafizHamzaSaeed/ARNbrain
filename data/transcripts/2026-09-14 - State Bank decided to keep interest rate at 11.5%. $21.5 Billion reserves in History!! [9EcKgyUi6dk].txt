[Published: 2026-09-14]

State Bank of Pakistan ne interest rates ko 11.5% pe maintain kar diya hai aur ye stock market ke liye jo hai kafi positive news hai. Thore se fears ho rahe the weekend pe jo oil prices barhi thi, ke shayad State Bank jo hai wo isko 12% ya 12.5% kar de, lekin is poori outlook mein State Bank ne interest rate ko maintain bhi kiya hai aur do aisi cheezein bhi batayi hain, jis se lagta hai ke aage bhi interest rate increase hone ke chances agar ye jang continue bhi hoti hai, to shayad na hon. Pehli cheez, State Bank ne acknowledge kiya ke Government of Pakistan aur refineries ne mil ke jo diesel margins ko reduce kiya tha, usne bahut bara role play kiya hai, diesel ki price ko control karne mein aur diesel ki price se hi asal inflation aati hai. Isi wajah se pichle ek do mahino mein bhi inflation us speed se nahi barhi, jo ke barh sakti thi, aur aage bhi kyunki ye continue rahega, to inflation controlled rehne ke chances hain. Is ke sath sath State Bank of Pakistan ne bahut bari khushkhabri sunayi aur aap ko jaan ke khushi hogi ke Pakistan ne apni poori history ke highest dollar reserves jo hain, wo aaj achieve kar liye hain of 21.4 billion dollars. Aaj tak kabhi State Bank of Pakistan ne 21.4 billion dollars ka number jo hai wo achieve nahi kiya. Jab hamare reserves itne achhe hote hain, to devaluation ke chances kam hote hain aur future mein inflation control rakhne ke chances bhi kafi hote hain. Isi wajah se aaj ki State Bank ki ye jo briefing thi, ye bahut positive hai aur is se aage ki outlook jo hai, wo strengthen hoti hai.
