kuch hi din pehle Government of Pakistan ne teen billion dollar Eurobond float karke ek naya record bana diya aur iska impact stock market pe aur aapki investing pe kya hoga? Number one isse market jo hai zyada resilient ho jaati hai. Aapne dekha tha jab jang shuru hui thi jab bhi oil hundred dollar pe jaata tha toh kis tareeqe se stock market jo hai woh 5,000 point, 6,000 point girti thi. Jab aapke reserves ache hote hain toh economy ke andar shock absorber aa jaate hain aur isi wajah se ab phir se jab oil hundred dollar ka hua hai toh stock market jo hai mushkil se 1% ke around jo hai woh neeche girti hai. So first point Eurobond se aapki economy zyada resilient ho jaati hai. Doosra important point hai ke aapne jo har saal apne friendly countries se loans liye hue the jinko aapko rollover karwana padta tha, uski amount jo hai ab kam ho jayegi. Aaj se ek saal pehle takreeban five billion dollar Saudi Arabia, four billion dollars China, aur teen billion dollar UAE, har saal aapko 12 billion dollar rollover karane hote the. Aur ab aapne UAE ka karza bhi wapas kar diya hai aur jo Saudi Arabia ka five billion dollar ka loan hai usme se bhi kuch portion jo hai woh do se teen saal ke liye extend ho chuka hai. Toh ab aapko takreeban China ke four billion dollar aur thode bohot jo hain Saudi Arabia ka jo loan hai woh rollover karana hoga har saal jiski amount total 12 billion dollar se gir ke paanch se che billion dollar pe aa jayegi. Toh again, iska bhi ek positive impact hoga, har waqt jo log sochte rehte hain ke har saal karze rollover karane hain, koi bhi problem ho gayi toh economy pe impact aa sakta hai, uska risk bhi kam ho gaya hai. Aur is Eurobond ka jo rate hai woh friendly countries se jo karza mila hua tha usse takreeban teen se chaar percent higher hai jiski agar hum amount differential ki calculate karein toh yeh takreeban saal ke 100 million dollars ban jaate hain. Toh mere hisab se rollover risk ko reduce karne ke liye aur apni friendly countries pe reliance kam karne ke liye agar aap saal ke 100 million dollar extra pay kar rahe hain toh yeh ek bura sauda nahi hai.
