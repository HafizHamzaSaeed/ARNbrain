Pakistan's first financial freedom movement ka phase 2 start ho gaya hai. Aur mein aa raha hoon aapke sheher, aapke school, aapke college, aur aapke office aapko financially literate banane, bilkul free of cost. Aur next 10 minutes mein, mein aapko bataunga ke kaise mein aur aap milkar, 10 lakh Pakistanis ko financial freedom achieve karwa sakte hain.

Assalam-o-alaikum dosto, mein hoon Abdul Rehman Najam, aapka investing friend. Pakistan mein bachpan se hum seekhte hain ke padhna kaise hai, career kaise banana hai, aur paisa kaise kamana hai. Lekin jo paisa hum poori zindagi kamate hain, usko manage kaise karna hai, us paise se aur paisa kaise banana hai, yeh humein properly sikhaya nahi jata. Schools mein bhi nahi sikhaya jata, colleges mein bhi nahi sikhaya jata, aur university mein bhi nahi sikhaya jata. Aur mere hisab se, Pakistan mein ab sirf financial literacy ka issue nahi raha. Yeh ek financial literacy crisis ban gaya hai. Isi crisis ko address karne ke liye kuch mahine pehle humne ek mission start kiya tha: 1 million Pakistanis ko financial freedom achieve karwane ka mission. Aur is mission ke first phase mein, 15,000 se zyada logon ne humein join kiya. Lahore, Karachi, aur Islamabad mein 1,500 se zyada logon ne physically hamare sessions ka hissa bane. Aur honestly dosto, is poori journey ne mujhe ek bohot hi important cheez realize karwayi. Agar humein genuinely 1 million Pakistanis tak financial literacy pohnchani hai, financial freedom achieve karwana hai, toh aapko hamare paas nahi, balki mujhe aapke paas aana hoga. Aur isiliye aaj se launch ho raha hai Pakistan ki first financial freedom movement ka phase 2. Aur is mein, mein exactly yahi karne ja raha hoon. Ab hum financial literacy ko Pakistan ke har school, har college, har university, har company office, har organization ke staff tak lekar jayenge. Maine full plan banaya hai ke har din ek se do session hum organize karein, kabhi school mein, kabhi let's say kisi bari company ke head office mein. Har tarah ka insaan aur uski needs ke hisab ki financial literacy. For example, agar hum Foodpanda ke riders ke liye session kar rahe hain, toh woh bohot different hoga ek college students ke session se. Aur yeh college students ka session jo hai, yeh bilkul different hoga let's say ek large multinational organization ke staff ke sath. Toh dosto, mera target hai ke har din agar hum 500 logon tak directly financial literacy pohnchayein, toh agle 3 se 5 saalon mein hum milkar 10 lakh gharon mein financial literacy pohncha sakte hain. Aur is mission mein mujhe aapki help chahiye. Agar aap kisi school, university, office, ya kisi bhi organization ke sath associated hain, aur chahte hain ke mein aur meri team aapki organization mein aakar aapke students ya staff ke sath financial literacy par ek zabardast session conduct karein, toh screen par diye gaye number par hamari team ko reach out karein. Aur yeh sessions ARN Financials ki taraf se bilkul free honge, even agar humein doosre sheher bhi travel karna pare. Aur dosto, yeh financial freedom movement ka jo phase 2 hai, yeh sirf different venues par seminars organize karne ka plan nahi hai. Is phase 2 ke peeche ek bohot important realization hai, aur woh realization yeh hai ke financial literacy sab ke liye important hai, lekin financial literacy sab ke liye same nahi ho sakti. Aur yeh baat mujhe sab se strongly realize pata hai kab hui? Jab mein TEDx Talk dekar stage se neeche utra. Us din bohot se log mere paas aaye, aur unhone mujhse ek aisi baat kahi, jiske baad honestly Pakistan's first financial freedom movement ka idea mere mind mein properly click hua. Agar aap mujhe start se follow kar rahe hain, toh aapko maloom hoga ke mein do se teen saal se YouTube, social media aur different platforms par investing aur financial freedom ke baare mein baat karta hua aa raha hoon. Lekin jab maine TEDx ke stage par financial freedom ke topic par baat karne ki opportunity mili, toh mere liye woh sirf ek talk nahi thi, woh ek mauka tha asaan lafzon mein logon ko batana ke aap financial freedom kaise achieve kar sakte hain. Toh dosto, mein stage par gaya, aur yeh taqreeban ek saal pehle ki baat hai, financial freedom ka framework explain kiya. Lekin mere liye sab se important cheez stage ke baad hui. TEDx Talk ke baad jab bohot se log mere paas aaye aur financial freedom aur financial literacy ke baare mein baat karna shuru ki, toh un mein se 90% logon ne mujhe basically yeh kaha ke humein yeh cheezein kisi ne kabhi sikhayi hi nahi. Kuch logon ka yeh kehna tha ke kaash humein yeh sab pehle pata hota, hum bhi apni zindagi behtar kar lete. Aur kuch log toh yeh keh kar hi khush ho gaye ke hamara time toh chala gaya, ab humein samajh aa gaya hai aur ab kam az kam hamari jo next generation hogi, woh hamari mistakes ko repeat nahi karegi. Toh dosto, yeh haalat hai Pakistan mein financial literacy ki. Aur us waqt mujhe ek cheez bohot strongly hit hui. Agar mera mission genuinely 1 million Pakistanis ko financial freedom achieve karwane ka hai, toh yeh mission sirf camera ke saamne baat kar ke achieve nahi ho sakta. Even though aaj kal social media ka daur hai aur aap jo hain kisi se bhi digitally connect ho sakte hain, lekin kisi jagah pe jaana aur physically kisi insaan ki problem samajh ke usko solutions provide karna, uski ek apni value hai. Mujhe logon ke paas khud jaana hoga, unse khud milna hoga, unke questions sunne honge aur samajhna hoga ke unki actual financial problems kya hain. Aur basically yahi Pakistan's First Financial Movement ki base hai, logon ki exact reality dekh kar har insaan ke hisab se jo hai unko financial freedom movement ka best path jo hai woh provide karna. Humne movement ko officially 16 October 2025 ko launch kiya tha, aur honestly mujhe nahi pata tha ke response itna zyada strong hoga. Sirf pehle do din mein 1,500 se 2,000 log jo hain hamari movement ka woh hissa ban gaye, aur ab tak 15,000 se zyada log jo hain is special movement ko join kar chuke hain. Aur isiliye Lahore mein hamara first event, humne financial freedom ko simple aur practical banane ke liye apna Four-Level of Financial Freedom Framework introduce karaya. Mera objective tha ke session ke baad har banda kam az kam do questions ka jawab de sake: Mein financially abhi kis level par hoon, aur mera next step kya hona chahiye. Kyunki mujhe lagta hai ke logon ko sirf motivation nahi chahiye, unko clarity aur plan chahiye. Aur yahin se phase 2 ka sab se important idea aata hai. Agar financial literacy har Pakistani tak pohnchni hai, toh hum har Pakistani ko same financial education jo hai woh nahi de sakte. Sochiye ek banda jo bilkul zero se start kar raha hai, for example ek delivery rider, uski financial priorities aur jo usko shuru mein karna chahiye woh bilkul different hai. Ek university student ke paas shayad abhi significant income na ho, lekin uske paas time hai. Ek salaried professional ko budgeting, saving, financial planning, aur investing ko apni monthly income ke sath kaise manage karna hai, usko woh seekhna hai. Aur ek aisa person jo life ke later stage mein hai aur already savings accumulate kar chuka hai, uski financial literacy ki needs naturally bohot different hongi. Isi realization ke around hum financial freedom movement ka phase 2 build kar rahe hain. October se hum movement ko schools, colleges, universities, offices, aur different organizations ke andar lekar ja rahe hain. Aur objective yeh nahi hai ke har jagah ja kar ek generic motivational speech dena aur wapas aa jana. Hum audience ke according financial education ko relevant banana chahte hain. Students ke sath un topics par baat hogi jo unki stage of life ke liye important hain. Professionals ke sath unki financial journey ke relevant issues discuss honge. Aur jo log already earning aur saving kar rahe hain, unke liye conversation us level ki hogi. Financial literacy, saving, investing, compounding, financial planning, aur productive assets create karna. Objective yeh hai ke jab koi bhi person hamare session se bahar nikle, toh woh sirf motivated na ho, use clarity ho. Mein financially kahan stand karta hoon? Mujhe kya improve karna hai? Aur mera next step kya hona chahiye? Kyunki motivation shayad kuch din chalti hai, lekin framework aapko apni financial journey samajhne mein help karta hai. Aur jaise maine bataya, yeh sessions bilkul free honge. Aap log kyunki hamare channel se associated hain, aapka kaam sirf itna hai ke aap humein apni organization tak pohncha dein, baqi responsibility hamari. Aur dosto, yahan mein aapko clear karta chaloon ke yeh aapki responsibility hai kyunki aap is stage pe jab Pakistan mein financial literacy bilkul nahi hai, aap hamare channel se associated hain, hamari baatein sunte hain, us se aapko fayda hota hai, toh yeh aapki bhi ek responsibility hai ke aap financial literacy mein Pakistan mein pioneer hain, ek early adopter hain, toh aapne isko next level tak har insaan tak pohnchane mein hamari madad bhi karni hai aur yeh aapki responsibility bhi hai. Toh dosto, financial freedom movement ka phase change ho raha hai. Target wahi hai: 1 million Pakistanis ko financial freedom achieve karwana.
