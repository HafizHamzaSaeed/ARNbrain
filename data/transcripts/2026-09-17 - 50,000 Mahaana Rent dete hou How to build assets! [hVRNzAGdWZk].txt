Agar aap har mahine 50,000 rupaye ka rent dete ho, aur har saal obviously rent jo hai 8 se 10 percent badhta bhi hai, aglay 25 saal mein aap denge takreeban 6 crore rupaye rent. Jee haan, 6 crore rupaye. Aur 25 saal baad uske badle aapke paas kya hoga? Ghar? Nahi, wo to malik makan ka hoga. Koi aur asset? Nahi, kuch bhi nahi. Sirf rent ki purani raseedein. Ab mein ye nahi keh raha ke abhi ghar khareed lo aur ye rent ke paise bacha lo. Ghar to kafi mehenga aata hai, dedh se do crore ka. Itne paise kiske paas hain aaj kal? To iske bajaye ek aur viable tareeqa aap se share karta hoon. Agar aap 50,000 rupaye rent de sakte ho, to shayad aap monthly 20,000 rupaye alag bhi kar sakte ho, savings ke liye, kharche control karke ya income ko badha kar. Aur ye 20,000 har mahine PSX mein, aur jaise har saal rent 8 se 10 percent badhta hai, is monthly contribution ko bhi aapne har saal 8 se 10 percent barhana hai. Aur PSX ki long term average compounding 17 percent ki jab apna kaam karegi, 25 saal mein aapka portfolio ban sakta hai 15 crore rupees. Aur inflation ke hisab se jo ghar aaj dedh crore ka hai, 25 saal baad wo 15 crore ke aas paas ho sakta hai. To aapko to rent dena parega, lekin aapke bacho ko ye rent ki aziyat se guzarna nahi parega, agar aap apne rent se aadhe se bhi kam raqam jo hai wo monthly save karlein aur invest kardien. Aur agar aap chahte hain ke hamari team aapko reach out kare, to hamari website pe jayein, Financial Freedom pe click karein, aur apni details share karein aur hum aapko reach out karenge.
