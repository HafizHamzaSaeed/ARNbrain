Doosra step hai ke aapne apne loan ki effective cost nikaalni hai, jo cost aapko aam tor par batayi jaati hai apne loan ke liye, woh nominal rate hota hai, usmein sirf interest rate shamil hota hai, lekin jag aap actually mein loan lete hain, to usmein kuch aur costs bhi jo hain, woh shamil ho rahi hoti hain, jaise ke loan ki processing fees ho gayi ya koi deegar charges ho gaye, to yeh saari costs milakar jo aapki effective cost hoti hai, woh badh jaati hai. To is liye aapko apne loan ki effective cost ka pata hona chahiye, yeh hai iska formula, jis ke zariye aap apne loan ki effective cost nikaal sakte hain aur yeh jo cost hai, yeh saalana buniyaad par check ki jaati hai. Jo loan ka nominal rate hota hai, woh to humein pata hai ke KIBOR rate ke saath change hota rehta hai. Khair, teesra step hai ke agar to yeh effective cost 10% se kam hai, jaisa ke Apna Ghar Scheme ke karze ki subsidy window mein thi, to fir karza pehle wapas karne ki zaroorat nahi, is case mein invest karna behtar hai. Lekin agar to yeh effective cost 15% se zyada hai, jaisa ke hamare conventional home loan wale case mein thi, ya jo aap gaari ke liye loan lete hain ya bank se personal loan milta hai, credit cards ke loans hote hain, in cases mein loan pehle wapas karna behtar hai, kyunki in loans ki jo effective cost hoti hai, woh bahut zyada hoti hai. Agar yeh effective cost 10% aur 15% ke beech mein hai, to fir aap dono kaam saath saath kar sakte hain. Thodi si extra amount jo hai, woh aap loan jaldi wapas karne mein laga dein aur thodi si extra amount jo hai, woh aap stock market mein invest kar dein.
