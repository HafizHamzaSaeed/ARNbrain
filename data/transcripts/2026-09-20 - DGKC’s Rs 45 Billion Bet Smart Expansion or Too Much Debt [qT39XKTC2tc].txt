Rafhan Maize acquisition. DGKC ne Rafhan Maize ka 32.7% Rs. 9,800 per share ki value pe acquire kiya hai. Jiske liye approximately 30 billion investment required hai. Jis par approximately saal ki jo interest cost hogi, wo koi 2 billion cross kar jayegi.
