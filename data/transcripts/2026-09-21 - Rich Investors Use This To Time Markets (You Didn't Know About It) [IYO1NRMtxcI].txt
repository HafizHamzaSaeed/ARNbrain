Iske sath sath jo humara KSE 100 Index hai na, uske andar jo dividends aate hain na, woh bhi usmein include ho jaate hain. Company ki value se dividend nikal jata hai kyunke aapke bank account mein aa jata hai woh value, lekin index mein include hoti hai. Woh koi dhaai se teen percent ki hoti hai saal ki kyunke sab companies toh nahi deti na dividend, toh index ki average 3% hoti hai. Toh chaar saalon mein agar 3-3% wahan se bhi aaye toh 12-13% aur aa jayega index karne ke liye, toh is tareeqe se hum 4,50,000 aur 5,00,000 ke beech mein kahin pahunch jaate hain.
